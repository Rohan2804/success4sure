Are You Interviewing Elsewhere? How to Answer Truthfully

Last updated 25 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · Free

The question: “Are you interviewing with other companies?”

Why interviewers ask this

Recruiters ask this for practical reasons: to estimate how quickly they need to move, how competitive your profile is, and whether you are focused on a particular kind of role. A candidate interviewing with only unrelated companies may not be serious about this field. On campus, placement rules sometimes restrict how many offers you can accept, so HR may also be checking your status. An honest, calm answer that shows focus and genuine preference for this role is far better than pretending they are your only option or bluffing about offers you do not have.

How to answer

  1. Be truthful. Saying you are exploring a few similar roles is normal and expected.
  2. Mention the type of roles or companies, not necessarily names, unless asked directly.
  3. Show consistency: the other roles should be in the same field, which proves your focus.
  4. Make clear why this role is a strong preference, so they do not see you as likely to walk away.

Sample answers

Fresher (off-campus job search)

Yes, I have applied to a few roles in banking operations and customer service at private banks and NBFCs, and I have one more interview next week. All of them are in the same area, because that is where I want to start my career. Among them, this role is my first preference because of the structured training programme and the chance to rotate between teams in the first year.

Experienced (holding an offer)

I will be open with you. I have an offer from another NBFC for a similar collections manager role, and I need to respond to them within about ten days. I am still interviewing here because your company's focus on rural markets and the team size are closer to what I want. If things move forward here within that time, this would be my preferred choice.

Campus placement (final-year student)

I am sitting for the placement process this season, and I have applied to three finance and analytics roles that match my specialisation. I have not received any offer yet. I prepared most carefully for this one because the role combines data work with lending, which is exactly what my internship was about.

Use these as a structure, not a script — replace the placeholders with your own facts.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Inventing offers to create pressure; these are easy to check and damage trust.
  • Naming every company and every round in detail.
  • Sounding desperate by saying this is your only chance.
  • Revealing applications to unrelated fields that make you look unfocused.

Likely follow-up questions

  • Which role would you choose if you got both offers?
  • What salary have they offered?
  • How soon do you need to decide?

FAQs

Should I tell them about an offer I already have?

Yes, if you genuinely have one and a deadline is approaching. It helps them plan, and some employers will speed up the process. Share the timeline politely, without making it sound like an ultimatum. You do not have to share the exact salary unless you choose to.

Is it okay to say I am not interviewing anywhere else?

Only if it is true. It is not a disadvantage, but recruiters may assume you are not actively searching or that your profile is limited. You can add that you are being selective and applied here first because of a specific reason, which turns it into a positive.
