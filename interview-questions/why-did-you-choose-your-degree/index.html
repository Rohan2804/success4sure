Why Did You Choose This Course? Answers for Freshers

Last updated 23 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · Free

The question: “Why did you choose your graduation stream?”

Why interviewers ask this

Bank and corporate panels often ask this, particularly when your degree and the job do not obviously match, such as an engineer applying for a bank PO post. They want to know whether your choices are thought through or simply followed the crowd, and whether you can connect your education to your present goals. It is fine to admit family or peer influence; Indian students often choose streams at seventeen. What matters is what you took from the degree and how it led you to this application.

How to answer

  1. Give an honest reason for your original choice, even if it was influenced by family or marks.
  2. Mention what you genuinely liked or learnt in the course.
  3. Connect the degree's skills to the job, especially if the link is not obvious.
  4. If your interest shifted, explain how and when, without dismissing your degree.

Sample answers

B.Com fresher applying for an accounts role

I chose commerce after Class 10 because I enjoyed maths and my uncle runs a small trading business. I used to watch him manage stock and accounts, and I wanted to understand how it worked. B.Com gave me a strong base in accounting, taxation and business law, and my favourite subject was Cost Accounting because it showed how small decisions affect profit. That is why an accounts role is a natural next step.

Engineering graduate applying for a bank PO role

To be honest, I chose engineering because most of my friends and my family saw it as a safe career. I liked the problem-solving, especially in subjects like Operations Research. In my final year I took an elective in engineering economics and also helped my father apply for a business loan, and I became interested in how banks assess borrowers. Engineering taught me analytical thinking and discipline, which I think will help in credit and operations work at a bank.

BA Economics fresher applying to an AMC

I chose economics because I was always curious about why prices go up and how the government decides budgets. In college, I enjoyed monetary economics most, especially topics like interest rates and inflation. That naturally led me to markets and investing. I have cleared the NISM Series V-A exam, and I want to start my career in mutual funds where that understanding is directly useful.

Use these as a structure, not a script — replace the placeholders with your own facts.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Saying 'my parents forced me' without showing any engagement with the subject.
  • Criticising your own degree as useless.
  • Being unable to name a subject you liked or something you learnt.
  • Giving a long story about Class 10 and 12 marks.

Likely follow-up questions

  • Why did you not take a job in your core field?
  • Which subject did you find hardest?
  • Will you regret leaving engineering?

FAQs

Is it a problem if my degree is unrelated to finance?

Not for most entry-level finance and banking roles, which accept graduates from any stream. What helps is showing how your skills transfer and what you have done to learn the field, such as a certification, course or self-study. Bank PO panels in particular see candidates from every background.

Should I mention my Class 10 and 12 choices?

Only briefly, if they explain how you ended up in your degree. Interviewers are mainly interested in your graduation and what it led to. Spend most of your answer on what you learnt and how it fits the job.

More Freshers questions

  • Why did you have backlogs in college?
  • Why are your marks or grades low?
  • Tell me about your final-year project.
  • You have no work experience. Why should we hire you?
  • What did you learn from your internship?
  • What are your short-term career goals?
  • Why are you not going for higher studies?
