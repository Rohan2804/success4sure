Interview questions and sample answers

100 common interview questions for freshers, bank jobs and finance roles — why they are asked, how to structure your answer, sample answers and mistakes to avoid.

Last updated 19 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · Free

HR basics

  • Tell me about yourself.
  • Why should we hire you?
  • What are your strengths?
  • What is your greatest weakness?
  • Where do you see yourself in five years?
  • Why do you want to work for our company?
  • Why did you leave your last job?
  • What do you know about our company?
  • Are you willing to relocate?
  • What motivates you?
  • How would you describe yourself in three words?
  • What are your hobbies and interests?
  • Do you have any questions for us?
  • Are you interviewing with other companies?
  • Why do you want to change your career?
  • How do you define success?
  • Are you comfortable working long hours or weekends?

Freshers

  • Why is there a gap in your education or career?
  • Why did you have backlogs in college?
  • Why are your marks or grades low?
  • Tell me about your final-year project.
  • Why did you choose your graduation stream?
  • You have no work experience. Why should we hire you?
  • What did you learn from your internship?
  • What are your short-term career goals?
  • Why are you not going for higher studies?
  • Are you willing to sign a service agreement or bond?
  • What is your biggest achievement so far?
  • What do you expect from your first job?

Behavioural

  • Tell me about a time you failed.
  • Tell me about a conflict you had with a colleague.
  • Tell me about a time you led a team.
  • How do you handle pressure and stress at work?
  • Tell me about a mistake you made at work and how you handled it.
  • Tell me about a time you disagreed with your manager.
  • How do you handle criticism or negative feedback?
  • Tell me about a time you had to manage several deadlines at once.
  • Tell me about a time you dealt with a difficult customer.
  • Tell me about a time you showed initiative.
  • Tell me about a time you faced an ethical dilemma at work.
  • Tell me about a time you had to adapt to a big change at work.
  • Tell me about a time you persuaded someone to change their mind.

Banking jobs

  • Why do you want to join the banking sector?
  • Why do you want to work in a public sector bank and not a private bank?
  • What is an NPA? Explain it in your own words.
  • What is the repo rate and how does it affect a bank's customers?
  • What is inflation and how does RBI control it?
  • A customer is shouting at your counter because his work is delayed. What will you do?
  • Are you ready to work in a rural branch or a remote area?
  • What is financial inclusion and why is it important for India?
  • Will digital banking make bank branches irrelevant?
  • Why do you want to join the Reserve Bank of India?
  • You are an engineer. Why do you want a banking job?
  • How would you explain UPI to a first-time rural customer?
  • Why is KYC important, and what will you do if a customer refuses to give documents?
  • A regular customer asks you to skip a procedure just this once. What will you do?
  • How would you increase CASA deposits at your branch?
  • What recent banking or economic news have you read?
  • What is the difference between CRR and SLR?
  • You are a postgraduate. Why are you applying for a clerk post?
  • A customer tells you money was stolen from his account after a phone call. What will you do?
  • Can you speak the local language of the state you have applied for?

Finance & advisory

  • Explain SIP to a client who has never invested in mutual funds.
  • A client's mutual fund has fallen 10% in a month and he is panicking. What do you tell him?
  • How do you decide which financial product is suitable for a client?
  • Why did you take the NISM certification, and how will it help in this role?
  • A client says he wants high returns with no risk. How will you respond?
  • Your manager asks you to push a high-commission product that does not suit the client. What will you do?
  • Why do you want to work in an asset management company?
  • Why do you want to work in a stock broking firm?
  • Pitch me a stock.
  • A client asks: should I put my money in a mutual fund or a fixed deposit?
  • A friend at a listed company tells you about an upcoming merger before it is announced. What do you do?
  • Why do you want to join an NBFC instead of a bank?
  • How do you keep yourself updated on financial markets?
  • Why should a client invest through you when direct plans have lower costs?
  • How will you build your client base as a new wealth or relationship manager?
  • What is the difference between a mutual fund distributor and an investment adviser?
  • You notice a colleague has uploaded client forms with signatures that do not look genuine. What will you do?
  • A client asks you which stock he should buy today. What do you say?

Sales roles

  • Sell me this pen.
  • How do you handle rejection in sales?
  • Are you comfortable working with sales targets?
  • How would you approach a cold call to a customer who has never heard of us?
  • How would you cross-sell to an existing customer without being pushy?
  • Sell me a term insurance policy.
  • Why do you want to work in sales?
  • What do you understand about the role of a relationship manager?
  • What will you do if you miss your monthly sales target?
  • How would you convince a kirana shop owner to start using our payment app or QR?
  • The customer says, 'I will think about it.' What do you do next?
  • Can you bring business from your own network?

Salary & offers

  • What are your salary expectations?
  • What is your current CTC?
  • How do I negotiate a job offer?
  • What is your notice period?
  • Why are you asking for such a big salary hike?
  • Would you accept a lower salary than you expected?
  • What questions should I ask about CTC, in-hand salary and variable pay?
  • What will you do if your current employer makes a counter-offer?
