What Is Your Current CTC? How to Answer Honestly

Last updated 26 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · Free

The question: “What is your current CTC?”

Why interviewers ask this

In India, recruiters commonly ask for current CTC and often expect a hike over it. They use it to judge your level and budget fit. Many employers verify it later through payslips, Form 16 or offer letters, so accuracy matters. The interviewer also checks whether you understand your own salary structure: fixed pay, variable pay, PF, gratuity and benefits. Candidates who are vague or inflate their figures risk losing the offer at verification. A clear breakdown, and a short explanation if your pay is unusually low or high, is the best approach.

How to answer

  1. Know your exact numbers: fixed salary, variable or incentive pay, employer PF, gratuity, and any benefits included in CTC.
  2. State the total CTC and briefly give the break-up of fixed and variable components.
  3. If your current pay is below market, explain briefly why, such as a small firm or an early role, and focus on your expected range.
  4. Never inflate your CTC. Keep payslips and offer letters ready for verification.

Sample answers

Experienced (2 years in an NBFC)

My current CTC is [₹X] lakh per annum. Of that, about [₹Y] lakh is fixed, and the rest is a performance-linked variable paid quarterly, plus employer PF and insurance. Last year I received most of my variable pay because I met my targets. My monthly in-hand salary is around [₹Z]. I can share my payslips and appraisal letter whenever needed.

Experienced (below market pay)

My current CTC is [₹X] lakh. I know that is lower than the market for my experience, mainly because I joined a small family-run firm as a fresher, and increments there have been modest. My work, though, has grown to include handling GST compliance for [number] clients on my own. For this role, I am expecting a package in the range of [₹Y] to [₹Z] lakh, which is in line with what the market pays for this level of responsibility.

Experienced (incentive-heavy sales role)

My fixed CTC is [₹X] lakh. On top of that, my incentives last year were about [₹Y], which varies by month depending on business. So my total earnings were around [₹Z]. I can share my incentive statements if required. For the new role, I would like my expected package to be compared with total earnings rather than only the fixed part.

Use these as a structure, not a script — replace the placeholders with your own facts.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Inflating your CTC, which can be discovered through payslips or Form 16.
  • Not knowing the break-up of your own salary.
  • Quoting gross or in-hand when they asked for CTC, causing confusion.
  • Getting defensive or refusing to answer without explanation.

Likely follow-up questions

  • What hike are you expecting?
  • When is your next appraisal due?
  • Do you have any pending bonus or incentives?

FAQs

Can I refuse to disclose my current CTC?

You can politely say you prefer to discuss what the role is worth, but in India most employers expect the figure and may verify it later. Refusing can slow the process or create doubts. Usually it is better to share it and steer the discussion towards your expected range.

What documents will they ask for to verify CTC?

Common requests include recent payslips, your current offer or increment letter, Form 16 and bank statements showing salary credits. Practices vary, so check with HR. Keep these ready before you reach the offer stage.
