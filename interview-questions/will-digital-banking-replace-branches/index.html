Will Digital Banking Replace Branches? Interview Answer

Last updated 20 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · Free

The question: “Will digital banking make bank branches irrelevant?”

Why interviewers ask this

Interviewers use this opinion question to see whether you can think in a balanced way about change in your own industry. Candidates often go to one extreme: either branches are dead or technology is overrated. Banks are investing heavily in mobile apps, video KYC and digital lending, yet they keep opening branches in underbanked areas and still need staff for complex work like credit, recovery and senior citizen services. The panel wants a reasoned view, awareness of trends like UPI and fintech partnerships, and a sense of how the role of a branch employee is changing, not disappearing.

How to answer

  1. Take a clear position early: digital will handle most routine transactions, but branches will remain important with a changed role.
  2. Give evidence for the digital side, such as UPI for payments, mobile app account opening, video KYC and pre-approved digital loans.
  3. Give reasons branches remain relevant: complex loans, documentation, rural and elderly customers, trust, cash handling, and fraud resolution.
  4. Describe how the branch is changing into an advisory and sales centre, with fewer counters and more relationship work.
  5. Connect it to yourself: say you are comfortable with technology and can help customers move to digital channels safely.

Sample answers

Fresher (SBI Clerk interview)

I do not think branches will become irrelevant, but their role is definitely changing. Today most people in cities pay through UPI and check balances on apps, so the counter rush for small transactions is going down. But a lot of customers still need a branch: senior citizens for pension and life certificates, small businesses for loans and documents, and people in villages who are new to banking. Also, when someone is cheated in a cyber fraud, they come to the branch for help. I think branches will become places for advice, loans and solving problems, while routine work moves online. As a clerk, I see part of my job as helping customers start using digital banking safely.

Candidate with fintech internship (IBPS PO interview)

My view is that digital will become the default and the branch will become the specialist. During my internship at [fintech company] I saw how quickly digital lending can approve a small personal loan. But I also saw that larger loans, MSME credit and agriculture lending still need physical verification and a relationship. Trust is another factor: many first-time users want to see a person before they deposit their savings. So I expect fewer, smarter branches with more officers doing advisory and credit work. That is actually why I want to join now, because a young officer who understands both sides will be useful.

Use these as a structure, not a script — replace the placeholders with your own facts.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Saying branches will disappear completely in a few years.
  • Dismissing technology as risky or unnecessary.
  • Ignoring rural, elderly and MSME customers in your reasoning.
  • Making up statistics on digital transaction growth.

Likely follow-up questions

  • What are digital banking units?
  • What are the risks of digital banking for new users?
  • What is the role of fintech companies in banking?
  • How would you teach a 60-year-old customer to use UPI?

FAQs

What are Digital Banking Units?

Digital Banking Units are specialised fixed-point outlets set up by banks, following RBI guidelines, with self-service and assisted digital infrastructure for opening accounts, applying for loans and other services. Mentioning them shows you follow developments, but explain them in a sentence rather than in detail.

Should I give a one-sided answer to sound confident?

No. A confident answer can still be balanced. State your view in the first sentence, support it with two or three reasons, and acknowledge the other side briefly. Panels prefer reasoned opinions over extreme ones.
