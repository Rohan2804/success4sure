How to Negotiate a Job Offer in India (With Scripts)

Last updated 23 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · Free

The question: “How do I negotiate a job offer?”

Why interviewers ask this

Negotiation is expected for most lateral hires and is sometimes possible even for freshers off campus. When an employer makes an offer, they have already decided they want you, so a polite, well-reasoned request rarely causes an offer to be withdrawn. What interviewers and HR assess here is how you handle a professional negotiation: whether you are prepared, reasonable and collaborative, or aggressive and unclear. This is the same skill many finance roles require with customers and partners, so your approach also signals how you will behave on the job.

How to answer

  1. Thank them and express genuine interest before discussing numbers.
  2. Ask for time to review the full offer, including fixed pay, variable pay, joining bonus, benefits, location and notice buyout.
  3. Make one clear, researched ask with a reason, such as market data, a competing offer or a specific skill you bring.
  4. Consider non-salary items if the budget is fixed: joining date, a review after six months, relocation support or a notice buyout.
  5. Confirm everything agreed in writing before resigning from your current job.

Sample answers

Experienced (asking for higher fixed pay)

Thank you for the offer; I am genuinely excited about the role and the team. I have reviewed the details, and I wanted to discuss the fixed component. Based on my research and the scope of the role, which includes managing a portfolio of [size], I was expecting fixed pay closer to [₹X] lakh. Is there any flexibility to move closer to that figure? If the fixed budget is limited, I would be happy to discuss a joining bonus or an earlier salary review instead.

Experienced (with a competing offer)

I want to be open with you. I have another offer at [₹X] lakh CTC for a similar role. Your company is my first preference because of the role and growth path. If you can come closer to that figure, I would be ready to accept and give my resignation this week.

Fresher (off-campus offer)

Thank you very much for the offer. I am keen to join. I understand fresher salaries are usually standard, so I wanted to ask whether there is any flexibility, or whether a review after the probation period is possible based on performance. Either way, I am happy to accept.

Use these as a structure, not a script — replace the placeholders with your own facts.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Accepting the first offer without reading the full break-up.
  • Bluffing about competing offers that you do not have.
  • Negotiating aggressively or issuing ultimatums.
  • Resigning before receiving the final offer letter in writing.

Likely follow-up questions

  • What is the minimum figure you would accept?
  • Can you join earlier if we increase the offer?
  • Why should we pay above our band?

FAQs

Can freshers negotiate salary in campus placements?

Campus offers are usually standard packages, and negotiating is generally not expected or possible. Off-campus fresher roles sometimes have slight flexibility. Even if salary is fixed, you can politely ask about joining dates, location preference or training details.

Will negotiating make the company withdraw the offer?

A polite, reasonable request rarely leads to withdrawal. The risk increases if you are aggressive, make unrealistic demands or keep changing your ask. Make one well-reasoned request, be clear about what would make you accept, and respond quickly once they reply.
