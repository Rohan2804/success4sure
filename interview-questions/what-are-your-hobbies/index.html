What Are Your Hobbies? How to Answer in a Job Interview

Last updated 23 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · Free

The question: “What are your hobbies and interests?”

Why interviewers ask this

Panels ask about hobbies to see the person behind the resume and to check whether what you wrote is true. In bank and PSU interviews especially, whatever you list as a hobby becomes fair game for detailed questions. If you write 'reading', expect to be asked about the last book you read and its author. If you write 'cricket', you may be asked about the rules or a recent series. The question also shows how you spend your time, whether you can talk with genuine enthusiasm, and what qualities your interests build.

How to answer

  1. List only hobbies you actually spend time on and can discuss for five minutes without preparation.
  2. Describe the hobby specifically: not just 'music' but 'I play the tabla and have been learning for six years'.
  3. Mention one quality the hobby builds, such as discipline, teamwork or patience, without forcing a link to the job.
  4. Prepare for factual questions linked to your hobby, especially in government and bank interviews.

Sample answers

Fresher (bank PO interview)

I enjoy reading non-fiction, mostly about Indian economic history. Recently I read [book title] by [author], which explained how the 1991 reforms happened. It helped me understand topics like the balance of payments crisis much better than my textbooks did. I also play badminton with friends three evenings a week. It is my way of switching off after study hours, and it keeps me fit.

Experienced (operations role)

I have been running for about four years. I started with short distances and have completed two half marathons in [city]. It taught me how to stick to a plan over months, especially on mornings when I do not feel like it. I also cook on weekends, mostly regional dishes from my grandmother's recipes, which my family enjoys more than my running stories.

Fresher (MBA, marketing and sales)

I run a small Instagram page where I review street food in [city]. It has a few thousand followers now. I started it for fun, but it taught me a lot about what kind of content people engage with, how to talk to shop owners and how to keep posting consistently. I also play chess online, mostly rapid games, which keeps my thinking sharp.

Use these as a structure, not a script — replace the placeholders with your own facts.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Listing hobbies you have not practised for years, like 'reading' when you cannot name a recent book.
  • Mentioning only passive activities like watching web series or scrolling social media.
  • Choosing hobbies that may raise concerns, such as risky activities, without context.
  • Giving one-word answers that end the conversation.

Likely follow-up questions

  • What was the last book you read?
  • Who is your favourite player and why?
  • How do you find time for this alongside work?

FAQs

Should I list hobbies on my resume at all?

For freshers, a short line of genuine hobbies can help start a conversation and fill out a thin resume. For experienced candidates, it is optional. Never list something you cannot discuss in depth, because panels in bank and government interviews often test your listed hobbies with detailed questions.

Is 'watching movies' an acceptable hobby?

It can be if you engage with it seriously, for example following a particular director, regional cinema or film reviews. Simply watching for entertainment rarely impresses. If you mention it, be ready to discuss a film's story, themes or craft rather than just naming titles.
