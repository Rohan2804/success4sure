CTC vs In-Hand Salary: What to Ask Before Accepting

Last updated 23 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · Free

The question: “What questions should I ask about CTC, in-hand salary and variable pay?”

Why interviewers ask this

Many freshers accept an offer quoting a CTC of, say, ₹6 lakh and are surprised when the monthly credit is much lower. CTC includes items you do not receive monthly, such as employer PF contribution, gratuity provision, insurance premium and performance-linked variable pay. When HR discusses the offer, they also notice whether you understand these components. Asking clear, informed questions shows maturity and protects you from misunderstandings that often lead to early resignations.

How to answer

  1. Ask for the full salary break-up: basic, HRA, special allowance, employer PF, gratuity, insurance and variable pay.
  2. Clarify variable pay: how it is calculated, how often it is paid, and what percentage people typically receive.
  3. Estimate your in-hand salary after employee PF, professional tax and income tax deductions, or ask HR for an indicative figure.
  4. Check one-time items like joining bonuses separately, including any clawback if you leave early.
  5. Ask about benefits outside CTC, such as medical cover for family, that add real value.

Sample answers

Fresher (questions to HR at the offer stage)

Thank you for the offer. Could you please share the detailed break-up of the CTC? I want to understand how much is fixed monthly salary and how much is variable or annual. Also, how is the variable component decided, and how often is it paid? It would help if you could give an approximate monthly in-hand figure after PF and tax deductions.

Experienced (comparing two offers)

I want to compare the offers properly. In your offer, the CTC is [₹X] lakh, but about 20 per cent appears to be variable. Could you tell me how the variable has paid out for this role in recent years? Also, is the joining bonus included in CTC, and is there a clawback if I leave within a year? That will help me make a fair comparison.

Worked example (illustrative only)

Suppose an offer says ₹6 lakh CTC. If it includes ₹60,000 variable, around ₹21,600 employer PF and about ₹14,400 for gratuity and insurance, the fixed gross is roughly ₹5 lakh, or about ₹42,000 a month. After employee PF and professional tax, the in-hand may be around ₹38,000 to ₹40,000 before income tax. Actual figures depend on the employer's structure and current tax rules.

Use these as a structure, not a script — replace the placeholders with your own facts.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Assuming CTC divided by twelve is your monthly salary.
  • Not asking how variable pay is calculated or paid.
  • Comparing offers only by CTC without seeing the break-up.
  • Ignoring clawback clauses on joining bonuses.

Likely follow-up questions

  • Is the variable pay guaranteed in the first year?
  • Is gratuity part of CTC here?
  • Which tax regime should I choose?

FAQs

Why is my in-hand salary so much lower than CTC?

CTC includes employer contributions like PF and gratuity that you do not receive monthly, variable pay paid later, and sometimes insurance premiums. Your monthly salary is also reduced by employee PF, professional tax and income tax. HR can usually provide an indicative in-hand figure.

Which tax regime gives higher in-hand salary?

It depends on your income and deductions, and rules change with each Budget. As of 2026, check the latest slabs on the income tax department's website or ask a qualified advisor. Your employer will usually ask you to declare your choice at the start of the year.
