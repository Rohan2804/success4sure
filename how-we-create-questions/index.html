How we create questions

Last updated 22 October 2026

Candidates rely on practice questions being correct. This page explains how we write, check and update every question on Success4Sure.

1. Start from the official syllabus

Each question bank is organised by the exam's official chapters or sections. For NISM exams we use the curriculum and test objectives NISM publishes for each certification; for other exams we use the conducting body's published syllabus and pattern. Questions are spread across chapters roughly in proportion to how much of the exam each chapter covers.

2. Write original questions

Every question is written fresh. We never copy questions from workbooks, past papers, coaching material or "dump" sites, and we never reproduce actual exam questions — exam bodies prohibit disclosing them. Questions are drafted with the help of AI writing tools and then checked using the process below.

3. Check accuracy

  • Every numerical question is re-solved from scratch, and puzzles are checked to have exactly one valid answer.
  • Automated checks confirm four distinct options, one correct answer, no duplicate questions, full chapter coverage and an even spread of correct-answer positions.
  • Facts that change often — tax rates, regulatory limits, fees, current affairs — are only used when we are confident they are current; otherwise we test the underlying concept.
  • Every answer carries a short explanation so you can see the reasoning.

4. Keep it current

Regulators and exam bodies revise rules and patterns. We review question banks when official notifications or regulations change, and each exam page shows when it was last updated.

5. Corrections

If you think an answer is wrong or out of date, tell us at the contact details on our Contact page with the exam, question and your reasoning. We review every report and correct or remove the question if needed.

What our questions are not

Our mock tests are practice material. They are not official exam questions and do not guarantee a result. Always read the official study material — see our free study material page.
