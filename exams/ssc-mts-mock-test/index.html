About the SSC MTS exam

The SSC MTS exam recruits Multi-Tasking (Non-Technical) Staff and Havaldars for central government offices, testing numerical ability, reasoning, general awareness and English at a foundational level.

Who should take it: Candidates who have passed Class 10 and are applying for MTS and Havaldar posts through SSC.

  1. 2 years
  2. 3 years
  3. 5 years

Rajya Sabha is a permanent body; members serve 6-year terms and one-third retire every second year.

Sample 5 · Numerical Ability

What is the HCF of 36 and 48?

  1. 6
  2. 18
  3. 24
  4. 12
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 12

36 = 2² × 3², 48 = 2⁴ × 3, so HCF = 2² × 3 = 12.

SSC MTS mock test FAQs

How many questions are there in the SSC MTS exam?

The SSC MTS paper has 90 multiple-choice questions for 270 marks, to be completed in 90 minutes. This is the indicative pattern — always confirm it in the latest official notification.

Is there negative marking in SSC MTS?

Yes. −1 mark per wrong answer (33.3% of the marks for that question). Our mock tests apply the same rule.

What is the target score for SSC MTS?

Cut-offs vary by year and category; our mock tests use 40% as a target score to aim for.

Are the SSC MTS mock tests free?

Yes. All 30 full-length SSC MTS mock tests are free, with no payment or plan. You can start without signing up; log in with Google if you want your results saved across devices. You can retake any test as often as you like.

How should I prepare for SSC MTS in 30 days?

Spend the first two weeks revising basic maths such as percentages, ratios and simplification, common reasoning question types, basic English grammar and static GK. In the last two weeks, take mock tests regularly, review wrong answers and revise current affairs. Steady daily practice is enough for this foundational-level exam.

What is the difference between SSC MTS and Havaldar posts?

Both are recruited through the same SSC MTS exam. Havaldar candidates must also clear a physical efficiency test and meet physical standards, while MTS posts do not need these. Check the latest notification on ssc.gov.in for the current requirements.

Is SSC MTS hard to crack?

The questions are at a foundational level, but the number of applicants is very large, so the competition is tough. Accuracy matters as much as speed. Regular practice with timed mock tests and careful revision of general awareness give you the best chance.
