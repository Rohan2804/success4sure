About the SSC MTS exam

The SSC MTS exam recruits Multi-Tasking (Non-Technical) Staff and Havaldars for central government offices, testing numerical ability, reasoning, general awareness and English at a foundational level.

Who should take it: Candidates who have passed Class 10 and are applying for MTS and Havaldar posts through SSC.

Sample 1 · Numerical Ability

Which of the following numbers is divisible by 9?

  1. 4535
  2. 4536
  3. 4537
  4. 4538
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 4536

A number is divisible by 9 if its digit sum is; 4 + 5 + 3 + 6 = 18 is divisible by 9.

Sample 2 · Reasoning Ability

Select the word that completes the analogy. Doctor : Hospital :: Teacher : ?

  1. Office
  2. Court
  3. Field
  4. School
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. School

A doctor works in a hospital; a teacher works in a school.

Sample 3 · General Awareness

In the Rajya Sabha, one-third of the members retire after every:

  1. 2 years
  2. 3 years
  3. 5 years
  4. 6 years
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 2 years

Rajya Sabha is a permanent body; members serve 6-year terms and one-third retire every second year.

Sample 4 · English Language

Select the most appropriate synonym of BRAVE.

  1. Coward
  2. Weak
  3. Fearful
  4. Courageous
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Courageous

Brave means ready to face danger, i.e. courageous.

Sample 5 · Numerical Ability

What is the HCF of 36 and 48?

  1. 6
  2. 18
  3. 24
  4. 12
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 12

36 = 2² × 3², 48 = 2⁴ × 3, so HCF = 2² × 3 = 12.

SSC MTS mock test FAQs

How many questions are there in the SSC MTS exam?

The SSC MTS paper has 90 multiple-choice questions for 270 marks, to be completed in 90 minutes. This is the indicative pattern — always confirm it in the latest official notification.

Is there negative marking in SSC MTS?

Yes. −1 mark per wrong answer (33.3% of the marks for that question). Our mock tests apply the same rule.

What is the target score for SSC MTS?

Cut-offs vary by year and category; our mock tests use 40% as a target score to aim for.

How many SSC MTS mock tests do I get?

One full-length mock test is unlocked for each day of your plan: 10, 15, 20 or 30 mock tests on the 10 Days, 15 Days, 20 Days or 30 Days plans. You can retake any unlocked test while your plan is active.
