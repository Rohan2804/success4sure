General Awareness: SSC MTS practice questions

15 free questions with answers and explanations on General Awareness, from the SSC MTS (Multi-Tasking (Non-Technical) Staff) syllabus.

Last updated 11 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · How we create questions

What this section covers

General Awareness in SSC MTS checks broad static knowledge: Indian Polity (Constitution, Parliament, Fundamental Rights and Duties), Indian history and the freedom movement, Indian and world geography, everyday science (physics, chemistry, biology), basic economics and banking terms, art and culture, and sports. Static facts carry most of the marks, so revise standard textbooks such as NCERT summaries. Focus on important Articles and Amendments, key battles and dates, rivers and states, vitamins and diseases, SI units and classical dances, and keep a short list of commonly confused pairs.

General Awareness questions

Question 1

The Fundamental Duties of citizens were added to the Indian Constitution by which Constitutional Amendment?

  1. 44th Amendment, 1978
  2. 52nd Amendment, 1985
  3. 42nd Amendment, 1976
  4. 73rd Amendment, 1992
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 42nd Amendment, 1976

The 42nd Amendment (1976), based on the Swaran Singh Committee's recommendations, inserted Part IV-A (Article 51A) listing Fundamental Duties. The 44th Amendment removed property from the Fundamental Rights.

Question 2

Which Article of the Indian Constitution guarantees protection of life and personal liberty?

  1. Article 14
  2. Article 21
  3. Article 19
  4. Article 32
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Article 21

Article 21 states that no person shall be deprived of life or personal liberty except by procedure established by law. Article 14 deals with equality and Article 32 with constitutional remedies.

Question 3

Who presides over a joint sitting of both Houses of the Indian Parliament?

  1. Vice-President of India
  2. Speaker of the Lok Sabha
  3. President of India
  4. Chairman of the Finance Commission
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Speaker of the Lok Sabha

Under Article 108 the President summons a joint sitting, but it is presided over by the Speaker of the Lok Sabha. The Vice-President chairs only the Rajya Sabha.

Question 4

The Battle of Plassey, which helped establish British power in Bengal, was fought in which year?

  1. 1757
  2. 1764
  3. 1761
  4. 1857
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 1757

Robert Clive defeated Siraj-ud-Daulah at Plassey in 1757. The Battle of Buxar followed in 1764, and 1761 is the Third Battle of Panipat.

Question 5

Which Harappan site is known for a dockyard, suggesting maritime trade?

  1. Mohenjo-daro
  2. Kalibangan
  3. Lothal
  4. Rakhigarhi
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Lothal

Lothal in Gujarat had a brick-lined dockyard linked to trade routes. Mohenjo-daro is famous for the Great Bath and Kalibangan for ploughed fields.

Question 6

Which is the longest river of peninsular India?

  1. Krishna
  2. Narmada
  3. Kaveri
  4. Godavari
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Godavari

The Godavari, rising at Trimbakeshwar in Maharashtra and flowing into the Bay of Bengal, is the longest peninsular river and is often called the 'Dakshin Ganga'.

Question 7

The Tropic of Cancer does NOT pass through which of the following states?

  1. Bihar
  2. Jharkhand
  3. Chhattisgarh
  4. Tripura
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Bihar

The Tropic of Cancer crosses eight states: Gujarat, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, West Bengal, Tripura and Mizoram. Bihar lies entirely north of it.

Question 8

Scurvy is caused by the deficiency of which vitamin?

  1. Vitamin A
  2. Vitamin D
  3. Vitamin K
  4. Vitamin C
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Vitamin C

Vitamin C (ascorbic acid) is needed for collagen formation; its lack causes bleeding gums and scurvy. Vitamin D deficiency causes rickets and Vitamin A deficiency night blindness.

Question 9

What is the SI unit of electric current?

  1. Volt
  2. Ohm
  3. Watt
  4. Ampere
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Ampere

Electric current is measured in amperes, one of the seven SI base units. The volt measures potential difference, the ohm resistance and the watt power.

Question 10

Which gas makes up the largest share of the Earth's atmosphere by volume?

  1. Nitrogen
  2. Oxygen
  3. Carbon dioxide
  4. Argon
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Nitrogen

Nitrogen forms about 78% of dry air, oxygen about 21% and argon nearly 0.9%. Carbon dioxide is only a small fraction despite its importance for climate.

Question 11

In which part of a plant cell does photosynthesis take place?

  1. Mitochondria
  2. Ribosome
  3. Golgi apparatus
  4. Chloroplast
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Chloroplast

Chloroplasts contain chlorophyll, which traps sunlight to make food. Mitochondria release energy through respiration and are called the powerhouse of the cell.

Question 12

In banking terminology, the 'repo rate' is the rate at which:

  1. commercial banks lend to their most creditworthy customers
  2. RBI borrows money from commercial banks
  3. RBI lends short-term funds to commercial banks
  4. banks pay interest on savings deposits
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. RBI lends short-term funds to commercial banks

Under a repo, banks borrow from the Reserve Bank of India by selling securities with an agreement to repurchase them. RBI borrowing from banks is the reverse repo concept.

Question 13

How many players of each team are on the court at a time in a standard game of kabaddi?

  1. 5
  2. 9
  3. 7
  4. 11
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 7

A kabaddi team fields seven players on court, with a few substitutes. Eleven is the number for cricket, hockey and football teams.

Question 14

Kathakali, a classical dance form known for elaborate make-up and costumes, belongs to which state?

  1. Tamil Nadu
  2. Odisha
  3. Manipur
  4. Kerala
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Kerala

Kathakali comes from Kerala. Bharatanatyam is from Tamil Nadu, Odissi from Odisha and Manipuri from Manipur.

Question 15

Who founded the Arya Samaj in 1875?

  1. Raja Ram Mohan Roy
  2. Swami Dayananda Saraswati
  3. Swami Vivekananda
  4. Ishwar Chandra Vidyasagar
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Swami Dayananda Saraswati

Swami Dayananda Saraswati founded the Arya Samaj in Bombay in 1875 with the call 'Go back to the Vedas'. Raja Ram Mohan Roy founded the Brahmo Samaj.
