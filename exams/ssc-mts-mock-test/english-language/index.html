English Language: SSC MTS practice questions

15 free questions with answers and explanations on English Language, from the SSC MTS (Multi-Tasking (Non-Technical) Staff) syllabus.

Last updated 23 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · How we create questions

What this section covers

English Language tests basic grammar and vocabulary: synonyms, antonyms, idioms and phrases, one-word substitution, spelling, error spotting, fill in the blanks (articles, prepositions, verbs, phrasal verbs), sentence improvement, active and passive voice, direct and indirect speech, and short reading comprehension. Revise subject–verb agreement rules (each, neither…nor, either…or), tense usage with since and for, and conditional sentences. Read short passages carefully and answer only from what is stated. Learning word lists with their opposites and practising narration and voice conversion rules gives quick, reliable marks.

English Language questions

Question 1

Choose the word closest in meaning to ABUNDANT as used in: 'The region has abundant rainfall.'

  1. Scarce
  2. Tiny
  3. Plentiful
  4. Hidden
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Plentiful

Abundant means existing in large quantities, so 'plentiful' is the synonym. 'Scarce' is its opposite, while 'tiny' and 'hidden' are unrelated.

Question 2

Which word is opposite in meaning to TRANSPARENT in: 'The bottle is made of transparent glass.'

  1. Clear
  2. Opaque
  3. Bright
  4. Glossy
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Opaque

Transparent means letting light pass through. Opaque means not letting light pass, so it is the antonym. 'Clear' is close to a synonym.

Question 3

Select the most appropriate meaning of the idiom 'to break the ice'.

  1. To start a conversation in an uneasy situation
  2. To end a long friendship
  3. To act very rudely
  4. To solve a difficult problem quickly and without any help
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. To start a conversation in an uneasy situation

'To break the ice' means to say or do something that relieves tension and gets people talking, for example at the start of a meeting with strangers.

Question 4

Select the one word for: 'A place where birds are kept'.

  1. Apiary
  2. Aquarium
  3. Kennel
  4. Aviary
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Aviary

An aviary is an enclosure for birds. An apiary is a place where bees are kept, an aquarium is for fish and a kennel is for dogs.

Question 5

Which of the following words has the correct spelling?

  1. Neccessary
  2. Necessary
  3. Necesary
  4. Neccesary
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Necessary

The correct spelling is 'necessary' — one 'c' and double 's'. A common memory aid: one collar, two sleeves.

Question 6

Identify the segment that contains a grammatical error. Each of the students / have submitted / the project on time.

  1. Each of the students
  2. the project on time
  3. No error
  4. have submitted
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. have submitted

'Each of' takes a singular verb, so it should be 'has submitted'. The subject is 'each', not 'students'.

Question 7

Fill in the blank with the most appropriate word. He has been living in Pune ______ 2018.

  1. for
  2. from
  3. since
  4. by
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. since

With the present perfect continuous, 'since' is used for a point of time (2018) and 'for' for a period of time (five years).

Question 8

Fill in the blank with the most appropriate verb. Neither the teacher nor the students ______ present at the meeting yesterday.

  1. was
  2. is
  3. were
  4. has been
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. were

With 'neither … nor', the verb agrees with the nearer subject. 'Students' is plural and the time is past, so 'were' is correct.

Question 9

Select the most appropriate improvement for the underlined part. 'If I was you', I would accept the offer.

  1. If I were you
  2. If I am you
  3. If I had been you
  4. No improvement
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. If I were you

Imaginary or hypothetical conditions use the subjunctive 'were' for all subjects: 'If I were you, I would…'. Hence 'was' must be replaced.

Question 10

Select the correct passive form of: The police have arrested the thief.

  1. The thief has been arrested by the police.
  2. The thief was arrested by the police.
  3. The thief had been arrested by the police.
  4. The thief is being arrested by the police.
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. The thief has been arrested by the police.

Present perfect active ('have arrested') becomes present perfect passive: has/have been + past participle. The singular subject 'thief' takes 'has been arrested'.

Question 11

Select the correct indirect form of: She said to me, "Please help me."

  1. She said to me that please help me.
  2. She requested me to help me.
  3. She told me that I should please help her.
  4. She requested me to help her.
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. She requested me to help her.

A request in direct speech becomes 'requested + object + to-infinitive', and 'please' is dropped. The pronoun 'me' changes to 'her' to refer to the speaker.

Question 12

Read the passage and answer the question. Rainwater harvesting means collecting rain from rooftops and storing it in tanks or letting it soak into the ground. In many towns, groundwater levels have fallen because more water is pumped out than is replaced. Harvesting rain helps refill underground water and reduces flooding of streets during heavy showers. According to the passage, why have groundwater levels fallen in many towns?

  1. Rain has stopped falling in towns
  2. More water is pumped out than is replaced
  3. Rooftops absorb most of the rain
  4. Streets are flooded during heavy showers every year
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. More water is pumped out than is replaced

The passage clearly says groundwater has fallen 'because more water is pumped out than is replaced'. Flooding is mentioned only as a problem harvesting helps reduce.

Question 13

Read the passage and answer the question. The village library opened with just two shelves of donated books. Within a year, children were coming every evening, and their parents began borrowing newspapers. The librarian, a retired teacher, also started a weekly reading hour for those who could not read well. Which word best describes the overall tone of the passage?

  1. Critical
  2. Encouraging
  3. Sorrowful
  4. Sarcastic
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Encouraging

The passage describes a small library that grows and helps children and adults, so its tone is positive and encouraging. Nothing in it criticises, mourns or mocks anyone.

Question 14

Pick the word opposite in meaning to HUMBLE in: 'Despite his success, he remained humble.'

  1. Modest
  2. Arrogant
  3. Polite
  4. Simple
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Arrogant

Humble means modest and not proud. Arrogant means having an exaggerated sense of one's importance, so it is the antonym. 'Modest' is a synonym.

Question 15

Fill in the blank with the most appropriate phrasal verb. The doctor advised him to ______ smoking.

  1. give up
  2. give in
  3. give off
  4. give away
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. give up

'Give up' means to stop doing something. 'Give in' means to surrender, 'give off' means to emit (smell or heat) and 'give away' means to donate or reveal.
