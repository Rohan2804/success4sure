Numerical Ability: SSC MTS practice questions

15 free questions with answers and explanations on Numerical Ability, from the SSC MTS (Multi-Tasking (Non-Technical) Staff) syllabus.

Last updated 20 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · How we create questions

What this section covers

Numerical Ability in SSC MTS tests school-level arithmetic: number system, divisibility, HCF/LCM, fractions and decimals, simplification (BODMAS), percentages, ratio and proportion, averages, profit, loss and discount, simple and compound interest, time and work, pipes and cisterns, time-speed-distance (including trains and boats) and basic mensuration of plane figures. Questions are short and calculation-based, so speed matters. While revising, memorise squares, cubes and percentage–fraction equivalents, practise unit conversions such as km/h to m/s, and learn the standard shortcuts for successive percentage change, work-rate and relative speed problems.

Numerical Ability questions

Question 1

What is the LCM of 18, 24 and 30?

  1. 360
  2. 180
  3. 720
  4. 120
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 360

18 = 2 × 3², 24 = 2³ × 3 and 30 = 2 × 3 × 5. Taking the highest power of each prime gives 2³ × 3² × 5 = 360. 180 is not divisible by 24.

Question 2

What is 12.5% of 640?

  1. 64
  2. 80
  3. 72
  4. 96
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 80

12.5% equals the fraction 1/8, so 12.5% of 640 = 640 ÷ 8 = 80. Remembering common fraction equivalents of percentages saves time in the exam.

Question 3

A shopkeeper marks his goods 40% above the cost price and then allows a discount of 15% on the marked price. What is his profit percentage?

  1. 25%
  2. 21%
  3. 19%
  4. 15%
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 19%

Take CP = ₹100. Marked price = ₹140. After a 15% discount, SP = 140 × 0.85 = ₹119. Profit = ₹19 on ₹100, i.e. 19%. Subtracting 15 from 40 to get 25% is the common trap.

Question 4

What is the compound interest on ₹8,000 at 10% per annum for 2 years, compounded annually?

  1. ₹1,680
  2. ₹1,600
  3. ₹1,760
  4. ₹1,648
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. ₹1,680

Amount = 8000 × (1.1)² = ₹9,680, so CI = 9,680 − 8,000 = ₹1,680. Simple interest would be only ₹1,600; the extra ₹80 is interest on the first year's interest.

Question 5

A train 240 m long runs at 72 km/h. How long will it take to cross a platform 160 m long?

  1. 12 seconds
  2. 18 seconds
  3. 20 seconds
  4. 24 seconds
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 20 seconds

72 km/h = 72 × 5/18 = 20 m/s. To cross a platform the train covers its own length plus the platform length: 240 + 160 = 400 m. Time = 400 ÷ 20 = 20 seconds.

Question 6

The average of five numbers is 42. When one number is removed, the average of the remaining four becomes 40. What is the number removed?

  1. 48
  2. 50
  3. 52
  4. 46
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 50

Sum of five numbers = 5 × 42 = 210. Sum of the remaining four = 4 × 40 = 160. The removed number = 210 − 160 = 50.

Question 7

Pipe A can fill a tank in 12 hours and pipe B can fill it in 18 hours. If both are opened together, in how much time will the tank be filled?

  1. 7 hours 30 minutes
  2. 6 hours
  3. 7 hours 12 minutes
  4. 15 hours
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 7 hours 12 minutes

In one hour the pipes fill 1/12 + 1/18 = 5/36 of the tank. Time = 36/5 = 7.2 hours, which is 7 hours and 0.2 × 60 = 12 minutes.

Question 8

Find the value of (0.25 × 0.4) ÷ 0.05.

  1. 2
  2. 0.2
  3. 20
  4. 0.5
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 2

0.25 × 0.4 = 0.1. Then 0.1 ÷ 0.05 = 10 ÷ 5 = 2. Shifting the decimal points equally in numerator and denominator avoids errors.

Question 9

What is the area of a circle of radius 14 cm? (Take π = 22/7)

  1. 616 cm²
  2. 88 cm²
  3. 308 cm²
  4. 1232 cm²
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 616 cm²

Area = πr² = 22/7 × 14 × 14 = 616 cm². 88 cm is the circumference (2πr), a frequent mix-up between the two formulas.

Question 10

15 men can complete a piece of work in 24 days. How many men are needed to complete the same work in 18 days?

  1. 18
  2. 22
  3. 25
  4. 20
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 20

Total work = 15 × 24 = 360 man-days. Men required for 18 days = 360 ÷ 18 = 20. More men are needed because fewer days are available (inverse proportion).

Question 11

If A : B = 2 : 3 and B : C = 4 : 5, then what is A : B : C?

  1. 8 : 12 : 15
  2. 2 : 3 : 5
  3. 8 : 12 : 16
  4. 6 : 9 : 10
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 8 : 12 : 15

Make B common: multiply the first ratio by 4 (8 : 12) and the second by 3 (12 : 15). So A : B : C = 8 : 12 : 15.

Question 12

The price of sugar rises by 25%. By what percentage must a family reduce its consumption so that its expenditure on sugar remains unchanged?

  1. 25%
  2. 15%
  3. 22%
  4. 20%
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 20%

Reduction = 25/(100 + 25) × 100 = 20%. Check: 1.25 × 0.80 = 1, so spending stays the same. Reducing by 25% would actually cut expenditure.

Question 13

What is the smallest number that must be added to 1000 so that the sum is exactly divisible by 23?

  1. 12
  2. 11
  3. 13
  4. 9
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 12

1000 ÷ 23 leaves remainder 11 (23 × 43 = 989). Adding 23 − 11 = 12 gives 1012 = 23 × 44. Choosing 11 confuses the remainder with the amount to be added.

Question 14

A boat's speed in still water is 15 km/h and the speed of the stream is 3 km/h. How long will the boat take to go 36 km downstream and come back the same distance?

  1. 4.8 hours
  2. 5 hours
  3. 5.5 hours
  4. 4 hours
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 5 hours

Downstream speed = 18 km/h, so 36 km takes 2 hours. Upstream speed = 12 km/h, so 36 km takes 3 hours. Total time = 2 + 3 = 5 hours.

Question 15

A sum of money doubles itself in 8 years at simple interest. What is the rate of interest per annum?

  1. 8%
  2. 10%
  3. 12%
  4. 12.5%
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 12.5%

If the sum doubles, interest equals the principal. So P × R × 8 / 100 = P, giving R = 100/8 = 12.5% per annum.
