Question 1 · Numerical Ability

Simplify: 45 + 15 × 3 − 20 ÷ 4

  1. 85
  2. 175
  3. 80
  4. 90
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 85

By BODMAS: 15 × 3 = 45 and 20 ÷ 4 = 5, so 45 + 45 − 5 = 85.

Question 2 · Reasoning Ability

Select the number that completes the analogy. 4 : 64 :: 5 : ?

  1. 125
  2. 25
  3. 100
  4. 120
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 125

4³ = 64, so 5³ = 125.

Question 3 · General Awareness

Who wrote India's National Song 'Vande Mataram'?

  1. Rabindranath Tagore
  2. Bankim Chandra Chattopadhyay
  3. Muhammad Iqbal
  4. Sarojini Naidu
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Bankim Chandra Chattopadhyay

Vande Mataram is from Bankim Chandra's novel Anandamath; Tagore wrote the National Anthem.

Question 4 · English Language

Select the most appropriate antonym of ANCIENT.

  1. Old
  2. Antique
  3. Modern
  4. Historic
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Modern

Ancient means very old; its opposite is modern.

Question 5 · Numerical Ability

What is 25% of 360?

  1. 80
  2. 72
  3. 96
  4. 90
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 90

25% = 1/4, and 360 ÷ 4 = 90.

Question 6 · Reasoning Ability

Select the odd one out. Apple, Mango, Potato, Banana

  1. Apple
  2. Mango
  3. Banana
  4. Potato
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Potato

Apple, mango and banana are fruits; potato is a vegetable (a tuber).

Question 7 · General Awareness

Who was the first Governor-General of independent India?

  1. C. Rajagopalachari
  2. Lord Canning
  3. Lord Wavell
  4. Lord Mountbatten
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Lord Mountbatten

Lord Mountbatten served first (1947–48); C. Rajagopalachari was the first and only Indian Governor-General.

Question 8 · English Language

Select the most appropriate meaning of the idiom 'once in a blue moon'.

  1. Very often
  2. At night
  3. Every month
  4. Very rarely
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Very rarely

Once in a blue moon means something happens very rarely.

Question 9 · Numerical Ability

Two numbers are in the ratio 3 : 5 and their sum is 64. What is the larger number?

  1. 24
  2. 32
  3. 40
  4. 36
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 40

8 parts = 64, so 1 part = 8 and the larger number = 5 × 8 = 40.

Question 10 · Reasoning Ability

Find the next term of the series. 1, 4, 9, 16, 25, ?

  1. 30
  2. 36
  3. 35
  4. 49
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 36

The terms are squares 1², 2², 3², 4², 5², so next is 6² = 36.

Question 11 · General Awareness

Where did Gautama Buddha attain enlightenment?

  1. Sarnath
  2. Lumbini
  3. Bodh Gaya
  4. Kushinagar
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Bodh Gaya

He attained enlightenment at Bodh Gaya, was born at Lumbini, preached first at Sarnath and died at Kushinagar.

Question 12 · English Language

Select the one word for: 'A person who cannot read or write'.

  1. Illiterate
  2. Ignorant
  3. Innocent
  4. Amateur
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Illiterate

An illiterate person is unable to read or write.

Question 13 · Numerical Ability

What is the average of the first 10 even natural numbers?

  1. 11
  2. 10
  3. 12
  4. 20
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 11

The numbers are 2, 4, …, 20; average = (2 + 20)/2 = 11.

Question 14 · Reasoning Ability

Find the next term of the series. AZ, BY, CX, ?

  1. DW
  2. DV
  3. EW
  4. DX
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. DW

The first letter moves forward (A, B, C, D) and the second moves backward (Z, Y, X, W).

Question 15 · General Awareness

The Quit India Movement was launched in which year?

  1. 1942
  2. 1940
  3. 1930
  4. 1946
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 1942

Gandhiji gave the 'Do or Die' call at the Bombay session in August 1942.
