Reasoning Ability: SSC MTS practice questions

15 free questions with answers and explanations on Reasoning Ability, from the SSC MTS (Multi-Tasking (Non-Technical) Staff) syllabus.

Last updated 20 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · How we create questions

What this section covers

Reasoning Ability covers verbal and non-verbal reasoning: analogies, classification (odd one out), number and letter series, coding-decoding, blood relations, directions and distance, ranking and order, calendar and clock problems, mathematical operations, syllogisms, inequalities, Venn diagrams and simple seating arrangements. Most questions can be solved in under a minute if you follow a method. Revise alphabet positions (A = 1 to Z = 26), draw quick diagrams for directions and family trees, apply odd-day rules for calendars and the 30H − 5.5M formula for clocks, and check every syllogism conclusion against all possible cases.

Reasoning Ability questions

Question 1

Select the word that completes the analogy. Bird : Nest :: Bee : ?

  1. Den
  2. Burrow
  3. Stable
  4. Hive
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Hive

A bird lives in a nest; a bee lives in a hive. A den is a lion's or fox's home, a burrow a rabbit's and a stable a horse's.

Question 2

Select the number that does not belong to the group. 27, 64, 125, 150

  1. 27
  2. 150
  3. 64
  4. 125
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 150

27 = 3³, 64 = 4³ and 125 = 5³ are perfect cubes. 150 is not a perfect cube, so it is the odd one out.

Question 3

Find the next term of the series. 3, 7, 15, 31, 63, ?

  1. 125
  2. 126
  3. 129
  4. 127
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 127

Each term is double the previous term plus 1: 3×2+1 = 7, 7×2+1 = 15, and so on. So 63 × 2 + 1 = 127.

Question 4

Find the next letter of the series. B, E, H, K, ?

  1. M
  2. O
  3. N
  4. L
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. N

The letters move forward three places each time: B(2), E(5), H(8), K(11). The next is position 14, which is N.

Question 5

In a certain code language, FRIEND is written as HUMJTK. How will CANDLE be written in that language?

  1. EDRJRL
  2. ECRIRL
  3. EDQIRL
  4. EDRIRL
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. EDRIRL

The letters are shifted forward by 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 and 7 places in order (F→H, R→U, I→M …). Applying the same shifts to CANDLE gives E, D, R, I, R, L = EDRIRL.

Question 6

If 2 ★ 3 = 13 and 4 ★ 1 = 17, then what is the value of 5 ★ 2?

  1. 27
  2. 10
  3. 29
  4. 25
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 29

The rule is a ★ b = a² + b²: 4 + 9 = 13 and 16 + 1 = 17. Hence 5 ★ 2 = 25 + 4 = 29.

Question 7

Pointing to a boy, Meena says, "He is the son of the only son of my father's father." How is the boy related to Meena?

  1. Cousin
  2. Father
  3. Nephew
  4. Brother
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Brother

The only son of Meena's paternal grandfather must be Meena's own father. The son of her father is her brother. Cousin would apply only if the grandfather had another son.

Question 8

A man walks 6 km towards North, turns right and walks 8 km, then turns right again and walks 6 km. How far and in which direction is he from his starting point?

  1. 8 km East
  2. 8 km West
  3. 10 km North-East
  4. 14 km East
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 8 km East

North 6 km, then East 8 km, then South 6 km. The north and south movements cancel out, leaving him 8 km due East of the starting point.

Question 9

In a row of 40 children, Anu is 12th from the left end. What is her position from the right end?

  1. 28th
  2. 30th
  3. 29th
  4. 27th
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 29th

Position from right = total − position from left + 1 = 40 − 12 + 1 = 29. Forgetting to add 1 gives the wrong answer 28th.

Question 10

If 1 January 2025 was a Wednesday, what day of the week was 1 January 2026?

  1. Wednesday
  2. Friday
  3. Thursday
  4. Tuesday
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Thursday

2025 is not a leap year, so it has 365 days = 52 weeks + 1 odd day. The same date next year moves one day forward, from Wednesday to Thursday.

Question 11

Statements: Some cats are dogs. No dog is a rat. Conclusions: I. Some cats are not rats. II. No cat is a rat.

  1. Only conclusion II follows
  2. Both I and II follow
  3. Only conclusion I follows
  4. Neither I nor II follows
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Only conclusion I follows

The cats that are dogs cannot be rats, so some cats are definitely not rats (I follows). Other cats may or may not be rats, so II is not certain.

Question 12

What is the angle between the hour hand and the minute hand of a clock at 3:40?

  1. 130°
  2. 120°
  3. 140°
  4. 150°
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 130°

Angle = |30H − 5.5M| = |90 − 220| = 130°. The hour hand moves 0.5° per minute, so it is not exactly on 3; ignoring this gives 150°.

Question 13

How many times does the digit 7 appear when all the numbers from 1 to 100 are written?

  1. 10
  2. 20
  3. 11
  4. 19
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 20

7 appears in the units place 10 times (7, 17 … 97) and in the tens place 10 times (70–79). 77 counts twice, giving 20 in total.

Question 14

If P > Q, Q ≥ R and R > S, which of the following is definitely true?

  1. Q = S
  2. P > S
  3. R > P
  4. S ≥ Q
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. P > S

Combining the statements gives P > Q ≥ R > S, so P > S is always true. Q is greater than S, so Q = S and S ≥ Q are false, and R > P is impossible.

Question 15

Five friends A, B, C, D and E sit in a row facing North. C sits in the middle. A sits to the immediate right of C. B sits at one of the extreme ends. D does not sit at an extreme end. E sits to the immediate right of A. Who sits at the extreme left end?

  1. D
  2. B
  3. E
  4. A
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. B

C is 3rd and A is 4th, so E is 5th (extreme right). B must take the other end, the 1st place, and D sits 2nd. Hence B is at the extreme left.
