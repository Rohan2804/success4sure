Sample 1 · Investment Landscape

Which of the following is the most important reason to invest rather than hold idle cash over the long term?

  1. Cash has high default risk
  2. Cash is taxed at a higher rate than equity
  3. Holding cash is prohibited for individuals
  4. Inflation erodes the purchasing power of uninvested money
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Inflation erodes the purchasing power of uninvested money

Investing aims to earn returns above inflation so real wealth is preserved or grows.

Sample 2 · Introduction to Securities Markets

Which of the following is a function of a clearing corporation in the securities market?

  1. Listing companies
  2. Issuing new shares
  3. Registering portfolio managers
  4. Acting as central counterparty and guaranteeing settlement of trades
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Acting as central counterparty and guaranteeing settlement of trades

Clearing corporations novate trades and guarantee settlement, reducing counterparty risk.

Sample 3 · Investing in Stocks

Using the dividend discount model, a stock paying ₹10 next year with dividends growing at 6% and a required return of 11% is worth:

  1. ₹200
  2. ₹90.9
  3. ₹166.7
  4. ₹100
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. ₹200

Value = D1 / (r − g) = 10 / (0.11 − 0.06) = ₹200.

Sample 4 · Investing in Fixed Income Securities

A zero-coupon bond with face value ₹1,000 maturing in 2 years is priced to yield 10% p.a. (annual compounding). Its price is approximately:

  1. ₹800
  2. ₹909
  3. ₹1,000
  4. ₹826
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. ₹826

Price = 1,000 / 1.1² = 1,000 / 1.21 ≈ ₹826.

Sample 5 · Derivatives

A portfolio worth ₹10 crore has a beta of 1.2. To fully hedge market risk using index futures, the notional value of futures to sell is:

  1. ₹10 crore
  2. ₹8.33 crore
  3. ₹1.2 crore
  4. ₹12 crore
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. ₹12 crore

Hedge notional = Portfolio value × beta = 10 × 1.2 = ₹12 crore.

NISM Series XXI-B mock test FAQs

How many questions are there in the NISM Series XXI-B exam?

The NISM Series XXI-B paper has 100 multiple-choice questions for 100 marks, to be completed in 180 minutes. This is the indicative pattern — always confirm it in the latest official notification.

Is there negative marking in NISM Series XXI-B?

Yes. −0.25 mark per wrong answer (25% of the marks for that question). Our mock tests apply the same rule.

What is the pass mark for NISM Series XXI-B?

You need 60% to pass. Our mock tests show pass or fail against the same mark.

Are the NISM Series XXI-B mock tests free?

Yes. All 30 full-length NISM Series XXI-B mock tests are free, with no payment or plan. You can start without signing up; log in with Google if you want your results saved across devices. You can retake any test as often as you like.

Who needs NISM Series XXI-B?

SEBI requires the principal officer and certain investment professionals of registered portfolio managers to hold this certification. Requirements can change, so confirm with your compliance team and confirm the current rules on nism.ac.in.

Is NISM Series XXI-B tough?

It is one of the more advanced NISM papers, covering a wide range of investment topics. Learners usually find portfolio theory, fixed income analytics, derivatives and performance attribution the hardest. Regular calculation practice and timed mock tests are essential.

How should I plan my NISM Series XXI-B preparation?

Allow four weeks or more. Cover markets and equity analysis in week one, fixed income and derivatives in week two, portfolio theory and behavioural finance in week three, and strategies, performance and regulation in week four, followed by timed mock tests.
