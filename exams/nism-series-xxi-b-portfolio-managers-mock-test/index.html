NISM Series XXI-B is the portfolio manager certification — securities markets, equity and fixed income analysis, derivatives, portfolio theory, behavioural finance, portfolio strategies, performance evaluation and rebalancing, and the PMS regulatory framework.

Sample 1 · Investment Landscape

Which of the following is the most important reason to invest rather than hold idle cash over the long term?

  1. Cash has high default risk
  2. Cash is taxed at a higher rate than equity
  3. Holding cash is prohibited for individuals
  4. Inflation erodes the purchasing power of uninvested money
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Inflation erodes the purchasing power of uninvested money

Investing aims to earn returns above inflation so real wealth is preserved or grows.

Sample 2 · Introduction to Securities Markets

Which of the following is a function of a clearing corporation in the securities market?

  1. Listing companies
  2. Issuing new shares
  3. Registering portfolio managers
  4. Acting as central counterparty and guaranteeing settlement of trades
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Acting as central counterparty and guaranteeing settlement of trades

Clearing corporations novate trades and guarantee settlement, reducing counterparty risk.

Sample 3 · Investing in Stocks

Using the dividend discount model, a stock paying ₹10 next year with dividends growing at 6% and a required return of 11% is worth:

  1. ₹200
  2. ₹90.9
  3. ₹166.7
  4. ₹100
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. ₹200

Value = D1 / (r − g) = 10 / (0.11 − 0.06) = ₹200.

Sample 4 · Investing in Fixed Income Securities

A zero-coupon bond with face value ₹1,000 maturing in 2 years is priced to yield 10% p.a. (annual compounding). Its price is approximately:

  1. ₹800
  2. ₹909
  3. ₹1,000
  4. ₹826
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. ₹826

Price = 1,000 / 1.1² = 1,000 / 1.21 ≈ ₹826.

Sample 5 · Derivatives

A portfolio worth ₹10 crore has a beta of 1.2. To fully hedge market risk using index futures, the notional value of futures to sell is:

  1. ₹10 crore
  2. ₹8.33 crore
  3. ₹1.2 crore
  4. ₹12 crore
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. ₹12 crore

Hedge notional = Portfolio value × beta = 10 × 1.2 = ₹12 crore.
