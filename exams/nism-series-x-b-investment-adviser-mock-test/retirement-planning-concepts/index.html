Retirement Planning Concepts: NISM Series X-B practice questions

8 free questions with answers and explanations on Retirement Planning Concepts, from the NISM Series X-B (Investment Adviser (Level 2)) syllabus.

Last updated 23 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · How we create questions

What this chapter covers

This chapter covers the concepts behind retirement planning: phases of retirement, estimating retirement expenses, income replacement, inflation and longevity risk, sequence-of-returns risk, health costs and the need for a corpus that lasts. It also covers the impact of starting early and the trade-off between saving more, working longer and retiring later. When revising, practise projecting expenses, understand why real returns matter, and know the main risks a retiree faces.

Retirement Planning Concepts questions

Question 1

Why does starting retirement savings ten years earlier usually reduce the monthly saving required?

  1. Inflation stops affecting long-term savings
  2. Retirement expenses fall when one starts early
  3. Government matches all early contributions
  4. Money compounds for longer
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Money compounds for longer

A longer accumulation period lets returns compound for more years, so each rupee saved early grows more. Inflation and expenses are unaffected by when saving begins.

Question 2

A client's current expenses are ₹30,000 a month and she retires in 25 years. At 6% inflation, her first-month retirement expense is closest to:

  1. ₹75,000
  2. ₹1,05,000
  3. ₹1,28,756
  4. ₹1,80,000
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. ₹1,28,756

Future expense = 30,000 × 1.06^25 ≈ ₹1,28,756. ₹75,000 adds 6% simple growth each year and understates the effect of compounding inflation.

Question 3

A retiree's corpus earns 7% while inflation is 7%. Her real rate of return is:

  1. Zero
  2. 7%
  3. 14%
  4. Negative 7%
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Zero

Real return = (1.07 ÷ 1.07) − 1 = 0. The corpus grows only enough to keep pace with inflation, so withdrawals in real terms simply deplete principal.

Question 4

Which retirement risk relates to rising medical costs with age?

  1. Reinvestment risk
  2. Healthcare cost risk
  3. Currency risk
  4. Credit risk
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Healthcare cost risk

Healthcare costs tend to rise faster than general inflation and increase with age, so retirement plans should include adequate health cover and a medical reserve.

Question 5

The accumulation phase of retirement planning refers to:

  1. Years after retirement when the corpus is spent
  2. The month of retirement only
  3. Working years when the corpus is built
  4. The period after the corpus is exhausted
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Working years when the corpus is built

During the accumulation phase, the client saves and invests to build the retirement corpus. The distribution phase starts at retirement, when the corpus funds expenses.

Question 6

Which action would most reduce a client's required retirement corpus, all else equal?

  1. Retiring earlier
  2. Assuming higher inflation
  3. Planning for a longer life expectancy
  4. Retiring later
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Retiring later

Retiring later shortens the retirement period and lengthens the saving period, reducing the corpus needed. Earlier retirement, higher inflation and longer life all raise it.

Question 7

Why should retirement planning assume a life expectancy beyond the average?

  1. Average life expectancy is fixed by law
  2. Many people outlive the average
  3. Insurers require it for every client
  4. Corpus need falls with longer life
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Many people outlive the average

Average life expectancy means about half of people live longer. Planning to an older age protects against outliving savings, the core of longevity risk.

Question 8

Expenses that typically fall after retirement include:

  1. Commuting and work-related costs
  2. Medical and health insurance costs
  3. Leisure travel in early retirement
  4. Domestic help as mobility reduces
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Commuting and work-related costs

Work-related costs such as commuting and work clothing usually fall after retirement, while health, leisure and assistance costs often rise.
