Retirement Products & Pension Schemes: NISM Series X-B practice questions

8 free questions with answers and explanations on Retirement Products & Pension Schemes, from the NISM Series X-B (Investment Adviser (Level 2)) syllabus.

Last updated 26 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · How we create questions

What this chapter covers

This chapter covers the products used to save for retirement in India: Employees' Provident Fund and EPS, Voluntary Provident Fund, Public Provident Fund, the National Pension System (Tier I and Tier II), Atal Pension Yojana, annuity products, senior citizens' schemes and retirement-oriented mutual funds. Learn eligibility, contribution rules, lock-in, withdrawal and exit rules, and tax treatment in broad terms. Limits and exit rules are revised from time to time, so focus on structure and suitability.

Retirement Products & Pension Schemes questions

Question 1

Under NPS, the subscriber's contributions are invested by:

  1. The subscriber's employer directly
  2. Banks as fixed deposits only
  3. The Employees' Provident Fund Organisation
  4. PFRDA-registered pension fund managers
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. PFRDA-registered pension fund managers

NPS contributions are managed by pension fund managers regulated by PFRDA, across equity, corporate bonds, government securities and alternative assets as chosen by the subscriber.

Question 2

The Voluntary Provident Fund allows a salaried employee to:

  1. Contribute more than the mandatory EPF share
  2. Withdraw EPF money every month
  3. Invest EPF money in equity directly
  4. Transfer EPF to his spouse's account
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Contribute more than the mandatory EPF share

VPF lets employees contribute above the statutory EPF contribution, earning the EPF interest rate. The employer is not required to match the extra contribution.

Question 3

An NPS subscriber can choose 'auto choice'. This means:

  1. All money is invested only in equity
  2. Asset mix changes with age
  3. The subscriber picks stocks personally
  4. Contributions are kept in a savings account
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Asset mix changes with age

Under auto choice, NPS assets are allocated across asset classes according to a life-cycle fund, reducing equity exposure as the subscriber ages. Active choice lets the subscriber set the mix.

Question 4

Which scheme is aimed mainly at workers in the unorganised sector, offering a guaranteed minimum pension?

  1. Employees' Provident Fund
  2. Senior Citizens' Savings Scheme
  3. Atal Pension Yojana
  4. Pradhan Mantri Vaya Vandana Yojana
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Atal Pension Yojana

APY offers a guaranteed minimum monthly pension after 60 depending on contribution, targeting unorganised sector workers. EPF is for organised sector employees and SCSS is a deposit scheme for seniors.

Question 5

An immediate annuity bought with a lump sum at retirement:

  1. Defers income for at least ten years
  2. Pays only on death of the annuitant
  3. Starts regular income straight away
  4. Allows withdrawal of capital at any time
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Starts regular income straight away

An immediate annuity converts a lump sum into regular income starting soon after purchase. A deferred annuity starts payments later, and annuities usually do not allow free capital withdrawal.

Question 6

Employer contributions to EPF are split between EPF and the Employees' Pension Scheme. The EPS portion provides:

  1. A monthly pension after retirement
  2. A lump sum gratuity on resignation
  3. Health cover for the family
  4. Life insurance of a fixed amount
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. A monthly pension after retirement

Part of the employer's contribution goes to EPS, which pays a monthly pension based on pensionable salary and service. EDLI provides insurance; gratuity is separate.

Question 7

Why might a retiree choose a 'joint life with return of purchase price' annuity?

  1. Highest possible annuity rate for one person
  2. No income until both spouses die
  3. Guaranteed equity-linked returns
  4. Income for both spouses, capital to heirs
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Income for both spouses, capital to heirs

This option pays income while either spouse lives and returns the purchase price to nominees after both die. The trade-off is a lower annuity rate than a single-life option.

Question 8

A retirement-oriented mutual fund scheme under SEBI's solution-oriented category has a lock-in of:

  1. No lock-in
  2. Five years or till retirement, whichever is earlier
  3. One year
  4. Until age 75
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Five years or till retirement, whichever is earlier

SEBI's retirement funds are solution-oriented schemes with a lock-in of at least five years or until retirement age, whichever is earlier, encouraging long-term saving.
