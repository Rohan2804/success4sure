Sample 1 · Risk Management & Insurance Concepts

For a risk that has low probability but very high potential financial loss, such as premature death of the main earner, the most appropriate risk-management technique is usually:

  1. Risk retention by paying losses from savings
  2. Risk transfer through insurance
  3. Ignoring the risk because it is unlikely
  4. Risk reduction by cutting household expenses
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Risk transfer through insurance

Low-frequency, high-severity risks cannot be absorbed by most households, so they are best transferred to an insurer.

Sample 2 · Life Insurance Products

Which feature correctly describes a pure term life insurance plan?

  1. It pays the sum assured plus bonuses on survival to maturity
  2. It invests premiums in market-linked funds chosen by the policyholder
  3. It provides cover for the whole of life and builds a high surrender value
  4. It gives the largest cover per rupee of premium and pays only on death during the term
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. It gives the largest cover per rupee of premium and pays only on death during the term

A term plan has no savings component, so premiums are low relative to the sum assured and the benefit is payable only on death within the policy term.

Sample 3 · Health & General Insurance

In a family floater health policy, the sum insured:

  1. Is separately available in full to each covered member
  2. Applies only to the eldest member
  3. Is shared by all covered family members and can be used by any one or more of them
  4. Is paid only on the death of a member
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Is shared by all covered family members and can be used by any one or more of them

A floater pools one sum insured across the family; one member's large claim can exhaust it for others in that policy year.

Sample 4 · Insurance Needs Analysis

Sanjay contributes ₹9 lakh a year to his family's needs and has 20 working years left. Using a 6% discount rate and assuming year-end contributions, what is his human life value (approximately)?

  1. ₹1.03 crore
  2. ₹1.80 crore
  3. ₹0.90 crore
  4. ₹1.25 crore
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. ₹1.03 crore

HLV = 9,00,000 × [1 − 1.06^−20] ÷ 0.06 = 9,00,000 × 11.47 ≈ ₹1.03 crore. ₹1.80 crore ignores discounting.

Sample 5 · Retirement Planning Concepts

In retirement planning, 'longevity risk' refers to the risk that:

  1. Interest rates rise just before retirement
  2. The retiree outlives the retirement corpus
  3. The retiree dies before retirement
  4. Equity markets fall in the first year of retirement
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. The retiree outlives the retirement corpus

Longevity risk is the danger of living longer than planned, so that savings run out while the retiree still needs income.

NISM Series X-B mock test FAQs

How many questions are there in the NISM Series X-B exam?

The NISM Series X-B paper has 100 multiple-choice questions for 100 marks, to be completed in 180 minutes. This is the indicative pattern — always confirm it in the latest official notification.

Is there negative marking in NISM Series X-B?

Yes. −0.25 mark per wrong answer (25% of the marks for that question). Our mock tests apply the same rule.

What is the pass mark for NISM Series X-B?

You need 60% to pass. Our mock tests show pass or fail against the same mark.

Are the NISM Series X-B mock tests free?

Yes. All 30 full-length NISM Series X-B mock tests are free, with no payment or plan. Create a free account (one click with Google) to start; your results are saved across devices. You can retake any test as often as you like.

Can I take NISM X-B before X-A?

NISM structures the investment adviser certification as two levels, and most candidates take X-A first because X-B builds on its concepts. For SEBI registration you will need both. Check nism.ac.in for any current rules on the order in which the two levels can be taken.

Why do learners find NISM Series X-B harder than X-A?

X-B relies heavily on case studies that combine tax, insurance, retirement and estate planning in one scenario. You must read carefully, pick the relevant data and complete multi-step calculations quickly. Regular timed practice with case-based questions is the most effective way to prepare.

How long is the NISM X-A and X-B certification valid?

NISM certificates are generally valid for three years from the date of passing. Investment advisers usually maintain their certification through NISM's Continuing Professional Education programme or by passing the exam again. Confirm the current rules on nism.ac.in and SEBI's IA regulations.
