Income Tax Fundamentals: NISM Series X-B practice questions

8 free questions with answers and explanations on Income Tax Fundamentals, from the NISM Series X-B (Investment Adviser (Level 2)) syllabus.

Last updated 20 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · How we create questions

What this chapter covers

This chapter covers the basics of Indian income tax for individuals: residential status, heads of income, the old and new tax regimes, deductions and exemptions, rebate, surcharge and cess, clubbing of income, set-off and carry forward of losses, advance tax and filing returns. The Income-tax Act, 2025 has replaced the 1961 Act from April 2026 with largely similar concepts, so focus on principles rather than section numbers, and use current slab and rebate figures only when sure of the year.

Income Tax Fundamentals questions

Question 1

A resident salaried individual opts for the new regime for FY 2025-26. Gross salary is ₹12,50,000 with no other income. After the standard deduction and rebate, his income tax is:

  1. ₹60,000
  2. Nil
  3. ₹71,500
  4. ₹1,25,000
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Nil

Taxable income = 12,50,000 − 75,000 standard deduction = ₹11,75,000, which is within the ₹12 lakh rebate limit for FY 2025-26, so tax becomes nil. ₹60,000 is the tax before rebate on ₹12 lakh.

Question 2

Rent received from a house property let out by an individual is taxed under the head:

  1. Income from other sources
  2. Profits and gains of business
  3. Income from house property
  4. Capital gains
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Income from house property

Rental income from buildings and land appurtenant is taxed as income from house property, with a standard deduction allowed on net annual value. It is not business income unless property letting is the business itself.

Question 3

A father transfers money to his minor son, who earns interest on it. In whose hands is this interest usually taxed?

  1. The parent with higher income
  2. The minor son alone
  3. Neither, as minors are exempt
  4. The bank paying the interest
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. The parent with higher income

Income of a minor child is generally clubbed with the income of the parent whose income is higher, subject to small exemptions and exceptions such as income from the child's own skill.

Question 4

Advance tax is payable by an individual when:

  1. He is a senior citizen with only pension income
  2. He files his return after the due date
  3. His income is entirely agricultural
  4. Estimated tax for the year exceeds a set threshold
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Estimated tax for the year exceeds a set threshold

Advance tax must be paid in instalments during the year if estimated liability exceeds the prescribed limit. Resident senior citizens without business income are exempt.

Question 5

Which of the following is a capital asset under income tax law?

  1. Listed shares held as investment
  2. Stock-in-trade of a trader
  3. Personal clothing worn by the owner
  4. Agricultural land in a rural area
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Listed shares held as investment

Investments such as listed shares are capital assets. Stock-in-trade, personal effects like clothing and specified rural agricultural land are excluded from the definition.

Question 6

An individual is resident in India for a tax year if, among other conditions, he is in India for at least:

  1. 30 days in that year
  2. 365 days in that year
  3. 182 days in that year
  4. 90 days in each of the last ten years
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 182 days in that year

Staying in India for 182 days or more in the year makes an individual resident. There is also an alternative 60-day plus 365-day test, with modifications for certain citizens.

Question 7

Health and education cess on income tax is levied on:

  1. Gross total income
  2. Only salary income
  3. Only capital gains
  4. Tax plus surcharge
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Tax plus surcharge

Cess of 4% is charged on the amount of income tax plus any surcharge, not on income directly. It applies across heads of income.

Question 8

Under the old regime, a deduction for life insurance premium and PPF contributions is available up to an overall limit for:

  1. Any expense on consumer goods
  2. Specified savings and payments
  3. Rent paid to parents only
  4. Losses from share trading
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Specified savings and payments

The old regime allows a deduction for specified investments and payments such as PPF, ELSS, life premiums and principal repayment of home loans, up to an overall ceiling. It is not available in the new regime.
