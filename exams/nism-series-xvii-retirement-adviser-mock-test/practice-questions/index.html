Free NISM Series XVII practice questions

Question 1 · Retirement Planning: An Overview

The National Pension System is an example of which type of pension arrangement?

  1. Defined benefit, where the pension is a fixed percentage of last salary
  2. Defined contribution, where the pension depends on contributions and investment returns
  3. A pay-as-you-go social security scheme
  4. A pure insurance product with guaranteed maturity value
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Defined contribution, where the pension depends on contributions and investment returns

In NPS the contribution is defined and the eventual benefit depends on accumulated corpus and returns.

Question 2 · Pension Systems & Regulatory Framework (PFRDA)

What is the main function of a Central Recordkeeping Agency (CRA) in NPS?

  1. Investing subscriber contributions in equities and bonds
  2. Selling annuities to retiring subscribers
  3. Setting the tax rules for NPS
  4. Maintaining subscriber records, issuing PRANs and providing account administration and grievance handling
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Maintaining subscriber records, issuing PRANs and providing account administration and grievance handling

The CRA is the record-keeping and administration backbone of NPS; investment is done by pension funds and annuities by insurers.

Question 3 · National Pension System – Architecture

Under the all-citizen model, up to what age can an Indian citizen join NPS?

  1. 60 years
  2. 70 years
  3. 65 years
  4. 75 years
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 70 years

PFRDA allows citizens aged 18 to 70 to open an NPS account.

Question 4 · NPS – Investment Options & Fund Management

Under the Auto Choice option in NPS, the allocation to equity:

  1. Reduces progressively as the subscriber ages, according to the chosen life-cycle fund
  2. Remains constant throughout
  3. Increases as the subscriber nears retirement
  4. Is decided monthly by the POP
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Reduces progressively as the subscriber ages, according to the chosen life-cycle fund

Life-cycle funds automatically shift from equity to debt with age, de-risking the corpus as retirement nears.

Question 5 · NPS – Exit, Withdrawal & Annuity

From whom must an NPS subscriber purchase the mandatory annuity on exit?

  1. The Point of Presence
  2. Any bank offering fixed deposits
  3. The Central Recordkeeping Agency
  4. A life insurer empanelled by PFRDA as an Annuity Service Provider
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. A life insurer empanelled by PFRDA as an Annuity Service Provider

Annuities are bought from IRDAI-registered life insurers empanelled by PFRDA as Annuity Service Providers.

Question 6 · Other Retirement Products (EPF, PPF, SCSS, APY, MFs)

Of the employer's 12% contribution under the EPF scheme, how much is diverted to the Employees' Pension Scheme (EPS)?

  1. The entire 12%
  2. 8.33%, computed on wages up to the statutory wage ceiling
  3. 3.67% of wages
  4. Nothing; EPS is funded only by the government
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 8.33%, computed on wages up to the statutory wage ceiling

8.33% of wages (subject to the wage ceiling) goes to EPS and the balance is credited to the employee's EPF account.

Question 7 · Retirement Planning Process & Corpus Estimation

What is the logical first step in the retirement planning process?

  1. Selecting an annuity provider
  2. Assessing the client's current financial situation and retirement goals
  3. Choosing a pension fund manager
  4. Drawing up a withdrawal schedule
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Assessing the client's current financial situation and retirement goals

Corpus estimation, product selection and withdrawal planning all depend on first understanding the client's present position and objectives.

Question 8 · Investment & Asset Allocation for Retirement

What is a 'glide path' in retirement investing?

  1. A planned reduction in equity exposure as the investor approaches retirement
  2. A rule to increase equity every year after retirement
  3. A fixed 50:50 allocation held for life
  4. A schedule of tax payments on withdrawals
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. A planned reduction in equity exposure as the investor approaches retirement

A glide path de-risks the portfolio gradually so that a market fall near retirement does less damage.

Question 9 · Distribution Phase & Annuities

For the same purchase price and age, which annuity option typically gives the highest periodic payout?

  1. Life annuity without return of purchase price
  2. Life annuity with return of purchase price
  3. Joint life last-survivor annuity with return of purchase price
  4. Joint life annuity with return of purchase price and annual increase
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Life annuity without return of purchase price

Because nothing is returned to nominees and only one life is covered, the insurer can pay the highest annuity rate under this option.

Question 10 · Taxation of Retirement Benefits

How is annuity income received from an NPS annuity plan taxed?

  1. It is fully exempt
  2. It is taxed as long-term capital gains
  3. It is taxable as income in the annuitant's hands at applicable slab rates
  4. It is taxed only once, at the time of annuity purchase
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. It is taxable as income in the annuitant's hands at applicable slab rates

Annuity payments are taxable as income in the year of receipt; the exemption applies only to the permitted lump-sum withdrawal.

Question 11 · Health, Insurance & Estate Aspects in Retirement

Which statement about a reverse mortgage loan for senior citizens is correct?

  1. The bank takes ownership of the house immediately
  2. The borrower must repay monthly EMIs
  3. Payments received are taxed as salary
  4. The homeowner keeps living in the house and receives payments, which are treated as loan and not as taxable income
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. The homeowner keeps living in the house and receives payments, which are treated as loan and not as taxable income

Under a reverse mortgage the elderly owner retains title and occupancy; the loan is settled from the property after death or move-out.

Question 12 · Ethics, Code of Conduct & Grievance Redressal

A retirement adviser can earn a higher commission by recommending a particular product. What does the code of conduct require?

  1. Recommend the higher-commission product without disclosure
  2. Avoid giving any recommendation
  3. Disclose the conflict and recommend only what is suitable for the client
  4. Let the product provider decide the recommendation
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Disclose the conflict and recommend only what is suitable for the client

Advisers must put client interests first, disclose conflicts of interest and base recommendations on suitability.

Question 13 · Retirement Planning: An Overview

For Central Government employees (other than armed forces), the National Pension System replaced the old defined-benefit pension for those joining service on or after:

  1. 1 April 2009
  2. 1 April 2015
  3. 1 January 2004
  4. 1 January 1998
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 1 January 2004

NPS became mandatory for new Central Government recruits from 1 January 2004; it opened to all citizens in 2009.

Question 14 · Pension Systems & Regulatory Framework (PFRDA)

In the NPS architecture, a Point of Presence (POP) is:

  1. The pension fund that manages the money
  2. The entity through which subscribers register and make contributions
  3. The insurer that pays annuities
  4. The trustee that holds legal title to all NPS assets
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. The entity through which subscribers register and make contributions

POPs, such as banks and other registered entities, are the customer-facing access points for NPS subscribers.

Question 15 · National Pension System – Architecture

Which condition must be met to open an NPS Tier II account?

  1. The subscriber must be above 60
  2. The subscriber must be a government employee
  3. The subscriber must close the Tier I account first
  4. The subscriber must hold an active Tier I account
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. The subscriber must hold an active Tier I account

Tier II is an optional add-on account available only to subscribers with an active Tier I account.
