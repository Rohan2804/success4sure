Pension Systems & Regulatory Framework (PFRDA): NISM Series XVII practice questions

8 free questions with answers and explanations on Pension Systems & Regulatory Framework (PFRDA), from the NISM Series XVII (Retirement Adviser) syllabus.

Last updated 16 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · How we create questions

What this chapter covers

This chapter covers the structure of the Indian pension system and its regulation. It explains the pillars of pension provision, the statutory schemes for organised-sector workers (EPF, EPS), government pension arrangements, and the role of the Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority under the PFRDA Act, 2013 in regulating the National Pension System and other pension schemes. It also introduces intermediaries such as the NPS Trust, central recordkeeping agencies, pension funds, trustee bank, custodian, points of presence, annuity service providers and retirement advisers. Revise each intermediary's role and the powers of PFRDA.

Pension Systems & Regulatory Framework (PFRDA) questions

Question 1

Which entity in the NPS architecture holds the assets of NPS in trust for the benefit of subscribers?

  1. The Central Recordkeeping Agency
  2. The NPS Trust
  3. The Point of Presence
  4. The Annuity Service Provider
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. The NPS Trust

The NPS Trust, set up by PFRDA, holds NPS assets for the benefit of subscribers and oversees pension funds. CRAs keep records, POPs onboard subscribers and ASPs pay annuities.

Question 2

Individuals who wish to act as retirement advisers to NPS subscribers are registered and regulated by:

  1. SEBI
  2. IRDAI
  3. EPFO
  4. PFRDA
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. PFRDA

Retirement advisers for NPS are registered with PFRDA under its retirement adviser regulations. SEBI regulates investment advisers for securities and IRDAI regulates insurance intermediaries.

Question 3

Which body administers the Employees' Provident Fund and the Employees' Pension Scheme for the organised private sector?

  1. Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority
  2. National Pension System Trust
  3. Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO)
  4. Life Insurance Corporation of India
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO)

EPF and EPS are administered by the EPFO under the EPF and Miscellaneous Provisions framework. PFRDA regulates NPS, not EPF.

Question 4

A bank appointed in the NPS structure to receive contributions from POPs and nodal offices and transfer them to pension funds is the:

  1. Trustee bank
  2. Custodian
  3. Annuity service provider
  4. Sponsor bank of the pension fund
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Trustee bank

The trustee bank manages the banking side of NPS: it receives contributions and passes them to pension fund managers and pays out withdrawals as instructed. The custodian holds securities.

Question 5

In NPS, which intermediary is responsible for the safekeeping of securities bought by pension fund managers?

  1. The trustee bank
  2. The custodian
  3. The Point of Presence
  4. The retirement adviser
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. The custodian

The custodian holds and settles the securities in which NPS money is invested. The trustee bank handles fund flows, not securities.

Question 6

Which of the following is a power of PFRDA under the PFRDA Act, 2013?

  1. Fixing interest rates on PPF and small savings schemes
  2. Deciding EPF interest rates each year
  3. Granting licences to life insurers to sell annuities
  4. Registering and regulating intermediaries and protecting subscriber interests
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Registering and regulating intermediaries and protecting subscriber interests

PFRDA registers and regulates NPS intermediaries and protects subscribers. Small savings rates are set by the government, EPF rates are decided under the EPF framework, and insurers are licensed by IRDAI.

Question 7

Annuity service providers that pay pensions to NPS subscribers on exit are:

  1. Life insurers empanelled by PFRDA
  2. Pension fund managers of NPS
  3. Scheduled commercial banks acting as POPs
  4. Mutual funds offering monthly income schemes
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Life insurers empanelled by PFRDA

Only IRDAI-registered life insurers empanelled by PFRDA can act as ASPs for NPS annuities. Pension fund managers manage accumulation, not annuity payment.

Question 8

Which pension arrangement is designed for workers in the unorganised sector and guarantees a fixed minimum pension backed by the government?

  1. Employees' Pension Scheme
  2. NPS corporate model
  3. Voluntary Provident Fund
  4. Atal Pension Yojana
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Atal Pension Yojana

APY targets unorganised-sector workers and offers a government-backed fixed pension based on contribution level. EPS covers organised-sector employees, and VPF is an EPF add-on.
