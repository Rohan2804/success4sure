What this chapter covers

This chapter explains what merchant banking is, how it evolved in India and the range of services merchant bankers provide, including issue management, underwriting, corporate advisory, project counselling, loan syndication and takeover advisory. It introduces the SEBI (Merchant Bankers) Regulations, 1992 and the distinction between activities that require registration and those that do not. Revise the core functions of a lead manager, the role of merchant bankers in protecting investors through due diligence, and how they coordinate other issue intermediaries.

Introduction to Merchant Banking questions

Question 1

Which activity of a merchant banker involves arranging finance for a large project from a group of lenders?

  1. Portfolio management for retail clients
  2. Loan syndication
  3. Market making in SME shares
  4. Credit rating of the project's bonds
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Loan syndication

Loan syndication means bringing together several banks or institutions to jointly lend to a project. Portfolio management and credit rating are separate regulated activities, and market making is a trading function in SME issues.

Question 2

In a public issue with several lead managers, how is the work usually divided?

  1. Only one lead manager is allowed to sign the due diligence certificate
  2. SEBI assigns each lead manager to a different class of investors
  3. The registrar decides which lead manager handles which task
  4. Their responsibilities are allocated and disclosed
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Their responsibilities are allocated and disclosed

When more than one lead manager is appointed, the inter se allocation of responsibilities is set out and disclosed in the offer document, so accountability for each activity is clear. All lead managers remain responsible for due diligence.

Question 3

A merchant banker advising a company on how to restructure its capital and raise funds is performing:

  1. Corporate advisory services
  2. Depository participant services
  3. Stock broking services
  4. Registrar and transfer agent services
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Corporate advisory services

Advising on capital structure, fund-raising options and restructuring is corporate advisory, a traditional merchant banking service. Depository, broking and RTA services are separate intermediary functions with their own registrations.

Question 4

Which of the following is the best description of the 'issue manager' role?

  1. Holding the issue proceeds in an escrow account for the issuer
  2. Crediting allotted shares to investors' demat accounts
  3. Planning the issue and filing documents with SEBI
  4. Matching buy and sell orders on the exchange after listing
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Planning the issue and filing documents with SEBI

The issue manager (lead manager) plans the issue, conducts due diligence, prepares the offer document, files it with SEBI and coordinates other intermediaries. Bankers hold funds, depositories credit shares and exchanges match trades.

Question 5

A merchant banker underwrites ₹10 crore of a public issue. If the issue is undersubscribed and investors take up only ₹6 crore of that portion, the merchant banker must:

  1. Return the ₹6 crore to the investors
  2. Ask SEBI to cancel the issue immediately
  3. Pay a penalty of ₹4 crore to the stock exchange
  4. Take up or procure the balance ₹4 crore
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Take up or procure the balance ₹4 crore

Underwriting is a commitment to take up the shortfall. The underwriter must subscribe to, or arrange subscribers for, the unsubscribed ₹4 crore of its commitment. The issue is not automatically cancelled.

Question 6

Why is a merchant banker described as a 'gatekeeper' of the primary market?

  1. Its due diligence screens issues before they reach investors
  2. It decides which investors may trade in the secondary market
  3. It fixes the listing fee charged by stock exchanges
  4. It guarantees a minimum listing-day gain for investors
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Its due diligence screens issues before they reach investors

Merchant bankers verify disclosures and compliance before an issue is launched, acting as a first line of protection for investors. They do not control secondary trading, set exchange fees or guarantee returns.

Question 7

Which of the following services would a merchant banker typically NOT provide?

  1. Managing public issues of securities
  2. Advising on mergers and takeovers
  3. Accepting public deposits like a commercial bank
  4. Underwriting issues of securities
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Accepting public deposits like a commercial bank

Accepting deposits from the public is a banking activity regulated by the RBI. Issue management, M&A advisory and underwriting are core merchant banking activities under SEBI regulations.

Question 8

In project counselling, a merchant banker mainly helps a client with:

  1. Collecting application forms from investors at bank branches
  2. Assessing project viability and preparing a financing plan
  3. Holding the client's shares in a depository account
  4. Clearing and settling the client's stock trades
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Assessing project viability and preparing a financing plan

Project counselling covers feasibility studies, cost estimates, means of finance and preparing project reports for lenders. The other options are functions of bankers, depositories and clearing corporations.
