Sample 1 · Introduction to Capital Market

Which feature best distinguishes the primary market from the secondary market?

  1. Securities are issued for the first time and the issuer receives the money
  2. Only listed securities are traded between existing investors
  3. Prices are always fixed by SEBI rather than by demand and supply
  4. Only institutional investors are permitted to participate
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Securities are issued for the first time and the issuer receives the money

In the primary market, new securities are created and sold so funds flow to the issuer; secondary market trades happen between investors.

Sample 2 · Introduction to Merchant Banking

Under the SEBI (Merchant Bankers) Regulations, 1992, the core activity that defines a merchant banker is:

  1. Accepting public deposits and granting loans to corporates
  2. Holding securities in electronic form on behalf of investors
  3. Issue management, including arranging the selling, buying or subscription of securities and acting as manager, consultant or adviser
  4. Executing trades on a stock exchange on behalf of clients
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Issue management, including arranging the selling, buying or subscription of securities and acting as manager, consultant or adviser

The regulations define a merchant banker by its issue-management business; deposit taking, custody and broking are other activities.

Sample 3 · Registration, Code of Conduct & Obligations

Which of the following is permitted to act as a merchant banker in India?

  1. Any chartered accountant in practice without SEBI registration
  2. A body corporate registered with SEBI as a merchant banker
  3. Any listed company for its own issues without registration
  4. An individual investor with a demat account
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. A body corporate registered with SEBI as a merchant banker

No one can carry on merchant banking activity without a certificate of registration from SEBI, and registration is granted to body corporates.

Sample 4 · Issue Management – Important Terms

What does the term 'red herring prospectus' (RHP) refer to in a book-built issue?

  1. A prospectus filed after the issue has closed containing allotment details
  2. An offer document that does not contain the final price or the exact number of shares offered
  3. A document used only for private placements to QIBs
  4. A prospectus that SEBI has rejected
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. An offer document that does not contain the final price or the exact number of shares offered

The RHP carries all disclosures except the final price and quantity, which are determined through book building and appear in the final prospectus.

Sample 5 · Issue Management – Process & Underwriting

Which of the following is one of the eligibility tests under Regulation 6(1) of SEBI ICDR for a main-board IPO?

  1. An average operating profit of at least ₹15 crore during the preceding three years, with operating profit in each of those years
  2. A net worth of at least ₹100 crore in each of the preceding five years
  3. A minimum of 10,000 shareholders before filing the draft offer document
  4. A track record of dividends for ten consecutive years
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. An average operating profit of at least ₹15 crore during the preceding three years, with operating profit in each of those years

Regulation 6(1) requires net tangible assets, an operating profit track record (average ₹15 crore over three years with profit each year) and net worth tests.
