General Awareness: SSC CGL practice questions

15 free questions with answers and explanations on General Awareness, from the SSC CGL (Combined Graduate Level (Tier 1)) syllabus.

Last updated 20 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · How we create questions

What this section covers

General Awareness covers static general knowledge and science. Key areas are Indian polity (Constitution, Fundamental Rights and Duties, Parliament, amendments), modern, medieval and ancient Indian history, Indian and world geography (rivers, passes, soils, peaks), basic economics (budget and deficit concepts, banking terms), and everyday physics, chemistry and biology. Culture, awards and sports also appear. Focus on stable facts that can be learnt once: important Articles and Schedules, landmark events with dates, physical geography, and the reasoning behind science facts. Revise with short lists and comparison tables so that similar-looking options can be told apart quickly.

General Awareness questions

Question 1

The idea of Fundamental Duties in the Indian Constitution was borrowed from the constitution of which country?

  1. United States of America
  2. Ireland
  3. Canada
  4. Erstwhile USSR
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Erstwhile USSR

Fundamental Duties (Article 51A) were inspired by the constitution of the former USSR and added by the 42nd Amendment in 1976. Ireland, by contrast, inspired the Directive Principles of State Policy.

Question 2

The Right to Education for children aged 6 to 14 years (Article 21A) was inserted into the Constitution by which amendment?

  1. 73rd Amendment Act, 1992
  2. 86th Amendment Act, 2002
  3. 42nd Amendment Act, 1976
  4. 44th Amendment Act, 1978
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 86th Amendment Act, 2002

The 86th Amendment Act, 2002 inserted Article 21A, making free and compulsory education a Fundamental Right for children aged 6–14. It also added a related Fundamental Duty for parents and guardians.

Question 3

Which Schedule of the Constitution of India lists the officially recognised languages?

  1. Ninth Schedule
  2. Tenth Schedule
  3. Seventh Schedule
  4. Eighth Schedule
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Eighth Schedule

The Eighth Schedule lists the languages recognised by the Constitution. The Seventh Schedule contains the Union, State and Concurrent Lists, and the Tenth deals with anti-defection.

Question 4

What is the minimum age required to become a member of the Rajya Sabha?

  1. 25 years
  2. 21 years
  3. 30 years
  4. 35 years
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 30 years

Article 84 sets the minimum age at 30 years for the Rajya Sabha and 25 years for the Lok Sabha. 35 years is the minimum age for the President and Vice-President.

Question 5

Who is credited with founding the Indian National Congress in 1885?

  1. Dadabhai Naoroji
  2. W. C. Bonnerjee
  3. Surendranath Banerjee
  4. A. O. Hume
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. A. O. Hume

Allan Octavian Hume, a retired British civil servant, took the initiative to found the Congress in 1885. W. C. Bonnerjee presided over its first session in Bombay, which makes him a tempting wrong choice.

Question 6

The Permanent Settlement of land revenue in Bengal (1793) was introduced by which Governor-General?

  1. Warren Hastings
  2. Lord Dalhousie
  3. Lord Cornwallis
  4. Lord Wellesley
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Lord Cornwallis

Lord Cornwallis introduced the Permanent Settlement in 1793, fixing revenue permanently with zamindars. Wellesley is linked to the Subsidiary Alliance and Dalhousie to the Doctrine of Lapse.

Question 7

The Buland Darwaza at Fatehpur Sikri was built by which Mughal ruler?

  1. Shah Jahan
  2. Humayun
  3. Aurangzeb
  4. Akbar
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Akbar

Akbar built the Buland Darwaza around 1575–76 to commemorate his Gujarat campaign. Fatehpur Sikri itself was Akbar's capital; Shah Jahan is associated with the Taj Mahal and Red Fort.

Question 8

Which mountain pass connects Srinagar with Leh?

  1. Zoji La
  2. Nathu La
  3. Bomdi La
  4. Shipki La
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Zoji La

Zoji La links the Kashmir valley with Ladakh on the Srinagar–Leh route. Nathu La is in Sikkim, Shipki La in Himachal Pradesh and Bomdi La in Arunachal Pradesh.

Question 9

Black soil (regur) of the Deccan plateau is best suited for growing which crop?

  1. Tea
  2. Rubber
  3. Cotton
  4. Jute
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Cotton

Black soil retains moisture well and is rich in lime and magnesium, making it ideal for cotton, hence the name 'black cotton soil'. Tea needs acidic hill soils and jute grows in alluvial delta soils.

Question 10

Which is the highest peak in South India?

  1. Dhupgarh
  2. Mahendragiri
  3. Anamudi
  4. Doddabetta
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Anamudi

Anamudi (about 2,695 m) in the Anaimalai Hills of Kerala is the highest peak south of the Himalayas. Doddabetta is the highest peak of the Nilgiris, and Dhupgarh is in the Satpura range.

Question 11

Fiscal deficit of the government is best defined as:

  1. Fiscal deficit minus interest payments on past debt
  2. Revenue expenditure minus revenue receipts
  3. Total expenditure minus total receipts excluding borrowings
  4. Total expenditure minus revenue receipts and all borrowings
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Total expenditure minus total receipts excluding borrowings

Fiscal deficit = total expenditure − (revenue receipts + non-debt capital receipts), i.e. it equals the government's borrowing requirement. Revenue expenditure minus revenue receipts is the revenue deficit; deficit minus interest is primary deficit.

Question 12

Which group of chemicals is chiefly responsible for depletion of the stratospheric ozone layer?

  1. Chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs)
  2. Nitrogen and argon
  3. Carbon dioxide and methane
  4. Sulphur dioxide and ammonia
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs)

CFCs release chlorine atoms in the stratosphere that break down ozone molecules. Carbon dioxide and methane are greenhouse gases linked to global warming, not the main cause of ozone depletion.

Question 13

Which type of mirror is used as a rear-view mirror in vehicles?

  1. Cylindrical mirror
  2. Convex mirror
  3. Concave mirror
  4. Plane mirror
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Convex mirror

A convex mirror always forms an erect, diminished image and gives a wider field of view, which helps drivers see more traffic behind. Concave mirrors are used in torches and shaving mirrors.

Question 14

The hormone insulin is secreted by which organ of the human body?

  1. Adrenal gland
  2. Pancreas
  3. Thyroid gland
  4. Liver
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Pancreas

Insulin is produced by the beta cells of the islets of Langerhans in the pancreas and lowers blood glucose. The liver stores glucose as glycogen but does not secrete insulin.

Question 15

Which element has the highest electronegativity on the Pauling scale?

  1. Chlorine
  2. Fluorine
  3. Nitrogen
  4. Oxygen
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Fluorine

Fluorine, with a value of about 4.0, is the most electronegative element. Oxygen (about 3.4) is second; chlorine has a higher electron affinity than fluorine but lower electronegativity, which often confuses students.
