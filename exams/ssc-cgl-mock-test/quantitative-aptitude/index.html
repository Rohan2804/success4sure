Quantitative Aptitude: SSC CGL practice questions

15 free questions with answers and explanations on Quantitative Aptitude, from the SSC CGL (Combined Graduate Level (Tier 1)) syllabus.

Last updated 25 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · How we create questions

What this section covers

Quantitative Aptitude covers arithmetic (number system, HCF-LCM, percentages, profit and loss, simple and compound interest, ratio, averages, mixtures, time and work, pipes, speed-distance-time, boats), algebra (identities), geometry, mensuration, trigonometry and data interpretation. Questions reward knowing shortcut formulas such as the 2xy/(x+y) average-speed rule, unit-digit cycles and (a + b)² identities, and avoiding traps such as changing bases in percentage questions. Revise standard trigonometric values, area and volume formulas, and practise calculating percentages from small tables quickly, since accuracy matters with negative marking.

Quantitative Aptitude questions

Question 1

What is the unit digit of 7⁹⁵?

  1. 7
  2. 1
  3. 3
  4. 9
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 3

Unit digits of powers of 7 repeat in a cycle of 4: 7, 9, 3, 1. Since 95 leaves remainder 3 when divided by 4, the unit digit equals that of 7³, which is 3.

Question 2

What is the LCM of 12, 18 and 30?

  1. 120
  2. 180
  3. 360
  4. 90
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 180

12 = 2²×3, 18 = 2×3² and 30 = 2×3×5. Taking the highest power of each prime: 2² × 3² × 5 = 180. 360 is a common multiple but not the least one.

Question 3

A's salary is 25% more than B's salary. By what percentage is B's salary less than A's?

  1. 22.5%
  2. 20%
  3. 15%
  4. 25%
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 20%

If B = 100, then A = 125. B is less than A by 25, and 25/125 × 100 = 20%. The base changes to A's salary, which is why the answer is not 25%.

Question 4

A trader sells two articles for ₹1,200 each. He gains 20% on one and loses 20% on the other. What is his overall result?

  1. Loss of 4%
  2. No profit, no loss
  3. Loss of 2%
  4. Gain of 4%
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Loss of 4%

Cost prices are 1200/1.2 = ₹1,000 and 1200/0.8 = ₹1,500, total ₹2,500. Total selling price is ₹2,400, so loss = 100/2500 × 100 = 4%. Equal gain and loss percentages on equal SP always give a loss.

Question 5

A sum of money doubles itself in 8 years at simple interest. What is the rate of interest per annum?

  1. 15%
  2. 10%
  3. 12.5%
  4. 8%
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 12.5%

Doubling means interest equals the principal, i.e. 100% in 8 years. Rate = 100/8 = 12.5% per annum, using SI = P × R × T/100 with SI = P.

Question 6

What is the compound interest on ₹8,000 for 3 years at 10% per annum, compounded annually?

  1. ₹2,400
  2. ₹2,840
  3. ₹2,600
  4. ₹2,648
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. ₹2,648

Amount = 8000 × (1.1)³ = 8000 × 1.331 = ₹10,648. CI = 10,648 − 8,000 = ₹2,648. ₹2,400 is the simple interest for the same period.

Question 7

₹7,800 is divided among A, B and C in the ratio 1/2 : 1/3 : 1/4. What is B's share?

  1. ₹2,400
  2. ₹2,600
  3. ₹3,600
  4. ₹1,800
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. ₹2,400

Multiplying by the LCM 12, the ratio becomes 6 : 4 : 3, totalling 13 parts. One part = 7800/13 = ₹600, so B gets 4 × 600 = ₹2,400. ₹3,600 is A's share.

Question 8

The average of five numbers is 24. When one number is removed, the average of the remaining numbers becomes 21. What is the number removed?

  1. 33
  2. 45
  3. 3
  4. 36
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 36

Sum of five numbers = 5 × 24 = 120; sum of remaining four = 4 × 21 = 84. The removed number = 120 − 84 = 36.

Question 9

Pipe A can fill a tank in 10 hours and pipe B can empty the full tank in 15 hours. If both are opened together in an empty tank, in how many hours will it be filled?

  1. 25 hours
  2. 12 hours
  3. 6 hours
  4. 30 hours
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 30 hours

Net filling per hour = 1/10 − 1/15 = 1/30 of the tank, so it fills in 30 hours. 6 hours would be the answer only if both pipes were filling.

Question 10

A man goes from his house to the office at 40 km/h and returns along the same route at 60 km/h. What is his average speed for the whole journey?

  1. 48 km/h
  2. 45 km/h
  3. 52 km/h
  4. 50 km/h
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 48 km/h

For equal distances, average speed = 2xy/(x + y) = 2 × 40 × 60 / 100 = 48 km/h. The simple average 50 km/h is wrong because more time is spent at the slower speed.

Question 11

A boat goes 18 km/h downstream and 12 km/h upstream. What is the speed of the stream?

  1. 15 km/h
  2. 5 km/h
  3. 6 km/h
  4. 3 km/h
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 3 km/h

Stream speed = (downstream − upstream)/2 = (18 − 12)/2 = 3 km/h. The value 15 km/h is the speed of the boat in still water.

Question 12

If a + b = 10 and ab = 21, what is the value of a² + b²?

  1. 100
  2. 79
  3. 58
  4. 42
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 58

a² + b² = (a + b)² − 2ab = 100 − 42 = 58. Check: a = 7 and b = 3 satisfy both conditions, and 49 + 9 = 58.

Question 13

What is the value of sin²30° + cos²60° + tan²45°?

  1. 5/4
  2. 2
  3. 3/2
  4. 1
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 3/2

sin 30° = cos 60° = 1/2, so each square is 1/4, and tan 45° = 1. The sum is 1/4 + 1/4 + 1 = 3/2.

Question 14

The circumference of a circle is 88 cm. What is its area? (Take π = 22/7)

  1. 1232 cm²
  2. 154 cm²
  3. 308 cm²
  4. 616 cm²
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 616 cm²

2πr = 88 gives r = 88 × 7/44 = 14 cm. Area = πr² = 22/7 × 14 × 14 = 616 cm². 154 cm² is the area for radius 7 cm.

Question 15

Monthly output of a factory (units): January 400, February 500, March 450, April 650. By what percentage did April's output exceed the average output of January to March?

  1. 30%
  2. 44 4/9%
  3. 62.5%
  4. 40%
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 44 4/9%

Average for January–March = (400 + 500 + 450)/3 = 450 units. April exceeded it by 200, and 200/450 × 100 = 44 4/9% (about 44.4%).
