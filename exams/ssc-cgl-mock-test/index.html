About the SSC CGL exam

SSC CGL Tier 1 is the first stage of the Staff Selection Commission's Combined Graduate Level exam for Group B and C posts in central government ministries and departments, testing reasoning, general awareness, quantitative aptitude and English.

Who should take it: Graduates applying for central government posts such as assistant, inspector and auditor through SSC CGL.

Sample 1 · General Intelligence & Reasoning

Select the number that completes the analogy. 7 : 50 :: 9 : ?

  1. 81
  2. 80
  3. 82
  4. 90
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 82

Rule: n² + 1. 7² + 1 = 50, so 9² + 1 = 82.

Sample 2 · General Awareness

Which Article of the Constitution of India abolishes untouchability?

  1. Article 14
  2. Article 17
  3. Article 21
  4. Article 32
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Article 17

Article 17 abolishes untouchability and forbids its practice in any form.

Sample 3 · Quantitative Aptitude

What is the remainder when 2³¹ is divided by 5?

  1. 1
  2. 2
  3. 3
  4. 4
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 3

2⁴ = 16 leaves remainder 1, and 31 = 4 × 7 + 3, so the remainder equals that of 2³ = 8, i.e. 3.

Sample 4 · English Comprehension

Select the most appropriate synonym of ABUNDANT.

  1. Scarce
  2. Rare
  3. Plentiful
  4. Meagre
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Plentiful

Abundant means existing in large quantities, i.e. plentiful.

Sample 5 · General Intelligence & Reasoning

Select the odd one out. 17, 23, 29, 33

  1. 33
  2. 17
  3. 23
  4. 29
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 33

17, 23 and 29 are prime numbers; 33 = 3 × 11 is not.

SSC CGL mock test FAQs

How many questions are there in the SSC CGL exam?

The SSC CGL paper has 100 multiple-choice questions for 200 marks, to be completed in 60 minutes. This is the indicative pattern — always confirm it in the latest official notification.

Is there negative marking in SSC CGL?

Yes. −0.5 mark per wrong answer (25% of the marks for that question). Our mock tests apply the same rule.

What is the target score for SSC CGL?

Cut-offs vary by year and category; our mock tests use 40% as a target score to aim for.

How many SSC CGL mock tests do I get?

One full-length mock test is unlocked for each day of your plan: 10, 15, 20 or 30 mock tests on the 10 Days, 15 Days, 20 Days or 30 Days plans. You can retake any unlocked test while your plan is active.
