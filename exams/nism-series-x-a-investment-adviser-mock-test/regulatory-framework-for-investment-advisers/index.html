Regulatory Framework for Investment Advisers: NISM Series X-A practice questions

8 free questions with answers and explanations on Regulatory Framework for Investment Advisers, from the NISM Series X-A (Investment Adviser (Level 1)) syllabus.

Last updated 26 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · How we create questions

What this chapter covers

This chapter covers the SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013 as amended: who must register, exemptions, qualification and certification, segregation of advisory and distribution, client agreements, risk profiling and suitability, fee modes, disclosures, record keeping and audit. Rules are revised periodically, so focus on principles: an adviser is paid only by clients, must keep advice independent of distribution, must profile risk and recommend suitable products, and must keep records and disclose conflicts.

Regulatory Framework for Investment Advisers questions

Question 1

A registered investment adviser wants to also distribute mutual funds to the same client and earn commissions. Under SEBI's framework this is:

  1. Allowed if the adviser waives her advisory fee
  2. Allowed for clients with small portfolios
  3. Allowed if disclosed only after the sale
  4. Not allowed, as advice and distribution are segregated
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Not allowed, as advice and distribution are segregated

SEBI requires client-level segregation of advisory and distribution so that an adviser does not earn commissions from products she recommends. Waiving fees does not cure the conflict.

Question 2

Before giving advice, a registered investment adviser must enter into:

  1. A written agreement with the client
  2. An agreement with the fund house
  3. An arrangement with a stock broker
  4. A contract with the stock exchange
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. A written agreement with the client

IA Regulations require a client agreement covering terms, fees, scope and disclosures before advisory services begin. Arrangements with product providers would create conflicts.

Question 3

Which fee arrangement is permitted for a SEBI-registered investment adviser?

  1. Commission from mutual fund houses
  2. A share of insurance premiums
  3. A fixed fee or a fee based on assets
  4. Brokerage on trades the client places
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. A fixed fee or a fee based on assets

SEBI allows advisers to charge either a fixed fee or a fee linked to assets under advice, within prescribed limits, from the client only. Commissions and brokerage are not permitted.

Question 4

Investment advisers must conduct risk profiling to:

  1. Decide which product pays the highest fee
  2. Ensure advice suits the client's risk
  3. Fulfil a tax filing requirement
  4. Rank clients by income for marketing
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Ensure advice suits the client's risk

Risk profiling assesses a client's capacity and willingness to take risk, so advice can be matched to it. It is a core suitability requirement, not a marketing or tax tool.

Question 5

An adviser's recommended scheme can be bought by the client through:

  1. Regular plans that pay the adviser commission
  2. Any plan, as long as the client signs a waiver
  3. Only through the adviser's own trading account
  4. Direct plans with no commission
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Direct plans with no commission

Advisers implementing advice must use direct plans or commission-free routes, since they cannot earn distribution income. Using regular plans would breach segregation norms.

Question 6

Which of the following is an obligation of a registered investment adviser?

  1. Guaranteeing returns on recommended products
  2. Maintaining records of advice and its basis
  3. Sharing client data with distributors
  4. Holding client funds in its own bank account
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Maintaining records of advice and its basis

Advisers must keep records of client profiles, advice given and the rationale for the prescribed period. They may not guarantee returns, share data improperly or handle client funds.

Question 7

An insurance agent who gives advice only on insurance products sold by him needs to register as an investment adviser?

  1. No, such advice is exempt
  2. Yes, under all circumstances
  3. Yes, if he sells more than ten policies a year
  4. Only if he advises on term insurance
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. No, such advice is exempt

IA Regulations exempt insurance agents and brokers who offer advice only on insurance products regulated by IRDAI. Advising on securities would require registration.

Question 8

Who regulates and supervises registered investment advisers on behalf of SEBI through a recognised body?

  1. AMFI
  2. NSDL
  3. BSE Administration and Supervision Ltd
  4. The Insurance Ombudsman
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. BSE Administration and Supervision Ltd

SEBI has recognised BSE Administration and Supervision Limited (BASL), a BSE subsidiary, as the body to administer and supervise investment advisers. AMFI is a mutual fund industry body.
