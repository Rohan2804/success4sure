About the NISM Series X-A exam

NISM Series X-A is Level 1 of the investment adviser certification — financial planning basics, time value of money, equity, debt, mutual funds and other products, asset allocation, behavioural finance and the SEBI investment adviser regulations.

Who should take it: Individuals and principal officers seeking SEBI registration as investment advisers, who must clear X-A along with X-B.

Sample 1 · Investment Advisory & Financial Planning Basics

In a structured financial planning engagement, which step must come before recommending any specific investment product?

  1. Executing transactions through the client's broker
  2. Reviewing the performance of the recommended portfolio
  3. Understanding the client's goals, financial position and risk profile
  4. Fixing the adviser's renewal fee for the next year
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Understanding the client's goals, financial position and risk profile

Recommendations can only be suitable once the adviser has gathered data on the client's goals, finances and risk profile; implementation and monitoring come later.

Sample 2 · Time Value of Money

A client invests ₹1,00,000 for 5 years at 8% per annum compounded annually. What will the investment be worth at maturity (approximately)?

  1. ₹1,40,000
  2. ₹1,58,687
  3. ₹1,48,024
  4. ₹1,46,933
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. ₹1,46,933

FV = 1,00,000 × (1.08)^5 = 1,00,000 × 1.46933 ≈ ₹1,46,933. ₹1,40,000 would be simple interest.

Sample 3 · Client Financial Position & Debt Management

A client owns a house worth ₹60 lakh, mutual funds of ₹15 lakh, bank balances of ₹5 lakh and a car worth ₹5 lakh. He owes ₹25 lakh on a home loan, ₹3 lakh on a car loan and ₹50,000 on a credit card. What is his net worth?

  1. ₹85 lakh
  2. ₹57 lakh
  3. ₹56.5 lakh
  4. ₹51.5 lakh
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. ₹56.5 lakh

Total assets = 60 + 15 + 5 + 5 = ₹85 lakh; total liabilities = 25 + 3 + 0.5 = ₹28.5 lakh; net worth = 85 − 28.5 = ₹56.5 lakh.

Sample 4 · Indian Financial Markets

Which of the following is a money market instrument?

  1. A 10-year Government of India dated security
  2. An equity share of a listed company
  3. A 91-day Treasury bill
  4. A 15-year infrastructure bond
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. A 91-day Treasury bill

Money market instruments have original maturities of up to one year; a 91-day T-bill qualifies, whereas the others are long-term capital market instruments.

Sample 5 · Securities Market Segments

When a company sells newly issued shares to the public for the first time through an IPO, the transaction takes place in the:

  1. Primary market
  2. Secondary market
  3. Over-the-counter derivatives market
  4. Money market
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Primary market

Fresh issuance of securities by the issuer to investors is a primary market activity; later trading between investors happens in the secondary market.
