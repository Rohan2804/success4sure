Sample 1 · Securities Market Basics

In the primary market:

  1. Investors trade already-listed shares among themselves
  2. Only government securities are traded
  3. Securities are issued by the company for the first time (or additionally) to raise capital
  4. Derivatives are settled daily
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Securities are issued by the company for the first time (or additionally) to raise capital

The primary market is the new-issue market where funds flow to the issuer; existing securities trade in the secondary market.

Sample 2 · Market Participants

The two depositories in India are:

  1. NSE and BSE
  2. SEBI and RBI
  3. NSE Clearing and ICCL
  4. NSDL and CDSL
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. NSDL and CDSL

National Securities Depository Ltd and Central Depository Services (India) Ltd hold securities in electronic form.

Sample 3 · Trading Membership

After SEBI's rationalisation (2018), existing sub-brokers had to either become trading members or migrate to the category of:

  1. Authorised Person
  2. Portfolio Manager
  3. Investment Adviser
  4. Depository Participant
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Authorised Person

SEBI merged the sub-broker category into Authorised Persons, who act as agents of a trading member.

Sample 4 · Trading System & Orders

Orders in NSE's trading system are matched on the basis of:

  1. Size of order
  2. Price-time priority
  3. Client's net worth
  4. Alphabetical order of broker codes
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Price-time priority

The best-priced order gets priority; among orders at the same price, the earlier one is matched first.

Sample 5 · Clearing & Settlement

The standard settlement cycle for equity trades in India's cash market is:

  1. T+1
  2. T+2
  3. T+3
  4. T+5
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. T+1

India completed migration to T+1 rolling settlement for all listed equities in January 2023.

NCFM mock test FAQs

How many questions are there in the NCFM exam?

The NCFM paper has 60 multiple-choice questions for 100 marks, to be completed in 105 minutes. This is the indicative pattern — always confirm it in the latest official notification.

Is there negative marking in NCFM?

Yes. −0.42 mark per wrong answer (25% of the marks for that question). Our mock tests apply the same rule.

You need 50% to pass. Our mock tests show pass or fail against the same mark.
