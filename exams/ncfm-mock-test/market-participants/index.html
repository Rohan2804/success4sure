Market Participants: NCFM practice questions

8 free questions with answers and explanations on Market Participants, from the NCFM (Capital Market (Dealers) Module) syllabus.

Last updated 11 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · How we create questions

What this chapter covers

This chapter describes who participates in the securities market and what each does: stock exchanges, clearing corporations, depositories (NSDL, CDSL) and depository participants, trading members, authorised persons, custodians, merchant bankers, registrars and transfer agents, and investors such as retail investors, mutual funds, insurers and foreign portfolio investors. Focus on the distinct role each intermediary plays in a trade's life cycle, who is regulated by whom, and which intermediary a client deals with for which service, such as opening a demat account or resolving a share transfer issue.

Market Participants questions

Question 1

A company wants an agency to maintain its register of shareholders, process transfers and send dividend warrants. It should appoint a:

  1. Merchant Banker
  2. Registrar and Share Transfer Agent
  3. Depository Participant of the investors
  4. Clearing Member
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Registrar and Share Transfer Agent

Registrars and transfer agents (RTAs) maintain the records of a company's shareholders and handle investor servicing such as dividends and transfers. A depository participant serves investors' demat accounts, not the issuer's register.

Question 2

Which intermediary manages a public issue, prepares the offer document and coordinates with SEBI and the exchanges?

  1. A merchant banker acting as book running lead manager
  2. A trading member of the stock exchange on which the shares will list
  3. A depository participant
  4. A registrar to the issue
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. A merchant banker acting as book running lead manager

The book running lead manager, a SEBI-registered merchant banker, manages the issue process, does due diligence and files the offer document. The registrar handles applications and allotment, which is a separate function.

Question 3

Which of these is primarily an institutional investor rather than an intermediary?

  1. A custodian holding securities for clients
  2. A depository participant opening and servicing demat accounts for retail clients
  3. A stock broker executing orders for clients
  4. An insurance company investing its policyholders' funds
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. An insurance company investing its policyholders' funds

An insurer invests its own and policyholders' money, so it is an investor. Brokers, depository participants and custodians provide services to other investors and are intermediaries in the market.

Question 4

Who is responsible for the guaranteed settlement of trades executed on a stock exchange?

  1. The issuing company
  2. The registrar and transfer agent
  3. The clearing corporation
  4. The depository
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. The clearing corporation

The clearing corporation clears and settles trades and guarantees settlement through novation and its settlement guarantee fund. Depositories only hold and transfer securities in electronic form on its instructions.

Question 5

A clearing member who clears and settles only his own trades and those of his clients is called a:

  1. Authorised person
  2. Professional clearing member
  3. Custodial participant
  4. Self-clearing member
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Self-clearing member

A self-clearing member clears only its own (proprietary and client) trades. A professional clearing member clears trades of other trading members and custodial participants, which is the tempting but wrong choice.

Question 6

An investor's shares are held electronically in a depository. Which entity actually provides the investor with account statements and executes instructions such as delivery instructions?

  1. The registrar of the issuing company
  2. The stock exchange on which the shares are listed
  3. The depository participant with whom the account is opened
  4. The clearing corporation of the exchange
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. The depository participant with whom the account is opened

Investors interact with the depository through a depository participant, which opens the demat account, accepts delivery instructions and sends statements. The depository itself does not deal directly with individual investors.

Question 7

Foreign portfolio investors in India are registered with SEBI through:

  1. Designated depository participants authorised by SEBI
  2. The Reserve Bank of India's regional offices and the authorised dealer banks
  3. Any trading member of a recognised exchange
  4. The Ministry of Corporate Affairs
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Designated depository participants authorised by SEBI

SEBI has authorised designated depository participants (DDPs) to grant FPI registration on its behalf. Brokers execute trades but do not register FPIs, and RBI does not handle FPI registration.

Question 8

A person appointed by a trading member to provide access to the trading platform as its agent, without being a member himself, is called a/an:

  1. Sub-depository
  2. Authorised person
  3. Custodial participant
  4. Professional clearing member
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Authorised person

An authorised person is appointed by a trading member, with exchange approval, to offer access to the trading platform as the member's agent. The trading member remains responsible for the authorised person's acts.
