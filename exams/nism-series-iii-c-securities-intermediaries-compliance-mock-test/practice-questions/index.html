Free NISM Series III-C practice questions

Question 1 · Introduction to the Financial System

In the capital market, long-term funds are raised through instruments such as:

  1. Equity shares and long-term bonds
  2. Call money and treasury bills
  3. Certificates of deposit
  4. Commercial paper
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Equity shares and long-term bonds

The capital market deals in long-term instruments; the other options are money market instruments.

Question 2 · Regulatory Framework – General View

Mutual funds, AIFs, REITs and InvITs are regulated by:

  1. Ministry of Corporate Affairs
  2. IRDAI
  3. RBI
  4. SEBI
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. SEBI

All four are pooled investment vehicles under SEBI's regulatory jurisdiction.

Question 3 · Introduction to Compliance

The compliance officer of a SEBI-registered fund intermediary should:

  1. Act only when an investor complains
  2. Monitor compliance with regulations and report non-compliance to SEBI and the board as required
  3. Approve investment decisions to maximise returns
  4. Be paid a share of trading profits
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Monitor compliance with regulations and report non-compliance to SEBI and the board as required

The compliance officer monitors adherence and reports deviations; the role must remain free of conflicts.

Question 4 · SEBI Act, 1992

Which of the following is a function of SEBI under Section 11 of the SEBI Act?

  1. Issuing currency
  2. Setting income tax rates
  3. Regulating insurance companies
  4. Regulating the business of stock exchanges and mutual funds
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Regulating the business of stock exchanges and mutual funds

Section 11 lists SEBI's functions, including registering and regulating mutual funds and exchanges.

Question 5 · SCRA, 1956 and SCRR, 1957

Which Act gives legal recognition to derivatives as securities in India?

  1. Depositories Act, 1996
  2. Indian Contract Act, 1872
  3. FEMA, 1999
  4. Securities Contracts (Regulation) Act, 1956
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Securities Contracts (Regulation) Act, 1956

The SCRA includes derivatives in the definition of securities and makes them legal when traded on recognised exchanges.

Question 6 · SEBI (Intermediaries) Regulations, 2008

Under the Intermediaries Regulations, if a registered intermediary materially changes its control, it must:

  1. Obtain SEBI's prior approval
  2. Only inform its clients
  3. Do nothing, since registration is permanent
  4. Inform SEBI within five years
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Obtain SEBI's prior approval

A change in control requires prior SEBI approval to ensure the new controllers are fit and proper.

Question 7 · SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations

Unpublished Price Sensitive Information (UPSI) includes information about:

  1. Details already in the annual report
  2. Financial results that have not yet been made public
  3. Historical share prices
  4. The company's registered office address
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Financial results that have not yet been made public

UPSI is non-public information likely to materially affect the price, such as results, dividends or mergers.

Question 8 · SEBI (PFUTP) Regulations, 2003

Which of the following is prohibited as a fraudulent practice under PFUTP?

  1. Mis-selling units of a mutual fund scheme by making false statements about returns
  2. Disclosing past performance in the prescribed format
  3. Charging the disclosed exit load
  4. Recommending a scheme suited to the investor's risk profile
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Mis-selling units of a mutual fund scheme by making false statements about returns

PFUTP specifically covers mis-selling of securities and services, including MF units.

Question 9 · Prevention of Money Laundering Act, 2002

Under PMLA, a Cash Transaction Report must be filed with FIU-IND by:

  1. The end of the financial year
  2. Within 24 hours of each transaction
  3. The 15th day of the following month
  4. Within 7 working days of the transaction
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. The 15th day of the following month

CTRs for the month are due by the 15th of the next month.

Question 10 · SEBI (KYC Registration Agency) Regulations, 2011

Which body maintains the Central KYC Records Registry in India?

  1. AMFI
  2. NSDL
  3. SEBI
  4. CERSAI
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. CERSAI

The Central Registry of Securitisation Asset Reconstruction and Security Interest of India (CERSAI) runs CKYCR.

Question 11 · SEBI (Foreign Portfolio Investors) Regulations, 2019

Which of the following would typically fall under Category I FPI?

  1. A family office
  2. An unregulated corporate body
  3. A foreign central bank or sovereign wealth fund
  4. An individual foreign investor
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. A foreign central bank or sovereign wealth fund

Category I includes government-related investors and appropriately regulated entities; individuals and family offices are Category II.

Question 12 · Foreign Exchange Management Act

FEMA, 1999 replaced FERA, 1973. A key difference is that contraventions under FEMA are:

  1. Civil in nature, attracting monetary penalties
  2. Dealt with by SEBI
  3. Not punishable at all
  4. Treated only as criminal offences
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Civil in nature, attracting monetary penalties

FEMA makes most contraventions civil offences, unlike the criminal approach of FERA.

Question 13 · Depositories Act, 1996

Under the Depositories Act, 1996, an investor who holds securities in demat form is the:

  1. Registered owner
  2. Issuer
  3. Custodian
  4. Beneficial owner
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Beneficial owner

The depository is the registered owner, and the investor is the beneficial owner with all rights.

Question 14 · SEBI (Mutual Funds) Regulations

Under the SEBI (Mutual Funds) Regulations, the minimum net worth required of an AMC is:

  1. ₹5 crore
  2. ₹100 crore
  3. ₹50 crore
  4. ₹10 crore
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. ₹50 crore

SEBI requires an AMC to have a net worth of at least ₹50 crore.

Question 15 · SEBI (AIF) Regulations

Under the SEBI (AIF) Regulations, the minimum investment by an investor in an AIF (other than employees, directors and certain specified investors) is:

  1. ₹1 crore
  2. ₹5 crore
  3. ₹50 lakh
  4. ₹10 lakh
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. ₹1 crore

The standard minimum investment per investor is ₹1 crore, with a lower threshold for employees and directors of the manager.
