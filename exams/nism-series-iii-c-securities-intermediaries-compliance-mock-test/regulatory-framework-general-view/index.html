Regulatory Framework – General View: NISM Series III-C practice questions

8 free questions with answers and explanations on Regulatory Framework – General View, from the NISM Series III-C (Securities Intermediaries Compliance (Fund)) syllabus.

Last updated 23 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · How we create questions

What this chapter covers

This chapter maps India's financial regulators and the laws they administer. Learn which body regulates which segment: SEBI for securities and commodity derivatives markets, RBI for banks, NBFCs, money, government securities and foreign exchange markets, IRDAI for insurance, PFRDA for pensions and the NPS, IFSCA for GIFT City, and the Ministry of Corporate Affairs for company law. Also note coordinating bodies such as the Financial Stability and Development Council and appellate routes such as the Securities Appellate Tribunal. Focus on matching activities to the correct regulator in scenario questions.

Regulatory Framework – General View questions

Question 1

Which regulator supervises insurance companies operating in India?

  1. IRDAI
  2. PFRDA
  3. NABARD
  4. SEBI
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. IRDAI

The Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (IRDAI) regulates insurers and insurance intermediaries. PFRDA regulates pension funds and the NPS, and NABARD is a development finance institution for agriculture and rural areas.

Question 2

The National Pension System (NPS) and pension fund managers are regulated by:

  1. RBI
  2. IRDAI
  3. PFRDA
  4. Ministry of Corporate Affairs
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. PFRDA

The Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority (PFRDA) administers the NPS and registers pension fund managers. IRDAI covers insurance and RBI covers banking, so they are not the primary regulator for the NPS.

Question 3

Trading in commodity futures on a national commodity derivatives exchange is currently regulated by:

  1. RBI
  2. Forward Markets Commission
  3. Ministry of Consumer Affairs
  4. SEBI
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. SEBI

The Forward Markets Commission was merged with SEBI in 2015, and since then SEBI regulates commodity derivatives exchanges and brokers. The FMC no longer exists as a separate regulator.

Question 4

The Companies Act, 2013 is administered primarily by:

  1. Securities Appellate Tribunal
  2. Ministry of Corporate Affairs
  3. Department of Economic Affairs
  4. Reserve Bank of India
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Ministry of Corporate Affairs

The Ministry of Corporate Affairs administers the Companies Act, 2013, along with bodies such as the Registrar of Companies. SEBI administers securities-specific provisions for listed companies, but company law as a whole sits with MCA.

Question 5

Which body, chaired by the Union Finance Minister, coordinates among financial sector regulators on financial stability matters?

  1. Board for Financial Supervision of the Reserve Bank
  2. Securities Appellate Tribunal
  3. Monetary Policy Committee
  4. Financial Stability and Development Council
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Financial Stability and Development Council

The Financial Stability and Development Council (FSDC) is chaired by the Finance Minister and includes heads of the financial regulators. The Monetary Policy Committee sets the policy rate and is headed by the RBI Governor.

Question 6

A fund manager wants to set up a fund in GIFT City, Gujarat. Which regulator will register and supervise this activity in the International Financial Services Centre?

  1. RBI
  2. IFSCA
  3. SEBI
  4. Gujarat State Government
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. IFSCA

The International Financial Services Centres Authority (IFSCA) is the unified regulator for financial services in IFSCs, including GIFT City. Fund management entities there register with IFSCA, not with SEBI.

Question 7

An insurance company is aggrieved by an order passed by IRDAI. Where does its appeal lie?

  1. Securities Appellate Tribunal
  2. Debt Recovery Tribunal
  3. National Company Law Tribunal
  4. Directly to the Supreme Court
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Securities Appellate Tribunal

The Securities Appellate Tribunal hears appeals against orders of SEBI, and its jurisdiction has been extended to orders of IRDAI and PFRDA. NCLT handles company law and insolvency matters, not insurance regulatory orders.

Question 8

Which of the following markets is primarily regulated by RBI rather than SEBI?

  1. Units of mutual fund schemes
  2. Listed equity shares on stock exchanges
  3. Government securities and money market
  4. Units of REITs and InvITs
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Government securities and money market

RBI regulates the government securities, money and foreign exchange markets. Listed equities, mutual funds, REITs and InvITs all fall under SEBI's jurisdiction as part of the securities market.
