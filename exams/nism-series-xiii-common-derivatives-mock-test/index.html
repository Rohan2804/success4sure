About the NISM Series XIII exam

NISM Series XIII is a combined derivatives certification covering equity, currency and interest rate derivatives — forwards, futures, options, strategies, trading and clearing, and the regulatory framework across segments.

Who should take it: Associated persons who deal across several derivatives segments and want one certification covering equity, currency and interest rate derivatives.

Sample 1 · Basics of Derivatives

Which of the following is an example of an exchange-traded derivative?

  1. Nifty 50 index options
  2. A currency forward booked with a bank
  3. A customised interest rate swap with a bank
  4. A bilateral forward rate agreement
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Nifty 50 index options

Index options are standardised and traded on an exchange; the others are OTC contracts.

Sample 2 · Introduction to the Underlying Markets

When computing free-float market capitalisation for an index, which holdings are typically excluded?

  1. Shares held by retail investors
  2. Shares held by mutual funds
  3. Shares held by foreign portfolio investors
  4. Shares held by promoters and promoter groups
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Shares held by promoters and promoter groups

Free float counts only shares readily available for trading, so promoter and locked-in holdings are left out.

Sample 3 · Introduction to Forwards and Futures

A key feature that distinguishes futures from forwards is that futures:

  1. Are customised to each client
  2. Are marked to market daily through a clearing corporation
  3. Carry no margin requirement
  4. Can only be settled by delivery
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Are marked to market daily through a clearing corporation

Daily MTM and central clearing are hallmarks of exchange-traded futures.

Sample 4 · Strategies Using Futures

A portfolio worth ₹90 lakh has a beta of 0.8. Each index futures contract is worth ₹12 lakh. The number of contracts to sell for a full hedge is:

  1. 7.5
  2. 9
  3. 6
  4. 4.8
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 6

Contracts = (90 lakh × 0.8) ÷ 12 lakh = 72 ÷ 12 = 6.

Sample 5 · Introduction to Options

Exchange-traded equity options and USD/INR options in India are of which exercise style?

  1. American
  2. Bermudan
  3. Asian
  4. European
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. European

Both can be exercised only on the expiry date.
