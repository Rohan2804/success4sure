Professionalism, Ethics & Compliance: NISM Series XXV-B practice questions

8 free questions with answers and explanations on Professionalism, Ethics & Compliance, from the NISM Series XXV-B (Persons Associated with Investment Advice (Sales and Other Non-Core Services)) syllabus.

Last updated 23 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · How we create questions

What this chapter covers

This chapter covers the regulatory and ethical framework for investment advice: SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013 as amended, registration and qualification requirements, fiduciary duty, segregation of advisory and distribution activities, fee limits and modes, client agreement and risk profiling, disclosures, use of AI tools, record keeping, the role of the IA Administration and Supervisory Body, grievance redressal, and the limits on what non-core staff may do. Focus on prohibited practices and on the responsibilities of persons in sales and support roles.

Professionalism, Ethics & Compliance questions

Question 1

Under SEBI's segregation requirement, an individual investment adviser:

  1. May earn commissions from products recommended to advisory clients
  2. May act as both adviser and distributor if the client agrees orally
  3. Must earn all its income from distribution commissions
  4. Cannot provide distribution services to its advisory clients
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Cannot provide distribution services to its advisory clients

SEBI requires advisory and distribution to be segregated at the client level, so advice is not influenced by commissions. Advisory clients cannot be offered distribution services.

Question 2

An investment adviser's client agreement must cover:

  1. Promise of a minimum annual return to the client
  2. The client's waiver of the right to complain
  3. The adviser's right to trade in the client's account at will
  4. Terms of services, fees and the adviser's obligations
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Terms of services, fees and the adviser's obligations

SEBI requires a client agreement with prescribed terms covering services, fees, conflicts, and obligations. Assured returns and waivers of client rights are not permitted.

Question 3

Fees charged by an investment adviser to clients must be paid through:

  1. Banking channels, such as cheques or online transfers
  2. Cash handed over at the client's home
  3. Gift cards and vouchers
  4. Cryptocurrency transfers
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Banking channels, such as cheques or online transfers

Fees must be paid through traceable banking channels; cash is not permitted. SEBI's centralised fee collection mechanism further helps ensure payment only to registered advisers.

Question 4

A sales staff member at an advisory firm is asked by a client to recommend a mutual fund scheme. The staff member should:

  1. Recommend whichever scheme has the highest recent return
  2. Recommend a scheme from the staff member's personal portfolio
  3. Give advice as long as the client signs a waiver
  4. Direct the request to a qualified adviser
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Direct the request to a qualified adviser

Personal investment advice must come from qualified persons associated with investment advice. Non-core staff should route such requests to the adviser.

Question 5

Which body has SEBI recognised as the Investment Adviser Administration and Supervisory Body (IAASB)?

  1. The Association of Mutual Funds in India
  2. The Reserve Bank of India
  3. The Institute of Chartered Accountants of India
  4. BSE Limited
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. BSE Limited

SEBI recognised BSE Limited, through a subsidiary, as IAASB to administer and supervise investment advisers. It handles enlistment, monitoring and complaint escalation.

Question 6

An investment adviser using AI tools to generate advice must:

  1. Avoid any disclosure, as AI use is a trade secret
  2. Pass responsibility for any errors to the AI provider
  3. Obtain the client's consent to waive suitability
  4. Disclose AI use and remain responsible for the advice
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Disclose AI use and remain responsible for the advice

SEBI's amended framework requires advisers to disclose AI use to clients and holds the adviser responsible for the advice, data security and compliance.

Question 7

An investment adviser must carry out risk profiling of clients mainly to:

  1. Decide how much commission to earn
  2. Choose the client's tax regime
  3. Replace KYC verification
  4. Ensure that advice is suitable
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Ensure that advice is suitable

Risk profiling assesses capacity and tolerance so that recommendations match the client. It is separate from KYC and from any commission considerations, which IAs cannot earn.

Question 8

A sales executive tells a client that a recommended portfolio 'cannot lose money'. This statement is:

  1. Misleading and prohibited
  2. Acceptable if said only verbally
  3. Required to build client confidence
  4. Allowed for conservative portfolios
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Misleading and prohibited

Assuring returns or no loss misrepresents risk and breaches SEBI's code of conduct. All investments carry risk, and communications must be fair and balanced.
