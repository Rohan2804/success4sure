About the NISM Series XXV-B exam

NISM Series XXV-B is for people in sales and non-core roles at investment advisers — personal financial planning, markets and asset classes, financial products, sales skills, professionalism, ethics and compliance.

Who should take it: Sales, client-servicing and other non-core staff associated with SEBI-registered investment advisers.

Sample 1 · Personal Financial Planning & Financial Markets

Which is usually the first step in the personal financial planning process?

  1. Establishing the client relationship and gathering information about goals and finances
  2. Selecting specific mutual fund schemes
  3. Executing investment transactions
  4. Rebalancing the portfolio
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Establishing the client relationship and gathering information about goals and finances

Planning starts with understanding the client and their goals; product selection and implementation come later.

Sample 2 · Securities Markets & Basics of Investment

Using the Rule of 72, approximately how many years will it take for money to double at 9% per annum?

  1. 6 years
  2. 8 years
  3. 9 years
  4. 12 years
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 8 years

Years to double ≈ 72 ÷ rate = 72 ÷ 9 = 8 years.

Sample 3 · Financial Asset Classes

Equity shareholders of a company are:

  1. Owners who share in profits and bear residual risk
  2. Lenders who receive fixed interest
  3. Guaranteed a fixed dividend
  4. Paid before creditors on liquidation
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Owners who share in profits and bear residual risk

Equity holders own the company, receive residual profits and are last in line on liquidation.

Sample 4 · Financial Products

In India, a mutual fund is constituted as a:

  1. Private limited company owned by investors
  2. Trust, with an asset management company managing its schemes
  3. Partnership firm
  4. Department of SEBI
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Trust, with an asset management company managing its schemes

Mutual funds are set up as trusts; the AMC manages the schemes under trustee oversight and SEBI regulation.

Sample 5 · Sales Skills

In an RIA setup, how does advisory-led sales differ from product selling?

  1. It starts from the client's needs and goals, and recommends solutions only if suitable
  2. It aims to sell the product with the highest commission
  3. It pushes the same product to every client
  4. It avoids risk profiling to save time
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. It starts from the client's needs and goals, and recommends solutions only if suitable

Advisory-based sales is client-centric and suitability-driven, in line with the IA Regulations.
