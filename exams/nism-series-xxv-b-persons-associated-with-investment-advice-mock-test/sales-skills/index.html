Sales Skills: NISM Series XXV-B practice questions

8 free questions with answers and explanations on Sales Skills, from the NISM Series XXV-B (Persons Associated with Investment Advice (Sales and Other Non-Core Services)) syllabus.

Last updated 11 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · How we create questions

What this chapter covers

This chapter covers the sales and relationship skills needed in an investment advisory setting, where the adviser acts in a fiduciary capacity. Topics include advisory-led versus product-led selling, prospecting ethically, client profiling, active listening, explaining fees and value, handling objections honestly, closing without pressure, documenting interactions, and maintaining long-term relationships. It also covers handling clients influenced by tips or 'finfluencers'. Expect scenarios asking for the most ethical and effective sales response.

Sales Skills questions

Question 1

An effective first meeting with a prospective advisory client should focus mainly on:

  1. Understanding the client's goals, situation and concerns
  2. Presenting the firm's best-performing recommendation of the past year
  3. Getting the client to sign the agreement before the meeting ends
  4. Explaining market forecasts for the next quarter in detail
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Understanding the client's goals, situation and concerns

Advisory relationships begin with discovery. Understanding needs builds trust and is required for suitable advice; product pitches and pressure to sign come at the wrong stage.

Question 2

A prospect says, 'I'll think about it.' The most appropriate response is to:

  1. Tell him the fee will double if he does not sign today
  2. Offer to answer questions and agree on a follow-up
  3. Keep calling him several times a day until he agrees
  4. Tell him other clients have made guaranteed profits
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Offer to answer questions and agree on a follow-up

Respecting the client's decision time while staying available builds credibility. False urgency, harassment or claims of guaranteed profits are unethical.

Question 3

Which is an open-ended question useful in client profiling?

  1. Do you want high returns?
  2. Are you a risk-taker?
  3. What would you like your money to achieve in ten years?
  4. Is ₹10 lakh your budget?
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. What would you like your money to achieve in ten years?

Open-ended questions invite detailed answers that reveal goals and attitudes. Yes/no questions limit understanding and may lead to superficial profiling.

Question 4

A client compares the adviser's fee with a 'free' online tip service. A fair response is to explain that:

  1. Tip services are illegal and every user will face prosecution
  2. Advice is personalised, fiduciary and accountable
  3. The adviser guarantees returns, unlike the tip service
  4. The adviser's fee is negotiable to zero if the client insists
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Advice is personalised, fiduciary and accountable

An adviser's value lies in personalised, suitable advice with fiduciary duty and regulatory accountability. The adviser should avoid exaggerated claims or guarantees.

Question 5

Documenting each client meeting in the CRM matters mainly because it:

  1. Lets the sales team share client details on social media
  2. Replaces the need for a written advisory agreement
  3. Guarantees that the client will renew
  4. Supports continuity, compliance and suitability records
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Supports continuity, compliance and suitability records

Records of discussions, preferences and decisions support service continuity when staff change and demonstrate compliance with the adviser's suitability and record-keeping requirements.

Question 6

A client insists on buying a stock tipped by a social media influencer. The best approach for sales staff is to:

  1. Refer the request to the adviser for suitability review
  2. Immediately agree and help the client buy as much as possible
  3. Ridicule the client for following social media advice
  4. Recommend a more speculative stock instead
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Refer the request to the adviser for suitability review

Sales staff should not give their own recommendations. The adviser can assess suitability and explain risks, including unregistered persons' recommendations.

Question 7

Active listening during client meetings involves:

  1. Planning your next pitch while the client is speaking
  2. Interrupting to correct the client quickly
  3. Focusing only on figures and ignoring concerns
  4. Paraphrasing and clarifying what the client says
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Paraphrasing and clarifying what the client says

Active listening means giving full attention, summarising the client's points and asking clarifying questions. It helps uncover real needs and builds trust.

Question 8

Closing an advisory engagement ethically means:

  1. Persuading the client to sign before reading the agreement
  2. Offering gifts to encourage a quick signature
  3. Hiding the fee structure until after the first invoice
  4. Confirming the client understands terms, fees and risks before agreeing
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Confirming the client understands terms, fees and risks before agreeing

Ethical closing ensures informed consent: the client knows the services, fees and risks. Pressure, inducements or hidden fees breach regulations and trust.
