Free SBI PO practice questions

Question 1 · English Language

Choose the word that best fills the blank. The new policy is expected to ________ the flow of credit to small businesses, which had slowed down sharply.

  1. curtail
  2. stall
  3. hinder
  4. boost
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. boost

Since credit had slowed, the policy is expected to increase (boost) it; the other words mean reduce or stop.

Question 2 · Quantitative Aptitude

Find the wrong number in the series: 4, 9, 25, 49, 121, 169, 288

  1. 121
  2. 49
  3. 288
  4. 25
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 288

The series is squares of consecutive primes: 2², 3², 5², 7², 11², 13², 17² = 289, not 288.

Question 3 · Reasoning Ability

Six persons A, B, C, D, E and F sit around a circular table facing the centre. • B sits third to the left of A. • E sits immediately to the right of B. • C is an immediate neighbour of E. • D is not an immediate neighbour of B. Who sits second to the right of D?

  1. A
  2. C
  3. E
  4. B
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. B

Clockwise order A, C, E, B, F, D. Moving right (anticlockwise) from D: F, then B.

Question 4 · English Language

Choose the word that best fills the blank. The speaker's arguments were so ________ that even the sceptics in the audience were convinced.

  1. tedious
  2. persuasive
  3. ambiguous
  4. incoherent
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. persuasive

Convincing sceptics requires persuasive arguments.

Question 5 · Quantitative Aptitude

What will come in place of the question mark (?) 12, 14, 19, 29, 46, ?

  1. 70
  2. 72
  3. 75
  4. 67
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 72

Differences are n² + 1: 2, 5, 10, 17, 26; 46 + 26 = 72.

Question 6 · Reasoning Ability

Seven persons P, Q, R, S, T, U and V were born in January, March, April, June, August, September and November of the same year, one in each month. • P was born in a month having 30 days. • Exactly three persons were born between P and S. • T was born immediately before P. • Q was born in a month having 31 days, but not in January. • U was born immediately after Q. • V was born after P but before Q. Who was born in June?

  1. Q
  2. S
  3. V
  4. T
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. V

Q in March leaves no room for V between P and Q, so Q = Aug, U = Sep; V between P and Q forces P = Apr, V = Jun, T = Mar, S = Nov, R = Jan.

Question 7 · English Language

Choose the pair of words that best fills both blanks. The bank's bad loans ________ sharply after it ________ its credit appraisal process, rejecting weak applications early.

  1. declined, tightened
  2. rose, tightened
  3. declined, relaxed
  4. rose, abandoned
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. declined, tightened

Rejecting weak applications means the process was tightened, which would reduce (decline) bad loans.

Question 8 · Quantitative Aptitude

Two equations I and II are given. Solve both and choose the correct relationship between x and y. I. x² − 4x − 21 = 0 II. y² + 9y + 18 = 0

  1. x > y
  2. x < y
  3. x ≥ y
  4. x ≤ y
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. x ≥ y

x = 7 or −3; y = −3 or −6. Every x is greater than or equal to every y (−3 = −3), so x ≥ y.

Question 9 · Reasoning Ability

Seven persons P, Q, R, S, T, U and V were born in January, March, April, June, August, September and November of the same year, one in each month. • P was born in a month having 30 days. • Exactly three persons were born between P and S. • T was born immediately before P. • Q was born in a month having 31 days, but not in January. • U was born immediately after Q. • V was born after P but before Q. How many persons were born between T and U?

  1. Two
  2. Three
  3. Four
  4. One
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Three

Order: R (Jan), T (Mar), P (Apr), V (Jun), Q (Aug), U (Sep), S (Nov). Between T and U are P, V and Q.

Question 10 · English Language

Identify the part of the sentence that contains an error. Not only the borrowers (A) / but also the guarantor (B) / were informed (C) / about the default notice. (D)

  1. (A)
  2. (B)
  3. (C)
  4. (D)
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. (C)

With 'not only…but also', the verb agrees with the nearer subject 'guarantor': 'was informed'.

Question 11 · Quantitative Aptitude

In an election between two candidates, 10% of the voters did not vote and 1,000 votes cast were invalid. The winner got 54% of the valid votes and won by 1,000 votes. How many voters were on the list?

  1. 15,000
  2. 13,500
  3. 12,500
  4. 14,000
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 15,000

Margin = 54% − 46% = 8% of valid votes = 1,000 → valid = 12,500; votes cast = 13,500 = 90% of voters → 15,000.

Question 12 · Reasoning Ability

Statements: Only a few loans are secured. All secured items are insured. Conclusions: I. Some loans are not secured. II. All secured items are definitely loans.

  1. Only conclusion II follows
  2. Only conclusion I follows
  3. Either I or II follows
  4. Both I and II follow
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Only conclusion I follows

'Only a few loans are secured' implies some loans are not secured, so I follows. Nothing restricts secured items to loans, so II does not follow.

Question 13 · English Language

Identify the part of the sentence that contains an error. The new rules, which came into effect (A) / last month, has made (B) / it easier for customers (C) / to open accounts online. (D)

  1. (A)
  2. (B)
  3. (C)
  4. (D)
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. (B)

The subject 'rules' is plural, so it should be 'have made'.

Question 14 · Quantitative Aptitude

A trader sells two articles for ₹1,980 each. On one he gains 10% and on the other he loses 10%. What is his overall result?

  1. No profit, no loss
  2. Loss of 1%
  3. Gain of 1%
  4. Loss of 2%
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Loss of 1%

CPs = 1,980/1.1 = 1,800 and 1,980/0.9 = 2,200; total CP 4,000, total SP 3,960 → loss ₹40 = 1%.

Question 15 · Reasoning Ability

Statements: No branch is a vault. Some vaults are lockers. All lockers are safes. Conclusions: I. Some safes are not branches. II. Some lockers are not branches.

  1. Only conclusion I follows
  2. Only conclusion II follows
  3. Both I and II follow
  4. Neither I nor II follows
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Both I and II follow

The lockers that are vaults cannot be branches (II), and those lockers are safes, so some safes are not branches (I).
