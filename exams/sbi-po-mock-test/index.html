Sample 1 · English Language

Read the passage and answer the question. Behavioural economists have found that people often prefer a smaller reward today over a larger one in the future, even when waiting would clearly make them better off. This tendency, known as present bias, helps explain why many people under-save for retirement. Automatic enrolment in pension schemes, where employees must actively opt out rather than opt in, has been shown to raise participation sharply. Which feature of automatic enrolment, as described in the passage, raises participation?

  1. It offers employees a larger reward today
  2. Employees must take action to stay out rather than to join
  3. It removes present bias from people's minds
  4. Employees are legally barred from opting out
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Employees must take action to stay out rather than to join

The passage says employees 'must actively opt out rather than opt in', so inertia keeps them enrolled.

Sample 2 · Quantitative Aptitude

What approximate value should come in place of the question mark (?) √624.8 × 7.96 + 39.97% of 449.9 = ?

  1. 360
  2. 400
  3. 420
  4. 380
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 380

≈ 25 × 8 + 40% of 450 = 200 + 180 = 380.

Sample 3 · Reasoning Ability

Six persons A, B, C, D, E and F sit around a circular table facing the centre. • B sits third to the left of A. • E sits immediately to the right of B. • C is an immediate neighbour of E. • D is not an immediate neighbour of B. Who sits immediately to the left of F?

  1. D
  2. B
  3. A
  4. C
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. D

Clockwise from A: A, C, E, B, F, D (right = anticlockwise when facing the centre). F's immediate left is D.

Sample 4 · English Language

Read the passage and answer the question. A bank's capital acts as a cushion against unexpected losses. If a bank holds too little capital, even a modest rise in bad loans can wipe out its equity and threaten depositors. Regulators therefore require banks to hold capital in proportion to the riskiness of their assets, so that riskier lending must be backed by more capital. Which of the following statements is supported by the passage?

  1. Banks with more capital never face bad loans
  2. Regulators prohibit banks from making risky loans
  3. Depositors are protected only by the government
  4. A bank that makes riskier loans must hold more capital against them
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. A bank that makes riskier loans must hold more capital against them

The last sentence states that riskier lending must be backed by more capital.

Sample 5 · English Language

Read the passage and answer the question. The report praises the scheme for widening access to credit but warns that rapid expansion without proper checks could lead to a rise in defaults. It recommends stronger verification of borrowers before further growth. The report's attitude towards the scheme is best described as:

  1. Openly hostile
  2. Completely indifferent
  3. Uncritically enthusiastic
  4. Cautiously supportive
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Cautiously supportive

It praises the scheme but adds warnings and safeguards, which is cautious support.

SBI PO mock test FAQs

How many questions are there in the SBI PO exam?

The SBI PO paper has 100 multiple-choice questions for 100 marks, to be completed in 60 minutes. This is the indicative pattern — always confirm it in the latest official notification.

Is there negative marking in SBI PO?

Yes. −0.25 mark per wrong answer (25% of the marks for that question). Our mock tests apply the same rule.

What is the target score for SBI PO?

Cut-offs vary by year and category; our mock tests use 45% as a target score to aim for.

Are the SBI PO mock tests free?

Yes. All 30 full-length SBI PO mock tests are free, with no payment or plan. Create a free account (one click with Google) to start; your results are saved across devices. You can retake any test as often as you like.

How do I prepare for SBI PO prelims in three months?

Use the first month to build fundamentals in arithmetic, data interpretation, puzzles and reading comprehension. In the second month, take sectional tests and work on speed. In the third month, attempt full mock tests regularly, analyse every test and focus on the question types where you lose the most time.

Is SBI PO tougher than IBPS PO?

Many candidates find SBI PO slightly tougher, with lengthier puzzles and data interpretation sets and fewer vacancies relative to applicants. The syllabus is similar, so preparation overlaps heavily. Practising at a higher difficulty level helps for both exams.

Is there a limit on the number of SBI PO attempts?

SBI has historically capped the number of attempts for SBI PO, with different limits for different categories. These rules are set in each recruitment notification, so check the latest notification on sbi.co.in/careers before applying.

Are these the actual exam questions?

No. Every question is an original practice question written to match the official syllabus and the exam's style. Reproducing real exam questions is not permitted, and we do not do it.
