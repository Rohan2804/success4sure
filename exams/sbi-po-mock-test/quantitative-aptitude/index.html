Quantitative Aptitude: SBI PO practice questions

15 free questions with answers and explanations on Quantitative Aptitude, from the SBI PO (SBI Probationary Officer (Prelims)) syllabus.

Last updated 23 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · How we create questions

What this section covers

Quantitative Aptitude for SBI PO covers simplification and approximation, number series, quadratic equations, arithmetic word problems (percentage, profit and loss, interest, ratio, partnership, time and work, speed and distance, mixtures), probability, permutations and combinations, mensuration and data interpretation. Mains adds caselets and data sufficiency. Learn shortcuts such as CI − SI for two years and harmonic-mean average speed, but always check units and time periods. Practise DI sets under time pressure and approximate wherever options are far apart.

Quantitative Aptitude questions

Question 1

Find the value of ? 35% of 840 + 2/3 of 432 = ?

  1. 572
  2. 582
  3. 594
  4. 588
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 582

35% of 840 = 294 and two-thirds of 432 = 288. Adding them gives 294 + 288 = 582. Computing percentages and fractions separately before adding avoids most calculation slips in simplification questions.

Question 2

Find the next term of the series: 3, 7, 16, 32, 57, ?

  1. 93
  2. 89
  3. 91
  4. 97
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 93

The differences are 4, 9, 16 and 25, which are squares of 2, 3, 4 and 5. The next difference is 6² = 36, so the next term is 57 + 36 = 93.

Question 3

One number in the series below does not follow the pattern. Identify it. 2, 5, 11, 23, 47, 96, 191

  1. 47
  2. 23
  3. 191
  4. 96
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 96

Each term is twice the previous term plus 1: 2→5→11→23→47→95→191. So 96 is wrong and should be 95. Note that 191 = 2 × 95 + 1 also confirms the pattern.

Question 4

Solve the two equations and find the relationship between x and y. I. x² − 11x + 30 = 0 II. y² − 13y + 42 = 0

  1. x > y
  2. x < y
  3. x ≤ y
  4. x ≥ y
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. x ≤ y

From I, x = 5 or 6. From II, y = 6 or 7. Every x is less than or equal to every y, and the case x = y = 6 rules out the strict relation x < y. Hence x ≤ y.

Question 5

A shopkeeper marks an article 40% above its cost price and then allows a discount of 15% on the marked price. What is his profit percentage?

  1. 25%
  2. 19%
  3. 21%
  4. 17%
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 19%

Take cost price as 100. Marked price = 140 and selling price = 140 × 0.85 = 119. Profit = 19 on 100, i.e. 19%. Simply subtracting 15 from 40 to get 25% is a common error.

Question 6

The difference between the compound interest (compounded annually) and the simple interest on a sum for 2 years at 8% per annum is ₹96. What is the sum?

  1. ₹15,000
  2. ₹12,000
  3. ₹16,000
  4. ₹18,000
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. ₹15,000

For 2 years, CI − SI = P × (r/100)². So 96 = P × 0.0064, giving P = ₹15,000. This shortcut works only for a two-year period with annual compounding.

Question 7

A starts a business with ₹24,000. Five months later B joins with ₹36,000. At the end of the year the profit is ₹30,000. What is B's share?

  1. ₹16,000
  2. ₹15,000
  3. ₹18,000
  4. ₹14,000
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. ₹14,000

Capital-time: A = 24,000 × 12 = 2,88,000; B = 36,000 × 7 = 2,52,000. Ratio 8 : 7. B's share = 30,000 × 7/15 = ₹14,000. Forgetting that B invested for only 7 months gives wrong options.

Question 8

A can finish a task in 20 days and B in 30 days. They work together for 6 days, after which A leaves. In how many more days will B finish the remaining task?

  1. 12 days
  2. 18 days
  3. 15 days
  4. 10 days
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 15 days

Together they do 1/20 + 1/30 = 1/12 per day, so in 6 days they finish half the task. B alone needs 30 × 1/2 = 15 days for the remaining half.

Question 9

A car travels from town X to town Y at 60 km/h and returns along the same road at 40 km/h. What is its average speed for the whole journey?

  1. 50 km/h
  2. 48 km/h
  3. 45 km/h
  4. 52 km/h
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 48 km/h

For equal distances, average speed = 2ab/(a + b) = 2 × 60 × 40/100 = 48 km/h. The simple average of 50 km/h is wrong because more time is spent at the slower speed.

Question 10

A train running at 72 km/h passes a signal pole in 15 seconds and crosses a platform in 25 seconds. What is the length of the platform?

  1. 200 m
  2. 180 m
  3. 250 m
  4. 300 m
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 200 m

72 km/h = 20 m/s. Train length = 20 × 15 = 300 m. Crossing the platform covers 20 × 25 = 500 m, so the platform is 500 − 300 = 200 m. The 300 m option is the train itself.

Question 11

A 40-litre mixture contains milk and water in the ratio 5 : 3. How much water must be added to make the ratio 1 : 1?

  1. 8 litres
  2. 12 litres
  3. 15 litres
  4. 10 litres
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 10 litres

Milk = 25 litres and water = 15 litres. For a 1 : 1 ratio, water must equal milk, i.e. 25 litres, so 25 − 15 = 10 litres of water must be added.

Question 12

A bag has 4 red, 5 blue and 3 green balls. Two balls are drawn at random together. What is the probability that both are of the same colour?

  1. 13/66
  2. 1/3
  3. 19/66
  4. 5/22
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 19/66

Total ways = 12C2 = 66. Same colour: 4C2 + 5C2 + 3C2 = 6 + 10 + 3 = 19. Probability = 19/66. Leaving out the green pair gives 16/66, a common slip.

Question 13

In how many ways can the letters of the word BANKER be arranged so that the vowels always come together?

  1. 120
  2. 240
  3. 720
  4. 360
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 240

The vowels A and E form one unit, giving 5 units that can be arranged in 5! = 120 ways. The vowels can swap among themselves in 2 ways, so the total is 120 × 2 = 240.

Question 14

Deposits (₹ crore) of three branches are given. Branch P: 2023 – 240, 2024 – 300 Branch Q: 2023 – 180, 2024 – 210 Branch R: 2023 – 280, 2024 – 340 What is the approximate percentage increase in the combined deposits of the three branches from 2023 to 2024?

  1. 21.4%
  2. 25.0%
  3. 18.6%
  4. 23.8%
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 21.4%

Combined deposits rise from 700 to 850 crore, an increase of 150. Percentage increase = 150/700 × 100 ≈ 21.4%. Branch P alone grew 25%, which is a distractor, not the combined figure.

Question 15

The length of a rectangular hall is increased by 20% and its breadth is decreased by 20%. What is the effect on its area?

  1. No change
  2. Increases by 4%
  3. Decreases by 2%
  4. Decreases by 4%
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Decreases by 4%

New area = 1.2 × 0.8 = 0.96 of the original, a 4% decrease. Equal percentage increase and decrease never cancel out; the net effect is always a fall of x²/100 per cent.
