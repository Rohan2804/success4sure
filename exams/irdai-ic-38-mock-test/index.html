About the IRDAI IC-38 exam

IC-38 is the IRDAI pre-recruitment exam for insurance agents, conducted using the Insurance Institute of India syllabus. It covers insurance principles, life, health and general insurance products, documentation and underwriting, claims, regulation and customer service.

Who should take it: People who want to become licensed insurance agents or point-of-sale persons for life, health or general insurers.

Sample 1 · Introduction to Insurance

Insurance is able to compensate the few who suffer a loss mainly because:

  1. The insurer invests every premium only in government bonds
  2. The government reimburses the insurer for every claim
  3. The premiums of many people facing a similar risk are pooled to pay the losses of the few
  4. Insured persons promise never to make a claim
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. The premiums of many people facing a similar risk are pooled to pay the losses of the few

Insurance works on pooling: many contribute small premiums so that the fund can pay the losses of the few who actually suffer them.

Sample 2 · Customer Service & Grievances

Under IRDAI's 2024 policyholder-protection framework, the free-look period for life and health insurance policies is:

  1. 7 days from the date of proposal
  2. 15 days from the date of the first premium
  3. 30 days from receipt of the policy document
  4. 45 days from the risk commencement date
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 30 days from receipt of the policy document

IRDAI's 2024 regulations extended the free-look period to 30 days from receipt of the policy document for life and health policies.

Sample 3 · Regulatory Aspects of Agency

Section 41 of the Insurance Act, 1938 prohibits:

  1. Offering a rebate of commission or premium as an inducement to take a policy
  2. Nomination by the policyholder
  3. Assignment of a policy to a bank
  4. Payment of premium by cheque
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Offering a rebate of commission or premium as an inducement to take a policy

Section 41 bars allowing or offering any rebate as an inducement to take or renew insurance; the agent cannot share commission with the customer.

Sample 4 · Legal Principles of Insurance

The principle of utmost good faith requires:

  1. Both proposer and insurer to voluntarily disclose all material facts accurately
  2. Only the insurer to disclose its financial results
  3. The proposer to disclose facts only if specifically asked
  4. The agent to guarantee the claim amount
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Both proposer and insurer to voluntarily disclose all material facts accurately

Insurance contracts are uberrimae fidei: each party must disclose all material facts, whether asked or not.

Sample 5 · Life Insurance & Financial Planning

Using the income-capitalisation method, what is the Human Life Value of a person who contributes ₹4,80,000 a year to the family if the assumed interest rate is 8%?

  1. ₹60,00,000
  2. ₹38,40,000
  3. ₹48,00,000
  4. ₹96,00,000
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. ₹60,00,000

HLV = annual contribution ÷ interest rate = ₹4,80,000 ÷ 0.08 = ₹60,00,000, the sum that would yield the same income.
