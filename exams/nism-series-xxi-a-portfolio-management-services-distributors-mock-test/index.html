Sample 1 · Investments

Investment differs from speculation mainly because investment:

  1. Always involves borrowed money
  2. Focuses on short-term price bets with high risk
  3. Guarantees capital protection
  4. Seeks returns from fundamental value over a reasonable horizon with assessed risk
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Seeks returns from fundamental value over a reasonable horizon with assessed risk

Investing relies on the asset's underlying value and an appropriate horizon; speculation bets on short-term price moves.

Sample 2 · Introduction to Securities Markets

In the Indian equity cash market, the standard settlement cycle is:

  1. T+3
  2. T+5
  3. T+1
  4. T+7
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. T+1

Indian exchanges moved fully to T+1 settlement for equity cash trades.

Sample 3 · Investing in Stocks

A stock trades at ₹400 and its EPS is ₹20. Its P/E ratio is:

  1. 8,000
  2. 20
  3. 5
  4. 0.05
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 20

P/E = Price / EPS = 400 / 20 = 20.

Sample 4 · Investing in Fixed Income Securities

When market interest rates rise, prices of existing fixed-coupon bonds generally:

  1. Rise
  2. Stay unchanged
  3. Fall
  4. Become equal to face value
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Fall

Higher prevailing yields make existing lower coupons less attractive, so prices drop.

Sample 5 · Derivatives

A portfolio manager holding a large equity portfolio fears a short-term market fall. A suitable hedge is to:

  1. Sell index futures
  2. Buy index futures
  3. Sell put options
  4. Buy more stocks
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Sell index futures

A short index futures position gains when the market falls, offsetting portfolio losses.

NISM Series XXI-A mock test FAQs

How many questions are there in the NISM Series XXI-A exam?

The NISM Series XXI-A paper has 100 multiple-choice questions for 100 marks, to be completed in 120 minutes. This is the indicative pattern — always confirm it in the latest official notification.

Is there negative marking in NISM Series XXI-A?

Yes. −0.25 mark per wrong answer (25% of the marks for that question). Our mock tests apply the same rule.

What is the pass mark for NISM Series XXI-A?

You need 60% to pass. Our mock tests show pass or fail against the same mark.

Are the NISM Series XXI-A mock tests free?

Yes. All 30 full-length NISM Series XXI-A mock tests are free, with no payment or plan. Create a free account (one click with Google) to start; your results are saved across devices. You can retake any test as often as you like.

Is NISM Series XXI-A mandatory for PMS distributors?

Yes, SEBI requires distributors of portfolio management services to hold this certification. Relationship managers at banks and wealth firms who sell PMS products usually need it. Confirm the current rules on nism.ac.in.

Which topics in NISM Series XXI-A need the most practice?

Performance measurement and risk-adjusted returns, PMS fee structures and taxation usually need the most attention. Learners also need to know the SEBI PMS regulations well, including disclosure and reporting requirements. Practise calculations and revise regulations with summary notes.

How long should I prepare for NISM Series XXI-A?

Two to three weeks is typical. Revise equity, debt and derivatives basics first, then study the PMS process, performance measurement and regulation. Take timed mock tests in the last few days and review every explanation.
