About the NISM Series XXI-A exam

NISM Series XXI-A covers Portfolio Management Services for distributors — investment basics across equity, debt and derivatives, the role and operations of portfolio managers, the portfolio process, performance measurement, taxation and SEBI PMS regulations.

Who should take it: Distributors and relationship managers who distribute PMS products, for whom this certification is required.

Free NISM Series XXI-A sample questions

Sample 1 · Investments

Investment differs from speculation mainly because investment:

  1. Always involves borrowed money
  2. Focuses on short-term price bets with high risk
  3. Guarantees capital protection
  4. Seeks returns from fundamental value over a reasonable horizon with assessed risk
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Seeks returns from fundamental value over a reasonable horizon with assessed risk

Investing relies on the asset's underlying value and an appropriate horizon; speculation bets on short-term price moves.

Sample 2 · Introduction to Securities Markets

In the Indian equity cash market, the standard settlement cycle is:

  1. T+3
  2. T+5
  3. T+1
  4. T+7
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. T+1

Indian exchanges moved fully to T+1 settlement for equity cash trades.

Sample 3 · Investing in Stocks

A stock trades at ₹400 and its EPS is ₹20. Its P/E ratio is:

  1. 8,000
  2. 20
  3. 5
  4. 0.05
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 20

P/E = Price / EPS = 400 / 20 = 20.

Sample 4 · Investing in Fixed Income Securities

When market interest rates rise, prices of existing fixed-coupon bonds generally:

  1. Rise
  2. Stay unchanged
  3. Fall
  4. Become equal to face value
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Fall

Higher prevailing yields make existing lower coupons less attractive, so prices drop.

Sample 5 · Derivatives

A portfolio manager holding a large equity portfolio fears a short-term market fall. A suitable hedge is to:

  1. Sell index futures
  2. Buy index futures
  3. Sell put options
  4. Buy more stocks
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Sell index futures

A short index futures position gains when the market falls, offsetting portfolio losses.
