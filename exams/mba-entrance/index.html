How to prepare for MBA entrance exams

Build reading speed for VARC, practise DI and LR sets to recognise structures quickly, and strengthen arithmetic, algebra and geometry for quant. Full-length mocks help you plan section-wise time and decide which questions to leave.

What our MBA mock tests include

CAT, CMAT and MAT-style papers with reading passages, DI/LR sets and quant problems at each exam's difficulty, the official timing and marking, and worked solutions.
