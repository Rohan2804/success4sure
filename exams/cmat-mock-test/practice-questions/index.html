Question 1 · Quantitative Techniques & Data Interpretation

What is the compound interest on ₹8,000 for 3 years at 10% per annum, compounded annually?

  1. ₹2,400
  2. ₹2,560
  3. ₹2,728
  4. ₹2,648
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. ₹2,648

Amount = 8000 × 1.1³ = 8000 × 1.331 = ₹10,648, so CI = ₹2,648.

Question 2 · Logical Reasoning

In a certain code, FRIEND is written as HUMJTK. How will CANDLE be written in that code?

  1. EDRJRL
  2. ECRIRL
  3. EDRIRL
  4. EDQIRL
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. EDRIRL

Letters shift by +2, +3, +4, +5, +6, +7: C→E, A→D, N→R, D→I, L→R, E→L.

Question 3 · Language Comprehension

Select the word most nearly opposite in meaning to LOQUACIOUS.

  1. Talkative
  2. Garrulous
  3. Reticent
  4. Verbose
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Reticent

Loquacious means talkative; reticent means reserved and quiet.

Question 4 · General Awareness

Which Article of the Constitution of India provides for the Finance Commission?

  1. Article 112
  2. Article 280
  3. Article 148
  4. Article 324
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Article 280

Article 280 provides for the Finance Commission; 112 is the Budget, 148 the CAG and 324 the Election Commission.

Question 5 · Innovation & Entrepreneurship

In India, the term of a patent is:

  1. 20 years from the date of filing the application
  2. 10 years from the date of grant
  3. 15 years from the date of filing
  4. 25 years from the date of grant
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 20 years from the date of filing the application

Under Section 53 of the Patents Act, 1970, a patent lasts 20 years from the filing date.

Question 6 · Quantitative Techniques & Data Interpretation

The average age of 30 students is 15 years. When the teacher's age is included, the average rises by 1 year. What is the teacher's age?

  1. 45 years
  2. 46 years
  3. 31 years
  4. 47 years
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 46 years

Teacher = 31 × 16 − 30 × 15 = 496 − 450 = 46 years.

Question 7 · Logical Reasoning

If A + B means A is the mother of B, A − B means A is the brother of B, and A × B means A is the father of B, what does P × Q − R + S indicate about P and S?

  1. P is the maternal grandfather of S
  2. P is the paternal grandfather of S
  3. P is the uncle of S
  4. P is the father of S
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. P is the maternal grandfather of S

P is the father of Q, Q is the brother of R, so P is R's father; R is S's mother, so P is S's maternal grandfather.

Question 8 · Language Comprehension

Fill in the blank with the most appropriate word. The new policy is intended to ______ the compliance burden on small businesses.

  1. alleviate
  2. aggravate
  3. exacerbate
  4. intensify
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. alleviate

A policy meant to help would lighten the burden; 'alleviate' means to reduce or ease.

Question 9 · General Awareness

The Comptroller and Auditor General of India is appointed by:

  1. the Prime Minister
  2. the President of India
  3. Parliament
  4. the Chief Justice of India
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. the President of India

Under Article 148 the CAG is appointed by the President by warrant under his hand and seal.

Question 10 · Innovation & Entrepreneurship

Which form of intellectual property protects a brand name or logo?

  1. Patent
  2. Trademark
  3. Copyright
  4. Geographical Indication
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Trademark

A trademark protects marks such as names, logos and symbols that identify the source of goods or services.

Question 11 · Quantitative Techniques & Data Interpretation

A boat covers 24 km upstream in 6 hours and 24 km downstream in 4 hours. What is the speed of the stream?

  1. 2 km/h
  2. 1 km/h
  3. 5 km/h
  4. 1.5 km/h
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 1 km/h

Upstream speed = 4, downstream = 6; stream speed = (6 − 4)/2 = 1 km/h.

Question 12 · Logical Reasoning

Statements: Some managers are leaders. All leaders are visionaries. Conclusions: I. Some managers are visionaries. II. All visionaries are leaders.

  1. Only conclusion I follows
  2. Only conclusion II follows
  3. Both I and II follow
  4. Neither I nor II follows
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Only conclusion I follows

The managers who are leaders are also visionaries, so I follows; 'All leaders are visionaries' cannot be reversed, so II does not.

Question 13 · Language Comprehension

Identify the part of the sentence that contains an error. One of my friends / have gone to Germany / for higher studies.

  1. One of my friends
  2. have gone to Germany
  3. for higher studies
  4. No error
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. have gone to Germany

The subject 'One' is singular, so it should be 'has gone'.

Question 14 · General Awareness

Who wrote 'The Discovery of India'?

  1. Jawaharlal Nehru
  2. Mahatma Gandhi
  3. B. R. Ambedkar
  4. Maulana Abul Kalam Azad
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Jawaharlal Nehru

Nehru wrote it during his imprisonment at Ahmednagar Fort (1942–46).

Question 15 · Innovation & Entrepreneurship

'Darjeeling Tea' is protected in India as which type of intellectual property?

  1. Trademark
  2. Patent
  3. Copyright
  4. Geographical Indication
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Geographical Indication

Darjeeling Tea was India's first registered Geographical Indication (2004–05).
