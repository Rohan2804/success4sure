Quantitative Techniques & Data Interpretation: CMAT practice questions

15 free questions with answers and explanations on Quantitative Techniques & Data Interpretation, from the CMAT (Common Management Admission Test) syllabus.

Last updated 23 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · How we create questions

What this section covers

This section tests arithmetic and data handling under time pressure: percentages, profit and loss, discounts, ratio and mixtures, time and work, pipes, speed and distance, simple and compound interest, mensuration, permutations, probability and short data-interpretation sets based on tables or charts. Revise the core formulas, practise converting fractions to percentages quickly, and learn shortcuts such as CI − SI for two years and the equal-distance average-speed formula. In DI sets, read units and base years carefully before calculating, and approximate where the options are far apart.

Quantitative Techniques & Data Interpretation questions

Question 1

An article has a marked price of ₹2,500. The shopkeeper allows successive discounts of 20% and 10%. What is the selling price?

  1. ₹1,750
  2. ₹1,800
  3. ₹1,825
  4. ₹1,875
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. ₹1,800

After 20% off, the price is ₹2,000; a further 10% off gives ₹1,800. Successive discounts of 20% and 10% equal a single discount of 28%, not 30%.

Question 2

A can finish a job in 12 days and B in 18 days. They work together for 4 days, after which A leaves. In how many more days will B finish the remaining work?

  1. 6 days
  2. 9 days
  3. 10 days
  4. 8 days
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 8 days

Together they do 1/12 + 1/18 = 5/36 per day, so 20/36 = 5/9 in 4 days. The remaining 4/9 at B's rate of 1/18 per day takes 8 days.

Question 3

A 240-metre-long train passes a signal pole in 12 seconds. How long will it take to cross a platform 360 metres long?

  1. 30 seconds
  2. 18 seconds
  3. 24 seconds
  4. 36 seconds
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 30 seconds

Speed = 240 ÷ 12 = 20 m/s. To cross the platform the train covers its own length plus the platform, 600 m, which takes 600 ÷ 20 = 30 seconds.

Question 4

What is the difference between the compound interest (compounded annually) and the simple interest on ₹12,000 for 2 years at 10% per annum?

  1. ₹100
  2. ₹240
  3. ₹120
  4. ₹1,200
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. ₹120

For 2 years, CI − SI = P(r/100)² = 12,000 × 0.01 = ₹120. The extra amount is simply interest earned in year two on the first year's interest of ₹1,200.

Question 5

If A : B = 3 : 4 and B : C = 6 : 7, what is A : B : C?

  1. 3 : 4 : 7
  2. 18 : 24 : 21
  3. 9 : 12 : 14
  4. 9 : 12 : 16
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 9 : 12 : 14

Make B common: 3:4 becomes 9:12 and 6:7 becomes 12:14. Hence A : B : C = 9 : 12 : 14. Simply joining 3:4 and 7 ignores the different values of B.

Question 6

Pipe X fills a tank in 20 minutes and pipe Y in 30 minutes, while outlet Z empties the full tank in 15 minutes. If all three are opened together on an empty tank, how long will it take to fill it?

  1. 60 minutes
  2. 12 minutes
  3. 36 minutes
  4. 45 minutes
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 60 minutes

Net rate = 1/20 + 1/30 − 1/15 = (3 + 2 − 4)/60 = 1/60 of the tank per minute, so the tank fills in 60 minutes.

Question 7

A trader sells a watch at a loss of 15%. Had he sold it for ₹360 more, he would have made a profit of 9%. What is the cost price of the watch?

  1. ₹1,200
  2. ₹1,600
  3. ₹2,400
  4. ₹1,500
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. ₹1,500

The gap between −15% and +9% is 24% of the cost price, which equals ₹360. So CP = 360 ÷ 0.24 = ₹1,500.

Question 8

A 60-litre mixture contains milk and water in the ratio 7 : 5. How much water must be added to make the ratio 1 : 1?

  1. 5 litres
  2. 10 litres
  3. 12 litres
  4. 15 litres
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 10 litres

The mixture has 35 litres of milk and 25 litres of water. For equal quantities, water must also be 35 litres, so 10 litres must be added.

Question 9

Annual sales of a company (₹ crore): 2019 – 120, 2020 – 150, 2021 – 135, 2022 – 180, 2023 – 198. In which year was the percentage growth in sales over the previous year the highest?

  1. 2022
  2. 2020
  3. 2021
  4. 2023
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 2022

Growth: 2020 = 25%, 2021 = −10%, 2022 = 45/135 ≈ 33.3%, 2023 = 10%. Although 2020 rose by ₹30 crore too, 2022's rise came on a smaller base.

Question 10

Annual sales of a company (₹ crore): 2019 – 120, 2020 – 150, 2021 – 135, 2022 – 180, 2023 – 198. What were the average annual sales over the five years?

  1. ₹150.0 crore
  2. ₹156.6 crore
  3. ₹159.0 crore
  4. ₹162.4 crore
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. ₹156.6 crore

Total sales = 120 + 150 + 135 + 180 + 198 = ₹783 crore. Dividing by 5 years gives an average of ₹156.6 crore.

Question 11

Two fair dice are thrown together. What is the probability that the sum of the numbers shown is 8?

  1. 1/6
  2. 1/9
  3. 7/36
  4. 5/36
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 5/36

Favourable outcomes are (2,6), (3,5), (4,4), (5,3) and (6,2): five out of 36 equally likely outcomes, so the probability is 5/36.

Question 12

In how many distinct ways can the letters of the word LEADER be arranged?

  1. 120
  2. 720
  3. 360
  4. 180
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 360

LEADER has 6 letters with E repeated twice. Number of arrangements = 6! ÷ 2! = 720 ÷ 2 = 360. Using 720 ignores the repetition.

Question 13

A car travels from town P to town Q at 40 km/h and returns along the same road at 60 km/h. What is its average speed for the whole journey?

  1. 50 km/h
  2. 45 km/h
  3. 52 km/h
  4. 48 km/h
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 48 km/h

For equal distances, average speed = 2xy/(x + y) = 2 × 40 × 60 ÷ 100 = 48 km/h. The simple average of 50 km/h is wrong because more time is spent at the slower speed.

Question 14

A sum of money doubles itself in 5 years at compound interest. In how many years will it become 8 times itself at the same rate?

  1. 20 years
  2. 40 years
  3. 15 years
  4. 10 years
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 15 years

Under compounding, the sum multiplies by 2 every 5 years: 2 → 4 → 8 takes three such periods, i.e. 15 years. The answer 40 years would apply to simple interest.

Question 15

What is the volume of a cylinder with radius 7 cm and height 10 cm? (Take π = 22/7)

  1. 440 cm³
  2. 1,540 cm³
  3. 1,440 cm³
  4. 3,080 cm³
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 1,540 cm³

Volume = πr²h = 22/7 × 49 × 10 = 1,540 cm³. The value 440 is the curved surface area (2πrh), not the volume.
