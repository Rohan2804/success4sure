Logical Reasoning: CMAT practice questions

15 free questions with answers and explanations on Logical Reasoning, from the CMAT (Common Management Admission Test) syllabus.

Last updated 26 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · How we create questions

What this section covers

Logical Reasoning covers number and letter series, coding-decoding, blood relations, directions, ranking, seating arrangements, syllogisms, inequalities, calendars and clocks, analogies, odd-one-out and critical reasoning such as strengthening or weakening an argument. Draw quick diagrams for seating and family trees, use Venn diagrams for syllogisms, and learn standard formulas for clock angles and odd days. In critical-reasoning items, look for the assumption linking evidence to conclusion; an alternative explanation usually weakens a causal claim.

Logical Reasoning questions

Question 1

Find the next term of the series. 3, 8, 15, 24, 35, ?

  1. 48
  2. 46
  3. 49
  4. 50
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 48

The terms are n² − 1 for n = 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, so the next is 7² − 1 = 48. Equivalently, the differences 5, 7, 9, 11 continue with 13.

Question 2

Find the next letter in the series. B, E, I, N, ?

  1. S
  2. T
  3. U
  4. R
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. T

Positions are 2, 5, 9, 14: the gaps increase 3, 4, 5, so the next gap is 6, giving position 20, which is T.

Question 3

In a certain code, TEACHER is written as VGCEJGT. How will STUDENT be written in that code?

  1. UVWEGPV
  2. TUVFGPV
  3. UWVFGPV
  4. UVWFGPV
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. UVWFGPV

Each letter is shifted two places forward: T→V, E→G, A→C and so on. Applying +2 to STUDENT gives U V W F G P V.

Question 4

Pointing to a young man, Riya says, "He is the son of the only son of my grandfather." Assuming Riya's grandfather is her paternal grandfather, how is the man related to Riya?

  1. Brother
  2. Cousin
  3. Uncle
  4. Nephew
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Brother

The only son of Riya's paternal grandfather is her father. The son of her father is her brother. A cousin would need the grandfather to have another child.

Question 5

Arun is facing east. He turns 90° clockwise, then 180°, and then 90° anticlockwise. Which direction is he facing now?

  1. East
  2. North
  3. West
  4. South
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. West

East turned 90° clockwise is South; turning 180° gives North; turning 90° anticlockwise from North gives West.

Question 6

In a class of 45 students, Mohan ranks 12th from the top. What is his rank from the bottom?

  1. 33rd
  2. 35th
  3. 34th
  4. 32nd
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 34th

Rank from bottom = total − rank from top + 1 = 45 − 12 + 1 = 34. Forgetting to add 1 gives 33rd, a common slip.

Question 7

Six friends A, B, C, D, E and F sit around a circular table facing the centre. A sits opposite D. B is immediately to the right of A. C sits opposite B. E is not adjacent to A. Who sits immediately to the left of A?

  1. F
  2. E
  3. C
  4. D
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. F

B is on A's right and E cannot touch A, so F must be on A's left. Then C opposite B and D opposite A leave E between B and D.

Question 8

Statements: All pens are books. No book is a table. Conclusions: I. No pen is a table. II. Some tables are pens.

  1. Only conclusion I follows
  2. Only conclusion II follows
  3. Both I and II follow
  4. Neither I nor II follows
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Only conclusion I follows

Every pen lies inside 'books', and books share nothing with tables, so no pen can be a table. Conclusion II directly contradicts I, so it cannot follow.

Question 9

Statement: P > Q ≥ R = S < T Conclusions: I. P > S II. T > Q

  1. Only conclusion I follows
  2. Only conclusion II follows
  3. Both I and II follow
  4. Either I or II follows
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Only conclusion I follows

From P > Q ≥ R = S we get P > S, so I is true. T and Q are both only linked to S, and both are at least S, so their mutual order cannot be fixed.

Question 10

If 1 January 2025 was a Wednesday, what day of the week was 1 January 2026?

  1. Wednesday
  2. Friday
  3. Tuesday
  4. Thursday
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Thursday

2025 is not a leap year, so it has 365 days = 52 weeks + 1 odd day. The weekday therefore moves forward by one, from Wednesday to Thursday.

Question 11

What is the angle between the hour hand and the minute hand of a clock at 3:40?

  1. 120°
  2. 130°
  3. 140°
  4. 150°
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 130°

Angle = |30H − 5.5M| = |90 − 220| = 130°. The hour hand moves too, so it is not simply the 120° between the 4 and 8 marks.

Question 12

The traffic police of a city claim that speed cameras installed in January reduced road accidents by 30% during that year. Which of the following, if true, most weakens this claim?

  1. A new metro line opened in the same January and took many cars off the roads
  2. The cameras were installed mainly on roads with the highest accident rates in the city
  3. Drivers caught by the cameras had to pay fines that were higher than in earlier years
  4. Accidents also fell in areas where the speed cameras were operating round the clock
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. A new metro line opened in the same January and took many cars off the roads

An alternative cause that coincides in time, the metro line reducing traffic, could explain the fall in accidents, so the cameras may not be responsible. The other options either fit or support the claim.

Question 13

Find the missing number. 7 : 50 :: 9 : ?

  1. 81
  2. 82
  3. 80
  4. 90
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 82

The relation is n² + 1: 7² + 1 = 50, so 9² + 1 = 82. Choosing 81 forgets the +1 step.

Question 14

Which one does NOT belong to the group? Mercury, Venus, Mars, Moon

  1. Mercury
  2. Venus
  3. Mars
  4. Moon
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Moon

Mercury, Venus and Mars are planets orbiting the Sun. The Moon is a natural satellite orbiting the Earth, so it is the odd one out.

Question 15

Among five friends A, B, C, D and E, A is taller than B but shorter than C. D is shorter than B, and E is taller than C. Who is the third tallest?

  1. B
  2. C
  3. A
  4. E
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. A

Combining the clues gives E > C > A > B > D. Counting from the tallest, A is third. C is second, and B is fourth.
