General Awareness: CMAT practice questions

15 free questions with answers and explanations on General Awareness, from the CMAT (Common Management Admission Test) syllabus.

Last updated 25 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · How we create questions

What this section covers

General Awareness in CMAT covers static GK and business-related awareness: the Indian Constitution and polity, history, geography, basic science, economic concepts, banking terminology, and international organisations and their headquarters, along with recent developments. Focus first on stable facts that recur, such as key Articles, landmark events and dates, RBI instruments like repo, CRR and SLR, and concepts such as inflation and fiscal deficit. Make short revision lists, and update current affairs from reliable sources in the months before the exam.

General Awareness questions

Question 1

Which Article of the Constitution of India guarantees the protection of life and personal liberty?

  1. Article 14
  2. Article 19
  3. Article 21
  4. Article 32
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Article 21

Article 21 states that no person shall be deprived of life or personal liberty except according to procedure established by law. Article 14 deals with equality before law.

Question 2

Which Article did Dr B. R. Ambedkar describe as the 'heart and soul' of the Constitution?

  1. Article 21
  2. Article 226
  3. Article 368
  4. Article 32
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Article 32

Article 32, the Right to Constitutional Remedies, lets citizens move the Supreme Court to enforce Fundamental Rights. Article 226 gives similar but wider writ powers to High Courts.

Question 3

What is the maximum strength of the Rajya Sabha permitted by the Constitution?

  1. 238
  2. 245
  3. 250
  4. 552
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 250

The Constitution allows up to 250 members: 238 representatives of states and union territories plus 12 nominated by the President. 245 is the present actual strength.

Question 4

Which is the largest state of India by area?

  1. Madhya Pradesh
  2. Rajasthan
  3. Maharashtra
  4. Uttar Pradesh
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Rajasthan

Rajasthan is the largest state by area, followed by Madhya Pradesh and Maharashtra. Uttar Pradesh is the largest by population, not area.

Question 5

The Tropic of Cancer does NOT pass through which of these states?

  1. Odisha
  2. Gujarat
  3. Jharkhand
  4. Mizoram
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Odisha

The Tropic of Cancer crosses Gujarat, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, West Bengal, Tripura and Mizoram. Odisha lies entirely south of it.

Question 6

The repo rate is the rate at which:

  1. banks lend money to their most creditworthy corporate customers
  2. the RBI lends short-term funds to banks against government securities
  3. the RBI borrows money from commercial banks by selling securities
  4. banks pay interest to customers on their savings account deposits
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. the RBI lends short-term funds to banks against government securities

Under a repo, banks borrow from the RBI by selling securities with an agreement to repurchase them. When the RBI absorbs funds from banks, that is a reverse repo or SDF operation.

Question 7

Under the RBI's flexible inflation targeting framework, the inflation target is defined in terms of which index?

  1. Wholesale Price Index
  2. GDP deflator
  3. Consumer Price Index for Industrial Workers
  4. Consumer Price Index (Combined)
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Consumer Price Index (Combined)

Since the framework was formalised in 2016, the target is set using headline CPI (Combined) inflation, which reflects retail prices across rural and urban India, rather than the WPI.

Question 8

The Goods and Services Tax (GST) was introduced in India through which Constitutional Amendment Act?

  1. 101st Amendment Act, 2016
  2. 73rd Amendment Act, 1992
  3. 86th Amendment Act, 2002
  4. 103rd Amendment Act, 2019
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 101st Amendment Act, 2016

The 101st Amendment Act, 2016 enabled GST and created the GST Council (Article 279A); GST came into force on 1 July 2017. The 73rd concerns panchayats.

Question 9

Deficiency of which vitamin causes scurvy?

  1. Vitamin A
  2. Vitamin D
  3. Vitamin C
  4. Vitamin K
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Vitamin C

Scurvy, marked by bleeding gums and weakness, results from a lack of vitamin C (ascorbic acid). Vitamin D deficiency causes rickets, and vitamin A deficiency night blindness.

Question 10

What is the SI unit of electrical resistance?

  1. Volt
  2. Ampere
  3. Ohm
  4. Watt
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Ohm

Resistance is measured in ohms (Ω). By Ohm's law, R = V/I, so one ohm is one volt per ampere. Volt measures potential difference and watt measures power.

Question 11

The Battle of Plassey, which laid the foundation of British political power in Bengal, was fought in:

  1. 1757
  2. 1764
  3. 1761
  4. 1857
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 1757

Robert Clive defeated Siraj-ud-Daulah at Plassey in 1757. The Battle of Buxar (1764) later secured the diwani; 1761 was the Third Battle of Panipat.

Question 12

Mahatma Gandhi's Dandi March, which launched the Civil Disobedience Movement, began in which year?

  1. 1919
  2. 1920
  3. 1942
  4. 1930
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 1930

The Salt March started from Sabarmati Ashram on 12 March 1930 and reached Dandi in April. 1920 marks Non-Cooperation and 1942 the Quit India Movement.

Question 13

Where are the headquarters of the International Monetary Fund (IMF) located?

  1. New York
  2. Washington, D.C.
  3. Geneva
  4. Paris
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Washington, D.C.

The IMF, like the World Bank, is headquartered in Washington, D.C. Geneva hosts the WTO and Paris the OECD, which are common distractors.

Question 14

'Stagflation' refers to an economic situation in which:

  1. high inflation coexists with stagnant growth and high unemployment
  2. prices fall steadily while output and employment keep rising
  3. rapid growth leads to full employment and low inflation
  4. the government balances its budget during a recession
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. high inflation coexists with stagnant growth and high unemployment

Stagflation combines rising prices with weak output and high unemployment, as in many economies in the 1970s. It is hard to tackle because anti-inflation measures can deepen the slowdown.

Question 15

Under the Cash Reserve Ratio (CRR), a scheduled commercial bank must keep a specified share of its net demand and time liabilities:

  1. in gold and approved securities with itself
  2. as a cash balance with the RBI
  3. as loans to priority-sector borrowers
  4. as fixed deposits with NABARD
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. as a cash balance with the RBI

CRR is held as cash balance with the RBI and earns no interest. Holding liquid assets such as gold, cash and government securities with the bank itself is the SLR requirement.
