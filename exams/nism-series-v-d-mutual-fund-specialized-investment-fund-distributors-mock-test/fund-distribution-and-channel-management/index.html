Fund Distribution and Channel Management: NISM Series V-D practice questions

8 free questions with answers and explanations on Fund Distribution and Channel Management, from the NISM Series V-D (Mutual Fund – Specialized Investment Fund (SIF) Distributors) syllabus.

Last updated 26 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · How we create questions

What this chapter covers

This chapter covers how mutual funds and SIFs are distributed. Revise ARN and EUIN, NISM certification requirements (V-A for mutual funds, V-D additionally for SIFs), trail-only commission, direct versus regular plans, due diligence of distributors by AMCs, and the line between distribution and investment advice. For SIFs, focus on suitability: the high minimum ticket and complex strategies mean distributors must assess the investor's wealth, experience and understanding carefully before recommending.

Fund Distribution and Channel Management questions

Question 1

A mutual fund distributor wants to distribute SIF strategies. In addition to holding a valid ARN, the distributor must:

  1. Register with RBI as an NBFC and maintain a minimum net worth
  2. Become a SEBI-registered portfolio manager
  3. Pass NISM Series V-D
  4. Hold a CA qualification
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Pass NISM Series V-D

SEBI requires distributors of SIF strategies to clear the NISM-Series-V-D certification, which tests knowledge of SIF strategies and derivatives, in addition to the regular MFD requirements.

Question 2

A client with a total investable corpus of ₹12 lakh wants to put ₹10 lakh in a long-short SIF. The distributor's best response is to:

  1. Point out the concentration risk
  2. Process it immediately, since the minimum is met and the client has asked for it
  3. Ask for ₹2 lakh more
  4. Suggest borrowing to invest more
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Point out the concentration risk

Meeting the minimum does not make a product suitable. Putting over 80% of savings in a complex strategy creates concentration risk; the distributor should assess suitability carefully.

Question 3

Commission paid to mutual fund distributors under current SEBI rules is:

  1. Paid fully upfront at the time of investment out of the AMC's own books
  2. Trail-based only
  3. Paid by SEBI
  4. Deducted from investors' redemption
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Trail-based only

Upfront commissions are not allowed. Distributors receive trail commission out of the scheme's expenses for as long as the client stays invested.

Question 4

Why do AMCs conduct due diligence on large distributors?

  1. To fix the NAV of the schemes distributed by them on each business day
  2. To decide the investor's tax rate
  3. To appoint them as trustees
  4. To ensure proper conduct and avoid mis-selling
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. To ensure proper conduct and avoid mis-selling

AMCs review the processes and conduct of distributors that meet set thresholds, including suitability checks and handling of conflicts, to protect investors.

Question 5

A distributor wants to advise clients for a fee and also earn commission on the same clients. Under SEBI norms:

  1. Advice and distribution must be segregated
  2. Both can be done freely for the same client by the same individual
  3. Only fee income is taxable
  4. AMFI must approve each client
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Advice and distribution must be segregated

SEBI requires a clear separation between advisory (fee from client) and distribution (commission from AMC) to avoid conflicts. The same person cannot do both for the same client.

Question 6

An investor who wants to invest in an SIF through a distributor will be allotted units of the:

  1. Direct plan, because SIFs have only one plan for all investors
  2. Regular plan
  3. Institutional plan
  4. Segregated plan
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Regular plan

Investments through distributors go into the regular plan, whose expenses include distributor commission. Investments without a distributor go into the direct plan.

Question 7

Which unique code identifies the specific employee of a distributing entity who dealt with the investor?

  1. ARN of the AMC's branch office where the form is submitted
  2. PAN of the distributor
  3. ISIN of the scheme
  4. EUIN
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. EUIN

The EUIN (Employee Unique Identification Number) tracks which employee or relationship manager interacted with the investor, improving accountability for advice.

Question 8

Which practice is acceptable for a mutual fund distributor?

  1. Promising guaranteed returns on long-short strategies to attract high net worth clients
  2. Collecting investor cheques in the distributor's own name
  3. Disclosing commissions received
  4. Signing forms on clients' behalf
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Disclosing commissions received

Distributors must disclose commissions for schemes they recommend. Promising returns, collecting money in their own name or signing for clients are prohibited.
