About the NISM Series V-D exam

NISM Series V-D covers Specialized Investment Funds (SIFs) — the SEBI product category between mutual funds and PMS — together with the mutual fund, equity derivatives and interest rate derivatives concepts needed to explain SIF strategies to investors.

Who should take it: Mutual fund distributors who want to distribute SIF strategies, and AMC sales teams supporting SIF products.

Sample 1 · Investment Landscape

An investor's 'risk capacity', as distinct from 'risk appetite', refers to

  1. The ability to bear losses given income, wealth, liabilities and time horizon
  2. The willingness to take risk
  3. The past returns of the portfolio
  4. The volatility of the market
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. The ability to bear losses given income, wealth, liabilities and time horizon

Risk capacity is objective financial ability to absorb losses; risk appetite is the psychological willingness to do so.

Sample 2 · Concept and Role of a Mutual Fund

What is the minimum investment an investor (other than an accredited investor) must make in a Specialized Investment Fund (SIF)?

  1. ₹1 lakh per strategy
  2. ₹50 lakh per strategy
  3. ₹10 lakh across all SIF strategies, at PAN level
  4. ₹1 crore across strategies
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. ₹10 lakh across all SIF strategies, at PAN level

SEBI's SIF framework sets a ₹10 lakh minimum investment per investor, aggregated across strategies at PAN level.

Sample 3 · Legal Structure of Mutual Funds

At least what proportion of an AMC's board must consist of independent directors?

  1. Two-thirds
  2. Half (50%)
  3. One-third
  4. One-fourth
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Half (50%)

SEBI requires at least 50% of an AMC's directors to be independent, while trustees need a two-thirds independent majority.

Sample 4 · Legal and Regulatory Framework

Which statement about the branding of a Specialized Investment Fund is correct?

  1. An SIF must use the same name as an existing equity scheme
  2. An SIF must carry branding distinct from the AMC's regular mutual fund schemes
  3. An SIF is branded as a portfolio management service
  4. An SIF need not be identified separately
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. An SIF must carry branding distinct from the AMC's regular mutual fund schemes

SEBI requires SIFs to have separate branding so investors can distinguish them from regular mutual fund products.

Sample 5 · Scheme Related Information

The offer document for each SIF investment strategy, comparable to an SID, is called the

  1. Key Information Memorandum
  2. Private Placement Memorandum
  3. Draft Red Herring Prospectus
  4. Investment Strategy Information Document (ISID)
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Investment Strategy Information Document (ISID)

Each SIF strategy is launched through an ISID that describes the strategy, asset allocation, risks and other terms.
