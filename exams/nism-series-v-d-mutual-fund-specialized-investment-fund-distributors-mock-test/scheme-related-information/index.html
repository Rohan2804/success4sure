Scheme Related Information: NISM Series V-D practice questions

8 free questions with answers and explanations on Scheme Related Information, from the NISM Series V-D (Mutual Fund – Specialized Investment Fund (SIF) Distributors) syllabus.

Last updated 23 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · How we create questions

What this chapter covers

This chapter covers the offer documents and disclosures for mutual funds and SIFs. For SIFs, the strategy-level document is the Investment Strategy Information Document (ISID), supported by statutory information about the fund house. Revise what the ISID contains (strategy, asset allocation, risk band, redemption frequency and notice period, loads), portfolio disclosure rules, the difference between the SID, SAI and KIM for regular schemes, and fundamental attributes that trigger an exit option.

Scheme Related Information questions

Question 1

An investor wants to know how often an SIF strategy allows redemptions and what notice period applies. The best source is the:

  1. Consolidated Account Statement issued by the depository for the previous month
  2. ISID
  3. AMC's balance sheet
  4. Distributor's visiting card
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. ISID

Redemption frequency and notice period are strategy terms, set out in the Investment Strategy Information Document. They can differ across SIF strategies.

Question 2

Why might an SIF strategy have a notice period for redemptions while most regular open-ended equity schemes do not?

  1. Because SEBI bans redemptions from SIFs during the first three years after the launch
  2. To earn extra fees from investors
  3. Because SIF NAVs are declared yearly
  4. To manage less liquid long-short positions
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. To manage less liquid long-short positions

Long-short and concentrated strategies may need time to unwind positions in an orderly way. A notice period protects remaining investors from forced selling.

Question 3

The document containing fund-house-level information common to all schemes, such as details of the sponsor and trustees, is the:

  1. SAI
  2. Key Information Memorandum given along with each application form
  3. Factsheet
  4. ISID
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. SAI

The Statement of Additional Information holds statutory details common to the fund house. Scheme or strategy-specific details are in the SID or ISID.

Question 4

Which of these would be a change in a fundamental attribute requiring an exit option for existing investors?

  1. Appointment of a new co-fund manager for the strategy alongside the existing one
  2. Change in the AMC's head office address
  3. Change in investment objective
  4. Revision of the factsheet design
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Change in investment objective

Investment objective, type of scheme and terms of issue are fundamental attributes. Changing them requires notice and an exit option at NAV without load.

Question 5

The Key Information Memorandum (KIM) is best described as:

  1. A detailed legal document containing the full trust deed and AMC agreements
  2. A monthly portfolio statement
  3. An audit report
  4. An abridged SID that accompanies forms
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. An abridged SID that accompanies forms

The KIM summarises the key features of a scheme, such as objective, asset allocation, loads and risk, and must be provided with every application form.

Question 6

A regular mutual fund scheme's riskometer must be reviewed:

  1. Monthly
  2. Only once at the time of the new fund offer, and never again
  3. Every five years
  4. Only when SEBI asks
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Monthly

Riskometers are evaluated monthly based on the portfolio, and changes are disclosed so investors know if the scheme's risk has changed.

Question 7

Which of the following must be disclosed by an SIF strategy in its offer document?

  1. The personal contact numbers of all the investors who have invested in the strategy
  2. A guaranteed return
  3. Its risk-band level
  4. The fund manager's salary
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Its risk-band level

The ISID must show the strategy's risk-band so investors can judge its risk. Investor details are confidential and returns cannot be guaranteed.

Question 8

Portfolio disclosure for SIF strategies helps investors mainly to:

  1. Trade the underlying stocks themselves on the same day at the scheme's cost price
  2. Check holdings against the mandate
  3. Avoid exit loads
  4. Calculate their tax liability exactly
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Check holdings against the mandate

Periodic disclosure shows what the strategy actually holds, including derivative positions, so investors can confirm it follows its stated approach.
