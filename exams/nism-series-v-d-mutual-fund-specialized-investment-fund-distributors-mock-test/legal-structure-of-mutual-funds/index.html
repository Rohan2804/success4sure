Legal Structure of Mutual Funds: NISM Series V-D practice questions

8 free questions with answers and explanations on Legal Structure of Mutual Funds, from the NISM Series V-D (Mutual Fund – Specialized Investment Fund (SIF) Distributors) syllabus.

Last updated 26 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · How we create questions

What this chapter covers

This chapter explains the structure that also governs SIFs: the sponsor, the mutual fund trust and trustees, the AMC and service providers such as custodians, RTAs and auditors. SIFs are offered by eligible AMCs of SEBI-registered mutual funds, under the oversight of the same trustees. Revise the duties of trustees and the AMC, independence requirements for boards, AMC eligibility routes for launching SIFs, and the role of the custodian, RTA and fund accountant.

Legal Structure of Mutual Funds questions

Question 1

A Specialized Investment Fund is launched and managed by:

  1. A portfolio manager registered under the SEBI PMS Regulations, separately from any mutual fund
  2. A bank's treasury department
  3. An insurance company
  4. An eligible mutual fund AMC
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. An eligible mutual fund AMC

SIFs are set up under the SEBI mutual fund framework by AMCs that meet the eligibility conditions, and are overseen by the mutual fund's trustees.

Question 2

Under one eligibility route, an AMC may launch SIFs if it has been operating for at least three years with average AUM over the preceding three years of at least:

  1. ₹500 crore across all its schemes taken together
  2. ₹10,000 crore
  3. ₹1,00,000 crore
  4. ₹50 crore
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. ₹10,000 crore

SEBI's first eligibility route requires a track record of at least three years and average AUM of at least ₹10,000 crore in the preceding three years, with no regulatory action in that period.

Question 3

Who is responsible for ensuring that an AMC launching SIF strategies complies with SEBI regulations and protects investors' interests?

  1. The distributors who sell the strategy and collect application forms from investors
  2. The stock exchange
  3. The trustees
  4. The investors' bankers
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. The trustees

Trustees oversee the AMC's activities, including SIF strategies, and must ensure compliance and fair treatment of investors.

Question 4

The custodian of a mutual fund scheme is responsible for:

  1. Safekeeping securities and settling trades
  2. Choosing which securities the fund manager should buy and sell for the portfolio
  3. Selling units to investors
  4. Fixing the scheme's TER
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Safekeeping securities and settling trades

Custodians hold securities in safe custody, settle purchases and sales, and track corporate actions. Investment decisions remain with the AMC.

Question 5

The Investment Management Agreement of a mutual fund is signed between:

  1. Sponsor and investors, at the time of every new fund offer of a scheme
  2. Trustees and the AMC
  3. AMC and distributors
  4. Custodian and SEBI
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Trustees and the AMC

The IMA appoints the AMC to manage the fund's money and sets out its duties. It is executed between the trustees and the AMC.

Question 6

Which entity maintains the record of unitholders and processes their transactions for most AMCs?

  1. The sponsor of the mutual fund, through its own branch network
  2. The custodian
  3. The RTA
  4. The statutory auditor
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. The RTA

Registrar and transfer agents handle investor records, transaction processing, statements and many investor service requests on behalf of the AMC.

Question 7

An AMC's board has 8 directors. Under SEBI norms, the minimum number who must be independent of the sponsor is:

  1. 4
  2. 2
  3. 6, plus the chairperson, in every case
  4. None
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 4

SEBI requires at least 50% of an AMC's directors to be independent of the sponsor and associates. For the trustee board the requirement is two-thirds.

Question 8

Under SEBI regulations, a mutual fund is constituted in the form of a:

  1. Private limited company owned by the investors of all schemes
  2. Partnership firm
  3. Co-operative society
  4. Trust
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Trust

Indian mutual funds are set up as trusts under the Indian Trusts Act, with the sponsor as settlor, the trustees holding property for unitholders and the AMC as manager.
