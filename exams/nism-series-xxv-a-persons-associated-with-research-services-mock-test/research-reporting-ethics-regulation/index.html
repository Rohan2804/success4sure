Research Reporting, Ethics & Regulation: NISM Series XXV-A practice questions

8 free questions with answers and explanations on Research Reporting, Ethics & Regulation, from the NISM Series XXV-A (Persons Associated with Research Services (Sales and Other Non-Core Services)) syllabus.

Last updated 19 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · How we create questions

What this chapter covers

This chapter covers SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations, 2014 as amended, and related ethical standards. Topics include registration, qualification and certification, research report content and disclosures, conflicts of interest and personal trading restrictions, separation of research from other activities, fee limits and modes of payment, the centralised fee collection mechanism, KYC and client agreement terms, record keeping, use of artificial intelligence tools, prohibition on assured returns, and grievance redressal through SCORES and ODR. Focus on what must be disclosed and what is prohibited.

Research Reporting, Ethics & Regulation questions

Question 1

Under SEBI's amended Research Analyst framework, if a research analyst uses AI tools in preparing research services, it must:

  1. Avoid any disclosure, since AI tools are confidential business secrets
  2. Transfer responsibility for the research to the AI tool's provider
  3. Disclose the use and remain responsible for the output
  4. Obtain separate SEBI registration for the AI tool itself
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Disclose the use and remain responsible for the output

The amended framework requires research analysts to disclose the extent of AI use to clients and holds them solely responsible for data security, confidentiality and the integrity of the output.

Question 2

A research report must include disclosures about:

  1. The personal phone numbers of every client who will receive the report
  2. The analyst's family members' salaries and bank balances
  3. The analyst's financial interests and conflicts in the company
  4. The share prices of all companies outside the sector
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. The analyst's financial interests and conflicts in the company

Reports must disclose material conflicts, such as holdings in the subject company, compensation received from it and other relationships, so clients can judge objectivity.

Question 3

SEBI's Centralised Fee Collection Mechanism (CeFCoM) for research analysts helps clients by:

  1. Guaranteeing a minimum return on all research recommendations
  2. Letting clients pay fees in cash at any bank branch
  3. Ensuring fees are paid only to registered research analysts
  4. Allowing unregistered entities to collect fees
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Ensuring fees are paid only to registered research analysts

CeFCoM routes fee payments through a mechanism that verifies the recipient is a registered RA, protecting clients from paying unregistered or fraudulent entities.

Question 4

For individual and HUF clients, SEBI's amended framework limits the fees a research analyst may collect in advance to:

  1. Fees for a period of up to one quarter
  2. Fees for up to ten years in a single lump sum
  3. Fees for the client's whole lifetime
  4. Any amount, as long as the client agrees
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Fees for a period of up to one quarter

To protect retail clients, SEBI allows RAs to collect advance fees from individual and HUF clients for at most one quarter. Longer advance collection is not permitted.

Question 5

A research analyst firm sends a WhatsApp message saying a stock 'will double in 3 months, guaranteed'. This is:

  1. Allowed, if the stock actually doubles later
  2. Allowed, if sent only to existing clients
  3. Prohibited, since assured or guaranteed returns cannot be promised
  4. Allowed for model portfolios
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Prohibited, since assured or guaranteed returns cannot be promised

SEBI prohibits research analysts from guaranteeing or assuring returns. Such statements mislead clients and may also amount to market manipulation.

Question 6

A research analyst has published a buy recommendation on a stock. Personal trading by the analyst against that recommendation is:

  1. Allowed at any time, since it is the analyst's own money
  2. Allowed if the analyst informs the sales team verbally
  3. Prohibited for the restricted period around the report
  4. Required to show independence
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Prohibited for the restricted period around the report

SEBI restricts RAs and their associates from dealing in recommended securities during specified periods before and after publication, and from trading against their own recommendations.

Question 7

Research analysts must maintain records of research reports and client communications for at least:

  1. Six months
  2. Five years
  3. One year
  4. Three months
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Five years

SEBI requires RAs to preserve records such as research reports, recommendations, client KYC and communications for a minimum of five years.

Question 8

If a research analyst does not resolve a client's complaint, the client may escalate it:

  1. First to SCORES, then to online dispute resolution
  2. Only to a civil court in the analyst's city
  3. To the client's bank manager
  4. To the stock exchange's listing department
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. First to SCORES, then to online dispute resolution

Clients of SEBI-registered RAs can lodge complaints on SCORES and, if unsatisfied, initiate online dispute resolution through the Smart ODR portal.
