Question 1 · Research Profession & Securities Markets

Which activity is typically within the scope of a person associated with research services in a sales or non-core role?

  1. Independently preparing buy/sell recommendations without supervision
  2. Signing off research reports as the research analyst
  3. Explaining published research reports to clients and handling service queries
  4. Guaranteeing returns on recommended stocks
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Explaining published research reports to clients and handling service queries

Non-core staff support distribution and servicing of research; preparing and signing recommendations is the role of the qualified research analyst.

Question 2 · Research Fundamentals & Market Analysis

If the RBI raises the repo rate, what is the most likely immediate effect?

  1. Borrowing costs rise, which tends to slow credit growth and may weigh on interest-sensitive sectors
  2. Loans become cheaper and credit growth accelerates
  3. Inflation rises immediately
  4. Corporate earnings increase automatically
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Borrowing costs rise, which tends to slow credit growth and may weigh on interest-sensitive sectors

A higher repo rate transmits into higher lending rates, cooling demand and affecting rate-sensitive sectors such as banks, autos and real estate.

Question 3 · Risk and Return Fundamentals

A stock with a beta of 1.5 is expected, on average, to:

  1. Move half as much as the market
  2. Move about 1.5 times the market's movement
  3. Not move with the market at all
  4. Always give 1.5% return
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Move about 1.5 times the market's movement

Beta measures sensitivity to market movements; a beta of 1.5 implies higher volatility than the market.

Question 4 · Research Reporting, Ethics & Regulation

Within what restricted window are research analysts and their associates prohibited from dealing in a security they cover?

  1. 1 day before and 1 day after the report
  2. 30 days before and 5 days after the publication of a research report
  3. 90 days before and 90 days after the report
  4. There is no such restriction
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 30 days before and 5 days after the publication of a research report

The RA Regulations bar dealing in the subject security from 30 days before to 5 days after publication of the report.

Question 5 · Client Interaction & Relationship Skills

Which statement reflects ethical objection handling in research sales?

  1. Overcome objections by promising guaranteed returns
  2. Pressure the client to decide immediately
  3. Address the client's concerns honestly without overstating benefits or understating risks
  4. Ignore the objection and repeat the pitch
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Address the client's concerns honestly without overstating benefits or understating risks

Objection handling must be truthful and balanced; pressure tactics and promises of returns are unethical.

Question 6 · Professional Efficiency & Tools

In the Eisenhower (urgent–important) matrix, tasks that are important but not urgent should generally be:

  1. Ignored
  2. Delegated immediately
  3. Done before urgent and important tasks
  4. Scheduled and given planned time
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Scheduled and given planned time

Important but not urgent tasks, such as learning and planning, should be scheduled so they are not crowded out.

Question 7 · Research Profession & Securities Markets

Which body has been recognised by SEBI to administer and supervise research analysts?

  1. AMFI
  2. Research Analyst Administration and Supervisory Body (RAASB), i.e., BSE
  3. NISM
  4. Clearing Corporation of India
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Research Analyst Administration and Supervisory Body (RAASB), i.e., BSE

SEBI recognised BSE as the RAASB to administer and supervise registered research analysts.

Question 8 · Research Fundamentals & Market Analysis

A company's EPS is ₹25 and its share price is ₹500. What is its P/E ratio?

  1. 12.5
  2. 25
  3. 20
  4. 0.05
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 20

P/E = price ÷ EPS = 500 ÷ 25 = 20.

Question 9 · Risk and Return Fundamentals

An investor buys a share at ₹200, receives a dividend of ₹10 and sells it a year later at ₹230. What is the holding period return?

  1. 15%
  2. 20%
  3. 5%
  4. 25%
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 20%

HPR = (230 − 200 + 10) ÷ 200 = 40 ÷ 200 = 20%.

Question 10 · Research Reporting, Ethics & Regulation

Which of the following is an essential quality of a good research report?

  1. Promises of assured returns to attract clients
  2. Hiding unfavourable information about the company
  3. Use of jargon to impress clients
  4. Clear, objective analysis with required disclosures of conflicts and risks
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Clear, objective analysis with required disclosures of conflicts and risks

Clarity, objectivity and full disclosure are expected of research reports under the RA Regulations.

Question 11 · Client Interaction & Relationship Skills

An investor holds on to a losing stock refusing to sell because selling would 'confirm the loss'. This bias is:

  1. Overconfidence
  2. Loss aversion
  3. Herd behaviour
  4. Anchoring
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Loss aversion

Loss aversion means the pain of losses outweighs equivalent gains, leading investors to hold losers too long.

Question 12 · Professional Efficiency & Tools

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, client personal data should be processed:

  1. For a lawful purpose with the consent of the data principal or other legitimate use, with reasonable security safeguards
  2. Freely and shared with any third party for marketing
  3. Only on paper, never electronically
  4. Without any security measures if the client is a retail investor
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. For a lawful purpose with the consent of the data principal or other legitimate use, with reasonable security safeguards

The DPDP Act requires lawful, purpose-limited processing with consent or legitimate use and security safeguards.

Question 13 · Research Profession & Securities Markets

The market in which existing listed shares are bought and sold among investors is the:

  1. Primary market
  2. Money market only
  3. Over-the-counter grey market
  4. Secondary market
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Secondary market

The secondary market provides liquidity for securities already issued, through stock exchanges.

Question 14 · Research Fundamentals & Market Analysis

Which type of analysis studies past price and volume data to forecast future price movements?

  1. Technical analysis
  2. Fundamental analysis
  3. Credit analysis
  4. Macroeconomic analysis
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Technical analysis

Technical analysis relies on charts, price and volume patterns rather than business fundamentals.

Question 15 · Risk and Return Fundamentals

Standard deviation of returns is used as a measure of:

  1. Average return
  2. Liquidity
  3. Dividend yield
  4. Total volatility (risk) of an investment
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Total volatility (risk) of an investment

Standard deviation measures dispersion of returns around the mean, indicating total risk.
