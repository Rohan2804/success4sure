Professional Efficiency & Tools: NISM Series XXV-A practice questions

8 free questions with answers and explanations on Professional Efficiency & Tools, from the NISM Series XXV-A (Persons Associated with Research Services (Sales and Other Non-Core Services)) syllabus.

Last updated 23 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · How we create questions

What this chapter covers

This chapter covers practical skills and tools that help persons associated with research services work efficiently and compliantly: time and task management, CRM systems, digital communication channels, record keeping, information security and data protection, responsible use of AI tools, continuing professional development and compliance with communication rules (approved channels, recorded lines). Focus on the safe handling of client data and on using technology without breaching regulatory obligations.

Professional Efficiency & Tools questions

Question 1

A sales executive wants to use a personal messaging app to share research with clients because it is convenient. The firm's policy should ensure that:

  1. Only approved, recorded channels are used
  2. Any messaging app may be used as long as clients do not complain
  3. Personal phones may be used without any record of messages
  4. Messages should be deleted after sending to save space
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Only approved, recorded channels are used

Firms must keep records of client communications. Using approved channels ensures recording, supervision and data security, which unrecorded personal apps cannot provide.

Question 2

Which is the strongest password practice for systems holding client data?

  1. Sharing one password among the team for convenience
  2. Unique passwords with multi-factor authentication
  3. Using the same password for all systems and personal e-mail
  4. Writing passwords on a note attached to the monitor
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Unique passwords with multi-factor authentication

Unique, strong passwords combined with multi-factor authentication greatly reduce the risk of unauthorised access. Sharing or reusing passwords creates serious vulnerabilities.

Question 3

A CRM system helps a research firm's sales team by:

  1. Generating research recommendations automatically without analysts
  2. Tracking client interactions, requests and follow-ups
  3. Guaranteeing that every prospect becomes a paying client
  4. Replacing the need for KYC procedures
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Tracking client interactions, requests and follow-ups

CRM tools centralise client information and interaction history, improving service and follow-up. They do not produce research or replace KYC.

Question 4

A staff member receives an e-mail appearing to be from the CEO asking for client data urgently via a personal e-mail address. The best response is to:

  1. Send the data immediately because the CEO asked
  2. Forward the e-mail to all clients for their information
  3. Verify the request through a separate known channel first
  4. Reply with the data but mark it confidential
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Verify the request through a separate known channel first

This is a typical phishing or impersonation pattern. Verifying through a known channel prevents data leaks, and the incident should be reported to IT security.

Question 5

A sales executive spends most of the day replying to routine internal chats and keeps missing scheduled client callbacks. The most effective fix is to:

  1. Reply to every internal chat instantly, since quick replies show commitment
  2. Stop making client callbacks and rely on clients to call back themselves
  3. Block time for priority tasks and batch routine messages
  4. Work late every night instead of changing how the day is organised
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Block time for priority tasks and batch routine messages

Time-blocking protects important work such as client callbacks, while batching routine messages reduces constant interruptions. Working longer hours or dropping callbacks does not address the real problem of poor prioritisation.

Question 6

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, a research firm should collect client personal data:

  1. In as much detail as possible for future marketing
  2. Without telling the client how it will be used
  3. And sell it to third parties for revenue
  4. Only for a specified lawful purpose
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Only for a specified lawful purpose

The DPDP Act requires personal data to be processed for lawful purposes with consent or legitimate use, limited to what is necessary, and protected with reasonable safeguards.

Question 7

A sales executive drafts client e-mails with a public generative AI tool. To stay compliant, she should:

  1. Paste the client's full portfolio and PAN for better results
  2. Send the AI output to clients without reading it
  3. Use the AI tool to create new stock recommendations
  4. Avoid entering personal or confidential client data
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Avoid entering personal or confidential client data

Public AI tools may store inputs, risking data leaks. Staff should not share confidential data, must review outputs and must never use AI to generate unapproved recommendations.

Question 8

Continuing professional education is important for persons associated with research services mainly because:

  1. Regulations, products and markets keep changing
  2. It allows them to issue their own research reports
  3. It replaces the need for NISM certification
  4. It guarantees higher returns for clients
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Regulations, products and markets keep changing

Keeping knowledge current helps staff explain research accurately and comply with evolving rules. It does not authorise them to perform core research functions.
