Who should take it: Sales, client-servicing and other non-core staff associated with SEBI-registered research analysts.

Sample 1 · Research Profession & Securities Markets

Research analysts in India are registered and regulated under:

  1. SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations, 2014
  2. SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013
  3. SEBI (Stock Brokers) Regulations, 1992
  4. RBI Master Directions on NBFCs
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations, 2014

Research analysts are governed by the SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations, 2014, as amended.

Sample 2 · Research Fundamentals & Market Analysis

Which indicator measures the total value of goods and services produced in an economy in a period?

  1. Consumer Price Index (CPI)
  2. Repo rate
  3. Current account deficit
  4. Gross Domestic Product (GDP)
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Gross Domestic Product (GDP)

GDP measures economic output; CPI measures inflation and the repo rate is a policy rate.

Sample 3 · Risk and Return Fundamentals

Which type of risk can be reduced through diversification?

  1. Systematic (market) risk
  2. Interest rate risk of the whole economy
  3. Inflation risk in the economy
  4. Unsystematic (company-specific) risk
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Unsystematic (company-specific) risk

Company-specific risk diversifies away across many holdings; market-wide risk cannot be diversified.

Sample 4 · Research Reporting, Ethics & Regulation

Under the SEBI RA Regulations, a research analyst must disclose in a research report if it or its associates hold actual or beneficial ownership in the subject company of:

  1. 10% or more at any time
  2. 25% or more only
  3. 1% or more at the end of the month immediately preceding the report
  4. Any holding acquired after the report
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 1% or more at the end of the month immediately preceding the report

Ownership of 1% or more of the subject company as at the end of the preceding month must be disclosed.

Sample 5 · Client Interaction & Relationship Skills

A client is upset after a recommended stock fell 20%. The best initial response by a PARS staff member is to:

  1. Listen empathetically, explain the rationale and risks in the original report, and escalate if needed
  2. Promise that the stock will recover within a month
  3. Blame the research analyst
  4. Avoid the client's calls until the market recovers
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Listen empathetically, explain the rationale and risks in the original report, and escalate if needed

Effective crisis handling involves empathy, factual explanation and escalation, without making promises.
