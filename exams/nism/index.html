How to prepare for a NISM exam

Read NISM's free official workbook for your series first — the exam is set from it. Then take full-length mock tests under the real time limit, review every explanation, and use the chapter-wise score to go back to weak chapters before the next test. Most NISM exams carry negative marking of 25% per wrong answer, so practise skipping questions you are unsure of.

What our NISM mock tests include

Each mock test has the same number of questions, time limit, pass mark and negative marking as the real NISM exam. Questions are spread across every workbook chapter, and every answer has an explanation.
