About the NISM Series V-A exam

NISM Series V-A is the core mutual fund distributor exam. It covers how mutual funds are structured and regulated, scheme types, NAV, loads and TER, taxation, investor services, risk and return, scheme performance and choosing the right scheme for an investor.

Who should take it: Anyone who wants to distribute mutual funds and obtain an ARN from AMFI, including independent advisers, bank relationship managers and employees of distribution firms.

Sample 1 · Investment Landscape

Which of the following is a real (physical) asset rather than a financial asset?

  1. Equity shares of a listed company
  2. A bank fixed deposit
  3. A residential flat held to earn rent
  4. Units of a debt mutual fund
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. A residential flat held to earn rent

Real assets are tangible, physical assets; shares, deposits and fund units are financial claims on an issuer.

Sample 2 · Concept and Role of a Mutual Fund

Which type of scheme lets investors buy and redeem units directly with the fund on an ongoing basis at NAV-based prices?

  1. Closed-ended scheme
  2. Fixed maturity plan
  3. Interval scheme
  4. Open-ended scheme
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Open-ended scheme

Open-ended schemes offer continuous sale and repurchase; closed-ended schemes and FMPs do not, and interval schemes allow it only during specified windows.

Sample 3 · Legal Structure of Mutual Funds

In India, a mutual fund is legally constituted as a

  1. Company under the Companies Act
  2. Limited liability partnership
  3. Trust
  4. Co-operative society
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Trust

Mutual funds are set up as trusts under the Indian Trusts Act, 1882, with trustees holding the assets for unitholders.

Sample 4 · Legal and Regulatory Framework

Which body is the primary regulator of mutual funds in India?

  1. RBI
  2. IRDAI
  3. AMFI
  4. SEBI
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. SEBI

Mutual funds are regulated by SEBI under the SEBI (Mutual Funds) Regulations, 1996.

Sample 5 · Scheme Related Information

Information common to all schemes of a mutual fund, such as details of the sponsor, AMC and trustees, is contained in the

  1. Scheme Information Document (SID)
  2. Key Information Memorandum (KIM)
  3. Monthly factsheet
  4. Statement of Additional Information (SAI)
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Statement of Additional Information (SAI)

The SAI carries statutory information about the fund house common to all its schemes, while the SID is scheme-specific.

NISM Series V-A mock test FAQs

How many questions are there in the NISM Series V-A exam?

The NISM Series V-A paper has 100 multiple-choice questions for 100 marks, to be completed in 120 minutes. This is the indicative pattern — always confirm it in the latest official notification.

Is there negative marking in NISM Series V-A?

Yes. −0.25 mark per wrong answer (25% of the marks for that question). Our mock tests apply the same rule.

What is the pass mark for NISM Series V-A?

You need 50% to pass. Our mock tests show pass or fail against the same mark.

Are the NISM Series V-A mock tests free?

Yes. All 30 full-length NISM Series V-A mock tests are free, with no payment or plan. Create a free account (one click with Google) to start; your results are saved across devices. You can retake any test as often as you like.

How do I prepare for NISM Series V-A in 15 days?

Read two to three workbook chapters a day for the first ten days, making short notes on regulations, NAV and load calculations, and taxation. Use the last five days for timed mock tests, reviewing every wrong answer. Revisit scheme selection, risk-return and taxation chapters, which commonly need more practice.

Is NISM Series V-A mandatory to sell mutual funds?

Yes, individuals who want to distribute mutual funds need to pass NISM Series V-A and then register with AMFI to obtain an ARN. Bank relationship managers and employees of distribution firms who sell mutual funds also need it. Confirm the latest requirements on nism.ac.in and the AMFI website.

How long is the NISM Series V-A certificate valid?

The NISM Series V-A certificate is generally valid for three years from the date of passing. Distributors usually renew it through NISM's Continuing Professional Education programme to keep their ARN active. Confirm the current rules on nism.ac.in.
