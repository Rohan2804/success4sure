NAV, TER and Pricing of Units: NISM Series V-A practice questions

8 free questions with answers and explanations on NAV, TER and Pricing of Units, from the NISM Series V-A (Mutual Fund Distributors) syllabus.

Last updated 11 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · How we create questions

What this chapter covers

This chapter explains how a scheme's Net Asset Value is computed, how expenses are charged through the Total Expense Ratio, and at what price units are bought and redeemed. Revise the NAV formula, daily accrual of expenses, SEBI's slab-based TER limits, exit load and its effect on redemption proceeds, valuation principles for debt securities, NAV publication timelines and how IDCW payouts or reinvestment affect NAV and units. Expect several short calculations.

NAV, TER and Pricing of Units questions

Question 1

A scheme has investments and accrued income totalling ₹612 crore, liabilities of ₹12 crore and 40 crore units outstanding. Its NAV is:

  1. ₹15.30
  2. ₹14.70
  3. ₹15.60
  4. ₹15.00
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. ₹15.00

NAV = (assets − liabilities) / units = (612 − 12) / 40 = ₹15.00. ₹15.30 ignores the liabilities, and ₹14.70 subtracts them twice.

Question 2

SEBI's limits on the Total Expense Ratio of open-ended schemes are structured so that:

  1. Every scheme can charge the same flat 2.5% expense ratio
  2. The maximum permitted TER falls as a scheme's AUM grows
  3. TER rises as AUM grows, to reward large schemes
  4. TER can be decided freely by the AMC each month
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. The maximum permitted TER falls as a scheme's AUM grows

TER limits are slab-based: higher AUM slabs carry lower maximum percentages, so investors benefit from economies of scale as schemes grow.

Question 3

A scheme with ₹1,000 crore of assets has a TER of 1.5% a year. Roughly how much expense is accrued each day (365-day year)?

  1. About ₹1.50 crore
  2. About ₹41.1 lakh
  3. About ₹4.11 lakh
  4. About ₹12.50 lakh
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. About ₹4.11 lakh

Annual expense = 1,000 crore × 1.5% = ₹15 crore. Daily accrual = 15 crore / 365 ≈ ₹4.11 lakh. Expenses are accrued daily, so the NAV is always shown net of them.

Question 4

An investor redeems 1,000 units at an NAV of ₹50 when a 1% exit load applies. The redemption proceeds (before taxes) are:

  1. ₹49,500
  2. ₹50,000
  3. ₹50,500
  4. ₹49,000
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. ₹49,500

Redemption price = NAV × (1 − exit load) = 50 × 0.99 = ₹49.50. For 1,000 units the proceeds are ₹49,500. Exit load reduces, never increases, the amount paid.

Question 5

How are most debt securities held by mutual fund schemes valued for NAV purposes?

  1. At the price paid by the scheme, until maturity of the bond
  2. Using prices from independent valuation agencies
  3. At face value, irrespective of the market conditions
  4. At a price decided by the fund manager each day
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Using prices from independent valuation agencies

To ensure fair and consistent NAVs, debt securities are valued using prices provided by independent valuation agencies. Holding at cost or face value would hide market movements.

Question 6

By what time must the NAV of most open-ended schemes be published on AMC and AMFI websites for each business day?

  1. 9:00 am on the next business day
  2. The end of the following week
  3. 11:00 pm on the same business day
  4. Only at month end
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 11:00 pm on the same business day

SEBI requires NAVs of open-ended schemes to be uploaded on the AMC and AMFI websites by 11 pm each business day (special timelines apply to some schemes such as fund of funds investing overseas).

Question 7

An investor holds 2,000 units under the IDCW reinvestment option. The scheme declares IDCW of ₹1.20 per unit and the ex-IDCW NAV is ₹24. Ignoring tax, how many additional units are allotted?

  1. 100 units
  2. 120 units
  3. 96 units
  4. 48 units
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 100 units

Amount reinvested = 2,000 × 1.20 = ₹2,400. Units allotted = 2,400 / 24 = 100. Reinvestment uses the ex-IDCW NAV, so total investment value is unchanged.

Question 8

Which of the following is NOT charged to the scheme within its TER?

  1. Fees paid to the custodian
  2. Trail commission paid to distributors in a regular plan
  3. Fees paid to the registrar and transfer agent
  4. Marketing costs of the AMC beyond permitted limits
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Marketing costs of the AMC beyond permitted limits

Only permitted expenses within SEBI limits may be charged to the scheme; any excess must be borne by the AMC. Custodian fees, RTA fees and distributor trail are standard scheme expenses.
