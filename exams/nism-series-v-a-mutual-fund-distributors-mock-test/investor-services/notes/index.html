Investor Services: NISM Series V-A revision notes

Investor Services is a large, practical chapter: how an investor opens a folio, completes KYC, transacts, and keeps records updated. It covers systematic plans, nomination, minors, NRIs and timelines for payments. Expect many scenario questions where you must pick the right service or the right document.

Last updated 19 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · Free

Key points

1. KYC process

KYC is a one-time process recorded with a KYC Registration Agency (KRA). PAN is mandatory, along with proof of identity and address, and in-person or video verification. Aadhaar-based eKYC is allowed. Central KYC (CKYC) gives a KYC Identification Number usable across financial sectors.

2. Micro investments

Investments up to ₹50,000 a year per investor per fund house (including SIPs) can be made without PAN using PAN-exempt KYC with another photo ID. These are often called micro SIPs. The limit is counted across all schemes of the same fund house, so the investor must track total yearly investments carefully.

3. Folio and CAS

A folio number identifies an investor's account with an AMC; one folio can hold many schemes. A Consolidated Account Statement is sent monthly if there is a transaction (otherwise half-yearly) and covers holdings across fund houses linked to the PAN, including demat holdings.

4. Systematic plans

SIP invests a fixed amount at regular intervals and benefits from rupee cost averaging. STP moves a fixed amount from one scheme (often liquid) to another of the same fund house. SWP pays out a fixed amount regularly from a scheme, useful for retirees.

5. Rupee cost averaging

Investing ₹1,000 a month buys 100 units at NAV ₹10 and 125 at ₹8, giving an average cost of about ₹8.89 - lower than the simple average NAV of ₹9. SIPs do not guarantee profit; they smooth the purchase price.

6. Nomination

Individuals holding singly or jointly can nominate persons, with percentage shares. Nomination or an explicit opt-out declaration is required for new folios. SEBI expanded nomination rules in 2025 - check the current maximum number of nominees. Nominees can be changed any time.

7. Minors

A minor's folio is held by the minor as sole holder with a parent or court-appointed guardian. Payment must come from the minor's bank account or a joint account with the guardian. On turning 18, the status must change to major with fresh KYC, or transactions are frozen.

8. NRIs and others

NRIs can invest on repatriable (NRE) or non-repatriable (NRO) basis; some AMCs restrict investors from the US and Canada due to FATCA. TDS applies on NRI redemptions. Companies, trusts and HUFs need their own documents such as board resolutions. NRIs must also update their overseas address.

9. Service timelines

Redemption proceeds must be paid within three working days for most schemes (longer for some schemes with overseas exposure). IDCW must be paid within seven working days of the record date. Delays attract interest payable by the AMC. The interest rate for delay is 15% a year.

10. Transmission and pledge

On death of a unit holder, units pass to the joint holder, then the nominee, then legal heirs through transmission with documents such as the death certificate. Units can also be pledged or marked with a lien for loans. Documents needed vary by claimant.

Formulas and rules of thumb

  • Average cost under SIP = Total amount invested / Total units bought

Common traps in the exam

  • Mixing STP and SWP: STP shifts money between schemes; SWP pays money to the investor.
  • Assuming rupee cost averaging guarantees gains - it only lowers average cost in fluctuating markets.
  • Thinking a nominee becomes legal owner in all cases; the nominee may hold units in trust for legal heirs.
  • Letting a minor's folio continue after 18 without changing status - transactions get frozen.

Quick recap

KYC once via KRA; PAN mandatory except micro SIP up to ₹50,000 a year. Folio = account; CAS consolidates holdings by PAN. SIP buys, STP moves, SWP withdraws. Redemption within three working days; IDCW within seven. Minors hold singly with a guardian and must convert at 18. Nominate or opt out explicitly.
