Scheme Related Information: NISM Series V-A practice questions

8 free questions with answers and explanations on Scheme Related Information, from the NISM Series V-A (Mutual Fund Distributors) syllabus.

Last updated 20 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · How we create questions

What this chapter covers

This chapter covers the documents and disclosures that tell investors about a scheme: the Scheme Information Document, Statement of Additional Information, Key Information Memorandum, Scheme Summary Document, addenda and annual reports. It also explains product labelling with the riskometer, the Potential Risk Class matrix for debt schemes, and fundamental attributes whose change triggers an exit option. Revise which document contains what information, the six riskometer levels, and the frequency of portfolio disclosures for different scheme types.

Scheme Related Information questions

Question 1

Product labelling of a mutual fund scheme mainly informs the investor about:

  1. The fund manager's personal investment record and qualifications over the last ten years
  2. The scheme's nature and its risk level on the riskometer
  3. The guaranteed return for the next year
  4. The commission structure for distributors
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. The scheme's nature and its risk level on the riskometer

Product labels describe the scheme's objective and type of investment in simple words and show a riskometer, helping investors judge whether the scheme suits them.

Question 2

Which of the following is NOT one of the six levels on SEBI's mutual fund riskometer?

  1. Low to Moderate
  2. Moderately High
  3. Very High
  4. Moderately Low
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Moderately Low

The six riskometer levels are Low, Low to Moderate, Moderate, Moderately High, High and Very High. 'Moderately Low' is not a riskometer level.

Question 3

The Potential Risk Class (PRC) matrix for debt schemes classifies a scheme on the basis of:

  1. Its maximum interest rate risk and maximum credit risk
  2. Its past three-year returns and expense ratio
  3. The size of its AUM and number of investors
  4. The fund manager's experience and tenure
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Its maximum interest rate risk and maximum credit risk

The PRC matrix shows the maximum interest rate risk (measured by Macaulay duration) and maximum credit risk (measured by a credit risk value) the scheme may take, so investors know its risk limits in advance.

Question 4

Which change would NOT be treated as a change in a scheme's fundamental attributes?

  1. Change in the type of scheme from open-ended to closed-ended
  2. Change in the scheme's investment objective
  3. Replacement of the scheme's fund manager
  4. Change in the terms of issue, such as fees and loads affecting investors
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Replacement of the scheme's fund manager

Fundamental attributes include the type of scheme, investment objective and terms of issue. A change of fund manager is disclosed, but it does not require giving unitholders an exit option without load.

Question 5

Changes to an SID between its periodic updates are communicated through:

  1. A fresh new fund offer
  2. A Consolidated Account Statement
  3. A letter to SEBI only
  4. An addendum to the document
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. An addendum to the document

When information in the SID changes, the AMC issues an addendum that is published and becomes part of the SID until the document is fully updated.

Question 6

A scheme's annual report (or its abridged version) must be sent or made available to unitholders within:

  1. Four months from the end of the financial year
  2. Two weeks from the end of the financial year
  3. Twelve months from the end of the financial year
  4. One week after the scheme's NFO closes
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Four months from the end of the financial year

SEBI requires mutual funds to publish annual reports, and make abridged reports available to unitholders, within four months of the close of the financial year.

Question 7

Which type of scheme is required to disclose its portfolio every fortnight, in addition to monthly disclosure?

  1. Equity schemes
  2. Index funds tracking Nifty
  3. Debt schemes
  4. ELSS schemes
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Debt schemes

To improve transparency on credit and liquidity risk, SEBI requires debt schemes to disclose portfolios fortnightly. Other schemes disclose portfolios monthly and half-yearly.

Question 8

Which SEBI-prescribed document presents key scheme information in a standard, machine-readable format on AMC and AMFI websites?

  1. Statement of Additional Information
  2. Scheme Summary Document
  3. Key Information Memorandum
  4. Consolidated Account Statement
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Scheme Summary Document

The Scheme Summary Document captures key data points of every scheme, such as fund manager, benchmark, loads and AUM, in a standard machine-readable format, making comparison across schemes easier.
