Legal Structure of Mutual Funds: NISM Series V-A practice questions

8 free questions with answers and explanations on Legal Structure of Mutual Funds, from the NISM Series V-A (Mutual Fund Distributors) syllabus.

Last updated 26 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · How we create questions

What this chapter covers

This chapter describes the three-tier structure of an Indian mutual fund: the sponsor who establishes it, the trust and trustees who protect unitholders, and the AMC that manages money. It also covers service providers such as custodians, RTAs and fund accountants, and the industry body AMFI. Revise sponsor eligibility, the trust deed, restrictions on AMC activities, trustees' reporting duties, the 'skin in the game' requirement, and the circumstances in which a scheme may be wound up.

Legal Structure of Mutual Funds questions

Question 1

Under the traditional eligibility route, a sponsor must contribute at least what share of the AMC's net worth?

  1. 26%
  2. 51%
  3. 10%
  4. 40%
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 40%

SEBI's traditional sponsor eligibility requires the sponsor to contribute at least 40% of the AMC's net worth, along with conditions on track record, profitability and net worth of the sponsor.

Question 2

Which constituent calculates the NAV of a scheme by recording income, expenses and valuations?

  1. Custodian
  2. Fund accountant
  3. Collecting banker
  4. Trustee
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Fund accountant

Fund accounting, whether done in-house by the AMC or outsourced, records all transactions and computes the daily NAV. The custodian holds securities and the collecting bank receives investor money.

Question 3

Which of the following activities is an AMC NOT permitted to undertake?

  1. Managing portfolios under PMS through a separate business unit
  2. Offering advisory services to pooled funds, subject to SEBI conditions
  3. Acting as a trustee of any mutual fund
  4. Managing its own schemes' investment portfolios
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Acting as a trustee of any mutual fund

SEBI regulations bar an AMC from acting as trustee of any mutual fund. AMCs may undertake certain other permitted businesses, such as PMS or advisory services, with safeguards against conflicts of interest.

Question 4

The document through which the sponsor creates the mutual fund as a trust is the:

  1. Trust deed
  2. Investment Management Agreement
  3. Scheme Information Document
  4. Memorandum of Association of the AMC
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Trust deed

The sponsor executes a trust deed in favour of the trustees, which is registered under the Indian Registration Act. The Investment Management Agreement is a separate contract between trustees and the AMC.

Question 5

CAMS and KFin Technologies are examples of which type of mutual fund service provider?

  1. Custodians
  2. Registrar and Transfer Agents
  3. Credit rating agencies
  4. Stock exchanges
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Registrar and Transfer Agents

RTAs process purchases, redemptions and switches, maintain unitholder records and send statements on behalf of AMCs. CAMS and KFin Technologies are the major RTAs in the Indian mutual fund industry.

Question 6

SEBI requires AMCs to invest a minimum amount in their own schemes, linked to each scheme's risk level. The main purpose is to:

  1. Provide the AMC with guaranteed income
  2. Allow the AMC to vote on behalf of investors
  3. Align the AMC's interests with those of unitholders
  4. Increase the scheme's expense ratio
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Align the AMC's interests with those of unitholders

This 'skin in the game' rule means the AMC shares in the scheme's outcomes, which encourages responsible management. Higher-risk schemes require larger minimum investments by the AMC.

Question 7

A scheme can be wound up if unitholders pass a resolution to that effect. What majority is required?

  1. Three-fourths (75%) of the unitholders
  2. A simple majority of more than 50%
  3. Two-thirds of the trustees only
  4. Ninety per cent of the AMC's directors
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Three-fourths (75%) of the unitholders

Regulations allow a scheme to be wound up if 75% of its unitholders pass a resolution, or when the trustees decide so for stated reasons, or when SEBI directs it in the interest of unitholders.

Question 8

Trustees of a mutual fund are required to submit reports on the fund's activities to SEBI:

  1. Only when a scheme is wound up
  2. Every five years
  3. Only if an investor complains
  4. Every half-year
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Every half-year

Trustees must file half-yearly reports with SEBI on the activities of the mutual fund, confirming compliance by the AMC. This is part of their supervisory role over the AMC.
