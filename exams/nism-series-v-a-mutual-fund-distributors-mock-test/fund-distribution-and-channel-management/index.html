Fund Distribution and Channel Management: NISM Series V-A practice questions

8 free questions with answers and explanations on Fund Distribution and Channel Management, from the NISM Series V-A (Mutual Fund Distributors) syllabus.

Last updated 16 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · How we create questions

What this chapter covers

This chapter explains how mutual fund schemes reach investors and how distributors are regulated and paid. It covers ARN and EUIN, the NISM certification and continuing education needed to keep an ARN valid, trail commission (upfront commission is not permitted), direct and regular plans, and the difference between a distributor and an investment adviser. Also revise channels such as banks, national distributors, stock exchange platforms, MF Utility and execution-only platforms, plus rules on how distributors may describe themselves.

Fund Distribution and Channel Management questions

Question 1

Under current SEBI norms, mutual fund distributors are paid:

  1. Only upfront commission at the time of purchase
  2. A fixed fee directly by SEBI
  3. Only trail-based commission, with no upfront commission
  4. A share of the scheme's capital gains
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Only trail-based commission, with no upfront commission

SEBI requires that all commissions be paid as trail from the scheme's expenses, without upfront payments. This reduces the incentive for churning investors across schemes.

Question 2

An investor who invests in a mutual fund through a SEBI-registered investment adviser (who charges a fee) will normally be allotted units of the:

  1. Direct plan
  2. Regular plan
  3. IDCW plan
  4. Institutional plan
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Direct plan

RIAs are paid fees by clients and do not earn commissions, so their clients invest in direct plans, which carry lower expenses. Regular plans include distributor commission.

Question 3

A distributor's NISM certificate is about to expire. To keep the ARN valid, the distributor can:

  1. Simply pay the renewal fee to AMFI and continue, without any further step
  2. Re-take the exam or complete the prescribed CPE
  3. Ask any AMC to extend the certificate
  4. Continue selling for two more years without action
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Re-take the exam or complete the prescribed CPE

ARN renewal requires a valid NISM certification, which can be renewed by re-taking the exam or attending the prescribed CPE programme before expiry.

Question 4

An Execution-Only Platform (EOP) in the mutual fund context is a platform that:

  1. Provides personalised investment advice for a fee
  2. Distributes regular plans and earns commission
  3. Manages portfolios on a discretionary basis
  4. Facilitates transactions in direct plans without providing advice
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Facilitates transactions in direct plans without providing advice

SEBI's EOP framework covers platforms that only execute transactions in direct plans. They neither advise nor earn distribution commission, so they cannot be used as disguised advisory or distribution channels.

Question 5

MF Utility (MFU) is best described as:

  1. A shared platform to transact across many fund houses
  2. A regulatory body under SEBI that approves new fund offers before they are launched
  3. A credit rating agency for debt schemes
  4. An NBFC that lends against mutual fund units
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. A shared platform to transact across many fund houses

MFU is an industry-backed shared services platform that lets investors and distributors transact in schemes of many participating AMCs through a single account number and form.

Question 6

A registered mutual fund distributor who is not an RIA wants to print business cards. Which description is appropriate?

  1. Independent Financial Adviser
  2. Mutual Fund Distributor, with the ARN mentioned
  3. Investment Adviser, SEBI-certified
  4. Wealth Adviser and Portfolio Manager
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Mutual Fund Distributor, with the ARN mentioned

SEBI and AMFI require distributors to describe themselves as 'Mutual Fund Distributor' and not use terms like 'adviser' that suggest advisory registration. This avoids misleading investors about their role.

Question 7

Before a distributor can sell a particular AMC's schemes and earn commission, the distributor must:

  1. Obtain a separate licence from RBI
  2. Own a minimum number of the AMC's shares
  3. Invest personally in every scheme sold
  4. Be empanelled with that AMC
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Be empanelled with that AMC

Each AMC empanels distributors after checking ARN and other details. Only empanelled distributors receive commission from that AMC for business they bring in.

Question 8

Stock exchange platforms for mutual fund transactions, such as BSE StAR MF and NSE's NMF platform, allow:

  1. Only institutional investors to buy closed-ended schemes
  2. AMCs to borrow money from each other
  3. Brokers and distributors to process purchases and redemptions for clients
  4. Investors to trade units with each other at negotiated prices
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Brokers and distributors to process purchases and redemptions for clients

These exchange platforms provide order routing for subscription and redemption of open-ended schemes, in demat or statement-of-account form, through brokers and mutual fund distributors.
