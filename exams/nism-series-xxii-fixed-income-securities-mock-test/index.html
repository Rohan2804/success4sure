Free NISM Series XXII sample questions

Sample 1 · Basics of Fixed Income Securities

The coupon of a bond is

  1. Its market price
  2. Its yield to maturity
  3. Its credit rating
  4. The interest rate paid on its face value
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. The interest rate paid on its face value

The coupon rate is applied to face value to determine periodic interest payments.

Sample 2 · Government Securities

Treasury bills are currently issued by the Government of India in which tenors?

  1. 30, 60 and 90 days
  2. 91, 182 and 364 days
  3. 1, 2 and 3 years
  4. 7, 14 and 28 days
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 91, 182 and 364 days

T-bills are short-term discounted instruments issued in 91-day, 182-day and 364-day tenors.

Sample 3 · Corporate Bonds

The main role of a debenture trustee is to

  1. Set the coupon rate
  2. Protect the interests of debenture holders
  3. Underwrite the issue
  4. Rate the debentures
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Protect the interests of debenture holders

Debenture trustees monitor the issuer's compliance with the terms and act for bondholders, including on default.

Sample 4 · Money Market Instruments

Commercial Paper can be issued with a maturity of

  1. 1 day to 7 days
  2. 1 year to 3 years
  3. 7 days to 1 year
  4. Only exactly 91 days
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 7 days to 1 year

RBI rules allow CP maturities between 7 days and 1 year.

Sample 5 · Bond Valuation

A 2-year bond with face value ₹100 pays an 8% annual coupon. If the required yield is 10%, what is its price?

  1. ₹100.00
  2. ₹98.00
  3. ₹96.53
  4. ₹103.55
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. ₹96.53

Price = 8 / 1.10 + 108 / 1.10^2 = 7.27 + 89.26 = ₹96.53.
