About the NISM Series XIX-D exam

NISM Series XIX-D is the manager certification specific to Category I and II AIFs — fund structuring, fees and waterfalls, investment process, valuation, monitoring and exits, taxation and SEBI AIF regulations.

Who should take it: Investment team members of Category I and II AIF managers, such as venture capital and private equity funds.

Sample 1 · Investments Landscape

Which investor type is typically best positioned to hold long-dated, illiquid private equity commitments?

  1. A household saving for a house purchase next year
  2. A money market fund
  3. A pension fund or endowment with long-term liabilities
  4. A corporate treasury managing daily working capital
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. A pension fund or endowment with long-term liabilities

Long-horizon institutions can tolerate illiquidity and earn the associated premium.

Sample 2 · Types of Investments

Venture debt provided by a Category II AIF to a start-up typically:

  1. Ranks below common equity
  2. Is guaranteed by the government
  3. Supplements equity funding and often carries warrants giving the lender equity upside
  4. Never carries interest
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Supplements equity funding and often carries warrants giving the lender equity upside

Venture debt lends to VC-backed companies, usually with warrants to compensate for higher risk.

Sample 3 · AIFs in India and Its Suitability

A fund that will invest in infrastructure projects such as roads and power, and that registers under a category regarded as socially or economically desirable, is a:

  1. Category III hedge fund
  2. Category I infrastructure fund
  3. Category II fund-of-funds
  4. Mutual fund infrastructure scheme
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Category I infrastructure fund

Infrastructure funds are a sub-category of Category I AIFs.

Sample 4 · AIF Ecosystem

The main role of a placement agent for a Category II AIF is to:

  1. Value portfolio companies
  2. Introduce the fund to prospective investors during fundraising
  3. Hold the fund's securities in custody
  4. Approve investments as part of the investment committee
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Introduce the fund to prospective investors during fundraising

Placement agents and distributors help raise commitments.

Sample 5 · AIF Structuring

In an AIF set up as a trust, the investment manager is appointed by:

  1. The investors individually
  2. SEBI directly
  3. The custodian
  4. The trustee through an investment management agreement
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. The trustee through an investment management agreement

The trustee delegates management to the manager under an investment management agreement.
