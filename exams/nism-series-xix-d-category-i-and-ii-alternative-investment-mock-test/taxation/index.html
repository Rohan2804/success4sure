Taxation: NISM Series XIX-D practice questions

8 free questions with answers and explanations on Taxation, from the NISM Series XIX-D (Category I and II Alternative Investment Fund Managers) syllabus.

Last updated 23 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · How we create questions

What this chapter covers

This chapter covers the tax regime for Category I and II AIFs: statutory pass-through for income other than business income, taxation of business income at fund level, TDS on distributions, loss pass-through conditions, taxation of non-resident investors and treaty access, characterisation of gains on portfolio exits, the tax treatment of carried interest and management fees (including GST), and investor reporting. As provisions have been renumbered under the new Income-tax Act, focus on principles rather than section numbers or rates.

Taxation questions

Question 1

Interest income earned by a Category I AIF from its debt investments is taxed:

  1. At the fund level only
  2. As capital gains
  3. Exempt in all cases
  4. In investors' hands as interest income
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. In investors' hands as interest income

Under pass-through, interest income retains its character and is taxed in investors' hands.

Question 2

Why is the classification of exit gains as capital gains or business income important for a Category II AIF?

  1. Capital gains are always exempt in the hands of AIF investors in India
  2. Business income is taxed at fund level, not passed through
  3. Business income is never taxed
  4. It decides SEBI registration
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Business income is taxed at fund level, not passed through

Pass-through applies to non-business income; business income is taxed in the fund, changing investors' tax outcome.

Question 3

A Category II AIF must deduct TDS on income credited to non-resident investors at:

  1. Zero in all cases
  2. A fixed 1%
  3. The resident TDS rate only, as treaty rates cannot be applied by AIFs
  4. Rates in force, or treaty rates where eligible
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Rates in force, or treaty rates where eligible

For non-residents, TDS applies at rates in force, and treaty rates may be applied if documentation is provided.

Question 4

An investor who sells AIF units before the minimum holding period may lose the ability to:

  1. Receive any distributions
  2. Claim TDS credit
  3. Set off losses passed through by the fund
  4. File a return
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Set off losses passed through by the fund

Loss pass-through is allowed only if units are held for the prescribed period.

Question 5

Carried interest structured through a separate unit class is often argued to be capital gains because:

  1. It arises from units held as investments
  2. It is always exempt
  3. SEBI classifies it as salary income of the investment team members
  4. It is paid by the government
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. It arises from units held as investments

Managers argue carry is investment return on carry units; authorities may contest this depending on facts.

Question 6

GST on the management fee of a Category II AIF effectively:

  1. Reduces the manager's income tax
  2. Increases the cost borne by investors
  3. Is paid by the investee companies
  4. Applies only to carry
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Increases the cost borne by investors

GST is charged on fees and usually cannot be offset by the fund, raising investor costs.

Question 7

The annual statement issued by a Category I or II AIF to investors helps them:

  1. Redeem units early
  2. Report each type of allocated income correctly
  3. Avoid KYC
  4. Compute the fund's IRR
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Report each type of allocated income correctly

Investors need the breakdown of income by nature to file returns under pass-through.

Question 8

A Category I AIF trust's business income, once taxed at fund level, is in investors' hands generally:

  1. Taxed again at slab rates
  2. Taxed as capital gains
  3. Exempt on distribution
  4. Subject to GST
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Exempt on distribution

Business income taxed at fund level is not taxed again when distributed.
