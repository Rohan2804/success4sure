AIF Ecosystem: NISM Series XIX-D practice questions

8 free questions with answers and explanations on AIF Ecosystem, from the NISM Series XIX-D (Category I and II Alternative Investment Fund Managers) syllabus.

Last updated 20 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · How we create questions

What this chapter covers

This chapter describes the participants in a Category I or II AIF: sponsor, manager, trustee, investment committee, custodian, registrar, administrator, valuer, auditor, merchant banker, legal and tax advisers, placement agents and distributors, and investors including LP advisory committees. It explains each party's duties under SEBI's framework, requirements for key personnel and certification, and the compliance officer's role. Focus on responsibilities and independence: which functions protect investors and who is accountable when something goes wrong.

AIF Ecosystem questions

Question 1

Which party in a trust-based Category II AIF usually appoints the investment manager?

  1. The trustee
  2. The investee companies
  3. The registrar
  4. The placement agent
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. The trustee

The trustee, acting for the trust, appoints the manager through an investment management agreement.

Question 2

Investors in a fund form an advisory committee mainly to:

  1. Run the day-to-day portfolio of the fund in place of the investment manager
  2. Review conflicts and give certain consents
  3. Audit the fund's financial statements
  4. Act as custodian of fund assets
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Review conflicts and give certain consents

LP advisory committees advise on conflicts of interest, valuation questions and consents required under the documents.

Question 3

A legal adviser to the fund primarily helps with:

  1. Selecting the stocks the fund will buy
  2. Calculating the NAV every day
  3. Raising capital from retail investors
  4. Drafting fund and deal documents
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Drafting fund and deal documents

Lawyers draft the PPM, trust deed, agreements and deal documents and advise on regulatory compliance.

Question 4

Who is responsible for ensuring that the AIF's operations comply with SEBI regulations on a daily basis?

  1. The manager, through its compliance officer
  2. The investee companies
  3. The investors
  4. The statutory auditor of the investee companies, through its annual audit
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. The manager, through its compliance officer

The manager is responsible for compliance and must appoint a compliance officer to monitor it.

Question 5

A sponsor of an AIF differs from the manager in that the sponsor:

  1. Makes all the investment and exit decisions for the fund's portfolio
  2. Holds custody of assets
  3. Sets up the fund and maintains continuing interest
  4. Prepares the audit report
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Sets up the fund and maintains continuing interest

The sponsor establishes the AIF and keeps the required continuing interest; the manager runs investments.

Question 6

A fund's administrator typically handles:

  1. Choosing which companies to invest in
  2. Capital calls, distributions and investor accounting
  3. Negotiating deal valuations and investment terms with the founders of investees
  4. Approving SEBI registration
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Capital calls, distributions and investor accounting

Fund administrators process drawdowns and distributions, maintain investor accounts and support reporting.

Question 7

Why does SEBI require at least one key investment team member to hold NISM certification?

  1. To exempt the manager from SEBI inspections and periodic regulatory reporting
  2. To guarantee investor returns
  3. To allow leverage
  4. To ensure a minimum standard of competence
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. To ensure a minimum standard of competence

Certification ensures the team has baseline knowledge of AIF regulations and practices.

Question 8

A tax adviser's main role in an AIF is to:

  1. Manage the fund's investments and take decisions on new deals
  2. Advise on fund structure and tax compliance
  3. Hold the fund's securities
  4. Distribute units to investors
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Advise on fund structure and tax compliance

Tax advisers help with structuring, pass-through compliance, TDS and investor tax reporting.
