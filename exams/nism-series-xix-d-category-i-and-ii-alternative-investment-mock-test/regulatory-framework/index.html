Regulatory Framework: NISM Series XIX-D practice questions

8 free questions with answers and explanations on Regulatory Framework, from the NISM Series XIX-D (Category I and II Alternative Investment Fund Managers) syllabus.

Last updated 25 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · How we create questions

What this chapter covers

This chapter covers SEBI's AIF Regulations and circulars applicable to Category I and II managers: registration, sponsor and manager eligibility, continuing interest, minimum corpus and investment, investor limits, accredited investors and large value funds, tenure and extension, investment conditions and concentration limits, temporary borrowing, PPM filing and changes, valuation, reporting, KMP certification, conflict rules, direct plans and distribution fees, investor charter, grievance redressal and penalties. Focus on the stable thresholds and the purpose of rules that SEBI frequently revises.

Regulatory Framework questions

Question 1

A Category I AIF has a corpus of ₹150 crore. The minimum continuing interest is:

  1. ₹3.75 crore
  2. ₹5 crore
  3. ₹7.5 crore
  4. ₹1.5 crore
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. ₹3.75 crore

Lower of 2.5% of corpus (₹3.75 crore) or ₹5 crore, so ₹3.75 crore.

Question 2

A Category I AIF with investable funds of ₹180 crore can invest at most how much in one investee company under the general limit?

  1. ₹45 crore
  2. ₹18 crore
  3. ₹36 crore
  4. ₹90 crore
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. ₹45 crore

Category I and II AIFs may invest up to 25% of investable funds in a single investee: 25% × 180 = ₹45 crore.

Question 3

The tenure of a close-ended Category II AIF can be extended by up to:

  1. Ten years at the manager's choice
  2. Two years, with two-thirds investor approval by value
  3. Any period without approval
  4. Six months only, and only with SEBI's prior written approval
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Two years, with two-thirds investor approval by value

Extension of up to two years is allowed with approval of two-thirds of unit holders by value.

Question 4

Investors with an unresolved complaint against an AIF can approach:

  1. The NPS Trust
  2. IRDAI Bima Bharosa
  3. SEBI SCORES and then the ODR mechanism
  4. The Income Tax Ombudsman
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. SEBI SCORES and then the ODR mechanism

AIF grievances go to SEBI's SCORES, with online dispute resolution available for unresolved disputes.

Question 5

Which statement on direct plans of AIFs is correct?

  1. They have a longer lock-in
  2. They guarantee returns
  3. They carry no distribution fee
  4. They are for retail investors only
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. They carry no distribution fee

Direct plans are for investors approaching directly or via registered advisers and carry no distribution or placement fee.

Question 6

For Category I and II AIFs, distribution fee paid upfront is limited because SEBI wants:

  1. Distributors paid only at fund exit
  2. Most commission paid as equal trail over the fund's life
  3. No distribution fees at all for any plan, whether direct or regular
  4. Higher upfront fees for large investors
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Most commission paid as equal trail over the fund's life

SEBI limits upfront payment to a portion of total distribution fee, with the rest on equal trail, aligning incentives.

Question 7

Before launching a scheme, an AIF must file its PPM with SEBI through:

  1. Its statutory auditor
  2. A stock exchange
  3. A SEBI-registered merchant banker
  4. The trustee alone
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. A SEBI-registered merchant banker

PPMs must be filed through a merchant banker who conducts due diligence.

Question 8

Which of these is an obligation of the AIF manager under SEBI's framework?

  1. Ensure investments follow the PPM and regulations
  2. Guarantee investors a minimum return each year on their contributed capital
  3. List units on an exchange
  4. Distribute profits every month
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Ensure investments follow the PPM and regulations

Managers must invest per the PPM, maintain records and comply with regulations; they cannot guarantee returns.
