Investment Process and Governance of Funds: NISM Series XIX-A practice questions

8 free questions with answers and explanations on Investment Process and Governance of Funds, from the NISM Series XIX-A (Alternative Investment Funds (Category I and II) Distributors) syllabus.

Last updated 23 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · How we create questions

What this chapter covers

This chapter covers how AIFs source, evaluate, approve, monitor and exit investments, and the governance that controls these activities. It explains deal sourcing, screening, due diligence, valuation and term negotiation, the role of the investment committee, conflict-of-interest policies, investor approvals for related-party transactions, investment restrictions and concentration limits, monitoring of portfolio companies and the responsibilities of the manager, trustee and compliance officer. Focus on the sequence of the process, who approves what, and governance practices that protect investors.

Investment Process and Governance of Funds questions

Question 1

In the investment process of a PE fund, 'screening' refers to:

  1. The detailed legal review of the share purchase agreement
  2. An initial filter of deals against the fund's strategy
  3. The final exit of a portfolio company
  4. Valuation of the fund's units for investors
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. An initial filter of deals against the fund's strategy

Screening is a quick initial assessment of many opportunities against the strategy, size and sector criteria before detailed due diligence on the few that pass.

Question 2

A member of an AIF's investment committee has a personal stake in a target company. Good governance requires that member to:

  1. Vote in favour to support the company
  2. Keep the interest confidential to avoid delays
  3. Disclose the interest and recuse from the decision
  4. Resign from the manager immediately in every case
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Disclose the interest and recuse from the decision

Conflicted members should disclose their interest and abstain from deliberation and voting. Resignation is not normally required, but secrecy breaches governance norms.

Question 3

Which entity in a trust-based AIF is responsible for monitoring the manager's compliance with the PPM and regulations?

  1. The trustee, supported by the compliance officer
  2. The investee companies
  3. The placement agent
  4. The largest investor alone
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. The trustee, supported by the compliance officer

The trustee oversees the manager, and the manager's compliance officer monitors adherence to regulations and the PPM. Investee companies and agents have no oversight role.

Question 4

A Category II AIF wants to buy shares from another scheme of the same manager. This is generally:

  1. Prohibited outright without exception
  2. A conflict needing approval as per regulations
  3. Permitted freely as both schemes are managed by the same entity
  4. Allowed only if the shares are listed
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. A conflict needing approval as per regulations

Transactions between schemes of the same manager raise conflicts; SEBI requires disclosure and investor consent as prescribed, plus fair valuation. They are not automatically prohibited.

Question 5

After investing, a PE fund typically monitors portfolio companies by:

  1. Board seats, regular MIS and covenant tracking
  2. Selling the shares within three months of purchase
  3. Relying only on annual audited accounts
  4. Leaving management entirely unsupervised
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Board seats, regular MIS and covenant tracking

Active monitoring through board representation, periodic reports and covenant checks lets the fund protect value and intervene early. Annual accounts alone are too infrequent.

Question 6

Which document defines the fund's investment strategy, which the investment process must follow?

  1. The custodian agreement
  2. The KYC form of the investor
  3. The private placement memorandum
  4. The investee company's articles of association
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. The private placement memorandum

The PPM sets out the strategy, restrictions and terms; investments must conform to it. Material deviations require investor consent.

Question 7

An AIF manager allocates a hot deal only to a fund where it earns higher carry, ignoring another fund with the same mandate. This breaches:

  1. The duty of fair allocation among funds
  2. Concentration limits only
  3. Reporting timelines
  4. Stewardship code voting rules
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. The duty of fair allocation among funds

Managers must allocate opportunities fairly as per a disclosed allocation policy, not based on their own economics. Concentration limits and voting rules are separate matters.

Question 8

Which practice best supports good governance in a fund's investment decisions?

  1. Approvals given orally by the chief investment officer alone, without any written record
  2. Rotating approval rights among junior analysts
  3. Allowing investee companies to vote on fund decisions
  4. Documented IC minutes with reasons for approval
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Documented IC minutes with reasons for approval

Written records of IC deliberations and reasons create accountability and an audit trail. Oral approvals by one person weaken governance.
