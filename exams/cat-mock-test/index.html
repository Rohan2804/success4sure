About the CAT exam

CAT is the Common Admission Test conducted by the IIMs for admission to their MBA/PGP programmes and used by many other B-schools. It tests Verbal Ability & Reading Comprehension, Data Interpretation & Logical Reasoning, and Quantitative Ability.

Who should take it: Graduates applying to IIMs and other top Indian business schools that accept CAT scores.

CAT exam pattern

Questions66 multiple-choice questions
Total marks198
Duration120 minutes
Target score50% (cut-offs vary by year and category)
Negative marking−1 mark per wrong answer

Indicative pattern. Exam bodies revise patterns — confirm the details in the latest official notification before your exam.

Sample 1 · Verbal Ability & Reading Comprehension

Read the passage and answer the question. When we store a phone number in a device rather than in memory, we engage in what psychologists call cognitive offloading. Critics treat this as a symptom of intellectual decline, but the history of writing suggests otherwise: Socrates worried that writing would erode memory, yet literate cultures went on to build bodies of knowledge far more complex than any oral tradition could sustain. The question, then, is not whether we offload but what we do with the capacity that is freed. Offloading becomes corrosive only when the freed attention is spent on nothing in particular. The author's primary purpose is to:

  1. argue that digital devices have caused a measurable decline in human intelligence
  2. reframe a common worry about cognitive offloading by shifting attention to how the freed capacity is used
  3. defend Socrates' view that writing weakens human memory
  4. show that oral traditions were more sophisticated than literate cultures
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. reframe a common worry about cognitive offloading by shifting attention to how the freed capacity is used

The author rejects the decline narrative and says the real issue is 'what we do with the capacity that is freed'.

Sample 2 · Data Interpretation & Logical Reasoning

Revenue (₹ crore) of four firms: Firm | 2021 | 2022 | 2023 P | 120 | 150 | 180 Q | 200 | 190 | 228 R | 80 | 100 | 125 S | 150 | 165 | 198 For how many firms was the percentage growth in revenue in 2023 (over 2022) strictly greater than the percentage growth in 2022 (over 2021)?

  1. 1
  2. 3
  3. 2
  4. 4
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 2

Growth 2022 vs 2023: P 25% vs 20%, Q −5% vs 20%, R 25% vs 25%, S 10% vs 20%; only Q and S qualify.

Sample 3 · Quantitative Ability

A 60-litre mixture contains milk and water in the ratio 7 : 3. How many litres of water must be added to make the ratio 3 : 2?

  1. 8
  2. 12
  3. 10
  4. 15
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 10

Milk = 42 L, water = 18 L; 42/(18 + x) = 3/2 gives 18 + x = 28, so x = 10.

Sample 4 · Verbal Ability & Reading Comprehension

Read the passage and answer the question. When we store a phone number in a device rather than in memory, we engage in what psychologists call cognitive offloading. Critics treat this as a symptom of intellectual decline, but the history of writing suggests otherwise: Socrates worried that writing would erode memory, yet literate cultures went on to build bodies of knowledge far more complex than any oral tradition could sustain. The question, then, is not whether we offload but what we do with the capacity that is freed. Offloading becomes corrosive only when the freed attention is spent on nothing in particular. Which of the following, if true, would most weaken the author's argument?

  1. Literate societies produced more written records than oral ones
  2. Socrates' concerns about writing were shared by other ancient philosophers
  3. Most people today store phone numbers on their devices rather than memorising them
  4. The act of offloading itself, regardless of how freed attention is used, impairs the ability to form new long-term memories
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. The act of offloading itself, regardless of how freed attention is used, impairs the ability to form new long-term memories

The author claims offloading is harmful only depending on how freed capacity is used; harm from offloading itself, independent of use, directly undercuts this.

Sample 5 · Verbal Ability & Reading Comprehension

Read the passage and answer the question. Fisheries managers once assumed that if a fish stock collapsed, the fleet would simply move elsewhere and the fish would recover. The cod off Newfoundland disproved this. Two decades after fishing was halted, the population remained a fraction of its former size, partly because the ecosystem had reorganised: smaller species that cod once preyed upon had multiplied and now fed on young cod. A collapse, in other words, can be self-perpetuating, because it alters the very conditions that made the earlier abundance possible. The managers' earlier assumption was flawed mainly because it:

  1. overestimated the fleet's willingness to move to other fishing grounds
  2. ignored the economic cost of halting fishing
  3. treated the ecosystem as a fixed backdrop that would remain unchanged while the stock recovered
  4. relied on population counts that had been deliberately inflated
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. treated the ecosystem as a fixed backdrop that would remain unchanged while the stock recovered

The passage says the ecosystem 'reorganised' after the collapse, which the managers' assumption of automatic recovery overlooked.

CAT mock test FAQs

How many questions are there in the CAT exam?

The CAT paper has 66 multiple-choice questions for 198 marks, to be completed in 120 minutes. This is the indicative pattern — always confirm it in the latest official notification.

Is there negative marking in CAT?

Yes. −1 mark per wrong answer (33.3% of the marks for that question). Our mock tests apply the same rule.

What is the target score for CAT?

Cut-offs vary by year and category; our mock tests use 50% as a target score to aim for.

How many CAT mock tests do I get?

One full-length mock test is unlocked for each day of your plan: 10, 15, 20 or 30 mock tests on the 10 Days, 15 Days, 20 Days or 30 Days plans. You can retake any unlocked test while your plan is active.

Are these the actual exam questions?

No. Every question is an original practice question written to match the official syllabus and the exam's style. Reproducing real exam questions is not permitted, and we do not do it.

Where can I get the official CAT study material?
