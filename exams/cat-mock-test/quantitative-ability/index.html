Quantitative Ability: CAT practice questions

15 free questions with answers and explanations on Quantitative Ability, from the CAT (Common Admission Test) syllabus.

Last updated 16 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · How we create questions

What this section covers

Quantitative Ability (QA) in CAT covers arithmetic (percentages, profit and loss, interest, ratio, mixtures, time-speed-distance, time and work), algebra (equations, inequalities, functions, progressions, logarithms), geometry and mensuration, number system, and modern maths such as permutations, combinations and probability. Arithmetic and algebra usually carry the most weight. Revise core formulas, but focus on approaches that save time: working with ratios instead of raw values, checking options by substitution, and recognising standard patterns like cyclicity of remainders or the inclusion-exclusion principle.

Quantitative Ability questions

Question 1

A sum of money lent at compound interest (compounded annually) amounts to ₹13,310 in 3 years and ₹14,641 in 4 years. What is the sum?

  1. ₹11,000
  2. ₹10,500
  3. ₹10,000
  4. ₹9,500
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. ₹10,000

Ratio of successive amounts 14,641/13,310 = 1.1, so the rate is 10%. Principal = 13,310 / 1.1³ = 13,310 / 1.331 = ₹10,000.

Question 2

Two runners start together from the same point on a circular track and run in opposite directions at 5 m/s and 7 m/s. At how many distinct points on the track will they meet?

  1. 12
  2. 35
  3. 7
  4. 2
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 12

For runners moving in opposite directions with speeds in the lowest ratio a : b, the number of distinct meeting points is a + b = 5 + 7 = 12. Running in the same direction would give |a − b| = 2.

Question 3

A vessel holds 80 litres of a 30% acid solution. How many litres of pure acid must be added to raise the concentration to 50%?

  1. 40
  2. 32
  3. 16
  4. 24
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 32

Acid now = 24 L. Adding x L: (24 + x)/(80 + x) = 0.5, so 48 + 2x = 80 + x and x = 32. Check: 56/112 = 50%.

Question 4

A and B together can finish a job in 10 days, B and C in 15 days, and A and C in 12 days. How long will A, B and C take working together?

  1. 6 days
  2. 7.5 days
  3. 8 days
  4. 9 days
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 8 days

Adding pair rates: 2(A + B + C) = 1/10 + 1/15 + 1/12 = 15/60 = 1/4. So A + B + C = 1/8 per day, i.e. 8 days together.

Question 5

Find the remainder left on dividing 2²⁰²⁶ by 7.

  1. 1
  2. 4
  3. 6
  4. 2
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 2

2³ = 8 leaves remainder 1 on division by 7, so powers of 2 repeat in a cycle of 3 (2, 4, 1). Since 2026 = 3 × 675 + 1, 2²⁰²⁶ leaves the same remainder as 2¹, which is 2.

Question 6

How many ordered triples of positive integers (x, y, z) satisfy x + y + z = 12?

  1. 55
  2. 78
  3. 45
  4. 66
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 55

By stars and bars, positive solutions of x + y + z = n number C(n − 1, 2). For n = 12 this is C(11, 2) = 55. C(12, 2) = 66 would count non-negative solutions of x + y + z = 11 instead.

Question 7

The sum of the first n terms of an arithmetic progression is 3n² + 5n. What is its 10th term?

  1. 68
  2. 58
  3. 66
  4. 62
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 62

T₁₀ = S₁₀ − S₉ = (300 + 50) − (243 + 45) = 350 − 288 = 62. In general Tₙ = 6n + 2, so the common difference is 6.

Question 8

What is the radius of the circle inscribed in a right-angled triangle whose legs are 9 cm and 12 cm?

  1. 3 cm
  2. 2.5 cm
  3. 4 cm
  4. 6 cm
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 3 cm

The hypotenuse is 15 cm. For a right triangle, inradius r = (a + b − c)/2 = (9 + 12 − 15)/2 = 3 cm. Equivalently, area 54 divided by semi-perimeter 18 gives 3.

Question 9

A number is picked at random from 1 to 50. What is the probability that it is divisible by 3 or by 5?

  1. 1/2
  2. 21/50
  3. 23/50
  4. 13/25
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 23/50

Multiples of 3: 16; of 5: 10; of 15: 3. By inclusion-exclusion, 16 + 10 − 3 = 23 favourable numbers, so the probability is 23/50.

Question 10

How many integers x satisfy |2x − 5| < 9?

  1. 10
  2. 8
  3. 7
  4. 9
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 8

−9 < 2x − 5 < 9 gives −4 < 2x < 14, i.e. −2 < x < 7. The integers are −1, 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6: eight values.

Question 11

If f(x) = 2x + 3 and g(x) = x² − 1, what is f(g(2)) − g(f(1))?

  1. −15
  2. −21
  3. 15
  4. −9
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. −15

g(2) = 3, so f(g(2)) = 9. f(1) = 5, so g(f(1)) = 24. The difference is 9 − 24 = −15; reversing the order of composition gives the tempting 15.

Question 12

A shopkeeper marks an item 40% above cost and then allows two successive discounts of 10% each. What is his profit percentage?

  1. 20%
  2. 12%
  3. 13.4%
  4. 15.2%
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 13.4%

Selling price = 1.40 × 0.90 × 0.90 = 1.134 times cost, so profit is 13.4%. Subtracting 20% from 40% ignores that the second discount applies to an already reduced price.

Question 13

A wooden cuboid measuring 12 cm × 8 cm × 6 cm is cut into identical cubes of the largest possible size with no wood wasted. How many cubes are obtained?

  1. 48
  2. 72
  3. 36
  4. 96
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 72

The largest cube edge is HCF(12, 8, 6) = 2 cm. Number of cubes = (12/2) × (8/2) × (6/2) = 6 × 4 × 3 = 72.

Question 14

Given log₁₀ 2 = 0.3010, how many digits does 2⁵⁰ have?

  1. 17
  2. 50
  3. 15
  4. 16
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 16

log₁₀(2⁵⁰) = 50 × 0.3010 = 15.05. A number whose logarithm has integer part 15 has 15 + 1 = 16 digits.

Question 15

A train passes a 200 m platform in 20 seconds and overtakes a man walking at 6 km/h in the same direction in 12 seconds. What is the length of the train?

  1. 270 m
  2. 225 m
  3. 200 m
  4. 250 m
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 250 m

Let speed be v m/s and length L. L + 200 = 20v and L = 12(v − 5/3). So 20v − 200 = 12v − 20, giving v = 22.5 m/s and L = 250 m.
