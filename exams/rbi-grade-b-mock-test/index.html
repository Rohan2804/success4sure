About the RBI Grade B exam

RBI Grade B Phase I is the first stage of the Reserve Bank of India's officer recruitment, testing General Awareness with a focus on economy and banking, English Language, Quantitative Aptitude and Reasoning.

Who should take it: Graduates and postgraduates applying for Grade B officer posts at the Reserve Bank of India.

Sample 1 · General Awareness

Under which Act was the Reserve Bank of India constituted?

  1. Banking Regulation Act, 1949
  2. Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934
  3. Companies Act, 1956
  4. Foreign Exchange Management Act, 1999
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934

The RBI was set up under the RBI Act, 1934 and began operations in April 1935.

Sample 2 · English Language

Read the passage and answer the question. Central banks face a classic trade-off when inflation and unemployment move in opposite directions. Raising interest rates may cool prices but can also slow hiring, while cutting rates may support jobs but risks letting inflation become entrenched. Credibility matters because if people believe the central bank will act decisively, their inflation expectations stay anchored and smaller policy moves are needed. According to the passage, why is credibility valuable to a central bank?

  1. It removes the trade-off between inflation and unemployment
  2. It allows the bank to keep interest rates unchanged permanently
  3. Anchored expectations let it achieve its goals with smaller policy moves
  4. It guarantees that unemployment falls whenever rates rise
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Anchored expectations let it achieve its goals with smaller policy moves

The last sentence links credibility to anchored expectations and smaller policy moves; the trade-off itself is not removed.

Sample 4 · Reasoning

Eight persons A, B, C, D, E, F, G and H live on eight different floors of a building numbered 1 (lowest) to 8 (top). • D lives on an odd-numbered floor above floor 3. • Exactly two persons live between D and C. • A lives on an even-numbered floor below D. • G lives immediately above C. • E lives below C. • Exactly four persons live between E and H. • B lives immediately above H. Who lives on floor 3?

  1. A
  2. C
  3. G
  4. E
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. G

D = 5 or 7. If D = 7, then C = 4, G = 5 and E (floor 1–3) puts H on 6, 7 or 8, but B must be immediately above H on a free floor — impossible. So D = 5, C = 2, G = 3, E = 1, H = 6, B = 7, A = 4, F = 8.

Sample 5 · General Awareness

Under the Cash Reserve Ratio (CRR), a scheduled bank must keep a specified share of its net demand and time liabilities in which form?

  1. As a cash balance with the RBI
  2. As gold in its own vaults
  3. As government securities in its own portfolio
  4. As deposits with other commercial banks
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. As a cash balance with the RBI

CRR is maintained as cash balances with the RBI; liquid assets held by the bank itself count towards SLR, not CRR.

RBI Grade B mock test FAQs

How many questions are there in the RBI Grade B exam?

The RBI Grade B paper has 200 multiple-choice questions for 200 marks, to be completed in 120 minutes. This is the indicative pattern — always confirm it in the latest official notification.
