Question 1 · General Awareness

Which RBI facility allows scheduled banks to borrow overnight, at a rate above the policy repo rate, by dipping into their SLR holdings up to a limit?

  1. Reverse repo
  2. Marginal Standing Facility
  3. Cash Reserve Ratio
  4. Open market sale
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Marginal Standing Facility

The MSF is a penal-rate overnight window above the repo rate under which banks may use a portion of their SLR securities.

Question 2 · English Language

Read the passage and answer the question. Proponents of universal basic income argue that unconditional cash transfers reduce administrative costs and avoid the exclusion errors that plague targeted schemes. Sceptics counter that a payment generous enough to matter would be fiscally prohibitive, while one that is affordable would be too small to replace existing welfare. The sceptics' argument is best described as:

  1. A claim that targeted schemes have no exclusion errors
  2. A dilemma between adequacy and affordability
  3. An objection based on administrative complexity
  4. A demand that transfers be linked to employment
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. A dilemma between adequacy and affordability

Sceptics say a large payment is unaffordable and an affordable one is inadequate — a two-sided dilemma.

Question 3 · Quantitative Aptitude

What will come in place of the question mark (?) 3, 4, 12, 39, 103, ?

  1. 216
  2. 232
  3. 228
  4. 224
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 228

Differences are cubes: 1, 8, 27, 64, 125; 103 + 125 = 228.

Question 4 · Reasoning

Eight persons A, B, C, D, E, F, G and H live on eight different floors of a building numbered 1 (lowest) to 8 (top). • D lives on an odd-numbered floor above floor 3. • Exactly two persons live between D and C. • A lives on an even-numbered floor below D. • G lives immediately above C. • E lives below C. • Exactly four persons live between E and H. • B lives immediately above H. How many persons live between A and B?

  1. One
  2. Three
  3. Two
  4. Four
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Two

Order (1→8): E, C, G, A, D, H, B, F. Between A (4) and B (7) are D and H.

Question 5 · General Awareness

When the RBI purchases government securities in the open market, what is the immediate effect on liquidity in the banking system?

  1. Liquidity is injected into the system
  2. Liquidity is absorbed from the system
  3. The CRR rises automatically
  4. Money supply falls
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Liquidity is injected into the system

The RBI pays for the securities it buys, adding reserves (rupee liquidity) to the banking system.

Question 6 · English Language

Choose the word that best fills the blank. The committee's recommendations, though ________ in theory, proved difficult to implement given the state's limited administrative capacity.

  1. flawed
  2. obsolete
  3. sound
  4. trivial
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. sound

'Though' signals contrast with 'difficult to implement', so the recommendations must be good in theory: sound.

Question 7 · Quantitative Aptitude

Two equations I and II are given. Solve both and choose the correct relationship between x and y. I. 6x² − 7x + 2 = 0 II. 12y² − 7y + 1 = 0

  1. x > y
  2. x < y
  3. x ≥ y
  4. Relationship cannot be established
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. x > y

(2x − 1)(3x − 2) = 0 → x = 1/2, 2/3; (3y − 1)(4y − 1) = 0 → y = 1/3, 1/4. Every x exceeds every y, so x > y.

Question 8 · Reasoning

Six persons L, M, N, O, P and Q sit around a circular table facing the centre. M sits second to the left of L. N sits opposite Q. P is an immediate neighbour of both M and N. Who sits immediately to the right of L?

  1. Q
  2. M
  3. O
  4. P
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. O

Clockwise from L: L, Q, M, P, N, O (facing the centre, left is clockwise). L's immediate right (anticlockwise) is O.

Question 9 · General Awareness

Which body determines the policy repo rate in India under the flexible inflation targeting framework?

  1. Monetary Policy Committee
  2. Finance Commission
  3. NITI Aayog
  4. Securities and Exchange Board of India
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Monetary Policy Committee

Under the amended RBI Act, the Monetary Policy Committee decides the policy repo rate.

Question 10 · English Language

Choose the phrase that best fills the blank. Persistent fiscal deficits can ________ private investment if government borrowing pushes up interest rates.

  1. crowd in
  2. bail out
  3. phase in
  4. crowd out
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. crowd out

Higher interest rates caused by government borrowing reduce, i.e. crowd out, private investment.

Question 11 · Quantitative Aptitude

A sum of money invested at compound interest (compounded annually) amounts to ₹6,050 in 2 years and to ₹6,655 in 3 years. What is the sum?

  1. ₹5,500
  2. ₹4,800
  3. ₹5,000
  4. ₹5,050
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. ₹5,000

Rate = (6,655 − 6,050)/6,050 = 10%; P = 6,050/1.1² = 6,050/1.21 = ₹5,000.

Question 12 · Reasoning

Statements: Some debts are bonds. No bond is a share. All shares are equities. Conclusions: I. Some equities are not bonds. II. Some debts being shares is a possibility.

  1. Only conclusion I follows
  2. Only conclusion II follows
  3. Both I and II follow
  4. Neither I nor II follows
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Both I and II follow

Shares are equities and no share is a bond, so some equities are not bonds. Debts outside the bond region may be shares, so II is possible.

Question 13 · General Awareness

How many members make up the Monetary Policy Committee constituted under the RBI Act, 1934?

  1. Four
  2. Six
  3. Five
  4. Seven
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Six

The MPC has six members: three from the RBI (including the Governor) and three external members appointed by the Government.

Question 14 · English Language

Identify the part of the sentence that contains an error. Scarcely had the regulator announced (A) / the new norms than (B) / several lenders revised (C) / their lending rates. (D)

  1. (A)
  2. (B)
  3. (C)
  4. (D)
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. (B)

'Scarcely' is followed by 'when', not 'than': 'the new norms when'.

Question 15 · Quantitative Aptitude

A and B can complete a job in 15 days and 20 days respectively. With the help of C, the three finish it in 6 days. If they are paid ₹3,600 in all, how much should C get?

  1. ₹1,200
  2. ₹1,080
  3. ₹1,440
  4. ₹900
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. ₹1,080

C's rate = 1/6 − 1/15 − 1/20 = 1/20; in 6 days C does 6/20 = 3/10 of the work, so C gets 3/10 × 3,600 = ₹1,080.
