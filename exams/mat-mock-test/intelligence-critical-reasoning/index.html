Intelligence & Critical Reasoning: MAT practice questions

15 free questions with answers and explanations on Intelligence & Critical Reasoning, from the MAT (Management Aptitude Test) syllabus.

Last updated 11 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · How we create questions

What this section covers

Intelligence and Critical Reasoning in MAT combines verbal and non-verbal reasoning with critical thinking. Common topics are number and letter series, coding-decoding, blood relations, directions, syllogisms, seating and ranking, analogies, odd one out, clocks and calendars, and statement-based questions on assumptions, courses of action and strengthening or weakening arguments. Practise by drawing diagrams for relations and directions, using Venn diagrams for syllogisms, and checking every condition in arrangement puzzles. For critical reasoning, identify the conclusion first and look for the option that attacks or supports the link to it.

Intelligence & Critical Reasoning questions

Question 1

Find the next term of the series. 3, 8, 15, 24, 35, ?

  1. 46
  2. 50
  3. 48
  4. 49
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 48

The terms are n² − 1 for n = 2, 3, 4, 5, 6: 3, 8, 15, 24, 35. The next is 7² − 1 = 48. Equivalently, the differences 5, 7, 9, 11 continue with 13.

Question 2

Find the next letter of the series. A, C, F, J, O, ?

  1. V
  2. U
  3. T
  4. S
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. U

The gaps increase by one each time: +2, +3, +4, +5. The next gap is +6, and O (15) + 6 = U (21).

Question 3

In a certain code, LAMP is written as OZNK. How will GOLD be written in that code?

  1. TLOW
  2. TMOW
  3. TLOX
  4. SLOW
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. TLOW

Each letter is replaced by its mirror letter in the alphabet (A↔Z, B↔Y, and so on; positions add up to 27). G→T, O→L, L→O, D→W, giving TLOW.

Question 4

Pointing to a man, Meera said, "His father is the only son of my father's father." How is the man related to Meera?

  1. Uncle
  2. Cousin
  3. Brother
  4. Nephew
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Brother

The only son of Meera's paternal grandfather is Meera's own father. So the man's father is Meera's father, which makes the man her brother.

Question 5

Ravi walks 5 km south, turns left and walks 3 km, then turns left again and walks 9 km. How far and in which direction is he now from his starting point?

  1. 7 km, North-East
  2. 5 km, North-West
  3. 4 km, North
  4. 5 km, North-East
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 5 km, North-East

Facing south, a left turn means east (3 km); another left means north (9 km). Net position: 3 km east and 4 km north, so he is √(9 + 16) = 5 km away towards the north-east.

Question 6

Statements: No doctor is a lawyer. Some lawyers are writers. Conclusions: I. Some writers are not doctors. II. Some doctors are writers.

  1. Both conclusions follow
  2. Neither conclusion follows
  3. Only conclusion II follows
  4. Only conclusion I follows
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Only conclusion I follows

The writers who are lawyers cannot be doctors, since no doctor is a lawyer, so some writers are certainly not doctors. Nothing links doctors to writers directly, so II is only a possibility.

Question 7

Statement: The city council has decided to plant 10,000 saplings along highways to reduce air pollution. Assumptions: I. Trees can help reduce air pollution. II. Air pollution in the city is caused only by vehicles on highways. Which assumption is implicit?

  1. Only assumption II is implicit
  2. Neither I nor II is implicit
  3. Only assumption I is implicit
  4. Both I and II are implicit
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Only assumption I is implicit

The decision makes sense only if trees help reduce pollution, so I is implicit. The council need not believe highways are the only source; planting along them can help even if other sources exist.

Question 8

A shop moved its umbrellas next to the entrance, and umbrella sales over the following month rose by 40%. The manager concluded that the new placement caused the rise. Which of the following, if true, most weakens this conclusion?

  1. The month after the move had unusually heavy and frequent rain.
  2. The shop also sells raincoats, which were kept in their original place.
  3. Umbrellas at the shop are priced slightly below those of nearby stores.
  4. Customers notice items placed near the entrance more often than others.
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. The month after the move had unusually heavy and frequent rain.

Heavy rain offers an alternative cause for higher umbrella sales, weakening the claim that placement was responsible. The point about customers noticing entrance items would actually support the manager's conclusion.

Question 9

In a row of 40 students, Ravi is 14th from the left end. Kiran is 9th to the right of Ravi. What is Kiran's position from the right end?

  1. 17th
  2. 18th
  3. 19th
  4. 23rd
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 18th

Kiran is 14 + 9 = 23rd from the left. Position from the right = 40 − 23 + 1 = 18th. 23rd is his position from the left, a common trap.

Question 10

Select the word that completes the analogy. Ornithology : Birds :: Entomology : ?

  1. Fossils
  2. Words
  3. Insects
  4. Fungi
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Insects

Ornithology is the study of birds; entomology is the study of insects. Words are studied in etymology, fungi in mycology and fossils in palaeontology.

Question 11

Select the letter group that does NOT belong with the others.

  1. RTW
  2. BDF
  3. HJL
  4. MOQ
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. RTW

In BDF, HJL and MOQ, each letter is two places after the previous one. In RTW the gaps are two and then three (R→T→W), so it is the odd one out.

Question 12

What is the angle between the hour and minute hands of a clock at 3:40?

  1. 110°
  2. 130°
  3. 120°
  4. 140°
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 130°

Angle = |30H − 5.5M| = |30 × 3 − 5.5 × 40| = |90 − 220| = 130°. Ignoring the hour hand's movement past 3 would wrongly give 120°.

Question 13

Five friends A, B, C, D and E sit in a row facing north. • B sits second to the left of D. • E sits immediately to the right of B. • A sits at one of the ends, to the left of C. • C is not next to A. Who sits in the middle?

  1. C
  2. B
  3. D
  4. E
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. E

A must be at the left end. B, E, D occupy three consecutive seats (B, E, then D), and C cannot be next to A, so the order is A, B, E, D, C. E is in the middle.

Question 14

Statement: Several cases of food poisoning were reported after a wedding feast in a town. Courses of action: I. The health department should collect samples of the food served and investigate. II. All weddings in the town should be banned for a year. Which course of action follows?

  1. Neither I nor II follows
  2. Only I follows
  3. Both I and II follow
  4. Only II follows
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Only I follows

Investigating the food is a practical step to find the cause and prevent repetition. Banning all weddings is an extreme reaction that does not address the actual problem, so only I follows.

Question 15

If 15 August 2026 falls on a Saturday, on which day will 15 August 2027 fall?

  1. Monday
  2. Saturday
  3. Sunday
  4. Friday
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Sunday

The period from 15 August 2026 to 15 August 2027 contains February 2027, which is not a leap month, so there are 365 days, or 1 odd day. Saturday + 1 = Sunday.
