Free MAT practice questions

Question 1 · Language Comprehension

Choose the correct meaning of the idiom 'to cut corners'.

  1. To take a shorter route while driving
  2. To do something in the easiest or cheapest way, often at the cost of quality
  3. To reduce the size of a document
  4. To avoid meeting people
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. To do something in the easiest or cheapest way, often at the cost of quality

Cutting corners means skimping on effort or quality to save time or money.

Question 2 · Intelligence & Critical Reasoning

Find the next letter of the series. Z, X, U, Q, L, ?

  1. F
  2. G
  3. E
  4. H
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. F

Positions 26, 24, 21, 17, 12 fall by 2, 3, 4, 5; next is 12 − 6 = 6 = F.

Question 3 · Mathematical Skills

Two numbers are in the ratio 5 : 7 and their LCM is 420. What is their HCF?

  1. 12
  2. 15
  3. 10
  4. 14
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 12

Numbers are 5k and 7k with LCM 35k = 420, so k = 12 = HCF.

Question 4 · Data Analysis & Sufficiency

Exports (₹ crore) of a company: 2019 – 250, 2020 – 200, 2021 – 300, 2022 – 360, 2023 – 450 What were the average annual exports over the five years?

  1. ₹300 crore
  2. ₹312 crore
  3. ₹320 crore
  4. ₹315 crore
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. ₹312 crore

Total = 250 + 200 + 300 + 360 + 450 = 1560; average = 1560/5 = ₹312 crore.

Question 5 · Language Comprehension

Choose the one word for: 'Government by the wealthy'.

  1. Autocracy
  2. Theocracy
  3. Bureaucracy
  4. Plutocracy
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Plutocracy

Plutocracy is rule by the rich; theocracy is rule by religious authority.

Question 6 · Intelligence & Critical Reasoning

If SKY is coded as 19-11-25, how is MAT coded?

  1. 13-2-20
  2. 12-1-20
  3. 14-1-21
  4. 13-1-20
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 13-1-20

Each letter is replaced by its alphabet position: M = 13, A = 1, T = 20.

Question 7 · Mathematical Skills

A sells an article to B at a 10% profit, and B sells it to C at a 20% profit. If C pays ₹1,320, what did A pay for it?

  1. ₹1,100
  2. ₹1,050
  3. ₹1,200
  4. ₹1,000
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. ₹1,000

A's cost × 1.1 × 1.2 = 1320, so A's cost = 1320/1.32 = ₹1,000.

Question 8 · Data Analysis & Sufficiency

A family's monthly income of ₹60,000 is spent as: Rent 25%, Food 30%, Education 15%, Transport 10%, Savings 20%. In a pie chart of this spending, what is the central angle for Food?

  1. 90°
  2. 120°
  3. 72°
  4. 108°
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 108°

Angle = 30% of 360° = 108°.

Question 9 · Language Comprehension

Fill in the blank with the most appropriate word. Despite his ______ efforts, the project could not be completed on time.

  1. lethargic
  2. feeble
  3. strenuous
  4. negligent
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. strenuous

'Despite' signals contrast, so the efforts must have been great; 'strenuous' fits.

Question 10 · Intelligence & Critical Reasoning

Introducing a woman, Arun said, "Her mother is the only daughter of my mother." How is the woman related to Arun?

  1. Sister
  2. Daughter
  3. Cousin
  4. Niece
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Niece

The only daughter of Arun's mother is Arun's sister; the woman is his sister's daughter, i.e. his niece.

Question 11 · Mathematical Skills

The speed of a boat in still water is 15 km/h and the speed of the stream is 3 km/h. How long will the boat take to go 36 km downstream and return?

  1. 4.8 hours
  2. 6 hours
  3. 5 hours
  4. 4.5 hours
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 5 hours

Time = 36/18 + 36/12 = 2 + 3 = 5 hours.

Question 12 · Data Analysis & Sufficiency

A family's monthly income of ₹60,000 is spent as: Rent 25%, Food 30%, Education 15%, Transport 10%, Savings 20%. How much more does the family spend on Food than on Transport each month?

  1. ₹10,000
  2. ₹18,000
  3. ₹12,000
  4. ₹6,000
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. ₹12,000

Difference = (30% − 10%) of ₹60,000 = ₹12,000.

Question 13 · Language Comprehension

Identify the part of the sentence that contains an error. The number of applicants / are increasing / every year.

  1. The number of applicants
  2. are increasing
  3. every year
  4. No error
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. are increasing

'The number of' takes a singular verb, so it should be 'is increasing'.

Question 14 · Intelligence & Critical Reasoning

A clock is placed so that at 12 noon its minute hand points North-East. In which direction will its hour hand point at 3:00 pm?

  1. South-East
  2. North-West
  3. South-West
  4. East
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. South-East

The 12 mark faces North-East; the 3 mark is 90° clockwise from it, i.e. South-East.

Question 15 · Mathematical Skills

If 3x − 2y = 4 and 2x + y = 12, what is the value of x + y?

  1. 6
  2. 8
  3. 10
  4. 7
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 8

From y = 12 − 2x: 3x − 24 + 4x = 4, so x = 4, y = 4 and x + y = 8.
