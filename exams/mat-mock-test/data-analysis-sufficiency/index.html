Data Analysis & Sufficiency: MAT practice questions

15 free questions with answers and explanations on Data Analysis & Sufficiency, from the MAT (Management Aptitude Test) syllabus.

Last updated 23 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · How we create questions

What this section covers

Data Analysis and Sufficiency in MAT presents data in tables, pie charts, bar and line graphs, and asks for totals, averages, percentages, ratios and growth rates. It also includes data sufficiency questions, where you decide whether the given statements are enough to answer a question rather than computing the answer itself. Practise quick percentage and ratio calculations, read the units and base of every chart carefully, and for sufficiency questions test each statement alone before combining them, without assuming facts not stated.

Data Analysis & Sufficiency questions

Question 1

Students enrolled in four courses at an institute: Course | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 BBA | 400 | 460 | 520 BCom | 600 | 570 | 630 BSc | 350 | 420 | 490 BA | 250 | 260 | 240 By what percentage did the total enrolment rise from 2022 to 2024?

  1. 15%
  2. 16.4%
  3. 18.2%
  4. 17.5%
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 17.5%

Total 2022 = 400 + 600 + 350 + 250 = 1,600. Total 2024 = 520 + 630 + 490 + 240 = 1,880. Rise = 280/1,600 × 100 = 17.5%.

Question 2

Students enrolled in four courses at an institute: Course | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 BBA | 400 | 460 | 520 BCom | 600 | 570 | 630 BSc | 350 | 420 | 490 BA | 250 | 260 | 240 BSc enrolment in 2024 was what percentage of BBA enrolment in 2022?

  1. 122.5%
  2. 130%
  3. 112.5%
  4. 118.4%
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 122.5%

BSc 2024 = 490 and BBA 2022 = 400. 490/400 × 100 = 122.5%. Take care to compare the right course and the right year.

Question 3

Students enrolled in four courses at an institute: Course | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 BBA | 400 | 460 | 520 BCom | 600 | 570 | 630 BSc | 350 | 420 | 490 BA | 250 | 260 | 240 In how many courses was the 2024 enrolment more than 20% higher than the 2022 enrolment?

  1. 3
  2. 2
  3. 1
  4. 4
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 2

BBA rose 30% (400 to 520) and BSc 40% (350 to 490). BCom rose only 5% and BA fell 4%. So two courses qualify.

Question 4

A company's annual budget of ₹45 crore is allocated as: Production 40%, Salaries 20%, Marketing 18%, R&D 12%, Administration 10%. What central angle would represent R&D in a pie chart of this budget?

  1. 36°
  2. 43.2°
  3. 48.6°
  4. 40.8°
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 43.2°

Central angle = share × 360°. R&D = 12% of 360° = 43.2°. 36° corresponds to Administration's 10%.

Question 5

A company's annual budget of ₹45 crore is allocated as: Production 40%, Salaries 20%, Marketing 18%, R&D 12%, Administration 10%. By how much does the Marketing allocation exceed the Administration allocation?

  1. ₹3.2 crore
  2. ₹8 crore
  3. ₹4.5 crore
  4. ₹3.6 crore
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. ₹3.6 crore

Marketing − Administration = 18% − 10% = 8% of ₹45 crore = ₹3.6 crore. ₹8 crore mistakes the percentage gap for a rupee amount.

Question 6

Annual profit (₹ lakh) of a firm: 2020 – 35, 2021 – 42, 2022 – 38, 2023 – 50, 2024 – 60. In which year was the percentage increase in profit over the previous year the highest?

  1. 2023
  2. 2024
  3. 2022
  4. 2021
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 2023

Changes: 2021 +20%, 2022 −9.5%, 2023 +31.6% (38 to 50), 2024 +20%. 2024 has the largest absolute rise (10), but 2023 has the highest percentage rise.

Question 7

Annual profit (₹ lakh) of a firm: 2020 – 35, 2021 – 42, 2022 – 38, 2023 – 50, 2024 – 60. By what percentage was the 2024 profit higher than the average annual profit for 2020–2024?

  1. 30%
  2. 40%
  3. 33.3%
  4. 25%
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 33.3%

Average = (35 + 42 + 38 + 50 + 60)/5 = 225/5 = ₹45 lakh. 2024 profit of ₹60 lakh is 15/45 × 100 ≈ 33.3% higher.

Question 8

In a survey of 500 people, 300 were men and 200 were women. 180 of the men and 90 of the women preferred tea; everyone else preferred coffee. What percentage of the people who preferred coffee were women?

  1. 55%
  2. 52.2%
  3. 45%
  4. 47.8%
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 47.8%

Coffee: men 300 − 180 = 120, women 200 − 90 = 110, total 230. Women's share = 110/230 × 100 ≈ 47.8%. 55% is the share of women who prefer coffee, a different base.

Question 9

In a survey of 500 people, 300 were men and 200 were women. 180 of the men and 90 of the women preferred tea; everyone else preferred coffee. What is the ratio of people who preferred tea to those who preferred coffee?

  1. 27 : 23
  2. 23 : 27
  3. 9 : 11
  4. 3 : 2
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 27 : 23

Tea = 180 + 90 = 270; coffee = 500 − 270 = 230. Ratio 270 : 230 = 27 : 23. Reversing the order gives 23 : 27.

Question 10

The question is followed by two statements. Decide whether the data in the statements are sufficient to answer it. What is the value of the integer x? I. x² = 16 II. x > 0

  1. Statement II alone is sufficient, but Statement I alone is not
  2. Statement I alone is sufficient, but Statement II alone is not
  3. Both statements together are needed, but neither alone is sufficient
  4. Each statement alone is sufficient
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Both statements together are needed, but neither alone is sufficient

I alone gives x = 4 or −4, and II alone gives any positive integer. Together they fix x = 4, so both are needed.

Question 11

The question is followed by two statements. Decide whether the data in the statements are sufficient to answer it. What is the cost price of an article? I. It was sold for ₹550 at a profit of 10%. II. It was marked at ₹700 and sold at a discount of ₹150.

  1. Statement II alone is sufficient, but Statement I alone is not
  2. Statement I alone is sufficient, but Statement II alone is not
  3. Each statement alone is sufficient
  4. Both statements together are needed, but neither alone is sufficient
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Statement I alone is sufficient, but Statement II alone is not

I gives cost price = 550/1.1 = ₹500. II gives only the selling price (₹550) with no profit information, so it cannot give the cost price.

Question 12

The question is followed by two statements. Decide whether the data in the statements are sufficient to answer it. Is the integer n divisible by 6? I. n is divisible by 12. II. n is divisible by both 2 and 3.

  1. Statement I alone is sufficient, but Statement II alone is not
  2. Both statements together are needed, but neither alone is sufficient
  3. Each statement alone is sufficient
  4. Statement II alone is sufficient, but Statement I alone is not
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Each statement alone is sufficient

Any multiple of 12 is a multiple of 6, so I answers 'yes'. Divisibility by both 2 and 3 (coprime) also means divisibility by 6. Each statement alone suffices.

Question 13

The question is followed by two statements. Decide whether the data in the statements are sufficient to answer it. How many days will B alone take to finish a piece of work? I. A and B together finish it in 6 days. II. A alone finishes it in 10 days.

  1. Both statements together are needed, but neither alone is sufficient
  2. Statement I alone is sufficient, but Statement II alone is not
  3. Each statement alone is sufficient
  4. Statement II alone is sufficient, but Statement I alone is not
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Both statements together are needed, but neither alone is sufficient

Neither statement alone gives B's rate. Together, B's rate = 1/6 − 1/10 = 1/15, so B takes 15 days. Both are needed.

Question 14

The question is followed by two statements. Decide whether the data in the statements are sufficient to answer it. What is the speed of a boat in still water? I. The speed of the stream is 2 km/h. II. The boat goes 24 km downstream in 2 hours and returns upstream in 3 hours.

  1. Statement I alone is sufficient, but Statement II alone is not
  2. Statement II alone is sufficient, but Statement I alone is not
  3. Both statements together are needed, but neither alone is sufficient
  4. Each statement alone is sufficient
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Statement II alone is sufficient, but Statement I alone is not

II gives downstream speed 12 km/h and upstream 8 km/h, so still-water speed = (12 + 8)/2 = 10 km/h. I alone gives only the stream's speed.

Question 15

The question is followed by two statements. Decide whether the data in the statements are sufficient to answer it. Who is the tallest among P, Q and R? I. P is taller than Q but shorter than R. II. R is taller than Q.

  1. Both statements together are needed, but neither alone is sufficient
  2. Statement II alone is sufficient, but Statement I alone is not
  3. Each statement alone is sufficient
  4. Statement I alone is sufficient, but Statement II alone is not
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Statement I alone is sufficient, but Statement II alone is not

I gives the full order R > P > Q, so R is tallest. II says nothing about P, who could be taller or shorter than R.
