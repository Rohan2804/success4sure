Mathematical Skills: MAT practice questions

15 free questions with answers and explanations on Mathematical Skills, from the MAT (Management Aptitude Test) syllabus.

Last updated 23 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · How we create questions

What this section covers

Mathematical Skills in MAT covers school-level arithmetic, algebra, geometry and mensuration. Key topics include percentages, profit and loss with discounts, simple and compound interest, ratio and partnership, mixtures and alligation, averages, ages, time and work, time-speed-distance with trains and boats, number properties such as HCF and LCM, quadratic equations, permutations and combinations, and basic probability. Revise standard formulas and shortcuts, practise mental calculation, and watch for common traps such as changing bases in percentage questions and overlaps in probability.

Mathematical Skills questions

Question 1

What is the difference between compound interest and simple interest on ₹12,000 for 2 years at 10% per annum (compounded annually)?

  1. ₹120
  2. ₹132
  3. ₹240
  4. ₹100
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. ₹120

For 2 years, CI − SI = P(r/100)² = 12,000 × 0.01 = ₹120. CI = ₹2,520 and SI = ₹2,400, confirming the difference.

Question 2

A's income is 20% less than B's. By what percentage is B's income more than A's?

  1. 22.5%
  2. 20%
  3. 24%
  4. 25%
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 25%

If B = 100, A = 80. B exceeds A by 20 on a base of 80, i.e. 20/80 × 100 = 25%. The base changes, so the answer is not 20%.

Question 3

A starts a business with ₹30,000. After 4 months, B joins with ₹40,000. Out of a profit of ₹17,000 at the end of the year, what is B's share?

  1. ₹8,000
  2. ₹9,000
  3. ₹7,500
  4. ₹8,500
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. ₹8,000

Capital × time: A = 30,000 × 12 = 3,60,000; B = 40,000 × 8 = 3,20,000. Ratio 9 : 8, so B gets 8/17 × 17,000 = ₹8,000.

Question 4

In what ratio must rice costing ₹40 per kg be mixed with rice costing ₹55 per kg to get a mixture worth ₹45 per kg?

  1. 3 : 2
  2. 1 : 2
  3. 3 : 1
  4. 2 : 1
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 2 : 1

By alligation, cheaper : dearer = (55 − 45) : (45 − 40) = 10 : 5 = 2 : 1. Check: (2 × 40 + 1 × 55)/3 = 135/3 = ₹45.

Question 5

Two trains 150 m and 100 m long run on parallel tracks in opposite directions at 54 km/h and 36 km/h. How long will they take to cross each other completely?

  1. 25 seconds
  2. 9 seconds
  3. 10 seconds
  4. 12.5 seconds
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 10 seconds

Relative speed = 54 + 36 = 90 km/h = 25 m/s. Distance = 150 + 100 = 250 m. Time = 250/25 = 10 seconds. Using the difference of speeds would wrongly give 25 seconds.

Question 6

The average of 11 numbers is 50. The average of the first six is 49 and that of the last six is 52. What is the sixth number?

  1. 51
  2. 56
  3. 54
  4. 60
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 56

The sixth number is counted in both groups: 6 × 49 + 6 × 52 − 11 × 50 = 294 + 312 − 550 = 56.

Question 7

Five years ago, the ratio of the ages of A and B was 3 : 4. Five years from now, it will be 5 : 6. What is A's present age?

  1. 30 years
  2. 25 years
  3. 15 years
  4. 20 years
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 20 years

Let ages five years ago be 3k and 4k. Then (3k + 10)/(4k + 10) = 5/6, so 18k + 60 = 20k + 50 and k = 5. A is 15 + 5 = 20 years now.

Question 8

An article marked at ₹1,200 is sold at a 15% discount and the seller still makes a 20% profit. What is its cost price?

  1. ₹900
  2. ₹816
  3. ₹850
  4. ₹880
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. ₹850

Selling price = 85% of 1,200 = ₹1,020. Since this includes 20% profit, cost price = 1,020/1.2 = ₹850. Taking 20% off 1,020 wrongly gives ₹816.

Question 9

What is the smallest number that leaves a remainder of 4 when divided by 6, 8 or 12, and is exactly divisible by 11?

  1. 220
  2. 172
  3. 244
  4. 196
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 220

Such numbers have the form 24k + 4 (LCM of 6, 8, 12 is 24). Testing k = 1, 2, 3, …, the first divisible by 11 is k = 9: 24 × 9 + 4 = 220 = 11 × 20.

Question 10

Three ropes of 84 m, 126 m and 210 m are to be cut into pieces of equal length, as long as possible, with nothing left over. How many pieces will there be in all?

  1. 14
  2. 10
  3. 8
  4. 12
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 10

The greatest common length is HCF(84, 126, 210) = 42 m. Pieces = 84/42 + 126/42 + 210/42 = 2 + 3 + 5 = 10.

Question 11

A right circular cone has base radius 7 cm and slant height 25 cm. What is its volume? (Take π = 22/7)

  1. 1,320 cm³
  2. 1,232 cm³
  3. 3,696 cm³
  4. 1,540 cm³
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 1,232 cm³

Height = √(25² − 7²) = 24 cm. Volume = (1/3) × (22/7) × 49 × 24 = 1,232 cm³. Forgetting the 1/3 gives 3,696 cm³.

Question 12

If α and β are the roots of x² − 7x + 12 = 0, what is the value of α² + β²?

  1. 49
  2. 24
  3. 25
  4. 37
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 25

α + β = 7 and αβ = 12. So α² + β² = (α + β)² − 2αβ = 49 − 24 = 25. The roots 3 and 4 confirm: 9 + 16 = 25.

Question 13

In how many different ways can the letters of the word MANAGER be arranged?

  1. 5,040
  2. 1,260
  3. 720
  4. 2,520
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 2,520

MANAGER has 7 letters with A repeated twice and the rest distinct. Arrangements = 7!/2! = 5,040/2 = 2,520.

Question 14

A card is drawn at random from a well-shuffled pack of 52 cards. What is the probability that it is a king or a heart?

  1. 4/13
  2. 1/4
  3. 5/13
  4. 17/52
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 4/13

Kings: 4, hearts: 13, king of hearts counted twice: 1. Favourable = 4 + 13 − 1 = 16, so probability = 16/52 = 4/13. Not subtracting the overlap gives 17/52.

Question 15

Walking at three-fourths of his usual speed, a man reaches his office 20 minutes late. What is his usual time for the journey?

  1. 45 minutes
  2. 80 minutes
  3. 60 minutes
  4. 40 minutes
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 60 minutes

At 3/4 of the speed, time becomes 4/3 of usual. Extra time = (4/3 − 1) × t = t/3 = 20 minutes, so t = 60 minutes.
