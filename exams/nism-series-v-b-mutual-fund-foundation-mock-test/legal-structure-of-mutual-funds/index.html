Legal Structure of Mutual Funds: NISM Series V-B practice questions

8 free questions with answers and explanations on Legal Structure of Mutual Funds, from the NISM Series V-B (Mutual Fund Foundation) syllabus.

Last updated 23 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · How we create questions

What this chapter covers

This chapter explains who is who in a mutual fund. A mutual fund is set up as a trust by a sponsor; trustees protect unitholders' interests; the AMC manages the money; and service providers such as the custodian, RTA, auditor and collecting banks support operations. Revise the role of each constituent and of AMFI, the industry body that registers distributors and promotes investor awareness. Questions test simple role-matching, so learn one clear line about each entity.

Legal Structure of Mutual Funds questions

Question 1

The mutual fund trust is registered with which regulator before it can launch schemes?

  1. RBI
  2. IRDAI
  3. PFRDA
  4. SEBI
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. SEBI

Every mutual fund in India must be registered with SEBI under the SEBI (Mutual Funds) Regulations, 1996. RBI regulates banks, IRDAI insurance and PFRDA pensions.

Question 2

Which constituent of a mutual fund makes buy and sell decisions for the scheme's portfolio?

  1. The custodian of the fund
  2. The AMC, through its fund managers
  3. The registrar and transfer agent
  4. The investors, by voting each week
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. The AMC, through its fund managers

The AMC employs fund managers and research analysts who decide which securities to buy and sell in line with the scheme's objective. Investors do not vote on daily decisions.

Question 3

The statutory auditor of a mutual fund scheme is responsible for:

  1. Selecting the stocks that the scheme buys and deciding when to sell them
  2. Processing investor KYC applications
  3. Auditing the scheme's accounts and reporting on them
  4. Holding the scheme's securities
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Auditing the scheme's accounts and reporting on them

The auditor examines and reports on the scheme's accounts independently. Stock selection belongs to the AMC, KYC to KRAs and intermediaries, and custody to the custodian.

Question 4

Which entity is responsible for keeping a check on the AMC to protect unitholders?

  1. The trustees
  2. The distributors
  3. The stock exchanges
  4. The rating agencies
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. The trustees

Trustees supervise the AMC and ensure it acts in unitholders' interests and complies with regulations. Distributors sell schemes; exchanges and rating agencies have different roles.

Question 5

AMFI is best described as:

  1. A government department that regulates AMCs and issues their licences
  2. The industry association of SEBI-registered AMCs
  3. A bank that holds mutual fund money
  4. A company that manages mutual fund schemes
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. The industry association of SEBI-registered AMCs

AMFI is an industry body formed by AMCs. It is not a statutory regulator; it registers distributors (ARN), frames codes of conduct and works on investor education.

Question 6

Banks that receive investors' application money on behalf of a scheme are called:

  1. Custodian banks of the scheme
  2. Sponsor banks
  3. Collecting bankers
  4. Trustee banks
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Collecting bankers

Collecting bankers accept investment money for the scheme at their branches or through electronic channels. A custodian holds the securities, a different role.

Question 7

The custodian of a mutual fund does NOT do which of the following?

  1. Decide which stocks the scheme should buy
  2. Hold the scheme's securities safely
  3. Settle securities bought and sold
  4. Keep track of corporate actions on holdings
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Decide which stocks the scheme should buy

The custodian keeps securities in safe custody, settles trades and tracks corporate actions like dividends and bonuses. Investment decisions are made by the AMC.

Question 8

In the mutual fund structure, the sponsor is comparable to:

  1. A customer of a bank
  2. An independent auditor
  3. A stock exchange
  4. The promoter of a company
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. The promoter of a company

Just as a promoter sets up a company, the sponsor establishes the mutual fund, sets up the trust and the AMC, and must meet SEBI eligibility conditions.
