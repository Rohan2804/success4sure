Concept and Role of a Mutual Fund: NISM Series V-B practice questions

8 free questions with answers and explanations on Concept and Role of a Mutual Fund, from the NISM Series V-B (Mutual Fund Foundation) syllabus.

Last updated 19 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · How we create questions

What this chapter covers

This chapter explains what a mutual fund is in simple terms: a pool of money from many investors, managed professionally, with each investor owning units in proportion to the amount invested. Revise the main benefits (diversification, professional management, liquidity, transparency, small ticket sizes), key terms such as NAV, unit, AUM and folio, and the difference between open-ended, closed-ended, active and passive schemes. Also understand basic categories such as equity, debt, hybrid and index funds.

Concept and Role of a Mutual Fund questions

Question 1

A 'unit' of a mutual fund scheme represents:

  1. A fixed deposit placed with the AMC that earns a stated rate of interest
  2. A loan given by the investor to SEBI
  3. A proportionate share in the scheme's assets
  4. A share in the AMC company's profits
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. A proportionate share in the scheme's assets

Each unit is a proportional claim on the scheme's net assets. Unitholders do not own shares of the AMC and do not lend money to the fund.

Question 2

Assets Under Management (AUM) of a scheme refers to:

  1. The total market value of investments managed in the scheme
  2. The minimum investment amount that a new investor must put into the scheme
  3. The AMC's own share capital
  4. The profit earned by the fund manager
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. The total market value of investments managed in the scheme

AUM is the total value of money the scheme manages on behalf of investors. It changes daily with market movements, purchases and redemptions.

Question 3

Which of these is an example of a passive fund?

  1. A flexi cap fund selecting stocks freely
  2. An index fund tracking the Nifty 50
  3. A value fund picking cheap stocks
  4. A focused fund holding 25 stocks
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. An index fund tracking the Nifty 50

A passive fund simply replicates an index without stock selection by the manager. Flexi cap, value and focused funds are actively managed.

Question 4

How can a small investor with ₹500 a month gain exposure to many companies at once?

  1. By buying one share of every Nifty company
  2. By opening multiple savings accounts
  3. By buying a single small cap share
  4. By investing through a mutual fund SIP
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. By investing through a mutual fund SIP

Mutual funds pool money from many investors, so even ₹500 buys a slice of a diversified portfolio. Buying one share of every index company would cost far more.

Question 5

A debt fund primarily invests in:

  1. Bonds, government securities and money market instruments
  2. Shares of listed companies across large, mid and small caps
  3. Real estate properties
  4. Physical gold and silver
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Bonds, government securities and money market instruments

Debt funds lend money by buying fixed income securities and earn interest and price gains. Equity funds buy shares, and gold ETFs hold gold.

Question 6

Which of the following is TRUE about returns from a mutual fund?

  1. They are guaranteed by the AMC, subject to the scheme completing three years
  2. They depend on the performance of the underlying investments
  3. They are fixed by SEBI every year
  4. They are insured by DICGC
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. They depend on the performance of the underlying investments

Mutual fund returns are market-linked and are not guaranteed by anyone. DICGC insurance applies to bank deposits, not mutual fund units.

Question 7

A hybrid fund is one that invests in:

  1. Only overnight money market instruments with a maturity of one business day
  2. Only shares of one sector
  3. Only units of foreign mutual funds
  4. A combination of asset classes such as equity and debt
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. A combination of asset classes such as equity and debt

Hybrid funds mix asset classes, usually equity and debt (sometimes gold), in proportions defined by their SEBI category, aiming to balance growth and stability.

Question 8

The 'folio' in a mutual fund helps an investor to:

  1. Get a guaranteed return from the AMC on all the schemes held together in that account
  2. Avoid paying any tax on gains
  3. Hold all investments in schemes of one fund house under one account number
  4. Trade units on the stock exchange
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Hold all investments in schemes of one fund house under one account number

A folio number is like an account number with a fund house. All investments in its schemes can be grouped in one folio, which simplifies statements and servicing.
