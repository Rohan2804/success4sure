About the NISM Series V-B exam

NISM Series V-B is a foundation-level mutual fund exam covering the basics of mutual funds, simple products, investor services and the regulatory environment — with a shorter paper than V-A.

Who should take it: New-cadre mutual fund distributors who sell a limited set of simple schemes, and beginners who want an entry-level mutual fund certification.

Free NISM Series V-B sample questions

Sample 1 · Investment Landscape

Which best describes the 'liquidity' of an investment?

  1. The highest return it can earn
  2. How quickly it can be converted into cash without significant loss of value
  3. Whether the capital is guaranteed
  4. How lightly it is taxed
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. How quickly it can be converted into cash without significant loss of value

Liquidity is about ease and speed of conversion to cash at a fair value.

Sample 2 · Concept and Role of a Mutual Fund

Which is a key benefit a mutual fund offers small investors?

  1. Professional management and diversification even with small amounts
  2. Guaranteed returns
  3. Freedom from all market risk
  4. A fixed dividend every year
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Professional management and diversification even with small amounts

Pooling lets small investors access a diversified, professionally managed portfolio; returns are not guaranteed.

Sample 3 · Legal Structure of Mutual Funds

The entity that establishes a mutual fund, similar to the promoter of a company, is the

  1. Trustee
  2. Custodian
  3. Sponsor
  4. Registrar and Transfer Agent
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Sponsor

The sponsor sets up the mutual fund trust and the AMC and must meet SEBI's eligibility criteria.

Sample 4 · Mutual Fund Products for New Cadre Distributors

An index fund aims to

  1. Beat the index through active stock picking
  2. Guarantee the index return
  3. Replicate the performance of its benchmark index
  4. Invest only in debt securities
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Replicate the performance of its benchmark index

Index funds passively hold the index constituents in the same weights, aiming to match the index before costs.

Sample 5 · Scheme Related Information

Where would an investor find a particular scheme's investment objective, asset allocation pattern and risk factors?

  1. Statement of Additional Information
  2. Consolidated Account Statement
  3. AMC's annual report
  4. Scheme Information Document
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Scheme Information Document

The SID is the scheme-specific offer document describing its objective, asset allocation, risks and costs.
