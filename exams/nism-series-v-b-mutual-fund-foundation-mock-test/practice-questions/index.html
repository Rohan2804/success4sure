Free NISM Series V-B practice questions

Question 1 · Investment Landscape

₹1,00,000 is invested at 10% per year compounded annually. What will it grow to in 2 years?

  1. ₹1,20,000
  2. ₹1,10,000
  3. ₹1,22,000
  4. ₹1,21,000
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. ₹1,21,000

1,00,000 x 1.10 x 1.10 = ₹1,21,000; ₹1,20,000 would be simple interest.

Question 2 · Concept and Role of a Mutual Fund

Investors in a mutual fund scheme are best described as

  1. Lenders to the AMC
  2. Shareholders of the AMC
  3. Owners of the scheme's assets in proportion to the units they hold
  4. Creditors of the sponsor
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Owners of the scheme's assets in proportion to the units they hold

Unitholders beneficially own the scheme's assets pro rata to their units; the AMC only manages them.

Question 3 · Legal Structure of Mutual Funds

Which entity typically processes investor transactions and maintains unitholder records on behalf of the AMC?

  1. Custodian
  2. Trustee
  3. Registrar and Transfer Agent
  4. SEBI
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Registrar and Transfer Agent

RTAs handle investor servicing such as processing applications, redemptions and maintaining records.

Question 4 · Mutual Fund Products for New Cadre Distributors

Unlike an index fund, units of an ETF are

  1. Transacted with the AMC only at end-of-day NAV
  2. Bought and sold on a stock exchange during market hours
  3. Available only during the NFO
  4. Never listed on any exchange
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Bought and sold on a stock exchange during market hours

ETFs are listed and trade continuously at market prices, so investors usually need a demat and broking account.

Question 5 · Scheme Related Information

The highest risk level shown on SEBI's riskometer is

  1. High
  2. Extreme
  3. Aggressive
  4. Very High
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Very High

The riskometer has six levels: Low, Low to Moderate, Moderate, Moderately High, High and Very High.

Question 6 · Fund Distribution and Channel Management

To sell mutual fund schemes and earn commission, an individual must

  1. Pass the relevant NISM certification and obtain an ARN from AMFI
  2. Register with RBI as a dealer
  3. Only have a bank account and PAN
  4. Register as a SEBI investment adviser
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Pass the relevant NISM certification and obtain an ARN from AMFI

Distribution requires NISM certification followed by AMFI registration (ARN).

Question 7 · Performance of Mutual Funds

A growth option NAV rose from ₹20 to ₹23 in one year. What was the return?

  1. 3%
  2. 13%
  3. 23%
  4. 15%
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 15%

Return = (23 - 20) / 20 = 15%.

Question 8 · Taxation

For income tax purposes, an equity-oriented fund is one that invests

  1. At least 25% in equity shares
  2. Only in large cap shares
  3. At least 50% in foreign equities
  4. At least 65% in equity shares of domestic listed companies
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. At least 65% in equity shares of domestic listed companies

Funds with at least 65% in domestic listed equity qualify as equity-oriented and get the equity capital gains treatment.

Question 9 · Investor Services

Which of the following is mandatory for KYC of a resident individual making a regular (non-micro) mutual fund investment?

  1. Passport
  2. PAN
  3. Income tax return
  4. Credit card
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. PAN

PAN is mandatory for mutual fund KYC, apart from limited exemptions such as micro investments.

Question 10 · Legal and Regulatory Environment

Mutual funds in India are governed by

  1. Banking Regulation Act, 1949
  2. SEBI (Portfolio Managers) Regulations, 2020
  3. Insurance Act, 1938
  4. SEBI (Mutual Funds) Regulations, 1996
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. SEBI (Mutual Funds) Regulations, 1996

The SEBI (Mutual Funds) Regulations, 1996 lay down the rules for setting up and operating mutual funds.

Question 11 · Investment Landscape

The risk that rising prices erode the purchasing power of investment returns is called

  1. Credit risk
  2. Inflation risk
  3. Liquidity risk
  4. Reinvestment risk
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Inflation risk

Inflation risk is the loss of real value when returns fail to keep pace with price rises.

Question 12 · Concept and Role of a Mutual Fund

How can an investor exit a closed-ended scheme before it matures?

  1. By redeeming with the AMC on any business day
  2. By selling the units on the stock exchange where they are listed
  3. By transferring the units to the trustee
  4. No exit is ever possible
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. By selling the units on the stock exchange where they are listed

Closed-ended schemes must be listed so investors can sell units on the exchange before maturity.

Question 13 · Legal Structure of Mutual Funds

Which constituent manages a scheme's portfolio on a day-to-day basis?

  1. Asset Management Company
  2. Trustee
  3. Custodian
  4. Sponsor
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Asset Management Company

The AMC, through its fund managers, makes investment decisions within the scheme's mandate.

Question 14 · Mutual Fund Products for New Cadre Distributors

Why is a diversified equity fund usually considered less risky than a sectoral fund?

  1. It spreads investments across sectors, so a downturn in one sector has limited impact
  2. It guarantees capital protection
  3. It invests mainly in government bonds
  4. It never invests in mid or small caps
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. It spreads investments across sectors, so a downturn in one sector has limited impact

Sectoral funds concentrate in one sector and are hit hard if that sector suffers; diversification lowers this concentration risk.

Question 15 · Scheme Related Information

A mutual fund's Statement of Additional Information (SAI) mainly contains

  1. The daily NAV history of each scheme
  2. An investor's transaction history
  3. Statutory information about the fund house that is common to all its schemes
  4. The portfolio of a single scheme
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Statutory information about the fund house that is common to all its schemes

The SAI, read with the SID, gives details about the sponsor, AMC, trustees and other common information.
