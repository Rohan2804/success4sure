Scheme Related Information: NISM Series V-B practice questions

8 free questions with answers and explanations on Scheme Related Information, from the NISM Series V-B (Mutual Fund Foundation) syllabus.

Last updated 26 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · How we create questions

What this chapter covers

This chapter explains the documents investors should read before investing: the Scheme Information Document (scheme-specific details), the Statement of Additional Information (fund-house-level details), and the Key Information Memorandum (summary that comes with the application form). It also covers the riskometer, factsheets and periodic portfolio disclosures. Revise which document contains what, what the riskometer conveys, and how investors can use factsheets to track holdings, expenses and performance.

Scheme Related Information questions

Question 1

Where would an investor find the exit load applicable to a particular scheme?

  1. In the AMC's annual report only
  2. In the Scheme Information Document and KIM
  3. In the investor's PAN card details
  4. In the stock exchange's daily bulletin
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. In the Scheme Information Document and KIM

Loads are terms of the offer, so they are disclosed in the SID and summarised in the KIM. Factsheets also usually show the current exit load.

Question 2

The riskometer of a scheme is meant to help investors:

  1. Find out the exact return that the scheme will deliver over the next one year
  2. Compare tax rates on different schemes
  3. See the distributor's commission
  4. Understand the level of risk in the scheme at a glance
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Understand the level of risk in the scheme at a glance

The riskometer shows risk on a six-level scale from Low to Very High. It does not predict returns or show costs.

Question 3

A monthly factsheet issued by an AMC typically shows:

  1. Holdings, AUM, TER and performance
  2. The personal details of each investor
  3. The names of investors who redeemed
  4. Guaranteed returns for the next year
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Holdings, AUM, TER and performance

Factsheets summarise key scheme data such as top holdings, sector mix, AUM, TER, fund manager and performance versus benchmark, helping investors monitor their schemes.

Question 4

Which document must be given along with every mutual fund application form?

  1. Statement of Additional Information
  2. Annual report of the AMC
  3. Key Information Memorandum
  4. Trust deed of the mutual fund
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Key Information Memorandum

The KIM, a short summary of the SID, must accompany every application form so that investors see the key features before investing.

Question 5

An investor wants information about the mutual fund's sponsor, trustees and AMC directors. The most relevant document is the:

  1. Key Information Memorandum
  2. Account statement
  3. Riskometer
  4. Statement of Additional Information
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Statement of Additional Information

The SAI contains statutory information common to all schemes of the fund house, such as details of the sponsor, AMC, trustees and their directors.

Question 6

If a scheme's riskometer moves from 'High' to 'Very High', this means:

  1. The assessed risk level of the scheme's portfolio has increased
  2. The scheme's returns are now guaranteed to rise in proportion to the higher level of risk
  3. The scheme will now be closed for redemptions
  4. The scheme's exit load has been removed
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. The assessed risk level of the scheme's portfolio has increased

Riskometers are reviewed monthly based on the portfolio. A move to Very High shows the portfolio has become riskier, which investors should note.

Question 7

The investment objective stated in an SID tells the investor:

  1. The exact return the scheme will earn
  2. The tax rate on redemption proceeds
  3. What the scheme aims to achieve and broadly how
  4. The NAV on the date of allotment
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. What the scheme aims to achieve and broadly how

The objective describes the scheme's goal, such as long-term capital growth by investing in large cap equity. It does not promise any return.

Question 8

Periodic disclosure of the scheme's portfolio is useful because it lets investors:

  1. Avoid paying any expense ratio
  2. Check whether the fund follows its stated mandate
  3. Trade the underlying shares directly
  4. Change the scheme's investment objective
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Check whether the fund follows its stated mandate

By seeing actual holdings, investors can confirm that the scheme invests as promised and judge concentration and credit quality.
