Taxation: NISM Series V-B practice questions

8 free questions with answers and explanations on Taxation, from the NISM Series V-B (Mutual Fund Foundation) syllabus.

Last updated 16 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · How we create questions

What this chapter covers

This chapter covers the basic tax rules that new investors and distributors should know. Revise how equity-oriented schemes are defined (at least 65% in domestic equity), the 12-month holding period for long-term equity gains, current tax rates on equity gains, taxation of IDCW at slab rates, the ELSS deduction under the old regime, and that switches between schemes are treated as redemptions. Also know that STT applies on redemption of equity-oriented units. Because rates change in Budgets, check the latest figures before the exam.

Taxation questions

Question 1

A scheme qualifies as 'equity-oriented' for tax purposes if it invests at least what share of its assets in domestic equity shares?

  1. 65%
  2. 50%
  3. 80%
  4. 35%
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 65%

An equity-oriented fund must invest at least 65% in equity shares of domestic listed companies. Such schemes get the concessional tax treatment applicable to equity.

Question 2

An investor sells equity fund units after 18 months at a gain. The gain is treated as:

  1. Short-term capital gain
  2. Income from salary
  3. Long-term capital gain
  4. Business income
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Long-term capital gain

For equity-oriented funds, units held for more than 12 months are long-term capital assets. Eighteen months exceeds this, so the gain is long-term.

Question 3

IDCW received by a resident investor from a mutual fund is taxed:

  1. At a flat 5% for all investors
  2. Only if it exceeds ₹10 lakh a year
  3. Never, as it is exempt income
  4. At the investor's income tax slab rate
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. At the investor's income tax slab rate

IDCW is added to the investor's income and taxed at the applicable slab rate. The fund may also deduct TDS above a threshold.

Question 4

An investor switches from an equity fund to a debt fund of the same AMC. For tax purposes, the switch is:

  1. A tax-free transfer within the same AMC
  2. Treated as a redemption, so capital gains tax may apply
  3. Taxed only when the debt fund is sold
  4. Treated as a fresh gift to the AMC
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Treated as a redemption, so capital gains tax may apply

A switch is a sale of units in one scheme and a purchase in another. Any gain on the units switched out is taxable, and exit load may also apply.

Question 5

Securities Transaction Tax on mutual funds is charged on:

  1. Purchase of units of debt schemes
  2. Redemption of units of liquid funds
  3. Redemption of units of equity-oriented schemes
  4. IDCW paid by all schemes
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Redemption of units of equity-oriented schemes

STT applies to the sale (redemption) of units of equity-oriented funds, at a very small rate collected by the fund. Debt fund transactions do not attract STT.

Question 6

Investment in ELSS is eligible for a tax deduction only for taxpayers who:

  1. Opt for the new tax regime
  2. Are non-residents
  3. Earn only capital gains
  4. Opt for the old tax regime
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Opt for the old tax regime

The new tax regime does not allow most deductions, including the ELSS deduction. Taxpayers must choose the old regime to claim this benefit.

Question 7

A long-term capital loss on mutual fund units can be set off against:

  1. Any salary income
  2. Only long-term capital gains
  3. Interest from fixed deposits
  4. Short-term gains and salary
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Only long-term capital gains

Long-term capital losses can only be set off against long-term capital gains, in the same year or the next eight years. They cannot reduce salary or interest income.

Question 8

Who deducts TDS on IDCW paid to a resident investor, where applicable?

  1. The mutual fund, before paying the IDCW
  2. The investor's employer
  3. The investor's bank, at the time of credit
  4. The distributor, from its commission
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. The mutual fund, before paying the IDCW

The mutual fund deducts TDS on IDCW above the prescribed threshold before paying it out, and the investor can claim credit for it while filing the return.
