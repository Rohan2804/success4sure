Fund Distribution and Channel Management: NISM Series V-B practice questions

8 free questions with answers and explanations on Fund Distribution and Channel Management, from the NISM Series V-B (Mutual Fund Foundation) syllabus.

Last updated 20 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · How we create questions

What this chapter covers

This chapter covers how mutual funds reach investors and what a new cadre distributor must do. Revise registration requirements (NISM certification and ARN from AMFI), EUIN, the difference between direct and regular plans, how distributors earn trail commission, KYC duties, and ethical selling practices including suitability and avoiding mis-selling. Also know the main channels such as individual distributors, banks, online platforms and stock exchange platforms, and the basics of the AMFI code of conduct.

Fund Distribution and Channel Management questions

Question 1

A distributor's EUIN is unique to:

  1. The scheme in which the investor invests
  2. The investor's bank account
  3. The individual employee who serves the investor
  4. The stock exchange used for transactions
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. The individual employee who serves the investor

EUIN identifies the specific employee or relationship manager of a distributor who served the investor. It helps track advice quality and prevent mis-selling.

Question 2

A new cadre distributor's main source of income from AMCs is:

  1. Trail commission based on the investor's assets
  2. A fixed salary paid by AMFI
  3. A share of the investor's capital gains
  4. A fee collected from SEBI
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Trail commission based on the investor's assets

Distributors earn trail commission, a percentage of the assets their clients hold in regular plans, paid periodically while the investment remains.

Question 3

An investor in the regular plan pays a slightly higher expense ratio than in the direct plan because:

  1. The regular plan holds riskier securities
  2. The regular plan includes distribution commission
  3. The direct plan is subsidised by SEBI
  4. The direct plan does not pay any custodian fees
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. The regular plan includes distribution commission

Both plans hold the same portfolio. The regular plan's TER includes trail commission to the distributor, so its NAV grows slightly slower.

Question 4

Before recommending a scheme, a distributor should first:

  1. Recommend the scheme that paid the highest return last month
  2. Recommend whatever the AMC is promoting this quarter
  3. Ask the investor to sign a blank form
  4. Understand the investor's goals, horizon and risk appetite
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Understand the investor's goals, horizon and risk appetite

Suitable advice starts with knowing the investor. Recommending based on recent returns or AMC campaigns, or taking blank signed forms, can lead to mis-selling.

Question 5

Which of these is an unethical practice for a distributor?

  1. Encouraging frequent switching to earn more commission
  2. Explaining the risks of the scheme clearly
  3. Giving the investor the KIM with the form
  4. Keeping records of the investor's risk profile
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Encouraging frequent switching to earn more commission

Churning investors between schemes for higher commission harms investors and violates the AMFI code of conduct. The other practices are good conduct.

Question 6

An investor can transact in mutual funds without any distributor by:

  1. Calling SEBI's helpline
  2. Buying the direct plan from the AMC
  3. Writing to the stock exchange
  4. Asking the custodian bank
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Buying the direct plan from the AMC

Direct plans can be bought from the AMC's own website, office or app, or through RTA and other direct channels, without a distributor and without commission.

Question 7

When a bank sells mutual fund schemes to its customers, it acts as a:

  1. Trustee of the mutual fund
  2. Custodian of the investor's units
  3. Regulator of the AMC
  4. Mutual fund distributor holding an ARN
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Mutual fund distributor holding an ARN

Banks distribute mutual funds as corporate distributors with an ARN, and their staff who sell must be NISM-certified with an EUIN.

Question 8

Which detail must a distributor's application form submission carry so that commission is paid correctly?

  1. The investor's mobile network provider
  2. The fund manager's name
  3. The distributor's ARN code
  4. The investor's employer's PAN
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. The distributor's ARN code

The ARN code on the form links the transaction to the distributor, so the AMC knows whom to pay trail commission. Without it the investment goes into the direct plan.
