Sample 1 · Securities Markets and Their Role

The main economic role of securities markets is to

  1. Fix interest rates for bank deposits
  2. Print currency
  3. Guarantee returns to investors
  4. Channel household savings into productive investment by companies and governments
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Channel household savings into productive investment by companies and governments

Securities markets help issuers raise capital from savers and give investors liquidity, supporting capital formation.

Sample 2 · Securities, Asset Allocation and Investing

Equity shareholders of a company have

  1. A fixed claim to interest payments
  2. A residual claim on profits and assets after all other claims are met
  3. Priority over lenders in liquidation
  4. A guaranteed return of capital
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. A residual claim on profits and assets after all other claims are met

Equity holders are owners and are paid last, which is why equity carries higher risk and higher potential reward.

Sample 3 · Primary Markets

An Initial Public Offer (IPO) is

  1. Any sale of shares on the stock exchange
  2. The first offer of shares to the public by an unlisted company
  3. An offer of shares only to existing shareholders
  4. A buyback of shares by a listed company
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. The first offer of shares to the public by an unlisted company

In an IPO, a company offers shares to the public for the first time and then gets listed.

Sample 4 · Secondary Markets

What is the standard settlement cycle for equity trades in the Indian cash market?

  1. T+1
  2. T+2
  3. T+3
  4. T+5
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. T+1

Indian exchanges moved fully to T+1 rolling settlement in January 2023.

Sample 5 · Mutual Funds

A scheme has net assets of ₹50 crore and 4 crore units outstanding. What is its NAV?

  1. ₹12.50
  2. ₹8.00
  3. ₹10.00
  4. ₹13.50
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. ₹12.50

NAV = 50 / 4 = ₹12.50.

NISM Series XII mock test FAQs

How many questions are there in the NISM Series XII exam?

The NISM Series XII paper has 100 multiple-choice questions for 100 marks, to be completed in 120 minutes. This is the indicative pattern — always confirm it in the latest official notification.

Is there negative marking in NISM Series XII?

No. There is no negative marking, so attempt every question. Our mock tests apply the same rule.

What is the pass mark for NISM Series XII?

You need 60% to pass. Our mock tests show pass or fail against the same mark.

Are the NISM Series XII mock tests free?

Yes. All 30 full-length NISM Series XII mock tests are free, with no payment or plan. You can start without signing up; log in with Google if you want your results saved across devices. You can retake any test as often as you like.

Is NISM Series XII worth it for students?

It is a recognised entry-level certification that shows employers you understand how Indian securities markets work. Students, fresh graduates and new joiners in broking, banking and wealth management often take it before moving on to role-specific NISM certifications. It is voluntary rather than mandatory.

How many days are needed to prepare for NISM Series XII?

Most beginners prepare in one to two weeks. Read the workbook once, noting the roles of exchanges, depositories, intermediaries and regulators. Then take timed mock tests and revise the chapters on derivatives and mutual funds, which beginners commonly find unfamiliar.

Which NISM exam should I take after Series XII?

That depends on the role you want. Mutual fund distribution needs Series V-A, F&O dealing needs Series VIII, broking operations need Series VII, and depository work needs Series VI. Series XII gives a useful base for all of them. Check nism.ac.in for the full list of certifications.
