About the NISM Series XII exam

NISM Series XII is a foundation exam on the Indian securities markets — primary and secondary markets, types of securities, mutual funds, derivatives and the investor's role.

Who should take it: Students, fresh graduates and new joiners in financial services who want a recognised entry-level securities market certification.

Sample 1 · Securities Markets and Their Role

The main economic role of securities markets is to

  1. Fix interest rates for bank deposits
  2. Print currency
  3. Guarantee returns to investors
  4. Channel household savings into productive investment by companies and governments
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Channel household savings into productive investment by companies and governments

Securities markets help issuers raise capital from savers and give investors liquidity, supporting capital formation.

Sample 2 · Securities, Asset Allocation and Investing

Equity shareholders of a company have

  1. A fixed claim to interest payments
  2. A residual claim on profits and assets after all other claims are met
  3. Priority over lenders in liquidation
  4. A guaranteed return of capital
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. A residual claim on profits and assets after all other claims are met

Equity holders are owners and are paid last, which is why equity carries higher risk and higher potential reward.

Sample 3 · Primary Markets

An Initial Public Offer (IPO) is

  1. Any sale of shares on the stock exchange
  2. The first offer of shares to the public by an unlisted company
  3. An offer of shares only to existing shareholders
  4. A buyback of shares by a listed company
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. The first offer of shares to the public by an unlisted company

In an IPO, a company offers shares to the public for the first time and then gets listed.

Sample 4 · Secondary Markets

What is the standard settlement cycle for equity trades in the Indian cash market?

  1. T+1
  2. T+2
  3. T+3
  4. T+5
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. T+1

Indian exchanges moved fully to T+1 rolling settlement in January 2023.

Sample 5 · Mutual Funds

A scheme has net assets of ₹50 crore and 4 crore units outstanding. What is its NAV?

  1. ₹12.50
  2. ₹8.00
  3. ₹10.00
  4. ₹13.50
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. ₹12.50

NAV = 50 / 4 = ₹12.50.
