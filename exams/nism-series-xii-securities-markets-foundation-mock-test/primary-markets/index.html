Primary Markets: NISM Series XII practice questions

8 free questions with answers and explanations on Primary Markets, from the NISM Series XII (Securities Markets Foundation) syllabus.

Last updated 17 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · How we create questions

What this chapter covers

This chapter covers how companies raise money by issuing securities to investors: public issues (IPOs and FPOs), rights issues, preferential allotments, QIPs, bonus issues and private placements. It explains book building and fixed price issues, price bands, investor categories, ASBA and UPI, allotment, listing and the roles of intermediaries such as lead managers and registrars. When revising, learn the steps of a public issue and who may bid in each category.

Primary Markets questions

Question 1

In a fixed price issue, investors know:

  1. Only a price band before applying
  2. The issue price before applying
  3. The price only after listing
  4. Nothing about the price until allotment
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. The issue price before applying

In a fixed price issue the price is stated in the offer document before bidding. In a book-built issue only a price band is known and the final price is set after bidding.

Question 2

A bonus issue means that a company:

  1. Sells new shares to the public at a discount
  2. Buys back shares from shareholders
  3. Gives free shares to existing holders
  4. Issues debentures to banks
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Gives free shares to existing holders

A bonus issue capitalises reserves into free shares for existing shareholders. It raises no fresh money and does not change each holder's proportionate stake.

Question 3

Which investors can apply in the anchor investor portion of an IPO?

  1. Qualified institutional buyers
  2. Retail individual investors
  3. Employees of the issuer only
  4. Non-resident individuals only
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Qualified institutional buyers

Anchor investors are QIBs who apply for a large minimum amount a day before the issue opens, giving price confidence. Retail investors bid in their own category.

Question 4

Who decides the final issue price in a book-built issue?

  1. SEBI
  2. The stock exchange
  3. The retail investors' association
  4. The issuer with the lead managers
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. The issuer with the lead managers

After bids close, the issuer and book running lead managers determine the final price within the band based on demand. SEBI does not set prices.

Question 5

A company allots shares to a select group of investors, such as a strategic partner, on a non-public basis. This is a:

  1. Rights issue
  2. Bonus issue
  3. Initial public offer
  4. Preferential allotment
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Preferential allotment

A preferential allotment issues shares to specific persons, subject to ICDR pricing and lock-in rules. A rights issue goes to all existing holders pro rata.

Question 6

What does the term 'oversubscribed' mean for an issue?

  1. Bids exceed the shares on offer
  2. Fewer bids than shares on offer
  3. The issue was cancelled by SEBI
  4. Shares listed below the issue price
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Bids exceed the shares on offer

An issue is oversubscribed when demand exceeds supply; allotment is then made as per the basis of allotment, with retail investors often receiving the minimum lot by lottery.

Question 7

Before investing in an IPO, an investor should read the:

  1. Company's advertisements only
  2. Offer document's risk factors
  3. Social media posts on the IPO
  4. Grey market premium updates
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Offer document's risk factors

The offer document gives detailed information including risk factors, financials and objects of the issue. Advertisements and social media are not a substitute.

Question 8

Which intermediary handles applications, allotment and refunds in a public issue?

  1. Credit rating agency
  2. Custodian
  3. Registrar to the issue
  4. Stock broker
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Registrar to the issue

The registrar processes applications, finalises allotment with the exchange and coordinates unblocking or refunds. Rating agencies, custodians and brokers have other roles.
