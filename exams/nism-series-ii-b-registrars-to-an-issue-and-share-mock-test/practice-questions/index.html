Free NISM Series II-B practice questions

Question 1 · Introduction to Securities

The secondary market primarily provides investors with:

  1. Guaranteed returns
  2. Tax-free income
  3. Liquidity, by allowing them to sell existing securities to other investors
  4. Fresh capital for issuers
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Liquidity, by allowing them to sell existing securities to other investors

Trading among investors in the secondary market lets holders exit their investments.

Question 2 · Characteristics of Equity Shares

Dividend on equity shares is paid:

  1. Only on liquidation
  2. As a fixed percentage of face value every year
  3. At the discretion of the company out of its profits
  4. Even when the company has no profits or reserves
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. At the discretion of the company out of its profits

Equity dividend is not a contractual obligation; the board recommends it based on profits.

Question 3 · Characteristics of Debt Securities

A bond whose coupon is reset periodically with reference to a benchmark rate is called a:

  1. Perpetual bond
  2. Zero-coupon bond
  3. Fixed rate bond
  4. Floating rate bond
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Floating rate bond

Floating rate bonds pay a coupon linked to a benchmark, so it changes at each reset.

Question 4 · Characteristics of Other Securities

Which of the following is a derivative instrument?

  1. Commercial paper
  2. Equity share
  3. Index futures contract
  4. Treasury bill
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Index futures contract

A futures contract derives its value from an underlying, such as an index.

Question 5 · SEBI – Role and Regulations

Which of the following is NOT a function of SEBI?

  1. Setting the repo rate
  2. Registering and regulating market intermediaries
  3. Prohibiting insider trading
  4. Promoting investor education
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Setting the repo rate

The repo rate is set by the RBI's Monetary Policy Committee; the other three are SEBI functions.

Question 6 · Basics of Registrars and Transfer Agents

In the mutual fund industry, an RTA is appointed by:

  1. AMFI
  2. The custodian
  3. The Asset Management Company, on behalf of the mutual fund
  4. The investor
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. The Asset Management Company, on behalf of the mutual fund

The AMC appoints the RTA to maintain unitholder records and process transactions for the fund's schemes.

Question 7 · Registrar and Transfer Agents Regulations

Under the SEBI (Registrars to an Issue and Share Transfer Agents) Regulations, 1993, a Category I registrant may act as:

  1. Only a share transfer agent
  2. A merchant banker and registrar
  3. Only a registrar to an issue
  4. Both registrar to an issue and share transfer agent
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Both registrar to an issue and share transfer agent

Category I covers both activities, while Category II permits only one of the two.

Question 8 · Basics of Mutual Funds

A key benefit of investing through a mutual fund is:

  1. Freedom from all market risk
  2. Direct voting rights in every portfolio company
  3. Diversification and professional management even with a small investment
  4. Guaranteed returns on all schemes
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Diversification and professional management even with a small investment

Pooling lets small investors get a diversified, professionally managed portfolio; returns are not guaranteed.

Question 9 · Mutual Fund Structure and Constituents

In India, a mutual fund is constituted as a:

  1. Trust
  2. Co-operative society
  3. Private limited company
  4. Partnership firm
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Trust

Mutual funds are set up as trusts under the Indian Trusts Act, 1882, with the sponsor as settlor.

Question 10 · Mutual Fund Products

Which scheme type is eligible for an income tax deduction under the old tax regime and has a statutory lock-in of three years?

  1. Liquid fund
  2. Equity Linked Savings Scheme
  3. Overnight fund
  4. Arbitrage fund
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Equity Linked Savings Scheme

ELSS is the tax-saving equity scheme category; it carries a three-year lock-in and qualifies for deduction only under the old tax regime.

Question 11 · Operational Concepts of Mutual Funds

For a purchase in a liquid fund, the cut-off time to get the same day's closing NAV (with funds realised in time) is:

  1. 10:00 a.m.
  2. 5:00 p.m.
  3. 1:30 p.m.
  4. 3:00 p.m.
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 1:30 p.m.

Liquid and overnight funds have a 1:30 p.m. cut-off for purchases, compared with 3:00 p.m. for most other schemes.

Question 12 · Investors in Mutual Funds

For a mutual fund folio held in the name of a minor, payment for investments must come from:

  1. The distributor's account
  2. Any third party's bank account
  3. Only the guardian's individual account
  4. A bank account of the minor or a joint account of the minor with the guardian
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. A bank account of the minor or a joint account of the minor with the guardian

SEBI requires investments in a minor's folio to be paid from the minor's own account or the minor's joint account with the guardian.

Question 13 · Banking Operations in Mutual Funds

Mutual funds generally do NOT accept which kind of payment for subscriptions?

  1. Payments through a registered bank mandate for SIPs
  2. Cheques from the investor's own account
  3. NEFT from the first holder's bank account
  4. Third-party payments, except in permitted cases
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Third-party payments, except in permitted cases

To curb money laundering, SEBI and AMFI norms bar third-party payments except for specified exceptions.

Question 14 · Financial Transactions

An investor invests ₹49,997.50 (after stamp duty) in a scheme at an NAV of ₹25. How many units are allotted?

  1. 1,999.900
  2. 1,999.500
  3. 2,000.000
  4. 1,250.000
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 1,999.900

Units = 49,997.50 ÷ 25 = 1,999.900.

Question 15 · Non-Financial Transactions

Which of the following is a non-financial transaction in a mutual fund folio?

  1. Switch-out
  2. Redemption
  3. SIP instalment
  4. Change of bank mandate
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Change of bank mandate

Changes to bank details, address or nominee alter records without moving money.
