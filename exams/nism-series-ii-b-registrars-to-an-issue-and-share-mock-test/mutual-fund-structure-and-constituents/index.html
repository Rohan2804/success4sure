Mutual Fund Structure and Constituents: NISM Series II-B practice questions

8 free questions with answers and explanations on Mutual Fund Structure and Constituents, from the NISM Series II-B (Registrars to an Issue and Share Transfer Agents – Mutual Fund) syllabus.

Last updated 11 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · How we create questions

What this chapter covers

This chapter explains the legal structure of a mutual fund in India and the roles of its constituents. A mutual fund is set up as a trust by a sponsor, with trustees overseeing it and an asset management company managing the schemes. Learners should know the documents linking them, such as the trust deed and the investment management agreement, key SEBI requirements on AMC net worth and independent directors and trustees, and the roles of service providers such as custodians, RTAs, auditors and distributors. Revise who is responsible for what, and newer measures aligning AMC employees' interests with unitholders.

Mutual Fund Structure and Constituents questions

Question 1

The structure of a mutual fund in India is commonly described as three-tier. The three tiers are:

  1. Sponsor, trustees and asset management company
  2. SEBI, AMFI and the asset management company
  3. Custodian, RTA and distributor
  4. Sponsor, stock exchange and depository
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Sponsor, trustees and asset management company

The sponsor establishes the mutual fund trust, the trustees hold its property for the benefit of unitholders and supervise, and the AMC manages the schemes. SEBI and AMFI are regulator and industry body, and custodians and RTAs are service providers.

Question 2

The trust deed of a mutual fund is executed:

  1. By the AMC in favour of SEBI
  2. By the custodian in favour of the AMC
  3. By unitholders in favour of the sponsor
  4. By the sponsor in favour of the trustees
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. By the sponsor in favour of the trustees

The sponsor creates the mutual fund trust by executing a trust deed in favour of the trustees, who then hold the trust property for unitholders. The trust deed is registered and must be approved under SEBI's regulations.

Question 3

The agreement under which the trustees appoint the AMC to manage the mutual fund's schemes is called the:

  1. Custodial services agreement
  2. Investment management agreement
  3. Memorandum of association
  4. Distribution agreement
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Investment management agreement

Trustees enter into an investment management agreement with the AMC, which sets out the AMC's duties in managing the schemes. Custodial and distribution agreements cover other service providers, and the memorandum relates to company incorporation.

Question 4

Under SEBI's regulations, what is the minimum net worth an AMC must maintain?

  1. ₹10 crore
  2. ₹25 crore
  3. ₹50 crore
  4. ₹100 crore
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. ₹50 crore

SEBI requires an AMC to have and maintain a net worth of at least ₹50 crore. This was raised from the earlier ₹10 crore requirement to ensure that AMCs have adequate financial strength to run their business.

Question 5

What proportion of the directors on an AMC's board must be independent, as per SEBI's requirements?

  1. At least one-third
  2. At least two-thirds
  3. At least 25%
  4. At least 50%
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. At least 50%

SEBI requires that at least half of the AMC's board consist of independent directors, that is, persons not associated with the sponsor. For the board of the trustee company, the requirement is stricter at two-thirds independent trustees.

Question 6

Which of these is a function of the AMC rather than the trustees?

  1. Making day-to-day investment decisions for the schemes
  2. Ensuring that the AMC acts in unitholders' interests
  3. Reviewing whether the AMC complies with SEBI regulations and the trust deed
  4. Approving the investment management agreement
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Making day-to-day investment decisions for the schemes

The AMC manages money, so investment decisions for the schemes are its job. The trustees play a supervisory role: they appoint the AMC, approve key agreements and check that the AMC complies with regulations and protects unitholders.

Question 7

SEBI requires a portion of the salary of key employees of an AMC to be paid in units of the schemes they manage or oversee. The main aim is to:

  1. Increase the assets under management of newly launched schemes of the AMC
  2. Align the interests of these employees with those of unitholders
  3. Reduce the tax liability of the AMC's employees
  4. Replace the sponsor's contribution to the AMC
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Align the interests of these employees with those of unitholders

Under the 'skin in the game' rules, a part of the compensation of designated employees is paid in scheme units with a lock-in. They gain or lose along with investors, encouraging responsible decisions. It is not aimed at raising AUM or saving tax.

Question 8

A mutual fund wants an independent entity to verify that its scheme accounts are fairly stated. It must appoint:

  1. The same auditor as the sponsor company
  2. The custodian to certify the accounts
  3. An auditor independent of the auditor of the AMC
  4. The RTA's internal audit team
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. An auditor independent of the auditor of the AMC

Scheme accounts must be audited by an independent auditor, who cannot be the auditor of the AMC. This separation strengthens objectivity. Custodians and RTAs are service providers and cannot act as statutory auditors of the schemes.
