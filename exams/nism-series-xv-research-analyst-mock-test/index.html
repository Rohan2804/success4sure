Sample 1 · Introduction to Research Analyst Profession

How does a buy-side analyst differ from a sell-side analyst?

  1. A buy-side analyst only recommends buying, never selling
  2. A buy-side analyst researches for the internal use of an investing institution such as an AMC, while a sell-side analyst's research is distributed to clients of a broker
  3. A sell-side analyst works only for insurance companies
  4. There is no difference; the terms are interchangeable
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. A buy-side analyst researches for the internal use of an investing institution such as an AMC, while a sell-side analyst's research is distributed to clients of a broker

Buy-side research supports the analyst's own institution's investment decisions; sell-side research is published for brokerage clients.

Sample 2 · Introduction to Securities Market

What role does a clearing corporation play in exchange-traded markets?

  1. It acts as the central counterparty, guaranteeing settlement of trades
  2. It lists companies and approves IPO prices
  3. It issues credit ratings for debt securities
  4. It decides the dividends paid by listed companies
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. It acts as the central counterparty, guaranteeing settlement of trades

By becoming buyer to every seller and seller to every buyer, the clearing corporation removes counterparty risk from exchange trades.

Sample 3 · Terminology in Equity and Debt Markets

A company splits each share of face value ₹10 into shares of face value ₹2. An investor holding 100 shares will now hold:

  1. 100 shares, with the face value unchanged
  2. 200 shares, with the price halving
  3. 500 shares, with the market price adjusting to about one-fifth
  4. 500 shares, with the price unchanged
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 500 shares, with the market price adjusting to about one-fifth

Splitting ₹10 into ₹2 creates five shares for each one held; the total value is unchanged, so the price falls to about one-fifth.

Sample 4 · Fundamentals of Research

Which statement correctly contrasts fundamental and technical analysis?

  1. Technical analysis relies on balance sheets, while fundamental analysis uses only charts
  2. Both rely only on management interviews
  3. Fundamental analysis estimates intrinsic value from economic, industry and company factors, while technical analysis studies price and volume patterns
  4. Fundamental analysis is used only for bonds
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Fundamental analysis estimates intrinsic value from economic, industry and company factors, while technical analysis studies price and volume patterns

Fundamental analysts ask what a security is worth; technical analysts study market data to judge likely price movement.

Sample 5 · Economic Analysis

In an economy, private consumption is ₹150 lakh crore, investment ₹50 lakh crore, government spending ₹40 lakh crore, exports ₹30 lakh crore and imports ₹35 lakh crore. What is GDP by the expenditure method?

  1. ₹305 lakh crore
  2. ₹235 lakh crore
  3. ₹270 lakh crore
  4. ₹240 lakh crore
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. ₹235 lakh crore

GDP = C + I + G + (X − M) = 150 + 50 + 40 + (30 − 35) = ₹235 lakh crore.

NISM Series XV mock test FAQs

How many questions are there in the NISM Series XV exam?

The NISM Series XV paper has 100 multiple-choice questions for 100 marks, to be completed in 180 minutes. This is the indicative pattern — always confirm it in the latest official notification.

Is NISM Series XV mandatory for research analysts?

Yes. SEBI requires individuals registering as research analysts, and persons associated with research services, to hold the NISM Series XV certificate along with meeting other qualification criteria. Confirm current requirements on sebi.gov.in and nism.ac.in before you apply.

Which topics in NISM Series XV need the most practice?

Financial statement analysis, ratio calculations and valuation methods such as DCF and relative valuation are usually the hardest. Questions often give you data and ask for a calculated figure, so timed practice matters. The RA regulations chapter is factual and rewards careful revision.

How long does it take to prepare for NISM Series XV?

Learners with a finance or commerce background often need three to four weeks; others may need longer. Begin with economics and industry analysis, then spend most of your time on financial statements and valuation. Finish with timed mock tests and a final pass over the regulations.
