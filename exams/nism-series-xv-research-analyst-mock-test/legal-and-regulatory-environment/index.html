Legal and Regulatory Environment: NISM Series XV practice questions

8 free questions with answers and explanations on Legal and Regulatory Environment, from the NISM Series XV (Research Analyst) syllabus.

Last updated 26 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · How we create questions

What this chapter covers

This chapter covers the legal framework for research analysts in India: the SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations, 2014 as amended, registration and qualification, certification requirements, client-level obligations, disclosures, restrictions on trading and conflicts, separation from investment banking, record keeping, and the role of the recognised supervisory body. It also covers insider trading and fraudulent practices regulations. Requirements are revised periodically, so focus on principles: registration, independence, disclosure and fair treatment.

Legal and Regulatory Environment questions

Question 1

A finance influencer gives specific buy and sell calls on listed stocks for a fee on social media. Under SEBI rules, he must:

  1. Do nothing further, as social media content is entirely unregulated
  2. Be registered as a research analyst or adviser
  3. Register only with the stock exchange
  4. Get approval from the companies he covers
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Be registered as a research analyst or adviser

Providing research or recommendations on securities for consideration requires SEBI registration. Unregistered persons offering such calls breach regulations.

Question 2

Within an integrated firm, research analysts must be kept separate from investment banking mainly to:

  1. Reduce office rent
  2. Increase investment banking fees
  3. Allow sharing of deal information
  4. Protect research independence
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Protect research independence

Separation prevents bankers from influencing ratings to win deals and stops leakage of confidential deal information to research.

Question 3

An analyst's compensation should not be linked to:

  1. Quality of research
  2. Accuracy of forecasts
  3. Specific investment banking deals
  4. Overall firm performance in general terms
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Specific investment banking deals

SEBI rules bar linking analyst pay to specific investment banking transactions, which would bias research. Quality and accuracy are acceptable criteria.

Question 4

A research analyst must disclose in the report if the subject company was a client in the past twelve months for:

  1. Investment banking or other services
  2. Buying stationery from the analyst
  3. Visiting the analyst's office
  4. Advertising in a newspaper
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Investment banking or other services

Business relationships such as investment banking, merchant banking or brokerage services with the subject company must be disclosed to inform readers of potential conflicts.

Question 5

Which body has SEBI recognised to administer and supervise research analysts?

  1. AMFI
  2. BSE Administration and Supervision Ltd
  3. The Insurance Ombudsman under the IRDAI
  4. CERSAI
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. BSE Administration and Supervision Ltd

SEBI recognised BASL, a BSE subsidiary, to administer and supervise research analysts and investment advisers. AMFI is a mutual fund body.

Question 6

Making a recommendation while holding unpublished price-sensitive information about the company is prohibited under:

  1. SEBI's insider trading regulations
  2. The Consumer Protection Act
  3. The Negotiable Instruments Act
  4. The Trade Marks Act
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. SEBI's insider trading regulations

Using or communicating UPSI in recommendations breaches SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations. The other laws do not govern securities markets.

Question 7

A research analyst appearing on a TV channel to recommend a stock must:

  1. Hide any holdings to avoid confusion
  2. Promise a guaranteed return
  3. Avoid naming the stock
  4. Disclose relevant conflicts of interest
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Disclose relevant conflicts of interest

Public appearances carry the same disclosure duties as reports, including financial interests in recommended securities. Guarantees of returns are prohibited.

Question 8

Research analysts must maintain records of their research and its basis to:

  1. Sell them to other firms
  2. Publish them on social media
  3. Allow verification and inspection
  4. Calculate their income tax only
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Allow verification and inspection

Records of reports, data and rationale let regulators and supervisors inspect whether research was well founded and compliant.
