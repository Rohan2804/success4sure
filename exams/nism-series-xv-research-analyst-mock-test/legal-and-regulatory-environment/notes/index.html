Legal and Regulatory Environment: NISM Series XV revision notes

This chapter covers the rules that govern research analysts in India. Expect questions on who must register, qualification and certification, separation from investment banking, disclosure duties, trading restrictions, record keeping and the role of the Research Analyst Administration and Supervisory Body. Requirements were substantially amended in December 2024, so focus on principles and check current figures.

Last updated 17 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · Free

Key points

1. Who must register

Any person who prepares and publishes research reports or makes buy, sell or hold recommendations on securities for consideration must register with SEBI under the SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations, 2014. Certain persons are exempt, such as those making recommendations only through public media in limited circumstances, subject to conditions.

2. Qualification and certification

Individual RAs and the persons associated with research must have the prescribed educational qualification and must pass NISM Series XV certification, keeping it current through recertification or continuing education. The 2024 amendments relaxed the education requirement to graduation in any discipline (check current rules).

3. Financial requirements

The 2024 amendments replaced net worth requirements with a deposit maintained with a bank based on the number of clients. This is meant to make the profession accessible while still providing some financial assurance.

4. Fees and client terms

For individual and HUF clients, SEBI caps fees per family per year (around ₹1.5 lakh as of 2026, indexed) and limits advance fee collection to a specified period. RAs must sign written terms with clients, disclose fees clearly and must never promise assured returns.

5. Separation from investment banking

Research must be independent of investment banking and other business activities. Research analysts cannot report to investment banking, cannot participate in pitches or roadshows for deals, and their compensation cannot be linked to specific banking or brokerage transactions.

6. Trading restrictions

An RA and its associates cannot trade in securities they cover from 30 days before to 5 days after publishing a report, and cannot trade against their own recommendation. Exceptions exist for emergencies with compliance approval.

7. Disclosures

Research reports must disclose financial interests, holdings of 1% or more at the end of the preceding month, material conflicts, compensation from the subject company in the previous 12 months, whether the entity managed an issue for it, and whether the analyst has served as an officer, director or employee. Use of artificial intelligence tools in research must also be disclosed.

8. Records and compliance

RAs must maintain records of research reports, the basis of recommendations, client agreements and communications for the prescribed period, generally five years. They must appoint a compliance officer where applicable and submit to annual compliance audits.

9. RAASB and SEBI oversight

BSE has been recognised as the Research Analyst Administration and Supervisory Body. It handles enrolment, monitoring, inspections and grievance handling for RAs, while SEBI retains regulatory and enforcement powers. Investors can complain through SCORES.

10. Related regulations

RAs must also comply with the SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations, which ban trading on unpublished price-sensitive information, and the PFUTP Regulations, which prohibit fraudulent and manipulative practices, such as publishing misleading reports to profit from price moves.

Common traps in the exam

  • Assuming free recommendations on social media are never regulated. Paid recommendations and finfluencer activity are covered and scrutinised.
  • Thinking the trading blackout is only after publication. It runs 30 days before to 5 days after.
  • Linking analyst compensation to banking deals. This is prohibited.
  • Confusing RAASB with SEBI. BSE as RAASB supervises; SEBI regulates and enforces.

Quick recap

Anyone giving research for consideration registers under the RA Regulations, 2014 (amended 2024). Need qualification and NISM XV. Deposit replaced net worth. Fee caps and written terms for individual clients. Separate from investment banking. No trading 30 days before to 5 days after a report. Disclose holdings ≥1%, conflicts and AI use. BSE is RAASB.
