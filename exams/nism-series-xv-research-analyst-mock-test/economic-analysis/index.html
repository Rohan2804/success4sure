Economic Analysis: NISM Series XV practice questions

8 free questions with answers and explanations on Economic Analysis, from the NISM Series XV (Research Analyst) syllabus.

What this chapter covers

This chapter covers macroeconomic analysis for equity research: GDP and its components, inflation measures, interest rates and monetary policy, fiscal policy and deficits, the balance of payments, exchange rates and business cycles. Analysts use these to judge how the economy will affect sectors and companies. When revising, understand the transmission of policy changes to markets, interpret key indicators such as PMI, IIP and CPI, and link economic scenarios to sector performance.

Economic Analysis questions

Question 1

India's current account deficit is ₹2 lakh crore and nominal GDP is ₹300 lakh crore. The CAD as a percentage of GDP is about:

  1. 0.67%
  2. 6.7%
  3. 1.5%
  4. 0.15%
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 0.67%

CAD ÷ GDP = 2 ÷ 300 ≈ 0.67%. Expressing CAD relative to GDP lets analysts compare external balance across years and countries.

Question 2

A Purchasing Managers' Index reading above 50 indicates:

  1. Contraction in activity
  2. No change in activity
  3. Deflation in prices
  4. Expansion in activity
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Expansion in activity

PMI readings above 50 signal expansion compared with the previous month, while readings below 50 signal contraction.

Question 3

Which sector is most likely to benefit from a fall in interest rates?

  1. Gold mining
  2. Defensive FMCG only
  3. Real estate and housing finance
  4. Exporters of software and IT services
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Real estate and housing finance

Lower rates reduce borrowing costs, boosting housing demand and loan growth. Rate-sensitive sectors such as real estate and banks typically benefit most.

Question 4

The Index of Industrial Production (IIP) measures:

  1. Retail inflation
  2. Changes in industrial output volume
  3. Stock market returns
  4. The central government's fiscal deficit for the year
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Changes in industrial output volume

IIP tracks output in mining, manufacturing and electricity. CPI measures retail inflation, and fiscal deficit relates to government finances.

Question 5

If the rupee depreciates sharply, which companies typically benefit?

  1. Importers of crude oil
  2. Exporters with dollar revenues
  3. Firms with large unhedged dollar debt
  4. Companies buying imported machinery
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Exporters with dollar revenues

A weaker rupee raises the rupee value of export earnings. Importers and firms with foreign currency debt face higher costs.

Question 6

Expansionary fiscal policy involves:

  1. Raising the repo rate
  2. Selling government bonds through OMOs
  3. Increasing the cash reserve ratio
  4. Higher spending or lower taxes
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Higher spending or lower taxes

Fiscal policy is set by the government through spending and taxes. Repo rate, OMOs and CRR are monetary policy tools of the RBI.

Question 7

During an economic slowdown, which type of stock tends to hold up better?

  1. Cyclical stocks like capital goods
  2. Luxury goods makers
  3. Defensive stocks like FMCG
  4. Commodity producers
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Defensive stocks like FMCG

Defensive sectors sell essentials with stable demand, so earnings hold up in slowdowns. Cyclical sectors' earnings fall with economic activity.

Question 8

Which inflation measure is based on wholesale prices of goods?

  1. WPI
  2. CPI
  3. GDP deflator only
  4. PMI
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. WPI

The Wholesale Price Index tracks prices at the wholesale level. CPI tracks retail prices paid by consumers and is the RBI's target measure.
