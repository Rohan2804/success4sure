Free NISM Series XXIV practice questions

Question 1 · Introduction to AML, CFT and PF

A person makes many small cash deposits, each just below the reporting threshold, into several accounts. This technique is called:

  1. Integration
  2. Round tripping through FDI
  3. Structuring (smurfing)
  4. Trade-based layering
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Structuring (smurfing)

Breaking large cash amounts into smaller deposits to evade reporting thresholds is known as structuring or smurfing, a placement technique.

Question 2 · Prevention of Money Laundering Act, 2002

The punishment for money laundering under Section 4 of the PMLA (where the scheduled offence is not under the NDPS Act) is rigorous imprisonment of:

  1. Not less than 1 year, which may extend to 3 years
  2. Not less than 3 years, which may extend to 7 years, and fine
  3. Not less than 7 years, which may extend to life
  4. Fine only, with no imprisonment
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Not less than 3 years, which may extend to 7 years, and fine

Section 4 prescribes 3 to 7 years of rigorous imprisonment plus fine; the upper limit extends to 10 years for NDPS-linked scheduled offences.

Question 3 · PML (Maintenance of Records) Rules, 2005

A suspicious transaction report (STR) must be furnished to FIU-IND:

  1. By the 15th of the next month
  2. Within 30 days of the transaction
  3. Only at the end of the financial year
  4. Within 7 working days of being satisfied that the transaction is suspicious
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Within 7 working days of being satisfied that the transaction is suspicious

The rules require an STR to be filed promptly, not later than seven working days after the principal officer concludes the transaction is suspicious.

Question 4 · Scheduled Offences

Which SEBI Act offence is included as a scheduled offence under the PMLA?

  1. Prohibition of manipulative and deceptive devices, insider trading and substantial acquisition (Section 12A read with Section 24)
  2. Failure to pay SEBI registration fees
  3. Delay in filing annual reports with the Registrar of Companies
  4. Non-attendance at a SEBI investor awareness programme
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Prohibition of manipulative and deceptive devices, insider trading and substantial acquisition (Section 12A read with Section 24)

Fraud, insider trading and unlawful substantial acquisition under Section 12A, punishable under Section 24 of the SEBI Act, are scheduled offences.

Question 5 · AML, CFT and PF Guidelines

Under Section 51A of the Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act, 1967, a securities intermediary that finds a client's name matches a designated individual on the UNSC sanctions list must:

  1. Continue transactions but monitor them more closely
  2. Freeze the funds and securities without delay and report to the authorities as prescribed
  3. Close the account and return the securities to the client
  4. Ask the client to confirm whether the match is correct before acting
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Freeze the funds and securities without delay and report to the authorities as prescribed

The UAPA Section 51A order requires prompt freezing of assets of designated persons and immediate reporting; the client must not be tipped off.

Question 6 · SEBI Guidelines on AML/CFT/PF Standards

Under the SEBI AML/CFT master circular, each registered intermediary must:

  1. Outsource all AML compliance to the stock exchange
  2. Adopt written AML/CFT policies and procedures approved by its board or senior management
  3. Report all transactions above ₹1,000 to SEBI daily
  4. Obtain a separate AML licence from FIU-IND
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Adopt written AML/CFT policies and procedures approved by its board or senior management

SEBI requires every intermediary to have documented AML/CFT policies and procedures covering CDD, monitoring, reporting and record-keeping.

Question 7 · SEBI Guidelines on KYC Norms

Which document is mandatory for any investor transacting in the Indian securities market, subject to limited exemptions?

  1. Passport
  2. Voter ID
  3. PAN (Permanent Account Number)
  4. Ration card
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. PAN (Permanent Account Number)

SEBI has made PAN the sole identification number for securities market transactions, with limited exemptions such as for certain government officials and residents of Sikkim.

Question 8 · Discussion on PMLA Cases

In Vijay Madanlal Choudhary v. Union of India (2022), the Supreme Court held that an Enforcement Case Information Report (ECIR):

  1. Must be registered with the local police before any investigation
  2. Is the same as a chargesheet filed in court
  3. Is an internal document of the ED and is not equivalent to an FIR, so supplying it to the accused is not mandatory
  4. Must be published in the Official Gazette
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Is an internal document of the ED and is not equivalent to an FIR, so supplying it to the accused is not mandatory

The Court held that an ECIR is an internal document and need not be supplied, though the accused must be informed of the grounds of arrest.

Question 9 · Introduction to AML, CFT and PF

How does terrorist financing typically differ from money laundering?

  1. Terrorist financing always involves proceeds of drug trafficking
  2. Funds for terrorism may come from legitimate sources, and the focus is on their intended use
  3. Terrorist financing involves only large amounts of money
  4. Terrorist financing cannot pass through the formal financial system
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Funds for terrorism may come from legitimate sources, and the focus is on their intended use

Unlike money laundering, which hides illicit origins, terrorist financing can use clean money; the concern is its use for terrorist purposes.

Question 10 · Prevention of Money Laundering Act, 2002

Under the PMLA, 'proceeds of crime' means:

  1. Any income on which tax has not been paid, regardless of source
  2. Property derived or obtained, directly or indirectly, as a result of criminal activity relating to a scheduled offence
  3. Profits earned by a company in a loss-making year
  4. Any property held in cash
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Property derived or obtained, directly or indirectly, as a result of criminal activity relating to a scheduled offence

Proceeds of crime are linked to criminal activity relating to a scheduled offence, including property of equivalent value.

Question 11 · PML (Maintenance of Records) Rules, 2005

The cash transaction report (CTR) for a month must be submitted to FIU-IND by:

  1. The last day of the same month
  2. Seven working days after each transaction
  3. The 15th day of the succeeding month
  4. The end of the quarter
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. The 15th day of the succeeding month

Monthly CTRs are due by the 15th of the following month.

Question 12 · Scheduled Offences

Which Companies Act, 2013 provision is a scheduled offence under the PMLA?

  1. Section 135 – corporate social responsibility
  2. Section 149 – appointment of independent directors
  3. Section 447 – punishment for fraud
  4. Section 123 – declaration of dividend
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Section 447 – punishment for fraud

Fraud punishable under Section 447 of the Companies Act, 2013 is included in the PMLA Schedule.

Question 13 · AML, CFT and PF Guidelines

India's legal framework for combating proliferation financing of weapons of mass destruction includes Section 12A of:

  1. The Companies Act, 2013
  2. The Foreign Exchange Management Act, 1999
  3. The Securities Contracts (Regulation) Act, 1956
  4. The Weapons of Mass Destruction and their Delivery Systems (Prohibition of Unlawful Activities) Act, 2005
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. The Weapons of Mass Destruction and their Delivery Systems (Prohibition of Unlawful Activities) Act, 2005

Section 12A of the WMD Act, 2005 prohibits financing of WMD-related activities and empowers freezing of funds.

Question 14 · SEBI Guidelines on AML/CFT/PF Standards

Under SEBI's AML guidelines, which of the following is a 'Client of Special Category' (CSC)?

  1. A resident individual with a salary account and low trading volumes
  2. A non-resident client
  3. A SEBI-registered mutual fund
  4. A Central Government ministry
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. A non-resident client

SEBI lists non-resident clients, HNIs, trusts and charities, PEPs, clients from high-risk countries and non-face-to-face clients among CSCs.

Question 15 · SEBI Guidelines on KYC Norms

What is the function of a KYC Registration Agency (KRA) in the securities market?

  1. To centrally hold KYC records so that an investor need not repeat KYC with every intermediary
  2. To execute trades on behalf of investors
  3. To issue PAN cards
  4. To approve IPO offer documents
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. To centrally hold KYC records so that an investor need not repeat KYC with every intermediary

KRAs registered under SEBI's KRA Regulations, 2011 maintain KYC records accessible to intermediaries, avoiding duplication.
