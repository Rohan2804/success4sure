PML (Maintenance of Records) Rules, 2005: NISM Series XXIV practice questions

8 free questions with answers and explanations on PML (Maintenance of Records) Rules, 2005, from the NISM Series XXIV (AML and CFT Provisions in Securities Markets) syllabus.

Last updated 17 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · How we create questions

What this chapter covers

This chapter covers the Prevention of Money-laundering (Maintenance of Records) Rules, 2005: the types of transactions to be recorded and reported (cash transactions, suspicious transactions, counterfeit currency, NPO transactions, cross-border wire transfers), the timelines for reports to FIU-IND, client due diligence and identification of beneficial owners, officially valid documents, the roles of the Designated Director and Principal Officer, and confidentiality of reports. Focus on reporting timelines, beneficial ownership thresholds and what each report covers.

PML (Maintenance of Records) Rules, 2005 questions

Question 1

A Non-Profit Organisation Transaction Report (NTR) under the PML Rules covers receipts by an NPO above:

  1. ₹50,000 in a single day only
  2. ₹1 crore in a financial year
  3. ₹2 lakh in any cash transaction
  4. ₹10 lakh or its equivalent in foreign currency
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. ₹10 lakh or its equivalent in foreign currency

Reporting entities must report all transactions involving receipts by non-profit organisations of value more than ₹10 lakh or its equivalent in foreign currency.

Question 2

The Designated Director of a reporting entity under the PML Rules is:

  1. An external auditor appointed by FIU-IND to inspect the reporting entity's AML systems every year
  2. The client's nominee who receives securities on death
  3. The officer of the stock exchange who supervises brokers
  4. A director nominated to ensure overall compliance with PMLA obligations
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. A director nominated to ensure overall compliance with PMLA obligations

Each reporting entity must designate a director — such as the managing director or a whole-time director — responsible for ensuring overall PMLA compliance. FIU-IND must be informed of the designation.

Question 3

When a client is a partnership firm, the PML Rules treat as beneficial owner a natural person who holds more than what share of capital or profits?

  1. 10%
  2. 25%
  3. 15%
  4. 50%
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 10%

Following the 2023 amendments, the threshold for partnership firms is ownership or entitlement to more than 10% of capital or profits, or control through other means.

Question 4

For an unincorporated association or body of individuals, the beneficial ownership threshold under the PML Rules is ownership or entitlement of more than:

  1. 10% of property, capital or profits
  2. 25% of property, capital or profits
  3. 15% of property, capital or profits
  4. 51% of property, capital or profits
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 15% of property, capital or profits

For unincorporated associations or bodies of individuals, the threshold is more than 15%. Companies and partnership firms have a 10% threshold after the 2023 amendments.

Question 5

Reports furnished to FIU-IND by a reporting entity must be kept confidential because:

  1. FIU-IND charges a fee for every report that is shared with clients
  2. Disclosing them to the client could tip off suspects
  3. The reports contain the entity's annual profit and loss accounts
  4. SEBI publishes them on its website after one month
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Disclosing them to the client could tip off suspects

The PML Rules require reporting entities to keep reports confidential. Tipping off a client could allow suspects to move funds or destroy evidence and defeat the investigation.

Question 6

A cross-border wire transfer report must be filed by applicable reporting entities for transfers above:

  1. ₹50,000, only for transfers within India
  2. ₹1 crore, only if paid in cash
  3. ₹5 lakh, where the source or destination is outside India
  4. ₹10,000, for any transfer by any person, whether within India or to a foreign country
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. ₹5 lakh, where the source or destination is outside India

The PML Rules require reporting of cross-border wire transfers of more than ₹5 lakh or equivalent where either the origin or destination of the funds is in India and the other end abroad.

Question 7

When an existing client's KYC documents become outdated, the reporting entity must:

  1. Continue using the old records indefinitely
  2. Update the client's identification records periodically
  3. Close the account without informing the client
  4. Ask the stock exchange to update the records
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Update the client's identification records periodically

Client due diligence is an ongoing obligation. Reporting entities must periodically update KYC information, more frequently for high-risk clients, and when there is reason to doubt records.

Question 8

A reporting entity forms a suspicion about a client on a Monday. Its suspicious transaction report must be furnished to FIU-IND:

  1. Only at the end of the financial year with other reports
  2. Within one hour in every case, before the trading session closes on the day of the suspicion
  3. Only if the client agrees to the report being filed
  4. Within seven working days of being satisfied that the transaction is suspicious
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Within seven working days of being satisfied that the transaction is suspicious

The PML Rules require an STR within seven working days of the Principal Officer concluding that a transaction is suspicious. Client consent is never sought, and tipping off is prohibited.
