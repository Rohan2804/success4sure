How to prepare for bank prelims

Bank prelims reward speed. Learn the common question types in each section — seating puzzles, inequalities and syllogisms in reasoning; simplification, series, data interpretation and arithmetic in quant; reading comprehension, cloze and error spotting in English — then practise full papers against the clock and track sectional accuracy.

What our banking mock tests include
