What this chapter covers

This paper combines statistics, human resource management and credit management for bank managers. Revise measures of central tendency and dispersion (mean, median, mode, standard deviation, coefficient of variation), basic probability, motivation theories (Maslow, Herzberg, McGregor), training and performance appraisal methods, and working capital assessment methods such as MPBF under Tandon Committee norms. Many questions are numerical, so practise short statistical and credit calculations.

Advanced Bank Management questions

Question 1

The arithmetic mean of 4, 8, 15, 23 and 42 is:

  1. 15.0
  2. 18.4
  3. 23.0
  4. 19.5
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 18.4

Mean = (4 + 8 + 15 + 23 + 42) / 5 = 92 / 5 = 18.4. The median of the series is 15, a different measure.

Question 2

A loan portfolio's monthly returns have a mean of 40 and a standard deviation of 6. The coefficient of variation is:

  1. 6.67%
  2. 24%
  3. 46%
  4. 15%
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 15%

CV = standard deviation / mean × 100 = 6 / 40 × 100 = 15%. It expresses relative variability and lets us compare series with different means.

Question 3

Using Tandon second-method norms, what is the MPBF for a unit whose projected chargeable current assets total ₹500 lakh while creditors and other non-bank current liabilities total ₹200 lakh?

  1. ₹175 lakh
  2. ₹225 lakh
  3. ₹300 lakh
  4. ₹125 lakh
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. ₹175 lakh

Method II: MPBF = 75% of current assets − other current liabilities = 375 − 200 = ₹175 lakh. Method I would give 75% of (500 − 200) = ₹225 lakh.

Question 4

A card is drawn from a well-shuffled pack of 52. The probability that it is a king or a heart is:

  1. 17/52
  2. 1/4
  3. 4/13
  4. 1/13
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 4/13

P(king or heart) = 4/52 + 13/52 − 1/52 = 16/52 = 4/13. Adding 4 and 13 without removing the king of hearts double-counts it.

Question 5

McGregor's Theory Y assumes that employees:

  1. Dislike work and must be closely controlled
  2. Are motivated only by money
  3. Avoid all forms of responsibility
  4. Can be self-directed and seek responsibility
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Can be self-directed and seek responsibility

Theory Y holds that people find work natural, can exercise self-control and seek responsibility. Theory X assumes the opposite and favours close supervision.

Question 6

Job rotation as a training method mainly helps employees to:

  1. Gain exposure to different roles
  2. Avoid performance appraisal
  3. Get a guaranteed promotion
  4. Reduce their working hours
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Gain exposure to different roles

Rotating staff across desks such as credit, forex and operations broadens skills and prepares them for wider responsibilities.

Question 7

In a series 3, 5, 5, 7, 9, 9, 9, 12, the mode is:

  1. 5
  2. 7
  3. 9
  4. 7.4
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 9

The mode is the value that occurs most often. 9 appears three times, more than any other value.

Question 8

A borrower's 'drawing power' in a cash credit account is mainly determined by:

  1. The borrower's net worth only
  2. Value of current assets less margin
  3. The number of years of banking relationship
  4. The interest rate on the loan
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Value of current assets less margin

Drawing power is computed from eligible stock and receivables minus the stipulated margin, and limits how much can be drawn within the sanctioned limit.
