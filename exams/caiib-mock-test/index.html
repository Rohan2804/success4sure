Sample 1 · Advanced Bank Management

The population variance of the observations 2, 4, 6, 8 and 10 is:

  1. 8
  2. 6
  3. 10
  4. 2.83
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 8

Mean = 6; squared deviations = 16, 4, 0, 4, 16 → sum 40; variance = 40 ÷ 5 = 8.

Sample 2 · Bank Financial Management

A bank has Tier 1 capital of ₹900 crore, Tier 2 capital of ₹300 crore and risk-weighted assets of ₹10,000 crore. Its CRAR is:

  1. 9%
  2. 3%
  3. 10%
  4. 12%
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 12%

CRAR = (900 + 300) ÷ 10,000 = 12%.

Sample 3 · Advanced Business & Financial Management

When the price of a product rises by 10%, quantity demanded falls by 20%. The price elasticity of demand (absolute value) is:

  1. 0.5
  2. 1
  3. 20
  4. 2
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 2

Elasticity = % change in quantity ÷ % change in price = 20 ÷ 10 = 2 (elastic demand).

Sample 4 · Banking Regulations & Business Laws

Under Section 5(b) of the Banking Regulation Act, 'banking' means accepting deposits of money from the public for the purpose of:

  1. Lending or investment, repayable on demand or otherwise and withdrawable by cheque, draft, order or otherwise
  2. Trading in commodities
  3. Only issuing credit cards
  4. Only remitting funds abroad
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Lending or investment, repayable on demand or otherwise and withdrawable by cheque, draft, order or otherwise

This statutory definition centres on accepting public deposits for lending/investment that are repayable and withdrawable.

Sample 5 · Advanced Bank Management

Karl Pearson's coefficient of correlation always lies between:

  1. −1 and +1
  2. 0 and 1
  3. −∞ and +∞
  4. 0 and 100
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. −1 and +1

Correlation is a bounded measure: +1 is perfect positive, −1 perfect negative and 0 no linear relationship.

CAIIB mock test FAQs

How many questions are there in the CAIIB exam?

The CAIIB paper has 100 multiple-choice questions for 100 marks, to be completed in 120 minutes. This is the indicative pattern — always confirm it in the latest official notification.

Is there negative marking in CAIIB?

No. There is no negative marking, so attempt every question. Our mock tests apply the same rule.

What is the pass mark for CAIIB?

You need 50% to pass. Our mock tests show pass or fail against the same mark.

Are the CAIIB mock tests free?

Yes. All 30 full-length CAIIB mock tests are free, with no payment or plan. You can start without signing up; log in with Google if you want your results saved across devices. You can retake any test as often as you like.

How do I prepare for CAIIB in three months?

Allocate roughly three weeks to each compulsory paper, starting with the one you find hardest. Practise numericals daily for ABM and BFM, and make short notes on laws and regulations for BRBL. Use the final weeks for paper-wise mock tests and revision of formulas and case questions.

Which CAIIB paper is the hardest?

Many candidates find Advanced Bank Management and Bank Financial Management the hardest because of statistics, risk management and treasury numericals. BRBL is theory-heavy but manageable with good notes. Your background will decide which paper needs the most time.

Is an elective paper part of CAIIB?

Under the current IIBF scheme, CAIIB has compulsory papers along with an elective chosen from a list of specialisations. The structure and rules can change, so check the latest CAIIB scheme on iibf.org.in before registering.
