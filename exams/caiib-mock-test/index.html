About the CAIIB exam

CAIIB is IIBF's advanced banking qualification. Its compulsory papers cover advanced bank management, bank financial management, advanced business and financial management, and banking regulations and business laws.

Who should take it: Bank employees who have cleared JAIIB and want the advanced CAIIB qualification for career growth.

Sample 1 · Advanced Bank Management

The population variance of the observations 2, 4, 6, 8 and 10 is:

  1. 8
  2. 6
  3. 10
  4. 2.83
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 8

Mean = 6; squared deviations = 16, 4, 0, 4, 16 → sum 40; variance = 40 ÷ 5 = 8.

Sample 2 · Bank Financial Management

A bank has Tier 1 capital of ₹900 crore, Tier 2 capital of ₹300 crore and risk-weighted assets of ₹10,000 crore. Its CRAR is:

  1. 9%
  2. 3%
  3. 10%
  4. 12%
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 12%

CRAR = (900 + 300) ÷ 10,000 = 12%.

Sample 3 · Advanced Business & Financial Management

When the price of a product rises by 10%, quantity demanded falls by 20%. The price elasticity of demand (absolute value) is:

  1. 0.5
  2. 1
  3. 20
  4. 2
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 2

Elasticity = % change in quantity ÷ % change in price = 20 ÷ 10 = 2 (elastic demand).

Sample 4 · Banking Regulations & Business Laws

Under Section 5(b) of the Banking Regulation Act, 'banking' means accepting deposits of money from the public for the purpose of:

  1. Lending or investment, repayable on demand or otherwise and withdrawable by cheque, draft, order or otherwise
  2. Trading in commodities
  3. Only issuing credit cards
  4. Only remitting funds abroad
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Lending or investment, repayable on demand or otherwise and withdrawable by cheque, draft, order or otherwise

This statutory definition centres on accepting public deposits for lending/investment that are repayable and withdrawable.

Sample 5 · Advanced Bank Management

Karl Pearson's coefficient of correlation always lies between:

  1. −1 and +1
  2. 0 and 1
  3. −∞ and +∞
  4. 0 and 100
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. −1 and +1

Correlation is a bounded measure: +1 is perfect positive, −1 perfect negative and 0 no linear relationship.
