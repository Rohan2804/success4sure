What this chapter covers

This paper covers corporate finance, business strategy and the economic environment. Revise capital budgeting (NPV, IRR, payback), cost of capital (CAPM, WACC), leverage (operating and financial), inventory models such as EOQ, valuation and mergers, and strategic tools such as Ansoff's matrix, BCG matrix and Porter's five forces. Practise calculations and know when each tool is applied in decision-making.

Advanced Business & Financial Management questions

Question 1

A project costs ₹1,00,000 and generates ₹50,000 at the end of each of the next 3 years. At a 10% discount rate, its NPV is closest to:

  1. ₹50,000
  2. ₹1,24,343
  3. ₹36,364
  4. ₹24,343
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. ₹24,343

PV of inflows = 50,000 × 2.4869 = ₹1,24,343. NPV = 1,24,343 − 1,00,000 = ₹24,343. ₹50,000 ignores discounting.

Question 2

Annual demand is 10,000 units, ordering cost is ₹200 per order and holding cost is ₹4 per unit per year. The economic order quantity is:

  1. 500 units
  2. 1,000 units
  3. 2,000 units
  4. 707 units
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 1,000 units

EOQ = √(2 × D × S / H) = √(2 × 10,000 × 200 / 4) = √10,00,000 = 1,000 units. At EOQ, ordering and holding costs are equal.

Question 3

The risk-free rate is 7%, the market return is 12% and a share's beta is 1.2. By CAPM, its cost of equity is:

  1. 14.4%
  2. 12%
  3. 13%
  4. 19%
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 13%

Cost of equity = 7 + 1.2 × (12 − 7) = 7 + 6 = 13%. 14.4% wrongly multiplies the whole market return by beta.

Question 4

A firm's EBIT is ₹50 crore and interest is ₹10 crore. Its degree of financial leverage is:

  1. 1.25
  2. 5.00
  3. 0.80
  4. 4.00
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 1.25

DFL = EBIT / (EBIT − interest) = 50 / 40 = 1.25. It shows that a 1% change in EBIT changes EPS by about 1.25%.

Question 5

The internal rate of return of a project is the discount rate at which:

  1. Payback equals project life
  2. NPV equals zero
  3. Profit is maximum
  4. Cash inflows double
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. NPV equals zero

IRR is the rate that makes the present value of inflows equal to the initial outlay, so NPV is zero. Projects with IRR above the cost of capital add value.

Question 6

In Ansoff's matrix, selling existing products in new markets is called:

  1. Product development
  2. Diversification
  3. Market development
  4. Market penetration
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Market development

Market development takes current products to new customer segments or geographies. Product development offers new products to existing markets.

Question 7

A project costs ₹6,00,000 and returns ₹1,50,000 a year for 4 years and ₹2,00,000 in year 5. Its payback period is:

  1. 4 years
  2. 3 years
  3. 5 years
  4. 4.5 years
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 4 years

Cumulative inflows reach 1,50,000 × 4 = ₹6,00,000 by the end of year 4, recovering the cost exactly, so payback is 4 years.

Question 8

Which of the following increases a firm's operating leverage?

  1. Higher interest payments on the firm's borrowed funds
  2. Lower fixed costs
  3. A higher dividend payout
  4. Higher fixed costs relative to variable costs
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Higher fixed costs relative to variable costs

Operating leverage arises from fixed operating costs. Interest affects financial leverage, not operating leverage.
