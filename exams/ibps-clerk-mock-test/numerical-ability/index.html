Numerical Ability: IBPS Clerk practice questions

15 free questions with answers and explanations on Numerical Ability, from the IBPS Clerk (IBPS Clerk (Prelims)) syllabus.

Last updated 20 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · How we create questions

What this section covers

Numerical Ability covers simplification and approximation, number series, quadratic equations, and arithmetic: percentages, profit and loss with discount, simple and compound interest, ratio and proportion, averages, ages, time and work, speed and distance, mensuration, probability and simple data interpretation. Speed matters as much as accuracy, so learn squares up to 30, cubes up to 20, and percentage–fraction equivalents. Use BODMAS correctly and approximate smartly. Practise mixed sets under time pressure to decide which questions to attempt first.

Numerical Ability questions

Question 1

What will come in place of the question mark (?) 45% of 640 + 15² − 18 × 7 = ?

  1. 387
  2. 377
  3. 397
  4. 367
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 387

45% of 640 = 288, 15² = 225 and 18 × 7 = 126. So 288 + 225 − 126 = 387. Follow BODMAS: finish percentage, square and product before adding and subtracting.

Question 2

What approximate value should come in place of the question mark (?) 24.98 × 12.03 + 299.97 ÷ 6.02 = ?

  1. 340
  2. 360
  3. 330
  4. 350
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 350

Round each number: 25 × 12 = 300 and 300 ÷ 6 = 50. The total is about 300 + 50 = 350. Division is done before addition, as BODMAS requires.

Question 3

What will come in place of the question mark (?) in the series? 2, 6, 15, 31, 56, ?

  1. 88
  2. 87
  3. 92
  4. 94
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 92

The differences are 4, 9, 16, 25, which are squares of 2, 3, 4, 5. The next difference is 6² = 36, so the missing term is 56 + 36 = 92.

Question 4

Find the roots of each quadratic below and decide how x relates to y. I. x² + 5x + 6 = 0 II. y² + 7y + 12 = 0

  1. x > y
  2. x ≥ y
  3. x ≤ y
  4. Relationship cannot be established
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. x ≥ y

I gives x = −2 or −3; II gives y = −3 or −4. Every x is greater than or equal to every y, and x = y = −3 is possible, so x ≥ y. Watch the signs with negative roots.

Question 5

A student scored 36% marks and failed by 18 marks. If the pass mark is 45%, what are the maximum marks of the exam?

  1. 200
  2. 180
  3. 250
  4. 225
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 200

The gap between the pass mark and the student's score is 45% − 36% = 9% of the maximum marks, which equals 18 marks. So maximum marks = 18 ÷ 0.09 = 200.

Question 6

The marked price of a ceiling fan is ₹2,000. The shopkeeper allows a 10% discount and still makes a 20% profit. What is the cost price of the fan?

  1. ₹1,600
  2. ₹1,440
  3. ₹1,550
  4. ₹1,500
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. ₹1,500

Selling price = 90% of 2,000 = ₹1,800. This includes a 20% profit, so cost price = 1,800 ÷ 1.2 = ₹1,500. Taking 20% off ₹1,800 gives ₹1,440, a common error.

Question 7

At simple interest, a sum of money amounts to ₹7,200 in 2 years and to ₹8,400 in 4 years. What is the rate of interest per annum?

  1. 12%
  2. 8%
  3. 10%
  4. 15%
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 10%

The extra ₹1,200 over 2 years means SI of ₹600 per year. Principal = 7,200 − 2 × 600 = ₹6,000. Rate = 600 ÷ 6,000 × 100 = 10% per annum.

Question 8

An amount of ₹1,350 is divided among A, B and C in the ratio 2 : 3 : 4. What is C's share?

  1. ₹450
  2. ₹600
  3. ₹300
  4. ₹540
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. ₹600

Total parts = 2 + 3 + 4 = 9, so one part is 1,350 ÷ 9 = ₹150. C gets 4 parts = ₹600. ₹450 is B's share and ₹300 is A's share.

Question 9

The average age of 30 students in a class is 14 years. When the teacher's age is included, the average rises by 1 year. What is the teacher's age?

  1. 45 years
  2. 44 years
  3. 46 years
  4. 40 years
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 45 years

Total age of students = 30 × 14 = 420. With the teacher, total = 31 × 15 = 465. Teacher's age = 465 − 420 = 45 years.

Question 10

The sum of the present ages of a mother and her daughter is 50 years. Five years ago, the mother was seven times as old as the daughter. What is the mother's present age?

  1. 35 years
  2. 42 years
  3. 38 years
  4. 40 years
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 40 years

Let the daughter be d; mother = 50 − d. Then 45 − d = 7(d − 5), so 8d = 80 and d = 10. Mother is 40 years old. Check: five years ago 35 = 7 × 5.

Question 11

A can complete a piece of work in 20 days and B in 30 days. A works alone for 10 days and then leaves. In how many days will B finish the remaining work?

  1. 12 days
  2. 18 days
  3. 15 days
  4. 10 days
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 15 days

In 10 days A completes 10/20 = half the work. B does 1/30 per day, so the remaining half takes 30 × 1/2 = 15 days.

Question 12

A car covers a certain distance at 60 km/h in 5 hours. At what speed must it travel to cover the same distance in 4 hours?

  1. 70 km/h
  2. 75 km/h
  3. 80 km/h
  4. 72 km/h
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 75 km/h

Distance = 60 × 5 = 300 km. To cover 300 km in 4 hours, speed = 300 ÷ 4 = 75 km/h. Speed and time are inversely proportional for a fixed distance.

Question 13

The area of a rectangular plot is 432 m² and its length is 24 m. What is the perimeter of the plot?

  1. 84 m
  2. 72 m
  3. 96 m
  4. 80 m
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 84 m

Breadth = 432 ÷ 24 = 18 m. Perimeter = 2 × (24 + 18) = 84 m. Remember to add length and breadth before doubling, not just to double one side.

Question 14

One card is drawn at random from a well-shuffled pack of 52 cards. What is the probability that it is a king or a heart?

  1. 17/52
  2. 1/4
  3. 5/13
  4. 4/13
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 4/13

There are 13 hearts and 4 kings, but the king of hearts is counted twice. Favourable cards = 13 + 4 − 1 = 16, so P = 16/52 = 4/13. Not subtracting the overlap gives 17/52.

Question 15

The number of savings accounts opened by a bank branch on five days of a week was: Monday – 45, Tuesday – 60, Wednesday – 38, Thursday – 52, Friday – 55 What is the average number of accounts opened per day?

  1. 48
  2. 52
  3. 50
  4. 54
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 50

Total accounts = 45 + 60 + 38 + 52 + 55 = 250. Average = 250 ÷ 5 = 50 accounts per day. Add carefully before dividing; one slip in the total changes the answer.
