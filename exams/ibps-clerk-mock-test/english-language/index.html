English Language: IBPS Clerk practice questions

15 free questions with answers and explanations on English Language, from the IBPS Clerk (IBPS Clerk (Prelims)) syllabus.

Last updated 20 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · How we create questions

What this section covers

IBPS Clerk English checks basic grammar, vocabulary and reading skills. Expect a short passage with factual and word-meaning questions, fillers, error spotting, sentence improvement, para jumbles and vocabulary items such as synonyms, antonyms, idioms, one-word substitution and spelling. Revise subject–verb agreement, tenses, prepositions, commonly confused words (their/there) and use of adverbs. In passages, answer only from what is stated. Daily reading of a newspaper and noting new words is the most reliable way to improve speed and accuracy.

English Language questions

Question 1

Read the passage and answer the question. Sunita, a farmer near Nashik, used to borrow from a local moneylender who charged very high interest. After she joined a self-help group, she could take small loans at a much lower rate and repay them in easy instalments. Within two years she bought a water pump and her onion yield nearly doubled. What helped Sunita get loans at a lower rate of interest?

  1. Joining a self-help group
  2. Selling her onion crop early
  3. Borrowing from the moneylender
  4. Buying a new water pump
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Joining a self-help group

The passage says that after she joined a self-help group she could take small loans at a much lower rate. The moneylender charged high interest, and the pump was bought later with the loan.

Question 2

Read the passage and answer the question. Sunita, a farmer near Nashik, used to borrow from a local moneylender who charged very high interest. After she joined a self-help group, she could take small loans at a much lower rate and repay them in easy instalments. Within two years she bought a water pump and her onion yield nearly doubled. Which of the following best describes what the passage is mainly about?

  1. Why moneylenders charge very high interest
  2. How onions are grown in Nashik district
  3. Why farmers should avoid all kinds of loans
  4. How cheaper credit improved a farmer's life
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. How cheaper credit improved a farmer's life

The passage traces Sunita's move from costly moneylender loans to cheaper group loans and the resulting rise in her yield. Its focus is on how affordable credit improved her livelihood.

Question 3

Read the passage and answer the question. Sunita, a farmer near Nashik, used to borrow from a local moneylender who charged very high interest. After she joined a self-help group, she could take small loans at a much lower rate and repay them in easy instalments. Within two years she bought a water pump and her onion yield nearly doubled. As used in the passage, the word 'yield' most nearly means:

  1. Surrender
  2. Profit share
  3. Output
  4. Interest
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Output

In farming, 'yield' means the amount of crop produced, that is the output. 'Surrender' is another meaning of the verb 'yield', but it does not fit the context of onion harvests.

Question 4

Choose the word that best fills the blank. Please ________ the account-opening form in capital letters.

  1. fall
  2. fill
  3. feel
  4. fell
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. fill

One fills (in) a form, so 'fill' is correct. 'Fall', 'feel' and 'fell' are similar-sounding words but make no sense with 'the account-opening form'.

Question 5

Choose the word that best fills the blank. The cashier counted the notes ________ before handing them over to the customer.

  1. carefully
  2. careful
  3. care
  4. careless
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. carefully

The blank describes how the cashier counted, so an adverb is needed: 'carefully'. 'Careful' is an adjective and 'care' is a noun; 'careless' is an adjective with the opposite sense.

Question 6

Choose the word that best fills the blank. The loan amount will be credited ________ your savings account within two working days.

  1. at
  2. of
  3. by
  4. to
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. to

Money is credited to an account, so 'to' is the correct preposition. 'At', 'of' and 'by' do not combine with 'credited' to show the destination of the money.

Question 7

Identify the part of the sentence that contains an error. My brother and I (A) / goes to the bank (B) / every Saturday (C) / to deposit money. (D)

  1. (A)
  2. (B)
  3. (C)
  4. (D)
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. (B)

Two subjects joined by 'and' (My brother and I) take a plural verb. So 'goes' must be 'go'. The error lies in part (B); the other parts are correct.

Question 8

Identify the part of the sentence that contains an error. The news (A) / about the merger (B) / were announced (C) / yesterday morning. (D)

  1. (A)
  2. (B)
  3. (C)
  4. (D)
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. (C)

'News' looks plural but is an uncountable singular noun, so it takes 'was': 'The news was announced'. The error is in part (C).

Question 9

Identify the part of the sentence that contains an error. All the customers were (A) / asked to stand (B) / in a queue and (C) / wait for there turn. (D)

  1. (A)
  2. (B)
  3. (C)
  4. (D)
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. (D)

'There' is an adverb of place; the possessive pronoun needed here is 'their', as in 'wait for their turn'. So part (D) contains the error.

Question 10

Choose the option that best replaces the part in capitals. If no change is needed, choose 'No improvement required'. If it rains tomorrow, we WILL STAYED at home.

  1. will stay
  2. would stayed
  3. will staying
  4. No improvement required
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. will stay

After the modal 'will', the base form of the verb is used: 'will stay'. In a first conditional, the if-clause takes simple present (rains) and the main clause takes 'will' + base verb.

Question 11

Rearrange the sentences to form a meaningful paragraph. P: First, she took a withdrawal slip from the counter and filled it in. Q: Asha reached the bank at ten in the morning. R: After checking her signature, the cashier gave her the cash. S: Then she stood in the queue and handed the slip to the cashier.

  1. QSPR
  2. PQSR
  3. QPSR
  4. QPRS
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. QPSR

Q opens with Asha arriving, P begins the process with 'First', S follows with 'Then', and R ends with the cashier paying her. The time words fix the order as QPSR.

Question 12

Select the word that means the same as GENUINE.

  1. Fake
  2. Authentic
  3. Rude
  4. Fragile
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Authentic

Genuine means real or truly what it is said to be, so 'authentic' is the synonym. 'Fake' is its antonym, and rude or fragile are unrelated in meaning.

Question 13

Pick the antonym of EXPAND.

  1. Contract
  2. Extend
  3. Enlarge
  4. Spread
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Contract

To expand is to become larger. 'Contract' means to become smaller, so it is the opposite. Extend, enlarge and spread are all close in meaning to expand.

Question 14

Choose the correct meaning of the idiom: 'To turn over a new leaf'.

  1. To start reading a new book
  2. To plant trees in a new garden
  3. To move to a new house or city
  4. To change one's behaviour for the better
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. To change one's behaviour for the better

'Turn over a new leaf' means to make a fresh start by improving one's conduct or habits. The literal readings about books, plants or houses are traps.

Question 15

Choose the one word for: 'A list of items to be discussed at a meeting'.

  1. Minutes
  2. Memo
  3. Agenda
  4. Notice
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Agenda

An agenda lists the topics to be taken up at a meeting. Minutes are the written record of what was discussed afterwards, a common confusion, while a memo or notice is a general communication.
