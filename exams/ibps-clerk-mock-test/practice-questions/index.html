Question 1 · English Language

Choose the word that best fills the blank. She was so tired after the long journey that she fell ________ as soon as she lay down.

  1. sleeping
  2. slept
  3. asleep
  4. sleepy
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. asleep

The fixed expression is 'fall asleep'.

Question 2 · Numerical Ability

What approximate value should come in place of the question mark (?) 19.97 × 15.02 − 149.99 ÷ 5.01 = ?

  1. 250
  2. 300
  3. 270
  4. 285
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 270

≈ 20 × 15 − 150 ÷ 5 = 300 − 30 = 270.

Question 3 · Reasoning Ability

Five boxes A, B, C, D and E are kept one above the other. C is at the top. Only one box is kept between C and A. B is kept immediately below A. D is not at the bottom. Which box is at the bottom?

  1. B
  2. D
  3. E
  4. A
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. E

From top: C (5), A (3), B (2); D cannot be at 1, so D = 4 and E is at the bottom.

Question 4 · English Language

Choose the word that best fills the blank. The bank will remain ________ on Sunday.

  1. close
  2. closed
  3. closing
  4. closure
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. closed

After 'remain', an adjective is needed; 'closed' describes the state of the bank.

Question 5 · Numerical Ability

What will come in place of the question mark (?) in the series? 3, 8, 18, 38, 78, ?

  1. 156
  2. 158
  3. 148
  4. 160
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 158

Each term = previous × 2 + 2: 78 × 2 + 2 = 158.

Question 6 · Reasoning Ability

Five persons F, G, H, I and J attend a training programme on different days from Monday to Friday. H attends on Wednesday. Exactly two persons attend between G and F. I attends on a day after F. J attends immediately before H. F does not attend on Monday. Who attends on Friday?

  1. F
  2. I
  3. G
  4. J
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. I

J = Tuesday. G and F must be Monday/Thursday; F is not Monday, so G = Mon, F = Thu, and I (after F) = Friday.

Question 7 · English Language

Choose the word that best fills the blank. He has been working in this branch ________ 2019.

  1. for
  2. by
  3. at
  4. since
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. since

'Since' is used with a point of time (2019) in the perfect continuous tense.

Question 8 · Numerical Ability

Two equations I and II are given. Solve both and choose the correct relationship between x and y. I. x² = 49 II. y² − 14y + 49 = 0

  1. x > y
  2. x ≥ y
  3. x ≤ y
  4. x < y
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. x ≤ y

x = +7 or −7; (y − 7)² = 0 gives y = 7. So x is either equal to or less than y: x ≤ y.

Question 9 · Reasoning Ability

Statements: All pens are books. Some books are bags. Conclusions: I. Some pens are bags. II. Some bags are books.

  1. Only conclusion I follows
  2. Both I and II follow
  3. Neither I nor II follows
  4. Only conclusion II follows
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Only conclusion II follows

Pens and bags need not overlap, so I does not follow. 'Some books are bags' converts to 'some bags are books', so II follows.

Question 10 · English Language

Identify the part of the sentence that contains an error. Each of the students (A) / have submitted (B) / the assignment (C) / on time. (D)

  1. (A)
  2. (B)
  3. (C)
  4. (D)
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. (B)

'Each of' takes a singular verb: 'has submitted'.

Question 11 · Numerical Ability

40% of a number is 64 more than 20% of the same number. What is the number?

  1. 160
  2. 256
  3. 320
  4. 400
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 320

40% − 20% = 20% of the number = 64, so the number = 64 × 5 = 320.

Question 12 · Reasoning Ability

Statements: No apple is a mango. All mangoes are fruits. Conclusions: I. Some fruits are not apples. II. No fruit is an apple.

  1. Only conclusion I follows
  2. Only conclusion II follows
  3. Either I or II follows
  4. Both I and II follow
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Only conclusion I follows

The fruits that are mangoes are not apples, so I follows. Other fruits may be apples, so II is not definite.

Question 13 · English Language

Identify the part of the sentence that contains an error. She is one of the best (A) / cashier who have (B) / ever worked (C) / at this branch. (D)

  1. (A)
  2. (B)
  3. (C)
  4. (D)
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. (B)

'One of the best' must be followed by a plural noun: 'cashiers who have'.

Question 14 · Numerical Ability

By selling an article for ₹1,035, a shopkeeper gains 15%. What is its cost price?

  1. ₹900
  2. ₹880
  3. ₹920
  4. ₹875
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. ₹900

CP = 1,035 × 100/115 = ₹900.

Question 15 · Reasoning Ability

Statements: M ≥ N = O < P ≤ Q Conclusions: I. M ≥ O II. Q > N

  1. Only conclusion I follows
  2. Both I and II follow
  3. Only conclusion II follows
  4. Neither I nor II follows
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Both I and II follow

M ≥ N = O gives M ≥ O. Q ≥ P > O = N gives Q > N. Both follow.
