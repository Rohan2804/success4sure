Free NISM Series XXIII practice questions

Question 1 · Social Sector Ecosystem

Which of the following is a typical legal form of a not-for-profit organisation in India?

  1. A limited liability partnership distributing profits to partners
  2. A private limited company paying dividends
  3. A company registered under Section 8 of the Companies Act, 2013
  4. A sole proprietorship
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. A company registered under Section 8 of the Companies Act, 2013

Trusts, societies and Section 8 companies are the main NPO forms; they cannot distribute profits to members.

Question 2 · SSE: Introduction, Funding Structures & Instruments

Which two types of 'Social Enterprises' can access the Social Stock Exchange?

  1. Not-for-profit organisations (NPOs) and for-profit social enterprises (FPEs)
  2. Listed banks and insurance companies
  3. Political parties and religious organisations
  4. Mutual funds and portfolio managers
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Not-for-profit organisations (NPOs) and for-profit social enterprises (FPEs)

The SSE framework recognises NPOs and FPEs with social intent and impact as Social Enterprises.

Question 3 · Registration and Listing on SSE

An NPO registered on the SSE has not raised any funds through the exchange. Which statement is correct?

  1. It has no obligations at all until it raises funds
  2. It must delist immediately
  3. It still has ongoing disclosure obligations as a registered NPO
  4. It must convert into a for-profit enterprise
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. It still has ongoing disclosure obligations as a registered NPO

Registration carries annual disclosure obligations even if the NPO has not yet raised funds through the SSE.

Question 4 · Logic Model in Social Programs

An NGO runs a skilling programme. 'Number of youth who completed the 3-month training' is an example of:

  1. An output
  2. An input
  3. An impact
  4. An assumption
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. An output

Outputs are the direct, countable products of activities; outcomes would be changes such as youth getting jobs.

Question 5 · Social Impact Assessment & Assessors

A social auditor is asked to assess an NPO where her brother is the chief executive. What should she do?

  1. Accept, but give the NPO a slightly lower rating
  2. Accept, as long as she discloses it after the report is issued
  3. Accept, because family relationships are irrelevant in social audit
  4. Decline the engagement because of a conflict of interest that impairs independence
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Decline the engagement because of a conflict of interest that impairs independence

Independence is fundamental; a close relationship with management creates a conflict of interest that should lead to declining the engagement.

Question 6 · SIA Frameworks, Techniques & Standards

In the Social Return on Investment (SROI) method, an NPO invested ₹20 lakh and the present value of social benefits is estimated at ₹70 lakh. What is the SROI ratio?

  1. 1:3.5
  2. 3.5:1
  3. 2.5:1
  4. 0.29:1
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 3.5:1

SROI = present value of benefits ÷ value of inputs = 70 ÷ 20 = 3.5, i.e., ₹3.5 of social value per ₹1 invested.

Question 7 · SIA Reporting

Under the SSE framework, the Annual Impact Report (AIR) of a Social Enterprise must be:

  1. Prepared only for internal use and never disclosed
  2. Assessed by a social auditor and submitted to the SSE
  3. Approved by SEBI before publication
  4. Audited only by the statutory financial auditor
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Assessed by a social auditor and submitted to the SSE

The AIR, covering qualitative and quantitative aspects of impact, must be assessed by a social auditor and submitted to the exchange.

Question 8 · SIA Case Studies

A water NGO reports '500 handpumps installed'. In the assessment, the auditor finds only 300 are functional after a year. Which is the most meaningful impact indicator to examine next?

  1. Change in households' access to safe drinking water and related health outcomes
  2. The total cost of handpumps purchased
  3. The number of staff employed by the NGO
  4. The number of press releases issued
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Change in households' access to safe drinking water and related health outcomes

Impact relates to changes in beneficiaries' lives, such as safe water access and health, not merely outputs or inputs.

Question 9 · Disclosure Norms, Reporting & Penalties

Who is primarily responsible for the accuracy of the information disclosed by a Social Enterprise to the SSE?

  1. The investors who funded it
  2. The stock exchange
  3. The beneficiaries
  4. The Social Enterprise and its management
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. The Social Enterprise and its management

The enterprise is responsible for its disclosures; the social auditor provides independent assessment of the impact reported.

Question 10 · Taxation

Registration of a charitable trust under the income-tax law (as an approved charitable institution) primarily allows:

  1. Donors to receive guaranteed returns
  2. The trust's income applied to charitable purposes to be exempt from income tax, subject to conditions
  3. The trust to distribute profits to trustees tax-free
  4. The trust to avoid maintaining books of account
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. The trust's income applied to charitable purposes to be exempt from income tax, subject to conditions

Registration as a charitable institution exempts income applied to charitable objects, subject to prescribed conditions.

Question 11 · Key Regulations

Receipt of foreign contributions by an Indian NPO is regulated mainly by:

  1. The Foreign Contribution (Regulation) Act, 2010
  2. The Securities Contracts (Regulation) Act, 1956
  3. The Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016
  4. The Depositories Act, 1996
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. The Foreign Contribution (Regulation) Act, 2010

The FCRA, 2010 governs acceptance and use of foreign contributions by NGOs and requires registration or prior permission.

Question 12 · Social Sector Ecosystem

Under Section 135 of the Companies Act, 2013, a company meeting the CSR thresholds must spend on CSR in a financial year at least:

  1. 5% of its turnover of the preceding year
  2. 2% of its average net profits of the three immediately preceding financial years
  3. 1% of its net worth
  4. 10% of its dividend payout
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 2% of its average net profits of the three immediately preceding financial years

Section 135 requires qualifying companies to spend at least 2% of average net profits of the preceding three years on CSR activities.

Question 13 · SSE: Introduction, Funding Structures & Instruments

A Zero Coupon Zero Principal (ZCZP) instrument issued by an NPO on the SSE:

  1. Pays a fixed coupon but no principal
  2. Repays principal at maturity with no coupon
  3. Converts into equity shares of the NPO
  4. Pays no interest and does not repay the principal at maturity
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Pays no interest and does not repay the principal at maturity

ZCZP instruments are effectively donations through the exchange: investors receive neither coupon nor principal, but receive impact reporting.

Question 14 · Registration and Listing on SSE

A for-profit social enterprise wishes to list its equity shares. Which statement is correct?

  1. It must meet the listing requirements applicable to the platform it lists on, in addition to the SSE social impact requirements
  2. It is exempt from all listing requirements because it is a social enterprise
  3. It can list only ZCZP instruments
  4. It can list only on a foreign exchange
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. It must meet the listing requirements applicable to the platform it lists on, in addition to the SSE social impact requirements

FPEs follow the ICDR and listing requirements of the relevant board, together with the additional social-intent and impact-reporting norms.

Question 15 · Logic Model in Social Programs

In the same skilling programme, '60% of trained youth secured jobs within six months and increased household income' is best classified as:

  1. An input
  2. An activity
  3. An output
  4. An outcome
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. An outcome

Outcomes are the changes experienced by beneficiaries as a result of outputs, such as employment and income gains.
