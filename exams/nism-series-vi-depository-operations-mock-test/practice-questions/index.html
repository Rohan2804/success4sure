Free NISM Series VI practice questions

Question 1 · Introduction to the Indian Capital Market

Which of these is a market infrastructure institution in the Indian securities market?

  1. A research analyst
  2. A depository
  3. A mutual fund distributor
  4. An investment adviser
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. A depository

Stock exchanges, clearing corporations and depositories are market infrastructure institutions.

Question 2 · Introduction to Depository

The depository system in India is governed primarily by:

  1. The Depositories Act, 1996 and SEBI (Depositories and Participants) Regulations, 2018
  2. FEMA, 1999
  3. The Banking Regulation Act, 1949
  4. The Indian Trusts Act, 1882
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. The Depositories Act, 1996 and SEBI (Depositories and Participants) Regulations, 2018

The Depositories Act provides the legal basis and SEBI regulations govern depositories and DPs.

Question 3 · Depository and its Business Partners

Which business partner of the depository electronically confirms demat and remat requests on behalf of the issuer?

  1. The issuer's Registrar and Transfer Agent
  2. The stock exchange
  3. The clearing bank
  4. The investor's broker
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. The issuer's Registrar and Transfer Agent

The RTA verifies certificates and confirms demat or remat requests to the depository.

Question 4 · DP Functions – Account Opening

What is the maximum number of holders permitted in a single demat account?

  1. Five
  2. Three
  3. Two
  4. Four
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Three

A demat account can have a first holder and up to two joint holders.

Question 5 · DP Functions – Account Operations

An investor wants to add a second holder to an existing sole-holder demat account. The correct approach is to:

  1. Send a request to SEBI
  2. Open a new joint account and transfer the holdings to it
  3. Submit a request to add the name to the existing account
  4. Ask the RTA to change the holder's name
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Open a new joint account and transfer the holdings to it

Holders cannot be added or removed in an existing account; a new account is opened and holdings are moved.

Question 6 · DP Functions – Transmission and Nomination

A sole holder of a demat account has died and a nominee is registered. To claim the securities, the nominee should submit:

  1. A rematerialisation request
  2. Only a verbal request at the DP office
  3. A DIS signed by the deceased
  4. A transmission request form with a copy of the death certificate and KYC documents
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. A transmission request form with a copy of the death certificate and KYC documents

Transmission to a nominee requires the prescribed form, death certificate and the nominee's KYC.

Question 7 · DP Functions – Dematerialisation

An investor submits a demat request, but the RTA rejects it because the signature does not match its records. The likely next step is to:

  1. Credit the shares anyway
  2. Transfer the shares to the DP
  3. Cancel the shares permanently
  4. Return the certificates to the investor so that the signature can be updated and the request resubmitted
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Return the certificates to the investor so that the signature can be updated and the request resubmitted

Rejected requests are returned with reasons so the investor can correct the discrepancy.

Question 8 · DP Functions – Trading and Settlement

An investor sells shares on a Thursday under the T+1 cycle (no holidays). By when must the securities reach the clearing corporation for pay-in?

  1. The following Monday
  2. Friday, the settlement day
  3. Thursday evening
  4. The following Tuesday
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Friday, the settlement day

Pay-in and pay-out of securities happen on T+1, i.e. Friday.

Question 9 · Special Services – Pledge & Hypothecation

In a pledge of dematerialised securities, the pledge request is initiated by:

  1. The pledgor (the owner of the securities)
  2. The depository
  3. The pledgee
  4. The RTA
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. The pledgor (the owner of the securities)

The pledgor initiates the request through their DP, and the pledgee accepts it through theirs.

Question 10 · Special Services – Public Offering / Corporate Actions

Shares allotted in an IPO are credited to investors' demat accounts by:

  1. The broker, by transferring from its pool
  2. The stock exchange, after listing
  3. SEBI
  4. The RTA, which executes a corporate action through the depositories
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. The RTA, which executes a corporate action through the depositories

The RTA uploads allotment details and the depositories credit investors' accounts.

Question 11 · Special Services – Debt Instruments & Government Securities

Commercial paper and certificates of deposit in India are issued:

  1. Only in dematerialised form
  2. Either form at the issuer's choice
  3. Only as bearer certificates
  4. Only in physical form
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Only in dematerialised form

RBI rules require CPs and CDs to be issued and held in demat form.

Question 12 · Foreign Portfolio Investors (FPI)

A foreign pension fund wants to invest in Indian listed shares as an FPI. It obtains registration from:

  1. The RBI directly
  2. The stock exchange
  3. A Designated Depository Participant
  4. The Indian embassy
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. A Designated Depository Participant

DDPs grant FPI registration on behalf of SEBI.

Question 13 · Investor Services

A DP must send a demat account holder a transaction statement:

  1. Daily
  2. Only once a year
  3. Only on request
  4. Monthly if there are transactions, and otherwise at least quarterly
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Monthly if there are transactions, and otherwise at least quarterly

Depository norms require statements at these minimum frequencies, and they can be sent electronically.

Question 14 · Introduction to the Indian Capital Market

Which of the following is NOT a capital market instrument?

  1. Convertible debenture
  2. Long-term corporate bond
  3. Equity share
  4. 91-day Treasury bill
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 91-day Treasury bill

A 91-day T-bill is a short-term money market instrument.

Question 15 · Introduction to Depository

A depository is best compared to a bank because it:

  1. Pays interest on securities held
  2. Lends money to investors against deposits
  3. Holds securities in electronic accounts and transfers them through book entries
  4. Issues currency
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Holds securities in electronic accounts and transfers them through book entries

Like a bank holding funds, a depository holds securities and moves them by book entry.
