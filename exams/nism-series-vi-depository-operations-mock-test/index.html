About the NISM Series VI exam

NISM Series VI covers depository operations — the depository system, NSDL and CDSL, account opening, dematerialisation, transfers, pledge, transmission and nomination, corporate actions and investor services at a depository participant.

Who should take it: Employees of depository participants (banks, brokers and custodians) who handle demat account operations and client service.

Sample 1 · Introduction to the Indian Capital Market

Which segment of the capital market is used when a company sells its shares to the public for the first time?

  1. Secondary market
  2. Primary market
  3. Money market
  4. Derivatives market
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Primary market

An IPO is a primary market transaction in which the issuer raises fresh funds.

Sample 2 · Introduction to Depository

Which of the following is a key advantage of holding securities in a depository?

  1. Exemption from capital gains tax
  2. No risk of loss, theft or mutilation of physical certificates
  3. Higher face value of shares
  4. Guaranteed dividends
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. No risk of loss, theft or mutilation of physical certificates

Electronic holdings remove the risks and paperwork of physical certificates.

Sample 3 · Depository and its Business Partners

A depository participant acts as:

  1. An investment adviser
  2. A stock exchange
  3. An agent of the depository providing depository services to investors
  4. The issuer's share registrar
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. An agent of the depository providing depository services to investors

DPs are the depository's interface with investors and are registered with SEBI.

Sample 4 · DP Functions – Account Opening

Which document is mandatory for opening a demat account by an individual resident investor?

  1. PAN card
  2. Passport
  3. Voter ID
  4. Ration card
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. PAN card

PAN is the mandatory identifier; other proofs of address and identity can vary.

Sample 5 · DP Functions – Account Operations

A client wants to stop all debits from their demat account for some time. The DP can:

  1. Reject the request because freezing is not allowed
  2. Close the account and reopen it later
  3. Transfer holdings to the DP's own account
  4. Freeze the account for debits as per the client's request
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Freeze the account for debits as per the client's request

Clients can request freezing of debits, credits or both for a period.

NISM Series VI mock test FAQs

How many questions are there in the NISM Series VI exam?

The NISM Series VI paper has 100 multiple-choice questions for 100 marks, to be completed in 120 minutes. This is the indicative pattern — always confirm it in the latest official notification.

Is there negative marking in NISM Series VI?

Yes. −0.25 mark per wrong answer (25% of the marks for that question). Our mock tests apply the same rule.

What is the pass mark for NISM Series VI?

You need 60% to pass. Our mock tests show pass or fail against the same mark.

Are the NISM Series VI mock tests free?

Yes. All 30 full-length NISM Series VI mock tests are free, with no payment or plan. Create a free account (one click with Google) to start; your results are saved across devices. You can retake any test as often as you like.

Is NISM Series VI mandatory for DP employees?

SEBI requires associated persons of depository participants who handle demat operations and investor services to hold this certification. That includes many staff at banks, brokers and custodians who work on DP operations. Check with your compliance team, and confirm the current rules on nism.ac.in.

Which topics in NISM Series VI need the most practice?

Account opening documentation, transmission, pledge and the processes for corporate actions usually need the most attention because they involve specific steps and forms. Make a short checklist for each process, then practise mock test questions until you can recall the sequence quickly.

How long does it take to prepare for NISM Series VI?

Most learners prepare in about two weeks. If you already work at a depository participant, many processes will be familiar, so focus on regulatory provisions and less common services. Finish with two or three timed mock tests to check speed and accuracy.
