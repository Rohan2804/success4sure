Investor Services: NISM Series VI practice questions

8 free questions with answers and explanations on Investor Services, from the NISM Series VI (Depository Operations) syllabus.

Last updated 23 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · How we create questions

What this chapter covers

This chapter covers services and protections for demat account holders. Revise transaction and holding statements and their frequency, the Consolidated Account Statement, SMS and email alerts, investor grievance channels (DP, depository, SCORES, online dispute resolution), investor protection funds of depositories, and safe practices like not signing blank DIS. Questions usually test the correct timeline, the right escalation path or a practical safeguard.

Investor Services questions

Question 1

A CAS covering demat and mutual fund holdings is sent to an investor who had no transactions in the period:

  1. Weekly
  2. Half-yearly
  3. Never
  4. Every two years
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Half-yearly

Investors with transactions get a monthly CAS. Those with no transactions still get a CAS every six months showing their holdings.

Question 2

SMS or email alerts for debits from a demat account help investors to:

  1. Earn interest on holdings
  2. Lower taxes
  3. Avoid KYC
  4. Detect unauthorised debits quickly
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Detect unauthorised debits quickly

Depositories send alerts directly to investors for debits and other key changes, so investors can spot and report any unauthorised transaction promptly.

Question 3

An investor's complaint against a DP is not resolved by the DP. The next level is:

  1. The depository
  2. The stock exchange
  3. RBI
  4. The issuer
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. The depository

Investors can escalate unresolved complaints against DPs to the depository, and then to SEBI through SCORES and to online dispute resolution.

Question 4

Which practice protects a demat account holder?

  1. Leaving the DIS booklet with the broker
  2. Sharing the online password with an agent
  3. Never signing blank DIS
  4. Ignoring alerts from the depository
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Never signing blank DIS

Blank signed DIS can be misused to transfer securities. Investors should keep the booklet safe, fill instructions fully and act on alerts.

Question 5

A depository's Investor Protection Fund is used for:

  1. Paying the salaries and bonuses of DP staff members
  2. Funding IPOs
  3. Paying market losses
  4. Investor education and eligible claims
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Investor education and eligible claims

The IPF of a depository is used for investor education and awareness and for eligible claims as set out in its rules, not for market losses.

Question 6

E-voting for shareholders of listed companies is facilitated by:

  1. The depositories
  2. SEBI directly
  3. The RBI
  4. The broker only
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. The depositories

Depositories provide e-voting platforms, allowing beneficial owners to vote on resolutions of listed companies through their demat logins.

Question 7

Which online platform can investors use to file complaints against a DP?

  1. UPI
  2. NPS app
  3. SCORES
  4. ASBA
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. SCORES

SEBI's SCORES platform accepts complaints against intermediaries including DPs, and tracks their resolution.

Question 8

An investor wants to see all securities she holds across NSDL and CDSL accounts in one document. She should rely on the:

  1. Delivery Instruction Slip
  2. Consolidated Account Statement
  3. Client Master List
  4. Contract note
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Consolidated Account Statement

The CAS, generated by the depositories using PAN, combines holdings across demat accounts and mutual fund folios in one statement.
