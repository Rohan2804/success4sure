Depository and its Business Partners: NISM Series VI practice questions

8 free questions with answers and explanations on Depository and its Business Partners, from the NISM Series VI (Depository Operations) syllabus.

Last updated 20 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · How we create questions

What this chapter covers

This chapter describes the entities that work with a depository: depository participants (DPs), issuers and their RTAs, clearing corporations, and banks or financial institutions involved in pledges. Revise who can become a DP, the DP's role as agent of the depository, how RTAs confirm demat and corporate actions, how clearing corporations interface for settlement, and how the depository supervises DPs through inspections. Questions usually ask which partner performs a given function.

Depository and its Business Partners questions

Question 1

The investor's direct point of contact with the depository system is the:

  1. Registrar and Transfer Agent
  2. Clearing corporation
  3. Issuer company
  4. Depository Participant
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Depository Participant

Investors open and operate demat accounts through DPs, who act as agents of the depository. RTAs and clearing corporations interact with the depository, not usually with the investor directly.

Question 2

A DP must obtain a certificate of registration from:

  1. RBI, as the regulator of payment systems
  2. SEBI, through the depository
  3. The stock exchange
  4. AMFI
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. SEBI, through the depository

A DP is registered with SEBI on the recommendation of the depository it wishes to join. It must meet SEBI's eligibility and net worth norms.

Question 3

The interface between a depository and an issuer for crediting securities in a corporate action is usually the:

  1. Investor's bank
  2. Stock broker
  3. Issuer's RTA
  4. Custodian of an FPI
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Issuer's RTA

RTAs maintain the issuer's records and electronically instruct the depository to credit securities for allotments, bonuses and other corporate actions.

Question 4

Which partner provides the depository with net settlement obligations of members after trading?

  1. The clearing corporation
  2. The investor's depository participant
  3. SEBI
  4. The RTA
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. The clearing corporation

After trading, the clearing corporation determines obligations of each clearing member and coordinates with depositories to move securities at pay-in and pay-out.

Question 5

Depositories monitor their DPs mainly through:

  1. Daily changes to each DP's share price
  2. Periodic inspections and audits
  3. Annual elections among investors
  4. Reports from mutual funds
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Periodic inspections and audits

Depositories inspect DPs periodically and require internal or concurrent audits to check compliance with operational and investor protection norms.

Question 6

Which of the following is eligible to become a DP?

  1. A sole proprietor with no SEBI registration
  2. A listed manufacturing company
  3. A scheduled commercial bank
  4. A partnership of retail investors
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. A scheduled commercial bank

Eligible entities include banks, financial institutions, custodians, stock brokers and certain NBFCs meeting SEBI conditions. Ordinary businesses and unregistered persons are not eligible.

Question 7

An investor's demat account is opened with:

  1. A DP of NSDL or CDSL
  2. The issuer company directly, through its company secretary
  3. SEBI
  4. The stock exchange
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. A DP of NSDL or CDSL

Demat accounts are opened through a DP of either depository. The DP maintains the account on the depository's system.

Question 8

Before its securities can be admitted to a depository, an issuer must:

  1. Distribute a dividend in the preceding year
  2. List on at least two exchanges
  3. Obtain approval from RBI
  4. Enter into an agreement with the depository
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Enter into an agreement with the depository

Admission requires a tripartite or bipartite agreement among the issuer, RTA and depository, after which an ISIN is activated for demat holding.
