DP Functions – Account Opening: NISM Series VI practice questions

8 free questions with answers and explanations on DP Functions – Account Opening, from the NISM Series VI (Depository Operations) syllabus.

Last updated 26 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · How we create questions

What this chapter covers

This chapter covers opening demat accounts: types of accounts (individual, joint, minor, HUF, corporate, NRI, clearing member), KYC and in-person verification, the Basic Services Demat Account, nomination choices, client master records and documents to be provided to clients such as the rights and obligations document. Revise who can hold accounts, how many holders are allowed, how HUF and minor accounts are opened, and the purpose of BSDA. Expect scenario questions about which documents or procedures apply.

DP Functions – Account Opening questions

Question 1

A demat account for a Hindu Undivided Family is opened in the name of:

  1. The Karta, for the HUF
  2. All adult members of the family jointly as co-holders
  3. The eldest daughter
  4. The family's bank
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. The Karta, for the HUF

An HUF account is opened in the name of the Karta on behalf of the HUF, with the HUF's PAN. Joint holding by family members is not used for HUF accounts.

Question 2

The Basic Services Demat Account (BSDA) is designed for:

  1. Corporate clients holding securities worth crores
  2. Clearing members only
  3. Small investors with low holdings
  4. FPIs
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Small investors with low holdings

BSDA offers lower or nil annual maintenance charges for individuals with small holdings and only one demat account across depositories, making demat affordable for small investors.

Question 3

Which of the following is NOT required to open an individual resident demat account?

  1. PAN card details verified with the Income Tax database
  2. Proof of address
  3. In-person or video verification
  4. A recommendation letter from the issuer
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. A recommendation letter from the issuer

KYC requires PAN, identity and address proof and in-person or video-based verification. No issuer recommendation is needed.

Question 4

When opening a demat account, a client who does not want to nominate anyone must:

  1. Nominate the DP's branch manager by default
  2. Submit a declaration opting out
  3. Close the account after a year
  4. Pay a penalty
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Submit a declaration opting out

SEBI requires either a nomination or an explicit opt-out declaration. This ensures the investor makes a conscious choice about succession.

Question 5

An NRI wants to hold shares bought on a repatriation basis. The demat account should be linked to:

  1. An NRO account
  2. A resident savings account
  3. An NRE bank account
  4. A minor's account
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. An NRE bank account

Repatriable holdings require an NRE-linked demat account. Non-repatriable holdings use an NRO-linked account.

Question 6

A demat account for a minor:

  1. Can have the minor as a joint holder with a parent
  2. Can be operated by the minor directly
  3. Cannot be opened
  4. Must have the minor as sole holder
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Must have the minor as sole holder

Minor accounts are held solely by the minor and operated by the guardian. Minors cannot be joint holders.

Question 7

To open a demat account for a company, the DP will require:

  1. The personal tax returns of every shareholder of the company
  2. A board resolution
  3. A letter from SEBI
  4. A succession certificate
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. A board resolution

Corporate accounts require a board resolution authorising the account and naming authorised signatories, along with company KYC documents.

Question 8

Which document explains the rights and obligations of the investor and the DP?

  1. The Rights and Obligations document
  2. The Client Master List of the investor
  3. The DIS booklet
  4. The holding statement
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. The Rights and Obligations document

SEBI prescribes a standard Rights and Obligations document, which the DP must give the client at account opening. It sets out duties of both parties.
