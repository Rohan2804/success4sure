About the NISM Series III-A exam

NISM Series III-A covers the compliance framework for non-fund intermediaries — the SEBI Act, SCRA, intermediaries regulations, insider trading and PFUTP rules, PMLA obligations, KYC and the regulations for brokers, depository participants, merchant bankers, research analysts and investment advisers.

Who should take it: Compliance officers and compliance team members of stock brokers, depository participants, RTAs, merchant bankers and other non-fund intermediaries.

Which of these is the main economic role of a financial system?

  3. Fixing the prices of goods and services

Financial markets and intermediaries mobilise savings and allocate them to productive uses.

  2. IRDAI

Answer: C. PFRDA

Sample 3 · Introduction to Compliance

Which of the following best describes the compliance function in a SEBI-registered intermediary?

  3. Maximising trading revenue for the firm

Answer: D. Ensuring the firm's activities follow applicable laws, regulations and internal policies, and reporting deviations

Sample 4 · SEBI Act, 1992

Under the SEBI Act, 1992, an appeal against an order of the Securities Appellate Tribunal lies with:

  1. The Ministry of Finance
  2. The Supreme Court
  3. The SEBI Board
  4. The High Court
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. The Supreme Court

Section 15Z of the SEBI Act provides for an appeal from SAT to the Supreme Court.

NISM Series III-A mock test FAQs

How many questions are there in the NISM Series III-A exam?

The NISM Series III-A paper has 100 multiple-choice questions for 100 marks, to be completed in 120 minutes. This is the indicative pattern — always confirm it in the latest official notification.

Is there negative marking in NISM Series III-A?

Yes. −0.25 mark per wrong answer (25% of the marks for that question). Our mock tests apply the same rule.

What is the pass mark for NISM Series III-A?

You need 60% to pass. Our mock tests show pass or fail against the same mark.

Are the NISM Series III-A mock tests free?

Yes. All 30 full-length NISM Series III-A mock tests are free, with no payment or plan. Create a free account (one click with Google) to start; your results are saved across devices. You can retake any test as often as you like.

Is NISM Series III-A mandatory for compliance officers?

SEBI requires compliance officers of many non-fund intermediaries, and certain staff in their compliance teams, to hold this certification. Requirements depend on the type of intermediary and your role, so confirm with your firm. Confirm the current rules on nism.ac.in.

How do I prepare for NISM Series III-A?

Because the paper is regulation-heavy, read each chapter with the actual obligation in mind: who must do what, by when and with which disclosure. Make a one-page summary per regulation, then take timed mock tests and note every rule you mix up. Revise intermediary-specific regulations last, as they produce many detail questions.

What is the difference between NISM III-A and III-C?

Series III-A is meant for compliance staff of non-fund intermediaries such as brokers, depository participants and merchant bankers. Series III-C covers fund-based intermediaries such as AMCs, AIF managers and REIT or InvIT managers. Both share the general securities law chapters but differ in the sector-specific regulations.
