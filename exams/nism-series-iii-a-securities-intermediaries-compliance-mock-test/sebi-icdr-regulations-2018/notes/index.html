SEBI (ICDR) Regulations, 2018: NISM Series III-A revision notes

The Issue of Capital and Disclosure Requirements Regulations, 2018 govern public issues, rights issues, preferential allotments, QIPs and bonus issues. For NISM III-A, IPO eligibility, investor allocation, promoter contribution, lock-in and anchor investor rules are the most tested. Numbers appear frequently in questions; learn the standard ones and remember that SEBI amends timelines often.

Last updated 23 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · Free

Key points

1. Eligibility under Regulation 6(1)

An issuer may do an IPO through the profitability route if it has net tangible assets of at least ₹3 crore in each of the preceding three years, average operating profit of at least ₹15 crore in the preceding three years with operating profit in each year, and net worth of at least ₹1 crore in each of the preceding three years, among other conditions.

2. Regulation 6(2) route

An issuer that does not meet the profitability tests can still go public if at least 75% of the net offer is allotted to qualified institutional buyers. Retail allocation is then capped at 10% and non-institutional at 15%. This route suits loss-making technology companies; if the QIB portion is not met, money must be refunded.

3. Allocation under 6(1)

For issues under 6(1), not more than 50% of the net offer goes to QIBs, not less than 15% to non-institutional investors and not less than 35% to retail individual investors (applications up to ₹2 lakh). Up to 5% of the QIB portion is reserved for mutual funds.

4. Anchor investors

Up to 60% of the QIB portion can be allotted to anchor investors one working day before the issue opens, with a minimum application of ₹10 crore. A share of the anchor portion is reserved for domestic mutual funds (and, after 2025 changes, for insurers and pension funds; check current SEBI text). Half of anchor shares are locked in for 30 days and the rest for 90 days after allotment.

5. Promoter contribution and lock-in

Promoters must contribute at least 20% of post-issue capital, locked in for 18 months from allotment (three years where the issue objects include capital expenditure for a project). Promoter holding in excess of the minimum contribution is locked in for six months. Pre-IPO non-promoter shares are generally locked in for six months.

6. Pricing and price band

Issues can be fixed price or book built. In book building, the cap of the price band must not exceed 120% of the floor, and the cap must be at least 105% of the floor. The price band is announced at least two working days before opening. Retail investors may bid at the cut-off price.

7. Minimum subscription and timelines

If minimum subscription of 90% of the fresh issue is not received, the entire amount is refunded. Issues stay open at least three working days. Listing must happen within three working days of issue closure (T+3), as of 2026 — check SEBI's latest circular for any changes.

8. Offer documents

The draft red herring prospectus is filed with SEBI for observations and made public for comments. The red herring prospectus omits final price and size; the prospectus filed after pricing contains them. SEBI observations are valid for a limited period (12 months) within which the issue must open.

Formulas and rules of thumb

  • Promoter minimum contribution = 20% x post-issue paid-up capital (e.g. post-issue capital ₹500 crore means at least ₹100 crore held by promoters and locked in).

Common traps in the exam

  • Under 6(2), QIBs get at least 75% and retail at most 10%; under 6(1) QIBs get at most 50% and retail at least 35%.
  • Anchor lock-in is 50% for 30 days and 50% for 90 days, not the whole for 30 days.
  • The 120% rule is the maximum cap over the floor; at least 105% is the minimum gap.
  • Minimum subscription is 90% of the fresh issue, not 90% of the total including OFS.

Quick recap

ICDR 2018: Reg 6(1) profitability route (NTA ₹3 crore, average operating profit ₹15 crore, net worth ₹1 crore); 6(2) route needs 75% QIB. Allocation under 6(1): QIB up to 50%, NII at least 15%, retail at least 35%. Anchors up to 60% of QIB, locked 30/90 days. Promoters 20% locked 18 months. Cap up to 120% of floor. Minimum subscription 90%. Listing T+3.
