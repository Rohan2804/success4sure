Free NISM Series III-A practice questions

Question 1 · Introduction to the Financial System

Commercial paper, certificates of deposit and call money are all instruments of the:

  1. Commodity derivatives market
  2. Insurance market
  3. Equity capital market
  4. Money market
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Money market

These are short-term instruments with maturities up to one year, which places them in the money market.

Question 2 · Regulatory Framework – General View

The Companies Act, 2013 is administered by:

  1. The Department of Revenue
  2. The Reserve Bank of India
  3. SEBI
  4. The Ministry of Corporate Affairs
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. The Ministry of Corporate Affairs

MCA administers company law; SEBI administers certain provisions on listed and public issues.

Question 3 · Introduction to Compliance

To be effective, a compliance officer should ideally:

  1. Be appointed only when SEBI issues a show-cause notice
  2. Report to the head of sales and earn incentives on sales targets
  3. Handle all front-office trading
  4. Be independent and have direct access to the board or senior management
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Be independent and have direct access to the board or senior management

Independence and access to the top management let the compliance officer escalate issues without conflicts.

Question 4 · SEBI Act, 1992

Which section of the SEBI Act requires intermediaries such as stock brokers to obtain a certificate of registration?

  1. Section 15A
  2. Section 26
  3. Section 12
  4. Section 11B
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Section 12

Section 12 bars intermediaries from dealing in securities without SEBI registration.

Question 5 · SCRA, 1956 and SCRR, 1957

Under the SCRA, a 'spot delivery contract' is one where delivery and payment take place:

  1. Only at the expiry of a derivatives series
  2. At any time agreed by the parties
  3. On the same day as the contract or the next day
  4. Within 30 days
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. On the same day as the contract or the next day

SCRA defines spot delivery contracts as settling the same day or the next day.

Question 6 · SEBI (Intermediaries) Regulations, 2008

Under the SEBI (Intermediaries) Regulations, 2008, the 'fit and proper person' criteria consider, among other things, whether the applicant:

  1. Is listed on a stock exchange
  2. Has integrity, honesty, reputation and no relevant disqualification
  3. Has an office in every state
  4. Has the highest market share
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Has integrity, honesty, reputation and no relevant disqualification

The criteria focus on integrity, reputation and absence of disqualifications such as convictions or winding-up orders.

Question 7 · SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations

A designated person of a listed company sells shares and buys them back two months later. Under the insider trading regulations this is:

  1. A prohibited contra trade, since the opposite trade was within six months
  2. Permitted, because the gap exceeds 30 days
  3. Permitted, if the shares are held in demat
  4. Prohibited only if the trade value exceeds ₹1 crore
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. A prohibited contra trade, since the opposite trade was within six months

Designated persons may not execute an opposite transaction within six months of a previous trade.

Question 8 · SEBI (PFUTP) Regulations, 2003

An entity repeatedly trades among its own group accounts to create misleading volumes in a stock. This is an example of:

  1. Insider trading only if UPSI is involved
  2. Legitimate arbitrage
  3. A manipulative and unfair trade practice prohibited under PFUTP
  4. Permitted market making
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. A manipulative and unfair trade practice prohibited under PFUTP

Circular or synchronised trading that creates a false appearance of trading is prohibited under PFUTP.

Question 9 · Prevention of Money Laundering Act, 2002

Under the PMLA framework, a reporting entity must furnish a Suspicious Transaction Report to FIU-IND within:

  1. 7 working days of being satisfied that the transaction is suspicious
  2. 30 days of the transaction
  3. The end of the financial year
  4. 24 hours of account opening
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 7 working days of being satisfied that the transaction is suspicious

STRs must be filed within 7 working days of the principal officer concluding that a transaction is suspicious.

Question 10 · SEBI (KYC Registration Agency) Regulations, 2011

The main purpose of KYC Registration Agencies is to:

  1. Maintain KYC records centrally so that an investor's KYC done through one intermediary can be used by others
  2. Rate the creditworthiness of investors
  3. Approve IPO allotments
  4. Collect stamp duty on securities transactions
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Maintain KYC records centrally so that an investor's KYC done through one intermediary can be used by others

KRAs avoid duplicate KYC by storing records that other SEBI intermediaries can access.

Question 11 · SEBI (Foreign Portfolio Investors) Regulations, 2019

Under the FPI Regulations, 2019, FPIs are classified into:

  1. Category I, II and III
  2. Category I and Category II
  3. Retail and wholesale
  4. Domestic and foreign
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Category I and Category II

The 2019 regulations replaced the earlier three categories with two.

Question 12 · SEBI (Stock Brokers) Regulations

A stock broker must issue a contract note to the client within:

  1. One month of the trade
  2. 24 hours of execution of the trade
  3. The end of the settlement week
  4. 7 days of the trade
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 24 hours of execution of the trade

Contract notes, physical or electronic, must reach the client within 24 hours of the trade.

Question 13 · SEBI (Merchant Bankers) Regulations etc.

A merchant banker should not lead-manage an issue if:

  1. The issue size exceeds ₹100 crore
  2. It is an associate of the issuer company
  3. The issue is a rights issue
  4. The issuer is a first-time issuer
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. It is an associate of the issuer company

Merchant banker regulations bar a merchant banker from leading an issue of an associate, to avoid conflicts of interest.

Question 14 · SEBI (ICDR) Regulations, 2018

In a book-built IPO under Regulation 6(1) of ICDR, the minimum allocation to retail individual investors is:

  1. 50% of the net offer
  2. 15% of the net offer
  3. 35% of the net offer
  4. 10% of the net offer
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 35% of the net offer

Under Regulation 6(1), RIIs get at least 35%, NIIs at least 15% and QIBs up to 50%.

Question 15 · Depositories Act, 1996

Under the Depositories Act, 1996, securities held in a depository are:

  1. Identified by individual certificate numbers
  2. Required to be held in physical form also
  3. Fungible, with no distinctive numbers identifying individual securities
  4. Owned by the depository participant
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Fungible, with no distinctive numbers identifying individual securities

Securities in a depository are fungible and carry no distinctive numbers.
