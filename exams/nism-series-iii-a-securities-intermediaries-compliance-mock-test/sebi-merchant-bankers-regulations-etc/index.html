SEBI (Merchant Bankers) Regulations etc.: NISM Series III-A practice questions

Last updated 23 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · How we create questions

What this chapter covers

This chapter covers merchant bankers and related issue intermediaries such as bankers to an issue and underwriters. It explains registration, categories and capital requirements of merchant bankers, their responsibilities as lead managers in public issues, due diligence, restrictions on associates, the code of conduct and disclosure obligations. It also covers the role of underwriters, underwriting agreements and the obligations of bankers to an issue in collecting application money and making refunds. When revising, focus on due diligence duties, conflicts of interest and the division of responsibilities among issue intermediaries.

SEBI (Merchant Bankers) Regulations etc. questions

Question 1

Before an offer document is filed, the lead manager must submit to SEBI a:

  1. Tax clearance certificate
  2. No objection certificate from the stock exchange
  3. Certificate of incorporation
  4. Due diligence certificate
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Due diligence certificate

The lead merchant banker certifies that it has exercised due diligence and that disclosures in the offer document are true, fair and adequate.

Question 2

A merchant banker's responsibility for the accuracy of disclosures in an offer document is:

  1. Not reduced by the issuer's own responsibility for the contents
  2. Limited to errors in the price band and the basis of issue price only
  3. Fully transferred to the issuer once the document is filed
  4. Applicable only if the issue is undersubscribed
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Not reduced by the issuer's own responsibility for the contents

Lead managers must independently verify disclosures. The issuer's responsibility does not absolve the merchant banker of its due diligence obligations.

Question 3

Which intermediary collects application money from investors in a public issue and handles refunds?

  1. Custodian
  2. Banker to an issue
  3. Debenture trustee
  4. Credit rating agency
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Banker to an issue

Bankers to an issue accept applications and money, maintain escrow and public issue accounts, and handle refunds in coordination with the registrar.

Question 4

An underwriter has agreed to underwrite part of an issue. If the issue is undersubscribed and the underwriting is called on, the underwriter must:

  1. Ask SEBI to extend the issue indefinitely
  2. Subscribe for the shortfall in its share within the time stated in the agreement
  3. Refund all investors' money and ask the issuer to reopen the issue at a lower price
  4. Withdraw from the agreement without consequences
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Subscribe for the shortfall in its share within the time stated in the agreement

An underwriter's commitment becomes binding when devolvement occurs, and it must subscribe to the unsubscribed portion as per the underwriting agreement.

Question 5

Which of the following is a typical activity of a merchant banker?

  1. Settling trades as a clearing corporation
  2. Issuing insurance policies to investors
  3. Managing public issues, including drafting the offer document and coordinating intermediaries
  4. Accepting public deposits like a bank
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Managing public issues, including drafting the offer document and coordinating intermediaries

Merchant bankers manage issues, advise on capital structure, conduct due diligence and coordinate other intermediaries. Accepting deposits and settlement are not their functions.

Question 6

A merchant banker learns price sensitive information about an issuer client during due diligence. It must:

  1. Use it to advise its other clients on trading
  2. Publish it on its website for transparency so that all investors have equal access
  3. Not use the information for its own trading or share it except as permitted
  4. Trade quickly before the issue closes
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Not use the information for its own trading or share it except as permitted

Merchant bankers are bound by confidentiality and insider trading rules and must not misuse information obtained from clients. Misuse could breach both PIT and PFUTP rules.

Question 7

Under the merchant banker framework, a lead manager must ensure that the advertisements for a public issue:

  1. Are released before SEBI's observations to build demand
  2. Highlight only positive aspects without risk factors
  3. Promise a guaranteed listing gain to attract retail investors
  4. Are consistent with the offer document and not misleading
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Are consistent with the offer document and not misleading

Issue advertisements must be truthful, fair and consistent with the offer document, and must not contain misleading statements or promises of returns.

Question 8

Following the post-issue period, the lead merchant banker is responsible for:

  1. Acting as the issuer's statutory auditor
  2. Running the company's day-to-day operations
  3. Monitoring allotment, refunds, listing and other post-issue activities until completion
  4. Nothing, as its role ends on the issue closing date
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Monitoring allotment, refunds, listing and other post-issue activities until completion

Post-issue, the lead manager coordinates with the registrar, bankers and exchanges to ensure timely allotment, refunds, unblocking and listing. Its responsibility ends only after these steps are complete.
