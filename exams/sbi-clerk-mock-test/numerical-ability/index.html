Numerical Ability: SBI Clerk practice questions

15 free questions with answers and explanations on Numerical Ability, from the SBI Clerk (SBI Junior Associate (Prelims)) syllabus.

Last updated 16 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · How we create questions

What this section covers

Numerical Ability in SBI Clerk covers simplification and approximation, number series, percentages, profit and loss, simple and compound interest, ratio and proportion, averages, ages, time and work, speed and distance, mensuration and basic data interpretation. Most questions are calculation-heavy rather than tricky, so speed is key. Memorise squares, cubes and fraction–percentage equivalents, practise BODMAS carefully and learn standard formulas such as the half-yearly compounding rule. In DI, find totals first and then calculate shares or growth.

Numerical Ability questions

Question 1

Find the value of ? 48 ÷ 6 × 9 − 25 + 7² = ?

  1. 86
  2. 96
  3. 106
  4. 94
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 96

Following BODMAS: 7² = 49, then 48 ÷ 6 = 8 and 8 × 9 = 72. Finally 72 − 25 + 49 = 96. Division and multiplication are done left to right before addition and subtraction.

Question 2

Find the approximate value of ? 24.98% of 639.9 + 15.02 × 6.97 = ?

  1. 265
  2. 245
  3. 285
  4. 225
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 265

Round off: 25% of 640 = 160 and 15 × 7 = 105. Adding gives 160 + 105 = 265. Rounding each value to the nearest convenient number keeps the error very small.

Question 3

Find the next term of the series: 7, 10, 16, 25, 37, ?

  1. 49
  2. 50
  3. 54
  4. 52
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 52

The differences are 3, 6, 9 and 12, increasing by 3 each time. The next difference is 15, so the next term is 37 + 15 = 52.

Question 4

Find the next term of the series: 3, 6, 18, 72, 360, ?

  1. 1800
  2. 2520
  3. 2160
  4. 1440
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 2160

Each term is multiplied by 2, 3, 4 and 5 in turn: 3×2=6, 6×3=18, 18×4=72, 72×5=360. The next multiplier is 6, so 360 × 6 = 2160.

Question 5

Identify the number that does not fit the pattern: 1, 4, 9, 16, 26, 36

  1. 16
  2. 26
  3. 36
  4. 9
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 26

The series lists perfect squares: 1², 2², 3², 4², 5², 6². The fifth term should be 25, so 26 is the wrong number. All other terms are correct squares.

Question 6

Ravi's monthly salary of ₹25,000 is first increased by 10% and then the new salary is reduced by 10%. What is his final salary?

  1. ₹24,750
  2. ₹25,000
  3. ₹24,500
  4. ₹25,250
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. ₹24,750

After the increase: 25,000 × 1.10 = 27,500. After the cut: 27,500 × 0.90 = 24,750. The 10% cut applies to a larger amount, so the salary does not return to ₹25,000.

Question 7

A shopkeeper buys a fan for ₹450 and sells it for ₹540. What is his profit percentage?

  1. 18%
  2. 16.67%
  3. 25%
  4. 20%
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 20%

Profit = 540 − 450 = ₹90. Profit percentage is calculated on cost price: 90/450 × 100 = 20%. Calculating on selling price would wrongly give 16.67%.

Question 8

What simple interest will ₹6,000 earn in 3 years at 8% per annum?

  1. ₹1,280
  2. ₹1,560
  3. ₹1,440
  4. ₹1,500
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. ₹1,440

Simple interest = P × R × T / 100 = 6,000 × 8 × 3 / 100 = ₹1,440. Interest is calculated only on the original principal each year.

Question 9

Find the compound interest on ₹10,000 for 1 year at 10% per annum, compounded half-yearly.

  1. ₹1,000
  2. ₹1,025
  3. ₹1,050
  4. ₹1,100
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. ₹1,025

Half-yearly compounding means a rate of 5% for 2 periods. Amount = 10,000 × 1.05² = ₹11,025, so CI = ₹1,025. Annual compounding would give only ₹1,000.

Question 10

₹1,800 is divided among A, B and C in the ratio 2 : 3 : 4. How much does C receive?

  1. ₹800
  2. ₹600
  3. ₹720
  4. ₹900
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. ₹800

Total parts = 2 + 3 + 4 = 9. C's share = 1,800 × 4/9 = ₹800. B would get ₹600 and A ₹400, which together with C's share total ₹1,800.

Question 11

The average of five numbers is 40. When one number is removed, the average of the remaining four becomes 38. What number was removed?

  1. 42
  2. 38
  3. 50
  4. 48
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 48

Sum of five numbers = 5 × 40 = 200. Sum of four numbers = 4 × 38 = 152. The removed number = 200 − 152 = 48.

Question 12

A 150 m long train is running at 54 km/h. How long will it take to pass an electric pole?

  1. 9 seconds
  2. 12 seconds
  3. 10 seconds
  4. 15 seconds
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 10 seconds

54 km/h × 5/18 = 15 m/s. To pass a pole, the train covers only its own length, so time = 150 / 15 = 10 seconds.

Question 13

A can complete a piece of work in 12 days and B in 24 days. In how many days can they finish it working together?

  1. 9 days
  2. 8 days
  3. 6 days
  4. 18 days
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 8 days

A does 1/12 and B does 1/24 of the work per day. Together: 1/12 + 1/24 = 3/24 = 1/8, so they finish in 8 days. Averaging 12 and 24 to get 18 is wrong.

Question 14

The present ages of two brothers are in the ratio 5 : 7. After 6 years the ratio will be 3 : 4. What is the present age of the elder brother?

  1. 42 years
  2. 35 years
  3. 28 years
  4. 48 years
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 42 years

Let ages be 5x and 7x. Then (5x + 6)/(7x + 6) = 3/4, so 20x + 24 = 21x + 18, giving x = 6. The elder brother is 7 × 6 = 42 years old.

Question 15

Loans disbursed (₹ lakh) by four branches in a month: Branch A – 45, Branch B – 60, Branch C – 75, Branch D – 70. What percentage of the total loans was disbursed by Branch C?

  1. 28%
  2. 32%
  3. 25%
  4. 30%
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 30%

Total disbursement = 45 + 60 + 75 + 70 = ₹250 lakh. Branch C's share = 75/250 × 100 = 30%. Check that the total is right before finding any percentage share.
