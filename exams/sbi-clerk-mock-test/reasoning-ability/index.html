Reasoning Ability: SBI Clerk practice questions

15 free questions with answers and explanations on Reasoning Ability, from the SBI Clerk (SBI Junior Associate (Prelims)) syllabus.

Last updated 23 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · How we create questions

What this section covers

Reasoning Ability for SBI Clerk includes seating arrangements and simple puzzles, syllogisms, inequalities, blood relations, direction sense, coding-decoding, ranking and ordering, alphabet and number analogies, and odd-one-out questions. Place fixed clues first in puzzles, then test the remaining cases. Draw family trees and direction diagrams instead of solving mentally, and use Venn diagrams for syllogisms. For inequalities, chain the relations and check whether a conclusion is definitely true. Regular timed practice builds speed for prelims.

Reasoning Ability questions

Question 1

Six persons J, K, L, M, N and O sit in a row facing north. • M sits fourth from the left end. • J sits second to the left of M. • N sits at one of the extreme ends. • K is an immediate neighbour of both M and N. • O sits immediately to the right of J. Who sits at the extreme left end?

  1. N
  2. J
  3. L
  4. O
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. L

M is 4th and J is 2nd. K must touch both M and N, so N is at the right end (6th) and K is 5th. O is 3rd, right of J, leaving L at the extreme left.

Question 2

Six persons J, K, L, M, N and O sit in a row facing north. • M sits fourth from the left end. • J sits second to the left of M. • N sits at one of the extreme ends. • K is an immediate neighbour of both M and N. • O sits immediately to the right of J. How many persons sit between L and K?

  1. Two
  2. Three
  3. Four
  4. One
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Three

The final order from left is L, J, O, M, K, N. Between L (1st) and K (5th) sit J, O and M, which makes three persons.

Question 3

Statements: All pens are books. Some books are bags. Conclusions: I. Some pens are bags. II. Some books are pens.

  1. Only conclusion II follows
  2. Only conclusion I follows
  3. Both I and II follow
  4. Neither I nor II follows
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Only conclusion II follows

Since all pens are books, some books are definitely pens, so II follows. The bags that are books may lie outside the pen circle, so I is not certain.

Question 4

Statements: No cup is a plate. All plates are bowls. Conclusions: I. Some bowls are not cups. II. Some cups are bowls.

  1. Only conclusion II follows
  2. Both I and II follow
  3. Neither I nor II follows
  4. Only conclusion I follows
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Only conclusion I follows

The bowls that are plates can never be cups, so some bowls are not cups and I follows. Cups may or may not overlap with bowls outside the plate region, so II is not definite.

Question 5

Statements: M ≥ N > O; O ≥ P; Q > M Conclusions: I. Q > O II. N > P

  1. Only conclusion I follows
  2. Only conclusion II follows
  3. Both I and II follow
  4. Neither I nor II follows
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Both I and II follow

Combining: Q > M ≥ N > O ≥ P. So Q > O is definitely true, and N > O ≥ P gives N > P. Both conclusions follow.

Question 6

A is the brother of B. B is the daughter of C. D is the father of C. How is A related to D?

  1. Son
  2. Grandson
  3. Nephew
  4. Granddaughter
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Grandson

A and B are children of C, and D is C's father. So A is D's grandchild; since A is a brother, A is D's grandson. 'Granddaughter' would fit B, not A.

Question 7

Sunita walks 5 km towards the east, then turns left and walks 12 km. What is the shortest distance between her and the starting point?

  1. 13 km
  2. 17 km
  3. 12 km
  4. 15 km
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 13 km

Turning left from east means walking north. Her path forms a right angle, so the distance = √(5² + 12²) = √169 = 13 km. 17 km is the total distance walked, not the shortest.

Question 8

A man is facing north. He turns 90° clockwise and then 135° anticlockwise. Which direction is he facing now?

  1. North-East
  2. South-West
  3. West
  4. North-West
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. North-West

After turning 90° clockwise he faces east. Turning 135° anticlockwise from east takes him past north by 45°, so he faces north-west. The net turn is 45° anticlockwise from north.

Question 9

If MONEY is written as NPOFZ in a code, how will CHEQUE be written?

  1. DIERVF
  2. BGDPTD
  3. DIFRVF
  4. DIFRUF
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. DIFRVF

Each letter is replaced by the next letter of the alphabet: M→N, O→P and so on. Applying this to CHEQUE gives D, I, F, R, V, F, i.e. DIFRVF.

Question 10

If A = 1, B = 2, C = 3 and so on up to Z = 26, what is the total value of the word LOAN?

  1. 40
  2. 42
  3. 44
  4. 38
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 42

L = 12, O = 15, A = 1 and N = 14. Adding them: 12 + 15 + 1 + 14 = 42. Learning the positions of letters such as E, J, O, T and Y speeds up such questions.

Question 11

Rohit is 15th from the top and 26th from the bottom in his class. How many students are there in the class?

  1. 40
  2. 41
  3. 39
  4. 42
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 40

Total = position from top + position from bottom − 1 = 15 + 26 − 1 = 40. We subtract 1 because Rohit is counted in both positions.

Question 12

Among five friends, P is taller than Q. R is shorter than Q but taller than S. T is the tallest of all. Who is the shortest?

  1. R
  2. Q
  3. P
  4. S
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. S

The order from tallest is T, P, Q, R, S. Since R is shorter than Q but taller than S, and no one else is placed below S, S is the shortest.

Question 13

Which of these numbers is the odd one out: 27, 64, 144, 216?

  1. 27
  2. 64
  3. 144
  4. 216
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 144

27, 64 and 216 are perfect cubes (3³, 4³ and 6³). 144 is a perfect square (12²) but not a perfect cube, so it is the odd one out.

Question 14

Complete the analogy: 6 : 35 :: 9 : ?

  1. 81
  2. 80
  3. 72
  4. 90
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 80

The rule is n² − 1: 6² − 1 = 35. Applying the same to 9 gives 81 − 1 = 80. The option 81 is only 9², missing the subtraction step.

Question 15

Which letter is 7th to the right of the 15th letter from the left end of the English alphabet?

  1. V
  2. U
  3. W
  4. T
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. V

The 15th letter from the left is O. Moving 7 places to the right means 15 + 7 = 22nd letter, which is V.
