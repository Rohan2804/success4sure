About the SBI Clerk exam

The SBI Clerk (Junior Associate) preliminary exam is the first stage of State Bank of India's clerical recruitment, testing English Language, Numerical Ability and Reasoning Ability.

Who should take it: Graduates applying for Junior Associate (customer support and sales) posts at State Bank of India.

Sample 1 · English Language

Read the passage and answer the question. Meera wanted to send money to her parents in another city. Instead of travelling to the bank, she used her phone to transfer the amount within seconds. Her parents received a message as soon as the money reached their account. How did Meera send the money?

  1. By travelling to her parents' city
  2. By posting a cheque
  3. Through a friend who was travelling
  4. Through a transfer made on her phone
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Through a transfer made on her phone

The passage says she used her phone to transfer the amount.

Sample 2 · Numerical Ability

What will come in place of the question mark (?) (15 × 12 ÷ 4) + 3³ − 18 = ?

  1. 54
  2. 45
  3. 63
  4. 72
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 54

15 × 12 ÷ 4 = 45; 45 + 27 − 18 = 54.

Sample 3 · Reasoning Ability

Seven persons A, B, C, D, E, F and G sit in a row facing north. • G sits exactly in the middle of the row. • A and D are the immediate neighbours of G. • E sits at one of the extreme ends. • Only one person sits between E and D. • C sits second to the left of A. • B sits second to the left of G. Who sits immediately to the right of B?

  1. C
  2. G
  3. A
  4. D
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. A

If A were 5th, C would be 3rd = D's seat; so A = 3, D = 5, E = 7, C = 1, B = 2, F = 6. Right of B is A.

Sample 4 · English Language

Read the passage and answer the question. Trees in cities do more than provide shade. They absorb dust, reduce noise and keep streets cooler in summer. Some municipal bodies now plant trees along busy roads to make them more pleasant for walkers. Which of the following is NOT mentioned as a benefit of city trees?

  1. They absorb dust
  2. They reduce noise
  3. They increase the price of land
  4. They keep streets cooler
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. They increase the price of land

Land prices are never mentioned; the other three benefits are listed.

Sample 5 · English Language

Read the passage and answer the question. Ravi kept missing his loan instalments because he never tracked his monthly expenses. After a bank officer showed him how to keep a simple budget, he cleared his dues within a year and even began saving. Which is the most suitable title for the passage?

  1. The Strict Bank Officer
  2. Why Loans Are Dangerous
  3. A Budget That Changed Things
  4. Saving Without Earning
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. A Budget That Changed Things

The turning point in the passage is learning to budget, which helped Ravi clear dues and save.

SBI Clerk mock test FAQs

How many questions are there in the SBI Clerk exam?

The SBI Clerk paper has 100 multiple-choice questions for 100 marks, to be completed in 60 minutes. This is the indicative pattern — always confirm it in the latest official notification.

Is there negative marking in SBI Clerk?

Yes. −0.25 mark per wrong answer (25% of the marks for that question). Our mock tests apply the same rule.

What is the target score for SBI Clerk?

Cut-offs vary by year and category; our mock tests use 45% as a target score to aim for.

How many SBI Clerk mock tests do I get?
