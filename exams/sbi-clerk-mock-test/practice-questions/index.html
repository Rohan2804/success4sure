Question 1 · English Language

Choose the word that best fills the blank. Please ________ your passbook updated regularly.

  1. kept
  2. keeps
  3. keeping
  4. keep
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. keep

An imperative sentence uses the base form of the verb: 'Please keep'.

Question 2 · Numerical Ability

What approximate value should come in place of the question mark (?) (64.02% of 499.9) ÷ 7.98 = ?

  1. 32
  2. 40
  3. 45
  4. 48
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 40

≈ 64% of 500 = 320; 320 ÷ 8 = 40.

Question 3 · Reasoning Ability

Seven persons A, B, C, D, E, F and G sit in a row facing north. • G sits exactly in the middle of the row. • A and D are the immediate neighbours of G. • E sits at one of the extreme ends. • Only one person sits between E and D. • C sits second to the left of A. • B sits second to the left of G. How many persons sit between C and F?

  1. Three
  2. Four
  3. Five
  4. Two
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Four

Order: C, B, A, G, D, F, E. Between C (1) and F (6): B, A, G, D.

Question 4 · English Language

Choose the option that best fills the blank. The train was delayed ________ heavy rain.

  1. due to
  2. despite
  3. although
  4. instead of
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. due to

The rain was the cause of the delay, so 'due to' fits.

Question 5 · Numerical Ability

What will come in place of the question mark (?) 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ?

  1. 40
  2. 36
  3. 42
  4. 44
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 42

Terms are n(n + 1): 1×2, 2×3, …, 6×7 = 42.

Question 6 · Reasoning Ability

Five persons V, W, X, Y and Z live on the five floors of a building numbered 1 (lowest) to 5 (top). Z lives on an odd-numbered floor but not on the top floor. Y lives immediately below Z. W lives below Y. X lives above V. Who lives on the floor immediately above Z?

  1. X
  2. Y
  3. V
  4. W
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. V

Z = 3 (Z = 1 leaves no room below for Y), Y = 2, W = 1; X above V gives V = 4, X = 5.

Question 7 · English Language

Choose the word that best fills the blank. She waited for ________ hour at the counter.

  1. a
  2. much
  3. many
  4. an
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. an

'Hour' begins with a vowel sound (silent h), so 'an hour'.

Question 8 · Numerical Ability

What will come in place of the question mark (?) 5, 11, 23, 47, 95, ?

  1. 191
  2. 189
  3. 190
  4. 193
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 191

Each term = previous × 2 + 1: 95 × 2 + 1 = 191.

Question 9 · Reasoning Ability

Statements: Some cards are coins. Some coins are notes. Conclusions: I. Some cards are notes. II. No card is a note.

  1. Only conclusion I follows
  2. Only conclusion II follows
  3. Either I or II follows
  4. Neither I nor II follows
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Either I or II follows

Neither is definite on its own, but I and II form a complementary pair, so either I or II follows.

Question 10 · English Language

Identify the part of the sentence that contains an error. My brother and I (A) / goes to the bank (B) / every Saturday (C) / to deposit money. (D)

  1. (A)
  2. (B)
  3. (C)
  4. (D)
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. (B)

A compound subject ('My brother and I') takes a plural verb: 'go'.

Question 11 · Numerical Ability

A student scored 432 marks out of 600. What percentage of marks did he score?

  1. 72%
  2. 70%
  3. 74%
  4. 68%
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 72%

432/600 × 100 = 72%.

Question 12 · Reasoning Ability

Statements: All clerks are officers. All officers are graduates. Conclusions: I. All clerks are graduates. II. Some graduates are clerks.

  1. Both I and II follow
  2. Only conclusion I follows
  3. Only conclusion II follows
  4. Neither I nor II follows
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Both I and II follow

Clerks ⊂ officers ⊂ graduates gives I; converting I gives 'some graduates are clerks' (II).

Question 13 · English Language

Identify the part of the sentence that contains an error. He is more taller (A) / than his brother (B) / but shorter (C) / than his father. (D)

  1. (A)
  2. (B)
  3. (C)
  4. (D)
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. (A)

'More taller' is a double comparative; it should be 'taller'.

Question 14 · Numerical Ability

The marked price of a watch is ₹2,500. If a discount of 12% is given, what is the selling price?

  1. ₹2,300
  2. ₹2,120
  3. ₹2,250
  4. ₹2,200
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. ₹2,200

SP = 2,500 × 0.88 = ₹2,200.

Question 15 · Reasoning Ability

Statements: P < Q ≤ R; S ≥ R > T Conclusions: I. T > P II. Q < S

  1. Neither I nor II follows
  2. Only conclusion I follows
  3. Only conclusion II follows
  4. Both I and II follow
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Neither I nor II follows

T and P are not connected by a definite relation. S ≥ R ≥ Q gives only S ≥ Q, so II is not certain. Neither follows.
