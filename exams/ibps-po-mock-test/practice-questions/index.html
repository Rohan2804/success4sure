Question 1 · English Language

Choose the word that best fills the blank. To curb rising inflation, the central bank decided to ________ the policy rate by 25 basis points.

  1. raise
  2. lower
  3. waive
  4. dilute
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. raise

Raising the policy rate makes borrowing costlier and reduces demand, which helps curb inflation.

Question 2 · Quantitative Aptitude

What will come in place of the question mark (?) in the series? 7, 15, 32, 67, 138, ?

  1. 279
  2. 283
  3. 281
  4. 276
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 281

Pattern ×2+1, ×2+2, ×2+3, ×2+4, ×2+5: 138 × 2 + 5 = 281.

Question 3 · Reasoning Ability

Seven persons A, B, C, D, E, F and G live on seven different floors of a building numbered 1 (lowest) to 7 (top). • C lives on an even-numbered floor which is neither the lowest nor the highest even-numbered floor. • Exactly two persons live between C and B. • E lives above B. • G lives immediately above E. • Only one person lives between G and F. • A lives on an even-numbered floor. • D lives above F. How many persons live between D and G?

  1. Three
  2. Two
  3. Four
  4. One
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Three

Arrangement (1→7): B, E, G, C, F, A, D. Between D (7) and G (3) are C, F, A – three persons.

Question 4 · English Language

Choose the word that best fills the blank. Despite repeated reminders, the borrower failed to ________ the loan instalments on time.

  1. reply
  2. repay
  3. repeal
  4. reprise
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. repay

A borrower 'repays' instalments; the other words do not collocate with 'loan instalments'.

Question 5 · Quantitative Aptitude

Two equations I and II are given. Solve both and choose the correct relationship between x and y. I. x² − 11x + 30 = 0 II. y² − 15y + 56 = 0

  1. x > y
  2. x < y
  3. x ≥ y
  4. Relationship cannot be established
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. x < y

x = 5, 6 (factors of 30 adding to 11); y = 7, 8. Every x is smaller than every y, so x < y.

Question 6 · Reasoning Ability

Six persons J, K, L, M, N and O sit in a row facing north. • M sits at one of the extreme ends. • Exactly two persons sit between M and J. • K sits immediately to the left of M. • L sits second to the left of O. • N is not an immediate neighbour of J. Who sits third from the right end?

  1. O
  2. J
  3. L
  4. K
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. O

K left of M forces M = 6, K = 5, J = 3; L and O take 2 and 4, N = 1. Order: N, L, J, O, K, M; third from right is O.

Question 7 · English Language

Choose the pair of words that best fills both blanks. The audit team was ________ to find that the records had been ________ maintained, with not a single entry missing.

  1. pleased, meticulously
  2. dismayed, carelessly
  3. pleased, poorly
  4. shocked, hardly
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. pleased, meticulously

'Not a single entry missing' shows careful record-keeping (meticulously), which would please the auditors.

Question 8 · Quantitative Aptitude

The population of a town increased by 10% in the first year and decreased by 10% in the second year. If the population at the end of two years was 29,700, what was the initial population?

  1. 29,700
  2. 30,300
  3. 27,000
  4. 30,000
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 30,000

P × 1.1 × 0.9 = 0.99P = 29,700, so P = 30,000.

Question 9 · Reasoning Ability

Statements: Only a few banks are branches. All branches are offices. No office is a vault. Conclusions: I. Some banks are offices. II. No branch is a vault.

  1. Both I and II follow
  2. Only conclusion I follows
  3. Only conclusion II follows
  4. Neither I nor II follows
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Both I and II follow

Some banks are branches and all branches are offices, so some banks are offices. Branches lie inside offices, which have nothing in common with vaults, so no branch is a vault.

Question 10 · English Language

Identify the part of the sentence that contains a grammatical error. Neither the manager nor his assistants (A) / was present in the branch (B) / when the auditors (C) / arrived on Monday morning. (D)

  1. (A)
  2. (B)
  3. (C)
  4. (D)
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. (B)

With 'neither…nor', the verb agrees with the nearer subject 'assistants', so 'was' should be 'were'.

Question 11 · Quantitative Aptitude

A shopkeeper marks an article 40% above its cost price and allows a discount of 15%. If he earns a profit of ₹171, what is the cost price?

  1. ₹950
  2. ₹900
  3. ₹855
  4. ₹1,140
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. ₹900

SP = 1.40 × 0.85 = 1.19 × CP, so profit = 19% of CP = 171 → CP = 171 ÷ 0.19 = ₹900.

Question 12 · Reasoning Ability

Statements: Some coins are notes. All notes are cheques. No cheque is a draft. Conclusions: I. Some coins are not drafts. II. Some notes are drafts.

  1. Only conclusion II follows
  2. Either I or II follows
  3. Only conclusion I follows
  4. Both I and II follow
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Only conclusion I follows

The coins that are notes are cheques and hence not drafts, so I follows. Notes lie within cheques, which have no drafts, so II is false.

Question 13 · English Language

Identify the part of the sentence that contains a grammatical error. The committee submitted its report (A) / to the board last week (B) / after discussing about the proposal (C) / for several months. (D)

  1. (A)
  2. (B)
  3. (C)
  4. (D)
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. (C)

'Discuss' is a transitive verb and takes no preposition; it should be 'after discussing the proposal'.

Question 14 · Quantitative Aptitude

The difference between the compound interest (compounded annually) and the simple interest on a sum for 2 years at 8% per annum is ₹96. What is the sum?

  1. ₹12,000
  2. ₹15,000
  3. ₹18,000
  4. ₹16,500
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. ₹15,000

For 2 years, CI − SI = P(r/100)² → P × 0.0064 = 96 → P = ₹15,000.

Question 15 · Reasoning Ability

Statements: P ≥ Q > R = S; T < S ≤ U Conclusions: I. P > T II. U > R

  1. Only conclusion II follows
  2. Either I or II follows
  3. Both I and II follow
  4. Only conclusion I follows
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Only conclusion I follows

P ≥ Q > R = S > T gives P > T. U ≥ S = R gives only U ≥ R, so II does not follow.
