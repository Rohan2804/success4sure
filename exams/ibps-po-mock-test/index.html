About the IBPS PO exam

The IBPS PO preliminary exam is the first stage of recruitment for Probationary Officers in participating public sector banks. It tests English Language, Quantitative Aptitude and Reasoning Ability in sectional, timed papers.

Who should take it: Graduates applying for officer (PO/MT) posts in public sector banks through IBPS.

Sample 1 · English Language

Read the passage and answer the question. Microfinance institutions extend small, collateral-free loans to borrowers who lack formal credit histories. Because many such borrowers are women in rural households, lenders often rely on group guarantees, where members of a group share responsibility for repayment. Critics argue that peer pressure within these groups can push borrowers into taking fresh loans merely to repay old ones. According to the passage, why do lenders rely on group guarantees?

  1. Because rural women prefer to borrow in large groups
  2. Because peer pressure guarantees high profits for lenders
  3. Because the borrowers usually cannot offer collateral or a credit record
  4. Because the government requires every microloan to be guaranteed
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Because the borrowers usually cannot offer collateral or a credit record

The passage says the loans are collateral-free and the borrowers lack credit histories, so the group guarantee replaces these securities.

Sample 3 · Reasoning Ability

Seven persons A, B, C, D, E, F and G live on seven different floors of a building numbered 1 (lowest) to 7 (top). • C lives on an even-numbered floor which is neither the lowest nor the highest even-numbered floor. • Exactly two persons live between C and B. • E lives above B. • G lives immediately above E. • Only one person lives between G and F. • A lives on an even-numbered floor. • D lives above F. Who lives on the floor immediately above C?

  1. A
  2. F
  3. G
  4. D
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. F

C = 4; B = 1 (B = 7 fails as E is above B); E–G = 2–3 (other pairs clash), F = 5, A = 6, D = 7. Floor 5 is F.

Sample 4 · English Language

Read the passage and answer the question. When a country's currency weakens, its exports become cheaper for foreign buyers, which can help exporters win orders. At the same time, imported goods such as crude oil and electronic components become costlier, and these higher costs can feed into domestic prices. Policymakers therefore rarely welcome a sharp fall in the currency, even if exporters gain from it. Which of the following can be inferred from the passage?

  1. A weaker currency always lowers domestic inflation
  2. A weaker currency has both benefits and costs for an economy
  3. Exporters lose orders when the currency weakens
  4. Policymakers prefer a sharp fall in the currency
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. A weaker currency has both benefits and costs for an economy

The passage lists a gain (cheaper exports) and a cost (costlier imports feeding inflation), so the effect is mixed.

Sample 5 · English Language

Read the passage and answer the question. The new branch manager was known for her candid assessments; she told her team plainly where they had fallen short and what needed to change. Though some found her manner abrupt at first, most came to value her honesty. Which word is most nearly the SAME in meaning as 'candid' as used in the passage?

  1. Secretive
  2. Polite
  3. Frank
  4. Hasty
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Frank

'Candid' means open and honest; the passage confirms this with 'told her team plainly' and 'honesty'.

IBPS PO mock test FAQs

How many questions are there in the IBPS PO exam?

The IBPS PO paper has 100 multiple-choice questions for 100 marks, to be completed in 60 minutes. This is the indicative pattern — always confirm it in the latest official notification.

Is there negative marking in IBPS PO?

Yes. −0.25 mark per wrong answer (25% of the marks for that question). Our mock tests apply the same rule.

What is the target score for IBPS PO?

Cut-offs vary by year and category; our mock tests use 45% as a target score to aim for.
