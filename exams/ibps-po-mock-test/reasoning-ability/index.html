Reasoning Ability: IBPS PO practice questions

15 free questions with answers and explanations on Reasoning Ability, from the IBPS PO (IBPS Probationary Officer (Prelims)) syllabus.

Last updated 23 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · How we create questions

What this section covers

Reasoning in IBPS PO includes seating and floor puzzles, syllogisms, inequalities, blood relations, direction sense, coding-decoding, ranking, alphanumeric series, ordering and critical reasoning such as assumptions. Puzzles carry the most marks, so practise drawing quick tables and testing cases systematically. For syllogisms use Venn diagrams and remember the 'possibility' type. In inequalities, link statements through common terms. Read each condition carefully and avoid assumptions about gender or direction not given in the question.

Reasoning Ability questions

Question 1

Five persons P, Q, R, S and T live on different floors of a five-storey building; the ground floor is numbered 1 and the top floor 5. • Only two persons live between P and T, and P lives above T. • S lives immediately above T. • Q lives on an even-numbered floor. • R lives below S. Who lives on floor 1?

  1. T
  2. Q
  3. S
  4. R
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. R

P above T with two between gives T on 1 or 2. T on 1 forces Q onto an odd floor, so T = 2, S = 3, P = 5. Q must be on 4 (even), leaving R on floor 1.

Question 2

Five persons P, Q, R, S and T live on different floors of a five-storey building; the ground floor is numbered 1 and the top floor 5. • Only two persons live between P and T, and P lives above T. • S lives immediately above T. • Q lives on an even-numbered floor. • R lives below S. How many persons live between Q and R?

  1. One
  2. Three
  3. None
  4. Two
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Two

The arrangement is R (1), T (2), S (3), Q (4), P (5). Between Q on floor 4 and R on floor 1 live T and S, that is two persons.

Question 3

Six persons A, B, C, D, E and F sit in a row facing north. • C sits third from the left end. • Only one person sits between C and F. • A sits immediately to the right of F. • B sits at one of the extreme ends. • D is not an immediate neighbour of C. Who sits second from the right end?

  1. E
  2. B
  3. D
  4. A
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. D

F cannot be at position 5 (A must be on its right), so F is 1st and A 2nd. B takes the right end. D cannot be 4th (next to C), so D is 5th and E 4th: F A C E D B.

Question 4

Statements: All accounts are deposits. Some deposits are loans. No loan is a bond. Conclusions: I. Some accounts are loans. II. Some deposits are not bonds.

  1. Only conclusion I follows
  2. Only conclusion II follows
  3. Both I and II follow
  4. Neither I nor II follows
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Only conclusion II follows

Accounts lie inside deposits, but the deposits that are loans may lie outside accounts, so I is not certain. The deposits that are loans can never be bonds, so some deposits are definitely not bonds. Only II follows.

Question 5

Statements: Some cards are coins. No coin is a key. Conclusions: I. Some cards are not keys. II. All keys being cards is a possibility.

  1. Only conclusion I follows
  2. Only conclusion II follows
  3. Both I and II follow
  4. Neither I nor II follows
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Both I and II follow

The cards that are coins cannot be keys, so I definitely follows. Nothing prevents keys from lying entirely inside the card circle (outside coins), so the possibility in II also holds. Both follow.

Question 6

In the following question, the relationship between different elements is shown. Decide which conclusion(s) definitely hold true. Given: P ≥ Q = R > S; T ≥ R Conclusions: I. T > S II. P > T

  1. Only conclusion I follows
  2. Only conclusion II follows
  3. Both I and II follow
  4. Neither I nor II follows
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Only conclusion I follows

Combining, T ≥ R > S gives T > S, so I follows. P and T are both only known to be ≥ R, so their relative order cannot be fixed and II does not follow. Only I follows.

Question 7

Pointing to a woman, Karan said, 'Her mother's only son is my father.' How is the woman related to Karan?

  1. Aunt
  2. Sister
  3. Mother
  4. Cousin
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Aunt

The only son of the woman's mother is the woman's brother, and he is Karan's father. So the woman is the sister of Karan's father, which makes her Karan's aunt (paternal aunt).

Question 8

P # Q means P is the father of Q; P @ Q means P is the sister of Q; P $ Q means P is the wife of Q. In the expression M $ N # O @ R, how is R related to M?

  1. Daughter only
  2. Son or daughter
  3. Niece
  4. Sister
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Son or daughter

M is N's wife and N is O's father, so M is O's mother. O is R's sister, so R is also a child of M. R's gender is not given, so R is M's son or daughter.

Question 9

Priya walks 8 m south, turns left and walks 6 m, turns left again and walks 14 m, then turns right and walks 2 m. How far and in which direction is she from her starting point?

  1. 10 m, North-East
  2. 10 m, South-East
  3. 14 m, North-East
  4. 8 m, North
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 10 m, North-East

South 8 then left (east) 6, left (north) 14, right (east) 2. Net: 8 m east and 6 m north of the start. Distance = √(8² + 6²) = 10 m, towards the North-East.

Question 10

In a certain code language, PLANT is written as QNDRY. How is GRAIN written in that code?

  1. HTDLS
  2. HSDMS
  3. HTCMS
  4. HTDMS
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. HTDMS

Each letter moves forward by its position: +1, +2, +3, +4, +5 (P→Q, L→N, A→D, N→R, T→Y). Applying the same to GRAIN: G→H, R→T, A→D, I→M, N→S, giving HTDMS.

Question 11

In a code language, 'bank gives loan' is written as 'mo ta zi', 'loan is cheap' as 'zi ka pu' and 'cheap bank rate' as 'pu mo xe'. What is the code for 'gives'?

  1. mo
  2. zi
  3. ta
  4. ka
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. ta

'bank' is common to sentences 1 and 3 (mo), 'loan' to 1 and 2 (zi), 'cheap' to 2 and 3 (pu). In sentence 1, the remaining code ta must stand for 'gives'.

Question 12

In a row of students, Kiran is 15th from the left end. When she moves six places to her right, she becomes 22nd from the right end. How many students are there in the row?

  1. 43
  2. 42
  3. 36
  4. 41
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 42

After moving six places right, Kiran is 21st from the left and 22nd from the right. Total = 21 + 22 − 1 = 42. Using her original 15th position with 22 would give the wrong answer.

Question 13

Study the arrangement: R 4 % T 7 K @ 2 M 9 # E 5 $ U 3 & B How many numbers in the arrangement are immediately followed by a symbol?

  1. Four
  2. Three
  3. Two
  4. Five
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Four

The number–symbol pairs are 4 %, 9 #, 5 $ and 3 &, so four numbers qualify. 7 is followed by K and 2 by M, so they are excluded.

Question 14

Among J, K, L, M and N, each of a different weight: K is heavier than both M and N but lighter than L. M is heavier than N. J is lighter than N. Who is the lightest?

  1. N
  2. M
  3. K
  4. J
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. J

From the clues, L > K > M > N, and J is lighter than N. So the order is L > K > M > N > J, making J the lightest. N is second-lightest, a tempting trap.

Question 15

Statement: A bank announces that customers who register their mobile number will receive an SMS alert for every transaction in their account. Which of the following is implicit in the statement?

  1. Customers who do not own mobile phones will close their accounts soon
  2. SMS alerts will completely eliminate all kinds of banking frauds
  3. Many customers would like to be informed of transactions in their account
  4. The bank has never informed its customers about any transaction earlier
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Many customers would like to be informed of transactions in their account

Offering alerts assumes customers value being informed about their transactions. Claims about closing accounts, ending all fraud, or past bank practice go beyond the statement and are not assumed.
