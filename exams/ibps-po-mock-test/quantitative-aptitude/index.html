Quantitative Aptitude: IBPS PO practice questions

15 free questions with answers and explanations on Quantitative Aptitude, from the IBPS PO (IBPS Probationary Officer (Prelims)) syllabus.

Last updated 23 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · How we create questions

What this section covers

This section covers simplification and approximation, number series, quadratic equations, and arithmetic topics such as percentages, profit and loss, simple and compound interest, partnership, time and work, speed and distance, boats, mixtures and alligation, mensuration, probability and permutations. Data interpretation sets carry heavy weight in the Mains. Focus on quick calculation tricks, fractions-to-percentage conversions, and multiplier methods for successive changes. Always write units and check whether a question asks for an exact or approximate value.

Quantitative Aptitude questions

Question 1

What approximate value should come in place of the question mark (?) 39.97% of 1200.05 + 14.98² = ?

  1. 705
  2. 685
  3. 725
  4. 745
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 705

Round off: 40% of 1200 = 480 and 15² = 225. So the value is about 480 + 225 = 705. Rounding to the nearest convenient numbers first saves time in the exam.

Question 2

What will come in place of the question mark (?) in the series? 5, 11, 24, 51, 106, ?

  1. 212
  2. 215
  3. 221
  4. 217
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 217

Each term is double the previous term plus an increasing number: ×2+1, ×2+2, ×2+3, ×2+4. Next: 106 × 2 + 5 = 217.

Question 3

Find the roots of each quadratic below and decide how x relates to y. I. x² − 9x + 20 = 0 II. y² − 11y + 30 = 0

  1. x < y
  2. x > y
  3. x ≤ y
  4. Relationship cannot be established
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. x ≤ y

I gives x = 4 or 5; II gives y = 5 or 6. Every x is less than or equal to every y, and x = y = 5 is possible, so the relationship is x ≤ y, not strictly x < y.

Question 4

Ravi's salary is first raised by 25% and then the new salary is reduced by 20%. What is the net change in his salary?

  1. 5% increase
  2. No change
  3. 5% decrease
  4. 2% increase
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. No change

Net multiplier = 1.25 × 0.80 = 1.00, so there is no change. Simply subtracting 20 from 25 to get a 5% increase is wrong because the 20% cut applies to the larger raised salary.

Question 5

A trader sells two mobile phones at ₹9,600 each. On one he gains 20% and on the other he loses 20%. What is his overall result?

  1. Loss of ₹800
  2. No profit, no loss
  3. Gain of ₹800
  4. Loss of ₹400
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Loss of ₹800

Cost prices are 9600/1.2 = ₹8,000 and 9600/0.8 = ₹12,000, total ₹20,000. Selling price is ₹19,200, so the loss is ₹800 (4%). Equal gain and loss percentages on equal SPs always mean a loss.

Question 6

What is the compound interest on ₹20,000 for 1 year at 10% per annum, compounded half-yearly?

  1. ₹2,000
  2. ₹2,100
  3. ₹2,025
  4. ₹2,050
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. ₹2,050

Half-yearly compounding gives 5% per half-year for 2 periods. Amount = 20,000 × 1.05² = ₹22,050, so CI = ₹2,050. Simple interest would be ₹2,000, the tempting wrong option.

Question 7

P, Q and R invest in a business in the ratio 3 : 4 : 5. P keeps the money for 12 months, Q for 9 months and R for 6 months. Out of an annual profit of ₹34,000, what is R's share?

  1. ₹12,000
  2. ₹9,000
  3. ₹10,000
  4. ₹8,500
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. ₹10,000

Profit is shared in the ratio of capital × time: 36 : 36 : 30 = 6 : 6 : 5. R's share = 5/17 × 34,000 = ₹10,000. Ignoring time and using 3 : 4 : 5 would wrongly favour R.

Question 8

A and B together can finish a job in 12 days, B and C together in 15 days, and C and A together in 20 days. In how many days can A alone finish the job?

  1. 24 days
  2. 30 days
  3. 40 days
  4. 20 days
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 30 days

Adding the three pairs: 2(A + B + C) = 1/12 + 1/15 + 1/20 = 1/5, so A + B + C = 1/10 per day. A = 1/10 − (B + C) = 1/10 − 1/15 = 1/30. A alone takes 30 days.

Question 9

Two trains 180 m and 120 m long are running in opposite directions on parallel tracks at 54 km/h and 36 km/h. In how many seconds will they completely cross each other?

  1. 12 seconds
  2. 15 seconds
  3. 20 seconds
  4. 10 seconds
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 12 seconds

In opposite directions speeds add: 90 km/h = 25 m/s. Total length to cover = 180 + 120 = 300 m. Time = 300 ÷ 25 = 12 seconds. Using the difference of speeds would give 60 seconds.

Question 10

A boat's speed in still water is 15 km/h and the speed of the stream is 3 km/h. The boat goes 36 km downstream and returns to the start. What is its average speed for the whole journey?

  1. 15 km/h
  2. 13.6 km/h
  3. 14 km/h
  4. 14.4 km/h
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 14.4 km/h

Downstream 18 km/h takes 2 h; upstream 12 km/h takes 3 h. Total distance 72 km in 5 h gives 14.4 km/h. It is less than 15 km/h because more time is spent at the slower upstream speed.

Question 11

In what ratio must rice costing ₹40 per kg be mixed with rice costing ₹55 per kg so that the mixture costs ₹45 per kg?

  1. 1 : 2
  2. 3 : 2
  3. 2 : 1
  4. 2 : 3
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 2 : 1

By alligation: cheaper : dearer = (55 − 45) : (45 − 40) = 10 : 5 = 2 : 1. Since ₹45 is closer to ₹40, more of the cheaper rice is needed, which rules out 1 : 2.

Question 12

What is the curved surface area of a cylinder with radius 7 cm and height 10 cm? (Take π = 22/7)

  1. 1,540 cm²
  2. 440 cm²
  3. 308 cm²
  4. 880 cm²
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 440 cm²

Curved surface area = 2πrh = 2 × 22/7 × 7 × 10 = 440 cm². 1,540 cm³ would be the volume (πr²h), a common confusion, and 880 cm² doubles the correct value.

Question 13

Three fair coins are tossed together. What is the probability of getting at least two heads?

  1. 1/2
  2. 3/8
  3. 5/8
  4. 1/4
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 1/2

Of 8 equally likely outcomes, exactly two heads occur in 3 (HHT, HTH, THH) and three heads in 1. So P = 4/8 = 1/2. 3/8 counts only 'exactly two heads' and misses HHH.

Question 14

In how many ways can a committee of 3 men and 2 women be formed from 6 men and 5 women?

  1. 150
  2. 300
  3. 252
  4. 200
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 200

Choose men and women independently and multiply: 6C3 × 5C2 = 20 × 10 = 200. 252 is 10C5, which ignores the condition of exactly 3 men and 2 women.

Question 15

The table shows total deposits (₹ crore) of a bank branch. 2021 – 420, 2022 – 480, 2023 – 552, 2024 – 600 What is the percentage increase in deposits from 2022 to 2023?

  1. 12%
  2. 18%
  3. 15%
  4. 13.5%
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 15%

Increase = 552 − 480 = 72. Percentage increase = 72 ÷ 480 × 100 = 15%. Dividing by the later year's value (552) gives about 13% and is a common mistake; always divide by the base year.
