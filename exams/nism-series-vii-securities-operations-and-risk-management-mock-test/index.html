About the NISM Series VII exam

NISM Series VII tests back-office operations in the securities market — trading, clearing and settlement, margins and risk management, the roles of exchanges and clearing corporations, and investor grievance redress.

Who should take it: Operations, settlement and risk-management staff of stock brokers and clearing members.

Sample 1 · Introduction to the Securities Market

Which authority regulates financial services in International Financial Services Centres such as GIFT City?

  1. Ministry of Commerce
  2. RBI only
  3. SEBI only
  4. International Financial Services Centres Authority (IFSCA)
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. International Financial Services Centres Authority (IFSCA)

IFSCA is the unified regulator for financial products and services in IFSCs.

Sample 2 · Market Participants in the Securities Market

Which of these is classified as a Qualified Institutional Buyer?

  1. A small partnership firm
  2. A SEBI-registered mutual fund
  3. A non-institutional HNI individual
  4. A retail individual investor
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. A SEBI-registered mutual fund

QIBs include mutual funds, FPIs (other than certain categories), banks and insurers.

Sample 3 · Introduction to Securities Broking Operations

Which is the correct order of the trade life cycle?

  1. Order placement → order matching → trade confirmation → clearing → settlement
  2. Order matching → settlement → order placement → clearing
  3. Clearing → order placement → settlement → confirmation
  4. Settlement → clearing → order placement → matching
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Order placement → order matching → trade confirmation → clearing → settlement

Orders are placed and matched, confirmed, then cleared to determine obligations and finally settled.

Sample 4 · Risk Management

VaR margin in the cash segment is designed to cover:

  1. All possible losses under any scenario
  2. The broker's operating expenses
  3. The likely loss on a position over a given time horizon at a specified confidence level
  4. Taxes payable on the trade
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. The likely loss on a position over a given time horizon at a specified confidence level

Value at Risk estimates potential loss over a time horizon at a set confidence level.

Sample 5 · Clearing Process

Which bank function is linked to clearing corporations for the settlement of funds?

  1. Rural banks lending to farmers
  2. Central bank currency issuance
  3. Clearing banks, through which members' funds pay-in and pay-out are made
  4. Investment banks underwriting IPOs
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Clearing banks, through which members' funds pay-in and pay-out are made

Clearing members keep settlement accounts with designated clearing banks.

NISM Series VII mock test FAQs

How many questions are there in the NISM Series VII exam?

The NISM Series VII paper has 100 multiple-choice questions for 100 marks, to be completed in 120 minutes. This is the indicative pattern — always confirm it in the latest official notification.

Is there negative marking in NISM Series VII?

Yes. −0.25 mark per wrong answer (25% of the marks for that question). Our mock tests apply the same rule.

What is the pass mark for NISM Series VII?

You need 50% to pass. Our mock tests show pass or fail against the same mark.

Are the NISM Series VII mock tests free?

Yes. All 30 full-length NISM Series VII mock tests are free, with no payment or plan. Create a free account (one click with Google) to start; your results are saved across devices. You can retake any test as often as you like.

Is NISM Series VII mandatory?

SEBI requires associated persons of stock brokers and clearing members who work in operations, such as settlement, risk management and back-office roles, to hold this certification. Your compliance team can confirm whether your role is covered. Confirm the current rules on nism.ac.in.

Which chapters of NISM Series VII are most important?

Clearing and settlement, margin types and risk management are the core of the paper and usually generate the most questions. Many learners also need extra practice on the roles of exchanges, clearing corporations and depositories in the settlement cycle. Revise investor grievance mechanisms as well, as they are easy marks.

How do I prepare for NISM Series VII in 10 days?

Cover two workbook chapters a day for the first week, writing short notes on settlement timelines and margin rules. Use the last three days for timed mock tests and targeted revision of wrong answers. Ten days is achievable for working professionals familiar with broking operations; beginners may need longer.
