Market Participants in the Securities Market: NISM Series VII practice questions

8 free questions with answers and explanations on Market Participants in the Securities Market, from the NISM Series VII (Securities Operations and Risk Management) syllabus.

What this chapter covers

This chapter describes the participants in the securities market: investors (retail, HNI, institutional, FPIs, NRIs), issuers, stock brokers, authorised persons, depository participants, custodians, clearing members, merchant bankers, RTAs, credit rating agencies and others, along with the market infrastructure institutions. Revise the role of each, how investors are categorised in IPOs, and which regulator or body governs each participant.

Market Participants in the Securities Market questions

Question 1

In a public issue, an individual investor applying for shares worth up to ₹2 lakh is classified as a:

  1. Non-institutional investor
  2. Qualified institutional buyer
  3. Retail individual investor
  4. Anchor investor
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Retail individual investor

Individuals applying for up to ₹2 lakh are retail individual investors. Applications above ₹2 lakh by individuals fall in the non-institutional category.

Question 2

Which intermediary assigns ratings to debt instruments based on the issuer's ability to repay?

  1. A credit rating agency
  2. A merchant banker
  3. A custodian
  4. A depository
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. A credit rating agency

Credit rating agencies, registered with SEBI, assess the creditworthiness of issuers and their debt instruments. Ratings help investors judge default risk.

Question 3

The intermediary that manages a public issue, prepares the offer document and conducts due diligence is the:

  1. Registrar to the issue
  2. Merchant banker
  3. Stock broker
  4. Clearing bank
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Merchant banker

Merchant bankers (book running lead managers) handle the issue process, due diligence and offer document. The registrar processes applications and allotments.

Question 4

Which participant settles funds of clearing members with the clearing corporation?

  1. Credit rating agency
  2. Authorised person
  3. Investment adviser
  4. Clearing bank
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Clearing bank

Clearing banks maintain settlement accounts of clearing members and move funds for pay-in and pay-out as instructed by the clearing corporation.

Question 5

An anchor investor in an IPO is:

  1. A QIB allotted shares before the issue opens
  2. Any retail investor who bids on the first day of the issue
  3. The promoter of the company
  4. A stock exchange
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. A QIB allotted shares before the issue opens

Anchor investors are QIBs who are allotted shares a day before the issue opens to the public, subject to lock-in, which helps signal institutional demand.

Question 6

Which of these is NOT a market infrastructure institution?

  1. A stock exchange
  2. A merchant banker
  3. A clearing corporation
  4. A depository
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. A merchant banker

MIIs are stock exchanges, clearing corporations and depositories. Merchant bankers are intermediaries registered with SEBI.

Question 7

A stock broker's agent who deals with clients and is approved by the exchange is called a(n):

  1. Sub-custodian
  2. Sponsor
  3. Trustee
  4. Authorised person
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Authorised person

Authorised persons act on behalf of a broker and must be approved by the exchange. Investors' contracts remain with the stock broker.

Question 8

A foreign pension fund investing in Indian listed shares would participate as a(n):

  1. Authorised person
  2. Domestic institutional investor
  3. Foreign portfolio investor
  4. Retail individual investor
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Foreign portfolio investor

Foreign institutional investors register with SEBI (through DDPs) as FPIs to invest in Indian listed securities.
