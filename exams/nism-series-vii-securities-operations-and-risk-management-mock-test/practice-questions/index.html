Free NISM Series VII practice questions

Question 1 · Introduction to the Securities Market

The call money market deals in funds borrowed and lent for:

  1. One day (overnight)
  2. One year
  3. Five years
  4. Ninety days only
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. One day (overnight)

Call money is overnight borrowing and lending, mostly between banks.

Question 2 · Market Participants in the Securities Market

Which entity acts as the central counterparty to trades executed on a stock exchange?

  1. The issuer
  2. The clearing corporation
  3. The stock broker
  4. The depository
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. The clearing corporation

The clearing corporation becomes buyer to every seller and seller to every buyer.

Question 3 · Introduction to Securities Broking Operations

Which function in a broking firm is mainly responsible for client acquisition and order entry?

  1. Back office
  2. Front office
  3. Internal audit
  4. Middle office
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Front office

The front office deals with clients, takes orders and executes trades.

Question 4 · Risk Management

Extreme Loss Margin (ELM) is collected to cover:

  1. Stamp duty
  2. Losses beyond what VaR margin covers, in extreme market conditions
  3. Settlement delays caused by holidays
  4. Brokerage charges
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Losses beyond what VaR margin covers, in extreme market conditions

ELM covers tail risk that VaR does not capture.

Question 5 · Clearing Process

Determining which member must pay or receive how much money and securities after trading is part of:

  1. Underwriting
  2. Clearing
  3. Order matching
  4. Listing
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Clearing

Clearing computes the obligations of each member, which are then met during settlement.

Question 6 · Settlement Process

Under T+1 settlement, a trade executed on a Friday (Monday being a working day) is settled on:

  1. Saturday
  2. Monday
  3. Tuesday
  4. Friday itself
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Monday

Settlement occurs on the next working day, which is Monday.

Question 7 · Investor Grievances and Arbitration

SEBI's online platform for investors to file complaints against listed companies and intermediaries is:

  1. NSDL e-voting
  2. MCA21
  3. SMART ODR only for companies
  4. SCORES
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. SCORES

SCORES allows online lodging and tracking of complaints.

Question 8 · Other Services Provided by Brokers

Under the Margin Trading Facility, a stock broker:

  1. Buys shares for clients using other clients' funds
  2. Guarantees profit on funded positions
  3. Funds part of a client's purchase of eligible securities, which are pledged as collateral
  4. Gives clients unsecured personal loans
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Funds part of a client's purchase of eligible securities, which are pledged as collateral

MTF lets brokers finance purchases of eligible stocks, with the purchased shares pledged to the broker.

Question 9 · Introduction to the Securities Market

Which of the following is a derivative product traded on Indian exchanges?

  1. Equity shares
  2. Stock options
  3. Exchange traded fund units
  4. Treasury bills
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Stock options

Options derive their value from an underlying stock or index.

Question 10 · Market Participants in the Securities Market

Which intermediary is an agent of a stock broker, approved by the exchange, who deals with clients on the broker's behalf?

  1. Depository
  2. Custodian
  3. Authorised person
  4. Sub-registrar
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Authorised person

Authorised persons are appointed by brokers with exchange approval to provide access to trading platforms.

Question 11 · Introduction to Securities Broking Operations

Real-time monitoring of client exposures and limits in a broking firm is typically handled by the:

  1. Middle office (risk management)
  2. Statutory auditor
  3. Back office accounts
  4. Front office sales team
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Middle office (risk management)

The middle office monitors risk and exposures against limits.

Question 12 · Risk Management

A client buys 100 shares at ₹500. The closing price that day is ₹480. What is the mark-to-market loss?

  1. ₹200
  2. ₹2,000
  3. ₹4,800
  4. ₹20,000
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. ₹2,000

MTM loss = (500 − 480) × 100 = ₹2,000.

Question 13 · Clearing Process

Institutional trades that a custodian must confirm before they are settled carry a:

  1. Custodial Participant (CP) code
  2. ISIN
  3. UCC of the exchange
  4. SEBI registration number
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Custodial Participant (CP) code

CP codes identify institutional trades settled through custodians.

Question 14 · Settlement Process

A trade is executed on Thursday and Friday is a trading holiday. Under T+1, settlement takes place on:

  1. Friday
  2. Tuesday
  3. Saturday
  4. Monday
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Monday

T+1 counts working days, so the next settlement day after Thursday is Monday.

Question 15 · Investor Grievances and Arbitration

If a dispute between an investor and a broker is not resolved through SCORES or direct engagement, the investor can use:

  1. The Securities Appellate Tribunal directly as a first step
  2. The consumer helpline of the telecom regulator
  3. The Online Dispute Resolution (ODR) mechanism for conciliation and arbitration
  4. The RBI Ombudsman
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. The Online Dispute Resolution (ODR) mechanism for conciliation and arbitration

SEBI's ODR framework lets investors escalate disputes to online conciliation and arbitration.
