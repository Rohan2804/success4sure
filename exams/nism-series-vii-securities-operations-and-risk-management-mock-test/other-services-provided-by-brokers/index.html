What this chapter covers

This chapter covers services brokers offer beyond basic trading: margin trading facility, securities lending and borrowing, portfolio management, mutual fund distribution through exchange platforms, research, internet and algorithmic trading, and depository services. Revise eligibility and permissions needed, how MTF works and its risks, the minimum investment for PMS, SLB mechanics, and safeguards for internet and algorithmic trading.

Other Services Provided by Brokers questions

Question 1

Under Securities Lending and Borrowing (SLB), a lender of shares earns:

  1. A lending fee
  2. The full market gain
  3. Voting rights only
  4. A guaranteed dividend
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. A lending fee

The lender earns a lending fee while lending shares through the SLB platform. The borrower uses the shares, often to cover short sales or settlement needs.

Question 2

In the Margin Trading Facility, the securities bought by the client are:

  1. Transferred to the broker's own account
  2. Rematerialised
  3. Pledged in favour of the broker
  4. Kept with SEBI
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Pledged in favour of the broker

MTF positions are held in the client's account and pledged to the broker as collateral for the funding provided.

Question 3

A broker offering algorithmic trading to clients must:

  1. Allow unlimited orders per second
  2. Avoid audits
  3. Hide the algos from the exchange
  4. Get exchange approval for the algos
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Get exchange approval for the algos

Algorithmic trading requires exchange approval, risk controls and audits so that algorithms do not disrupt markets or cause runaway orders.

Question 4

Which service requires a broker to register separately with SEBI?

  1. Order placement for cash market trades
  2. Portfolio management
  3. Issuing contract notes
  4. Providing a trading app
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Portfolio management

Portfolio management requires separate SEBI registration as a portfolio manager. The other activities are part of normal broking operations.

Question 5

A broker's research report recommending stocks is covered by:

  1. RBI's KYC Master Directions for banks
  2. IRDAI's guidelines
  3. SEBI Research Analyst Regulations
  4. The Depositories Act
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. SEBI Research Analyst Regulations

Research reports must comply with SEBI's Research Analyst Regulations, including disclosures on conflicts of interest and holdings.

Question 6

A key risk for a client using MTF is that:

  1. Brokerage is fully waived
  2. Dividends are lost
  3. Shares cannot be sold later
  4. Leverage magnifies losses
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Leverage magnifies losses

Borrowed money magnifies gains and losses. A fall in prices can trigger margin calls and forced sale of pledged shares.

Question 7

A broker offering demat services to clients must also be registered as a:

  1. Merchant banker
  2. Depository Participant
  3. Mutual fund
  4. Credit rating agency
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Depository Participant

Opening and maintaining demat accounts requires DP registration with SEBI through a depository.

Question 8

Mutual fund orders placed by a broker on an exchange platform are settled through:

  1. The clearing corporation
  2. The AMC's sponsor
  3. The trustee
  4. RBI
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. The clearing corporation

Exchange mutual fund platforms route orders to AMCs/RTAs, with funds and units settled through the clearing corporation.
