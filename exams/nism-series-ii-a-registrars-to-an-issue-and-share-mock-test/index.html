About the NISM Series II-A exam

NISM Series II-A covers the work of Registrars to an Issue and Share Transfer Agents (RTAs) for companies — public issue processing, basis of allotment, transfers, transmission, corporate actions, depository interface and investor servicing.

Who should take it: Staff of SEBI-registered RTAs who handle corporate shareholder records, and company secretarial teams who work closely with RTAs.

Sample 1 · Introduction to Securities

Which of the following best describes the role of the primary market?

  1. It is the market in which derivatives on listed shares are settled
  2. It is the market where only the RBI deals in government bonds
  3. It is where investors trade already-listed securities among themselves
  4. It is where issuers raise fresh capital by selling newly issued securities to investors
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. It is where issuers raise fresh capital by selling newly issued securities to investors

New securities are created and sold by issuers in the primary market; trading of existing securities happens in the secondary market.

Sample 2 · Characteristics of Equity Shares

In the event of liquidation, equity shareholders are paid:

  1. Only after all creditors and preference shareholders have been paid
  2. Pari passu with debenture holders
  3. Before preference shareholders but after creditors
  4. Before secured creditors
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Only after all creditors and preference shareholders have been paid

Equity holders carry residual risk and receive whatever remains after all prior claims are settled.

Sample 3 · Characteristics of Debt Securities

A bond with a face value of ₹1,000 carries a coupon of 8% per annum payable annually. What is the annual interest received?

  1. ₹8
  2. ₹80
  3. ₹108
  4. ₹800
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. ₹80

Coupon is calculated on face value: 8% of ₹1,000 = ₹80 per year.

Sample 4 · Characteristics of Other Securities

A warrant attached to a security gives the holder:

  1. A fixed coupon payable every quarter
  2. Voting rights immediately on issue
  3. The right, but not the obligation, to acquire shares at a specified price within a set period
  4. An obligation to buy shares at market price
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. The right, but not the obligation, to acquire shares at a specified price within a set period

A warrant is an option-like right to subscribe to shares at a pre-set price; the holder may choose not to exercise it.

Sample 5 · Basics of Mutual Funds

The Net Asset Value (NAV) of a mutual fund scheme per unit is computed as:

  1. (Assets + liabilities) ÷ units outstanding
  2. Total income ÷ number of unitholders
  3. Total assets ÷ face value of units
  4. (Market value of assets − liabilities) ÷ number of units outstanding
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. (Market value of assets − liabilities) ÷ number of units outstanding

NAV per unit is the scheme's net assets divided by units outstanding.
