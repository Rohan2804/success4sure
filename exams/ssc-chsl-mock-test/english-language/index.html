English Language: SSC CHSL practice questions

15 free questions with answers and explanations on English Language, from the SSC CHSL (Combined Higher Secondary Level (Tier 1)) syllabus.

Last updated 17 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · How we create questions

What this section covers

English Language tests vocabulary, grammar and comprehension. Expect synonyms, antonyms, idioms and phrases, one-word substitution and spelling, along with error spotting, fill-in-the-blanks, sentence improvement, active-passive voice, direct-indirect speech, sentence rearrangement and short comprehension passages. Revise subject-verb agreement, tenses, articles, prepositions and the subjunctive mood. Read regularly to build vocabulary in context, and for passages, answer only from what the text actually says rather than from your own general knowledge.

English Language questions

Question 1

Choose the word closest in meaning to OBSOLETE, as in 'obsolete machinery'.

  1. Obscure
  2. Essential
  3. Durable
  4. Outdated
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Outdated

Obsolete means no longer in use or out of date, so 'outdated' is the closest synonym. 'Obscure' looks similar but means unclear or little known.

Question 2

Choose the word opposite in meaning to FRUGAL, as in 'a frugal lifestyle'.

  1. Extravagant
  2. Thrifty
  3. Modest
  4. Careful
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Extravagant

Frugal means careful and sparing with money. Its opposite is extravagant, meaning spending too much. 'Thrifty' and 'careful' are close to synonyms, not antonyms.

Question 3

Select the most appropriate meaning of the idiom 'to burn the midnight oil'.

  1. To waste money on useless things
  2. To lose one's temper very quickly
  3. To work or study late into the night
  4. To start a quarrel without reason
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. To work or study late into the night

'To burn the midnight oil' means to stay up late working or studying, from the old practice of reading by oil lamps at night. It has nothing to do with wasting money or anger.

Question 4

Select the one word for: 'A speech delivered without any preparation'.

  1. Monologue
  2. Soliloquy
  3. Extempore
  4. Eulogy
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Extempore

An extempore speech is given on the spot without preparation. A soliloquy is speaking one's thoughts aloud alone, a eulogy praises someone (usually after death), and a monologue is a long speech by one person.

Question 5

Which of the following words is spelt correctly?

  1. Accomodate
  2. Acommodate
  3. Acomodate
  4. Accommodate
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Accommodate

The correct spelling is 'accommodate', with a double 'c' and a double 'm'. The other options drop one of these doubled letters, a very common spelling error.

Question 6

Identify the segment that contains a grammatical error. One of my friends / have gone / to Mumbai / for higher studies.

  1. One of my friends
  2. have gone
  3. to Mumbai
  4. for higher studies
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. have gone

The subject is 'one', which is singular, even though 'friends' is plural. The verb must agree with 'one', so 'have gone' should be 'has gone'.

Question 7

Fill in the blank with the most appropriate article. She is ______ honest woman.

  1. a
  2. the
  3. no article
  4. an
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. an

'Honest' begins with a silent 'h', so it starts with a vowel sound. The article 'an' is used before a vowel sound, giving 'an honest woman'.

Question 8

Fill in the blank with the most appropriate verb form. By the time we reached the station, the train ______.

  1. has left
  2. will leave
  3. had left
  4. leaves
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. had left

When one past action happened before another past action, the earlier one takes the past perfect. The train left before we reached, so 'had left' is correct.

Question 9

Select the most appropriate improvement for the underlined part. 'If I was you', I would accept the offer.

  1. If I were you
  2. If I am you
  3. If I had been you
  4. No improvement
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. If I were you

Imaginary or unreal situations in the present use the subjunctive 'were' for all subjects. So the correct form is 'If I were you, I would accept the offer'.

Question 10

Select the correct passive form of: The committee has approved the proposal.

  1. The proposal has been approved by the committee.
  2. The proposal was approved by the committee.
  3. The proposal had been approved by the committee.
  4. The proposal is being approved by the committee.
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. The proposal has been approved by the committee.

The active sentence is in the present perfect tense, so the passive uses 'has been' with the past participle: 'The proposal has been approved by the committee.' The other options change the tense.

Question 11

Select the correct indirect form of: He said, "I am tired."

  1. He said that I was tired.
  2. He said that he is tired.
  3. He says that he was tired.
  4. He said that he was tired.
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. He said that he was tired.

With a past reporting verb, the present 'am' changes to 'was' and the pronoun 'I' changes to 'he'. The reporting verb 'said' itself stays unchanged.

Question 12

Read the passage and answer the question. Rainwater harvesting is the practice of collecting and storing rain that falls on rooftops and open ground. In many Indian towns, the stored water is used to recharge wells and borewells, which reduces dependence on distant reservoirs. The method is cheap, but it works only when tanks and pipes are cleaned before the monsoon. According to the passage, rainwater harvesting works only when:

  1. the town is close to a large reservoir
  2. wells and borewells are dug again every year
  3. tanks and pipes are cleaned before the monsoon
  4. rain falls only on rooftops and not on open ground
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. tanks and pipes are cleaned before the monsoon

The last sentence states that the method works only when tanks and pipes are cleaned before the monsoon. The passage actually says harvesting reduces dependence on distant reservoirs, so proximity to one is not a condition.

Question 13

Read the passage and answer the question. Rainwater harvesting is the practice of collecting and storing rain that falls on rooftops and open ground. In many Indian towns, the stored water is used to recharge wells and borewells, which reduces dependence on distant reservoirs. The method is cheap, but it works only when tanks and pipes are cleaned before the monsoon. Which word is closest in meaning to 'dependence' as used in the passage?

  1. Freedom
  2. Distance
  3. Reliance
  4. Shortage
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Reliance

In the passage, harvested water reduces dependence on distant reservoirs, that is, the need to rely on them. 'Reliance' carries this meaning, while 'freedom' is almost its opposite.

Question 14

Arrange the sentences in a logical order. P: she boarded the bus to the city. Q: Early in the morning, Asha packed her bag. R: After a long ride, she reached her aunt's house. S: Then she walked to the bus stand.

  1. QPSR
  2. QSPR
  3. SQPR
  4. QSRP
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. QSPR

Q introduces Asha and the time, so it comes first. She then walks to the bus stand (S), boards the bus (P) and finally reaches her aunt's house after the ride (R), giving QSPR.

Question 15

Fill in the blank with the most appropriate word. The ______ of the new policy will be felt mostly by small traders.

  1. effect
  2. affect
  3. affection
  4. effort
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. effect

A noun meaning 'result' is needed after 'the', so 'effect' fits. 'Affect' is normally a verb meaning to influence, and 'effort' or 'affection' do not suit the sense of the sentence.
