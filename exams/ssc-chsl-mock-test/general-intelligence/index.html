General Intelligence: SSC CHSL practice questions

15 free questions with answers and explanations on General Intelligence, from the SSC CHSL (Combined Higher Secondary Level (Tier 1)) syllabus.

Last updated 20 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · How we create questions

What this section covers

This section tests logical and analytical reasoning through verbal and non-verbal questions: analogies, classification (odd one out), number and letter series, coding-decoding, blood relations, directions and distance, ranking, syllogisms, mathematical operations, calendars, clocks, mirror images and simple seating arrangements. Most questions follow a hidden pattern, so practise spotting rules quickly: alphabet positions, squares and cubes, and common series patterns. Draw quick diagrams for directions, family trees and arrangements, and use Venn diagrams for syllogisms. Speed and accuracy both matter because of negative marking.

General Intelligence questions

Question 1

Select the number that completes the analogy. 7 : 49 :: 12 : ?

  1. 124
  2. 132
  3. 169
  4. 144
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 144

The second number is the square of the first: 7 × 7 = 49. Applying the same rule, 12 × 12 = 144. The option 169 is the square of 13, not 12.

Question 2

Select the letter-cluster that completes the analogy. DOG : FQI :: CAT : ?

  1. ECV
  2. DBU
  3. EDV
  4. FCW
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. ECV

Each letter of DOG moves two places forward to give FQI (D→F, O→Q, G→I). Moving each letter of CAT two places forward gives C→E, A→C, T→V, so the answer is ECV.

Question 3

Select the odd number out. 121, 169, 225, 290

  1. 121
  2. 290
  3. 169
  4. 225
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 290

121, 169 and 225 are perfect squares of 11, 13 and 15 respectively. 290 lies between 17² = 289 and 18² = 324, so it is not a perfect square and is the odd one out.

Question 4

Find the next term of the series. 3, 8, 18, 38, 78, ?

  1. 156
  2. 148
  3. 162
  4. 158
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 158

Each term is double the previous term plus 2: 3×2+2 = 8, 8×2+2 = 18, 18×2+2 = 38, 38×2+2 = 78. Hence the next term is 78×2+2 = 158.

Question 5

Find the next letter in the series. B, E, I, N, ?

  1. S
  2. T
  3. U
  4. R
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. T

Using alphabet positions, B=2, E=5, I=9, N=14. The gaps increase by one each time: +3, +4, +5, so the next gap is +6, giving 14+6 = 20, which is the letter T.

Question 6

In a certain code language, MANGO is written as NBOHP. How will APPLE be written in that code language?

  1. BQQME
  2. BQQMF
  3. ZOOKD
  4. BPQMF
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. BQQMF

Every letter of MANGO is replaced by the next letter of the alphabet (M→N, A→B and so on). Applying the same +1 shift to APPLE gives A→B, P→Q, P→Q, L→M, E→F, that is BQQMF.

Question 7

If CAB = 6 in a certain code (the sum of the alphabet positions of its letters), what is the value of FACE?

  1. 14
  2. 16
  3. 15
  4. 18
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 15

In CAB, C=3, A=1 and B=2, totalling 6. Using the same rule, FACE gives F=6, A=1, C=3 and E=5, and their sum is 6+1+3+5 = 15.

Question 8

Pointing to a girl, Ravi said, "Her mother is the only daughter of my mother." How is the girl related to Ravi?

  1. Niece
  2. Daughter
  3. Sister
  4. Cousin
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Niece

The only daughter of Ravi's mother is Ravi's sister. The girl's mother is therefore Ravi's sister, which makes the girl the daughter of his sister, that is, Ravi's niece.

Question 9

Sunil walks 5 km towards the South, then turns left and walks 12 km. How far and in which direction is he now from his starting point?

  1. 13 km, South-West
  2. 17 km, South-East
  3. 13 km, South-East
  4. 7 km, North-East
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 13 km, South-East

Turning left while facing South means facing East. His position is 5 km South and 12 km East of the start, so the distance is √(5² + 12²) = 13 km and the direction is South-East.

Question 10

In a row of 40 students, Meena is 14th from the left end. What is her position from the right end?

  1. 26th
  2. 28th
  3. 27th
  4. 25th
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 27th

Position from the right = total − position from the left + 1 = 40 − 14 + 1 = 27. Forgetting to add 1 gives 26th, which is a common mistake.

Question 11

Statements: All pens are books. Some books are bags. Conclusions: I. Some pens are bags. II. Some books are pens. Which conclusion(s) follow?

  1. Only conclusion I follows
  2. Only conclusion II follows
  3. Both I and II follow
  4. Neither I nor II follows
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Only conclusion II follows

Since all pens are books, some books are definitely pens, so II follows. The books that are bags need not include any pens, so I is only a possibility and does not follow.

Question 12

If '+' means '×', '−' means '+', '×' means '÷' and '÷' means '−', what is the value of: 8 + 3 − 12 × 4 ÷ 5

  1. 19
  2. 25
  3. 16
  4. 22
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 22

Replacing the signs gives 8 × 3 + 12 ÷ 4 − 5. Following BODMAS, this is 24 + 3 − 5 = 22.

Question 13

If 1 January 2026 is a Thursday, which day of the week will 1 March 2026 be?

  1. Saturday
  2. Monday
  3. Sunday
  4. Friday
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Sunday

2026 is not a leap year, so January (31) and February (28) together have 59 days. 59 leaves a remainder of 3 when divided by 7, so the day moves three ahead from Thursday to Sunday.

Question 14

A wall clock shows the time 4:15. What time will its mirror image show?

  1. 7:45
  2. 8:45
  3. 7:15
  4. 8:15
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 7:45

For a mirror image, subtract the shown time from 11:60 (that is, 12:00). 11:60 − 4:15 = 7:45. Subtracting from 12:00 without borrowing correctly often leads to 8:45.

Question 15

Five friends P, Q, R, S and T sit in a row facing North. R sits in the middle. P sits at the extreme left end. T sits immediately to the right of R. Q is not an immediate neighbour of P. Who sits second from the left end?

  1. Q
  2. T
  3. R
  4. S
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. S

P is 1st, R is 3rd and T is 4th. Q and S occupy places 2 and 5. Q cannot sit next to P, so Q is 5th and S is 2nd from the left.
