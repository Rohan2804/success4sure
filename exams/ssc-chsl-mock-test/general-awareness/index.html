General Awareness: SSC CHSL practice questions

15 free questions with answers and explanations on General Awareness, from the SSC CHSL (Combined Higher Secondary Level (Tier 1)) syllabus.

Last updated 23 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · How we create questions

What this section covers

General Awareness covers static general knowledge and science: Indian polity and the Constitution, ancient, medieval and modern history, Indian and world geography, basic economics, and everyday physics, chemistry and biology. Questions are short and factual, so wide reading and regular revision matter more than depth. Focus on important Articles, Parliament procedures, key battles and reform movements, rivers and physical features, vitamins and diseases, and common chemical formulae. Make short revision notes and revisit them often, as this section can be attempted quickly and frees up time for others.

General Awareness questions

Question 1

Which Article of the Constitution of India abolishes untouchability and forbids its practice in any form?

  1. Article 14
  2. Article 21
  3. Article 17
  4. Article 19
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Article 17

Article 17 abolishes untouchability and makes its practice an offence punishable by law. Article 14 deals with equality before law, while Article 21 protects life and personal liberty.

Question 2

Who presides over a joint sitting of both Houses of the Indian Parliament?

  1. Speaker of the Lok Sabha
  2. President of India
  3. Vice-President of India
  4. Prime Minister of India
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Speaker of the Lok Sabha

Under Article 108, the President summons a joint sitting, but it is presided over by the Speaker of the Lok Sabha. In the Speaker's absence, the Deputy Speaker presides, not the Chairman of the Rajya Sabha.

Question 3

The Battle of Plassey, which helped the East India Company gain control over Bengal, was fought in which year?

  1. 1764
  2. 1761
  3. 1748
  4. 1757
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 1757

The Battle of Plassey was fought in 1757 between Robert Clive and Siraj-ud-Daulah. The Battle of Buxar (1764) later strengthened Company rule, and 1761 is the Third Battle of Panipat.

Question 4

Who founded the Arya Samaj in 1875?

  1. Raja Ram Mohan Roy
  2. Swami Dayanand Saraswati
  3. Swami Vivekananda
  4. Keshab Chandra Sen
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Swami Dayanand Saraswati

Swami Dayanand Saraswati founded the Arya Samaj in 1875 and gave the call 'Go back to the Vedas'. Raja Ram Mohan Roy founded the Brahmo Samaj, and Swami Vivekananda founded the Ramakrishna Mission.

Question 5

Which river of peninsular India is popularly called 'Dakshin Ganga'?

  1. Krishna
  2. Godavari
  3. Kaveri
  4. Narmada
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Godavari

The Godavari is the longest river of peninsular India and is therefore called Dakshin Ganga or the Ganga of the South. It rises at Trimbakeshwar in Maharashtra and drains into the Bay of Bengal.

Question 6

The Tropic of Cancer does NOT pass through which of the following Indian states?

  1. Tripura
  2. Odisha
  3. Rajasthan
  4. Gujarat
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Odisha

The Tropic of Cancer passes through Gujarat, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, West Bengal, Tripura and Mizoram. Odisha lies entirely to its south, so the line does not cross it.

Question 7

Scurvy, marked by bleeding gums and slow healing of wounds, is caused by a deficiency of which vitamin?

  1. Vitamin D
  2. Vitamin A
  3. Vitamin B1
  4. Vitamin C
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Vitamin C

Vitamin C (ascorbic acid) is needed to make collagen, so its deficiency causes scurvy. Vitamin D deficiency causes rickets, vitamin A deficiency causes night blindness and vitamin B1 deficiency causes beriberi.

Question 8

What is the pH of pure water at 25°C?

  1. 7
  2. 0
  3. 14
  4. 5.5
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 7

Pure water at 25°C is neutral, with equal concentrations of hydrogen and hydroxide ions, giving a pH of 7. Values below 7 are acidic and values above 7 are basic.

Question 9

The ancient Indian treatise 'Arthashastra' on statecraft and economics is attributed to:

  1. Kalidasa
  2. Banabhatta
  3. Kautilya
  4. Megasthenes
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. Kautilya

The Arthashastra is attributed to Kautilya, also known as Chanakya, adviser to Chandragupta Maurya. Megasthenes, a Greek envoy, wrote Indica, and Kalidasa was a poet and dramatist.

Question 10

What is the chemical formula of baking soda?

  1. Na₂CO₃
  2. NaCl
  3. NaHCO₃
  4. CaCO₃
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. NaHCO₃

Baking soda is sodium hydrogen carbonate, NaHCO₃. Na₂CO₃ is washing soda, NaCl is common salt and CaCO₃ is calcium carbonate, found in limestone and marble.

Question 11

In which of the following media does sound travel the fastest?

  1. Steel
  2. Water
  3. Air
  4. Vacuum
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Steel

Sound needs a medium and travels fastest in solids because their particles are tightly packed and elastic. It is slower in liquids, slower still in gases, and cannot travel through a vacuum at all.

Question 12

Which body releases the Consumer Price Index (CPI) for rural, urban and combined India?

  1. Reserve Bank of India
  2. National Statistical Office
  3. NITI Aayog
  4. Finance Commission
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. National Statistical Office

The CPI (Rural, Urban and Combined) is compiled and released by the National Statistical Office under the Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation. The RBI uses CPI inflation for its monetary policy but does not publish it.

Question 13

The idea of Fundamental Rights in the Indian Constitution was mainly borrowed from the Constitution of which country?

  1. United Kingdom
  2. United States of America
  3. Ireland
  4. Canada
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. United States of America

Fundamental Rights are modelled mainly on the Bill of Rights in the US Constitution. Directive Principles came from Ireland, parliamentary government from the United Kingdom and a federation with a strong Centre from Canada.

Question 14

The Battle of Talikota (1565) led to the decline of which empire?

  1. Mughal Empire
  2. Chola Empire
  3. Maratha Empire
  4. Vijayanagara Empire
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Vijayanagara Empire

In 1565 the combined Deccan Sultanates defeated the Vijayanagara army at Talikota, and the capital Hampi was sacked. The Marathas rose much later, and the Chola Empire had ended centuries earlier.

Question 15

Who was the first and only Indian to serve as Governor-General of independent India?

  1. Lord Mountbatten
  2. Dr. Rajendra Prasad
  3. Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel
  4. C. Rajagopalachari
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. C. Rajagopalachari

C. Rajagopalachari succeeded Lord Mountbatten in 1948 and served till the post was abolished in 1950. Mountbatten was the first Governor-General of independent India but was not Indian; Rajendra Prasad became the first President.
