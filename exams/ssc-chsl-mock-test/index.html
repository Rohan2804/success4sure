About the SSC CHSL exam

SSC CHSL Tier 1 is the first stage of the Combined Higher Secondary Level exam for posts such as LDC, JSA and DEO in central government offices, testing general intelligence, general awareness, quantitative aptitude and English.

Who should take it: Candidates who have passed Class 12 and are applying for central government clerical and data-entry posts.

Sample 1 · General Intelligence

Select the word that completes the analogy. Bird : Flock :: Fish : ?

  1. Herd
  2. Pack
  3. Swarm
  4. School
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. School

A group of birds is a flock; a group of fish is a school (or shoal).

Sample 2 · General Awareness

Fundamental Duties are contained in which Part of the Constitution of India?

  1. Part III
  2. Part IV-A
  3. Part IV
  4. Part V
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Part IV-A

Fundamental Duties (Article 51A) are in Part IV-A, inserted by the 42nd Amendment.

Sample 3 · Quantitative Aptitude

If a + b = 10 and ab = 21, what is the value of a² + b²?

  1. 79
  2. 58
  3. 42
  4. 62
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 58

a² + b² = (a + b)² − 2ab = 100 − 42 = 58.

Sample 4 · English Language

Select the most appropriate synonym of CANDID.

  1. Secretive
  2. Cunning
  3. Timid
  4. Frank
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Frank

Candid means honest and straightforward, i.e. frank.

Sample 5 · General Intelligence

Select the letter-cluster that completes the analogy. ACE : BDF :: GIK : ?

  1. HIJ
  2. HKM
  3. HJL
  4. IKM
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. HJL

Each letter moves one place forward: G→H, I→J, K→L.

SSC CHSL mock test FAQs

How many questions are there in the SSC CHSL exam?

The SSC CHSL paper has 100 multiple-choice questions for 200 marks, to be completed in 60 minutes. This is the indicative pattern — always confirm it in the latest official notification.

Is there negative marking in SSC CHSL?

Yes. −0.5 mark per wrong answer (25% of the marks for that question). Our mock tests apply the same rule.

What is the target score for SSC CHSL?

Cut-offs vary by year and category; our mock tests use 40% as a target score to aim for.

How many SSC CHSL mock tests do I get?

One full-length mock test is unlocked for each day of your plan: 10, 15, 20 or 30 mock tests on the 10 Days, 15 Days, 20 Days or 30 Days plans. You can retake any unlocked test while your plan is active.
