Privacy policy

Last updated 22 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data Success4Sure ("we") collects when you use success4sure.in, why, and your choices.

Data we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and mobile number when you sign up.
  • Payment details: payments are processed by our payment gateway partner. We receive the payment status and a transaction reference; we do not see or store your full card, UPI or bank credentials.
  • Usage data: your mock test attempts, answers and scores, so we can show results and analysis.
  • Technical data: basic logs such as IP address, browser and device type, used for security and to keep the service running.

How we use it

  • To provide your account, subscriptions and mock tests.
  • To process payments, issue GST invoices and handle refunds.
  • To respond to support requests and send service messages about your account.
  • To keep the service secure and improve it.

We do not sell your personal data.

Sharing

We share data only with service providers who help us run the service — such as hosting, payment processing and email — under appropriate safeguards, or where required by law.

Retention

We keep account and test data while your account is active and for a reasonable period afterwards for legal, tax and accounting purposes, then delete or anonymise it.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, or withdraw consent, by writing to the contact details on our Contact page. We will respond in line with applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Cookies and local storage

We use essential browser storage to keep you logged in and remember your progress. We do not use advertising cookies.

Changes

We may update this policy and will show the date of the latest version at the top of this page.
