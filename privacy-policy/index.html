Privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data Success4Sure ("we") collects when you use success4sure.in, why, and your choices.

Data we collect

  • Account details: if you sign in with Google, your name, email address and profile photo from your Google account; if you create an email account, the details you enter.
  • No payment data: Success4Sure is free, so we do not collect any card, UPI or bank details.
  • Usage data: your mock test attempts, answers and scores, so we can show results and analysis.
  • Technical data: basic logs such as IP address, browser and device type, used for security and to keep the service running.

How we use it

  • To provide your account, save your mock-test results and lesson progress, and show your analysis.
  • To respond to support requests and send service messages about your account.
  • To keep the service secure and improve it.

We do not sell your personal data.

Sharing

We share data only with service providers who help us run the service — such as hosting, sign-in (Google and Supabase), analytics (Google Analytics) and email — under appropriate safeguards, or where required by law.

Retention

We keep account and test data while your account is active and for a reasonable period afterwards, then delete or anonymise it. You can ask us to delete your account at any time.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, or withdraw consent, by writing to the contact details on our Contact page. We will respond in line with applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Cookies, local storage and analytics

We use essential browser storage to keep you logged in and remember your progress. We also use Google Analytics (through Google Tag Manager) to understand how the site is used — for example which pages are visited, which exams are practised and how often a link or banner is clicked. Google Analytics sets analytics cookies and receives information such as pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser; we do not send it your name, email address or answers. You can opt out with Google's Analytics opt-out browser add-on or by blocking cookies in your browser. We do not use advertising cookies.

Changes

We may update this policy and will show the date of the latest version at the top of this page.
