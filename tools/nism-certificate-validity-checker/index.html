NISM Certificate Validity Checker

NISM certificates are valid for three years from the date you pass the exam. Enter your series and the date you passed to see your expiry date and when to start renewing.

Last updated 23 November 2026 · Free to use

How the NISM validity checker works

An NISM certificate is valid for three years from the date of passing the certification examination.

To keep it valid you can pass the exam again, or — for series where NISM offers it — complete an accredited Continuing Professional Education (CPE) programme before expiry. Rules can change, so confirm on nism.ac.in.

If you work in a role that requires the certificate (for example as a mutual fund distributor, investment adviser or research analyst), let it lapse only after checking with your employer or the relevant registration body.

FAQs

How long is an NISM certificate valid?

Three years from the date you pass the examination. The expiry date is printed on your certificate and can be checked on the NISM portal.

Can I renew my NISM certificate without an exam?

For some series NISM accredits Continuing Professional Education (CPE) programmes that can be used to renew the certificate. Check the NISM website for the series and the time window in which CPE must be completed.

What happens if my NISM certificate expires?

You may not be able to continue in a role that requires it until you re-certify. Plan ahead: book the exam or CPE programme two to three months before expiry.
