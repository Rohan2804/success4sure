SIP Calculator

See what a monthly SIP could grow to over time. Add a yearly step-up to see how raising your SIP with each salary hike changes the result.

Last updated 23 November 2026 · Free to use

How the SIP calculator works

Each monthly instalment is invested at the start of the month and grows at the expected return, compounded monthly. With a step-up, the SIP amount rises by that percentage every 12 months.

Mutual fund returns are not fixed. Equity funds can fall in some years, and past returns do not indicate future returns. Use a conservative rate and treat the result as an illustration, not a promise.

This calculator is for education only and is not investment advice. Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks; read all scheme-related documents carefully.

FAQs

What return should I assume for a SIP?

There is no fixed answer because market-linked returns vary. Many people test a few rates — for example 8%, 10% and 12% for equity funds — to see a range of outcomes. A lower assumed rate gives a more conservative plan.

What is a step-up SIP?

A step-up SIP increases your monthly instalment by a fixed percentage each year, usually in line with salary hikes. Even a 5–10% yearly increase can make a large difference to the final amount over 10 years or more.

Is the SIP calculator result guaranteed?

No. It is a mathematical estimate based on the rate you enter. Actual mutual fund returns depend on markets and can be higher or lower, including negative in some periods.
