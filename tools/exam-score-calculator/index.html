Exam Score Calculator

Pick your exam and enter how many questions you got right and wrong. The calculator applies that exam's marks per question and negative marking, and tells you how far you are from the pass mark or target score.

Last updated 20 November 2026 · Free to use

How the Exam score calculator works

Score = (correct answers × marks per question) − (wrong answers × negative marks per wrong answer). Unattempted questions score zero.

Marks per question and the negative-marking rule come from the indicative pattern on each exam's page. Many exams deduct a quarter of a question's marks for each wrong answer; NISM exams with negative marking typically deduct 25%.

Competitive exams such as IBPS, SBI, SSC and CAT have sectional and overall cut-offs that change every year and by category, so the target score shown is a practice benchmark, not an official cut-off.

FAQs

Should I attempt questions I am unsure about?

With negative marking of one quarter, a guess between two options has a positive expected value, but a blind guess between four does not. Eliminate at least one or two options before guessing.

How is negative marking calculated?

If a question carries 1 mark and the penalty is 25%, each wrong answer deducts 0.25 marks. Four wrong answers cancel out one correct answer.

Is this my official result?

No. It is an estimate from your own count of right and wrong answers. Official scores may be normalised across shifts, and cut-offs are published by the exam body.
