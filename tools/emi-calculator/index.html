EMI Calculator

Work out the monthly EMI for any loan and see how much of what you repay is interest. Useful before taking a personal, education, car or home loan — or a credit card EMI.

Last updated 16 November 2026 · Free, no sign-up

How the EMI calculator works

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)ⁿ ÷ ((1 + r)ⁿ − 1), where P is the loan amount, r is the monthly interest rate (annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100) and n is the number of monthly instalments.

In the early years most of each EMI goes towards interest; later, more of it repays the principal. That is why prepaying early saves the most interest.

Lenders may add processing fees, insurance or GST on charges, and rates can change on floating-rate loans. Check the loan agreement for the exact figures.

FAQs

How can I reduce my EMI?

You can choose a longer tenure, make a larger down payment, or negotiate a lower interest rate. A longer tenure lowers the EMI but increases the total interest you pay, so compare the total cost, not just the EMI.

Is a no-cost EMI really free?

Often the interest is offset by a discount the seller gives the lender, but processing fees and GST on interest may still apply, and you may lose a cash discount. Read the terms and compare the total amount paid.

How much EMI is affordable?

A common rule of thumb is to keep all EMIs together below 30–40% of your monthly take-home pay, so you still have room for savings, emergencies and regular expenses.
