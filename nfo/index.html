What is an NFO?

A New Fund Offer is the first subscription period of a new mutual fund scheme, when units are usually offered at ₹10 each. Open-ended schemes reopen for continuous purchase shortly after the NFO closes; close-ended schemes can only be bought during the NFO (and later on the stock exchange, if listed). A ₹10 NFO price does not make a fund "cheap" — what matters is the strategy, costs and how it fits your goals. Learn more: finance glossary · mutual fund articles.
