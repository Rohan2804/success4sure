A Growth Mindset in Practice

Turn the idea that abilities can grow into everyday habits: rewriting self-limiting thoughts, using feedback as data and reviewing mistakes calmly. You will be able to pick stretch goals and track real progress.

Last updated 16 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team

What a growth mindset is — and what it is not

A growth mindset is the belief that your abilities can improve through effort, good strategies and help from others. A fixed mindset assumes abilities are set: you are either "good at maths" or not, "a natural speaker" or not.

It is easy to turn this into a slogan. A growth mindset is not positive thinking, and it does not mean anyone can become anything with enough willpower. Effort alone is not enough either — working hard with a poor method just tires you out. Research suggests simply believing in growth has modest effects; what matters is what you do differently because of that belief.

So treat this lesson as a set of habits, not a personality type. Most people are a mix: confident about growing in some areas and stuck in others.

Do
  • Pair effort with a better strategy
  • Ask for help when you are stuck
Don't
  • Treat growth mindset as just 'thinking positive'

In the full lesson

  • Catch and rewrite your fixed scripts
  • Use feedback as data, not a verdict
  • Review mistakes without beating yourself up
  • Stretch sensibly and track progress

Key takeaways

  • A growth mindset is a set of habits, not just a belief.
  • Rewrite fixed scripts with 'yet' and one next step.
  • Treat feedback as information about the work, not about you.
  • Review mistakes with: what happened, what I controlled, what next.
  • Pick stretch tasks one step beyond comfort and track progress.

Quick check

Which statement best reflects a growth mindset in practice?

  1. "Anyone can become anything at all if they believe hard enough."
  2. "Some people are just born good at this."
  3. "I can improve at this with practice, better methods and help."
  4. "Effort alone guarantees success."
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. "I can improve at this with practice, better methods and help."

Growth comes from effort combined with good strategies and support, not belief or effort alone.
