First-Time Manager

Make the jump from peer to boss — delegate, give feedback and lead your first team.

Last updated 5 November 2026 · Our editorial process

See plans & startRead lesson 1 free

Lessons in this course

LessonForReadAccess
From Peer to Boss
Handle the first weeks after being promoted over your former colleagues. You will learn how to reset relationships, set fair boundaries and earn credibility without trying to prove yourself overnight.
Everyone7 minFree
Delegating Well
Decide what to hand over, brief people so they can deliver, and check progress without micromanaging. You will learn a simple briefing structure and how to match the level of freedom to the person.
Everyone7 minPlan
Giving Feedback as a Manager
Give specific praise, useful corrective feedback and handle difficult conversations calmly. You will learn a simple situation-behaviour-impact structure and phrases you can use the same day.
Everyone8 minPlan
Running Useful One-on-Ones
Run regular one-on-ones that your team finds useful rather than a status check. You will learn how to set a rhythm, use good questions and follow through on what you hear.
Everyone7 minPlan
Leading Without the Title
Show leadership in your first job before you manage anyone. You will learn how to own your work, take sensible initiative, influence peers and make your interest in leading visible.
Freshers7 minPlan
Leading a Sales or Advisory Team Ethically
Lead relationship managers or advisers so that targets are met through suitable advice, not mis-selling. You will learn how to set balanced goals, coach client conversations, handle complaints and escalate compliance concerns.
Finance professionals8 minPlan

FAQs

Who is the First-Time Manager course for?

Freshers preparing for or starting their first job, and finance professionals such as mutual fund distributors, investment advisers and relationship managers. Core lessons suit everyone; one lesson is written for each of the two groups.

Full access to all 6 lessons, 60 quiz questions with explanations and 18 practice scenarios for the length of your plan: 10 Days ₹199, 15 Days ₹279, 20 Days ₹349, 30 Days ₹449. Prices include GST.

Can I try it first?

Yes. The first lesson, “From Peer to Boss”, is free with its quiz and scenarios.
