Leading a Sales or Advisory Team Ethically

Lead relationship managers or advisers so that targets are met through suitable advice, not mis-selling. You will learn how to set balanced goals, coach client conversations, handle complaints and escalate compliance concerns.

Last updated 5 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team

Your team copies what you reward

As a new branch or team manager in a bank, AMC distributor or advisory firm, you sit between business targets from above and clients who trust your team with their savings. That position carries real influence: your team will learn far more from what you celebrate than from any compliance training.

If the only thing praised in the morning huddle is the day's highest sale, people hear "sell at any cost." If you also recognise the relationship manager who talked a 62-year-old client out of a high-risk product that did not fit her needs, they hear that suitability matters here.

Targets are a normal part of financial services. The ethical question is not whether to have them but how they are met. Say it plainly and often: "I want the numbers, but only from the right product for the right client."

Do
  • Recognise good advice, not just big sales
  • Repeat that suitability comes first
Don't
  • Celebrate only the highest number of the day

In the full lesson

  • Set goals that do not reward mis-selling
  • Coach the client conversation
  • Handle complaints properly
  • Escalate compliance concerns

Key takeaways

  • Your team copies what you praise — recognise suitable advice, not just sales.
  • Review complaints, early exits and needs-analysis quality alongside volume.
  • Coach real conversations and correct any promise of returns.
  • Handle complaints by listening, investigating and logging them properly.
  • Escalate serious concerns through compliance; never cover them up.

Quick check

Which huddle message best supports ethical selling?

  1. "Whoever sells the most today gets an early leave."
  2. "I want the numbers, but only from the right product for the right client."
  3. "Focus on the product with the best payout this month."
  4. "Don't overthink suitability; clients are adults and can decide for themselves."
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. "I want the numbers, but only from the right product for the right client."

It sets the target and the standard for how it is met.
