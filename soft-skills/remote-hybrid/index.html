About this Remote & Hybrid Work course

Working from home or in a hybrid team needs more than a laptop and a good connection. This remote work skills course shows you how to write async messages a colleague can act on hours later, follow sensible Slack, Teams and WhatsApp etiquette, and stay visible to your manager without bragging. You also get work from home tips that fit Indian homes, with shared rooms, family and power cuts in the picture. Freshers learn to start a first job remotely, and finance professionals learn to serve clients safely over video and phone, building hybrid work skills that last.

What you will learn

  • Write async messages and updates colleagues can act on without a follow-up call
  • Choose the right channel for chats, threads, mentions, voice notes and WhatsApp groups
  • Send short weekly updates that keep your work visible to your manager
  • Set up a work spot, daily routine and backup plan for power cuts
  • Settle into a remote first job by asking smart questions and seeking feedback early
  • Run secure, compliant client calls with consent, safe screen sharing and written summaries

Who this course is for

  • Freshers starting a remote or hybrid first job
  • Early-career professionals working from home most days
  • Team members who feel unnoticed in online meetings
  • Distributors, advisers and RMs who serve clients over video calls

FAQs

Who is the Remote & Hybrid Work course for?

Freshers preparing for or starting their first job, and finance professionals such as mutual fund distributors, investment advisers and relationship managers. Core lessons suit everyone; one lesson is written for each of the two groups.

Is this course free?

Yes. All 6 lessons, 60 quiz questions with explanations and 18 practice scenarios are free. Create a free account (one click with Google) to start; your progress is saved across devices.

How can I be more productive when working from home?

Give your day a clear shape: a fixed start time, planned focus blocks and a proper end to the workday. Set up a dedicated work spot, even a small corner, agree quiet hours with family and keep a backup for power and internet problems. Small routines matter more than long hours.

What is async communication at work?

Async communication means sharing information so colleagues can respond in their own time instead of in a live call. A good async message stands on its own: bottom line first, full context, clear timelines and what you need from the reader. Switch to a quick call when things get confusing, then write down the outcome.

How do I stay visible to my manager when working remotely?

Send a short weekly update covering what you finished, what you are working on and where you are stuck. Speak up early in online meetings, keep your camera on when it is expected and build relationships deliberately with short one-to-one chats. Visibility comes from clarity, not from longer hours.

What is the right etiquette for WhatsApp and Teams at work?

Use each channel for its purpose: urgent items on a call, quick questions on chat, decisions in email or a shared document. Send one complete message rather than many fragments, keep replies in threads, use mentions sparingly and respect colleagues' off-hours, especially on personal WhatsApp numbers.
