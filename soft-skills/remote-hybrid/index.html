Remote & Hybrid Work

Communicate across time and distance, stay visible and stay disciplined when working remotely.

Lessons in this course

LessonForReadAccess
Async Communication That Moves Work Forward
Write messages and updates that a colleague can act on hours later without a follow-up call. You will learn to give full context, set clear timelines and know when to switch to a live conversation.
Everyone7 minFree
Teams, Slack and WhatsApp Etiquette for Work
Choose the right chat channel for each kind of message and write chats that respect colleagues' time and boundaries. You will learn practical habits for threads, mentions, voice notes and work WhatsApp groups.
Everyone7 minPlan
Being Visible When You Work Remotely
Make your work and contribution visible to your manager and team without bragging or working longer hours. You will learn to write short weekly updates, contribute in online meetings and build relationships deliberately.
Everyone7 minPlan
Self-Discipline When Working from Home
Set up a workable routine, space and backup plan for working from an Indian home, with family, shared rooms and power cuts in the picture. You will learn to protect focus time and end the workday on time.
Everyone8 minPlan
Starting Your First Job Remotely
Prepare for a remote or hybrid first job and settle in confidently during the first month. You will learn how to get set up, ask good questions, connect with teammates you haven't met and ask for early feedback.
Freshers8 minPlan
Serving Clients over Video and Phone
Run client conversations over video and phone that are secure, compliant and easy for the client to follow. You will learn to confirm identity and consent, share screens safely, explain products suitably and send clear follow-up summaries.
Finance professionals9 minPlan

FAQs

Who is the Remote & Hybrid Work course for?

Freshers preparing for or starting their first job, and finance professionals such as mutual fund distributors, investment advisers and relationship managers. Core lessons suit everyone; one lesson is written for each of the two groups.

What do I get with a plan?

Full access to all 6 lessons, 60 quiz questions with explanations and 18 practice scenarios for the length of your plan: 10 Days ₹199, 15 Days ₹279, 20 Days ₹349, 30 Days ₹449. Prices include GST.

Can I try it first?

Yes. The first lesson, “Async Communication That Moves Work Forward”, is free with its quiz and scenarios.
