Starting Your First Job Remotely

Prepare for a remote or hybrid first job and settle in confidently during the first month. You will learn how to get set up, ask good questions, connect with teammates you haven't met and ask for early feedback.

Last updated 5 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team

Before day one: get set up

Many freshers now join through online onboarding, sometimes after campus placements or a selection process that was also online. Read your joining email carefully and note the date, time, reporting manager, HR contact and any documents you need to submit. Keep scanned copies of your certificates, ID and address proof ready in a clearly named folder.

If a company laptop is couriered to you, check it as soon as it arrives. Log in, connect to your home Wi-Fi, and test the camera, microphone and any VPN or login steps described in the onboarding email. If something doesn't work, inform the IT helpdesk before your first day, not ten minutes into your first meeting.

Set up a quiet spot with a headset and good light, and keep a notebook or notes app ready. Day one will bring a lot of new names, tools and acronyms.

Do
  • Test the laptop, login and video tools before day one
  • Keep documents scanned and clearly named
Don't
  • Open the courier box for the first time on joining day
  • Ignore onboarding emails until your start date

In the full lesson

  • Learn how your team works
  • Ask for help the smart way
  • Connect with people you haven't met
  • Show progress and ask for feedback early

Key takeaways

  • Test your laptop, logins and tools before your first day.
  • Ask how the team communicates and keep a glossary of terms and people.
  • Try for a reasonable time, then ask, showing what you've tried.
  • Introduce yourself and request short chats with each teammate.
  • Share small wins and ask for specific feedback around 30 days.

Quick check

Your company laptop arrives three days before your joining date. What should you do?

  1. Leave it sealed until day one so IT can set it up with you
  2. Log in and test the camera, mic and VPN now
  3. Install your favourite games first
  4. Give it to IT to check
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Log in and test the camera, mic and VPN now

Testing early gives time to fix problems before your first meeting.
