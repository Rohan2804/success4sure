Working with AI

Use AI tools to work faster — write good prompts, check the output and keep data safe.

Last updated 5 November 2026 · Our editorial process

See plans & startRead lesson 1 free

Lessons in this course

LessonForReadAccess
Writing Prompts That Get Useful Answers
Write prompts for AI chat tools that give the task, the context and the format you need. You will learn to turn a one-line request into a clear brief and to improve answers through follow-up instead of starting over.
Everyone7 minFree
Checking AI Output Before You Use It
Spot made-up facts, wrong figures and one-sided answers in AI output before they reach your manager or client. You will learn a practical way to verify claims and to match the depth of checking to the stakes.
Everyone8 minPlan
Using AI Safely: Privacy, Confidentiality and Ethics
Decide what you can and cannot share with AI tools and follow your organisation's rules. You will learn to remove personal and confidential data from prompts, and to be honest about when you have used AI.
Everyone8 minPlan
Using AI for Emails, Research and Summaries
Use AI chat tools to draft emails, summarise long documents and start research faster, while keeping your own judgement in charge. You will learn practical prompts for each task and when doing it yourself is quicker.
Everyone8 minPlan
Using AI Honestly in Your Job Search
Use AI to tailor your resume, draft cover letters and practise interviews without inventing anything about yourself. You will learn where the line is during online tests and interviews so that a shortcut never costs you an offer.
Freshers8 minPlan
AI for Finance Professionals: Client Data and Compliance
Use AI to save time on drafting and preparation while keeping client data private and meeting conduct expectations. You will learn why suitability and advice stay your responsibility, how to check AI-generated figures and what records to keep.
Finance professionals9 minPlan

FAQs

Who is the Working with AI course for?

Freshers preparing for or starting their first job, and finance professionals such as mutual fund distributors, investment advisers and relationship managers. Core lessons suit everyone; one lesson is written for each of the two groups.

What do I get with a plan?

Full access to all 6 lessons, 60 quiz questions with explanations and 18 practice scenarios for the length of your plan: 10 Days ₹199, 15 Days ₹279, 20 Days ₹349, 30 Days ₹449. Prices include GST.

Can I try it first?

Yes. The first lesson, “Writing Prompts That Get Useful Answers”, is free with its quiz and scenarios.
