Stress, Burnout & Work–Life Balance

Spot burnout early, set boundaries politely and build routines that help you recover.

Last updated 5 November 2026 · Our editorial process

See plans & startRead lesson 1 free
Practical everyday advice, not medical advice. If stress, low mood or anxiety lasts for weeks or affects daily life, please talk to a doctor or qualified counsellor.

Lessons in this course

LessonForReadAccess
Spotting Burnout Early
Recognise the early signs of burnout before they pile up, and take sensible first steps. You will learn a simple weekly self-check and what to do when the warning lights come on.
Everyone7 minFree
Setting Boundaries at Work Politely
Set clear, polite limits around late-night messages, weekend work and workload without sounding difficult. You will learn ready-to-use phrases and how to agree boundaries with your manager.
Everyone7 minPlan
Saying No Without Guilt
Decline requests clearly and kindly, and handle the guilt that often follows. You will learn a simple three-part no, how to offer real alternatives and how to respond calmly to pushback.
Everyone7 minPlan
Daily Recovery: Sleep, Breaks and Switching Off
Build simple daily habits that help you recover from work: steadier sleep, real breaks and a clear end to the workday. You will put together a small routine that fits your schedule.
Everyone7 minPlan
Handling First-Job Pressure and Imposter Feelings
Understand why your first job can feel overwhelming and what imposter feelings are. You will learn practical ways to ask for help, track your progress and talk to yourself more fairly, and when to seek support.
Freshers7 minPlan
Handling Target Pressure in Finance and Sales Roles
Cope with sales and business targets in a healthy, ethical way. You will learn to break targets into daily actions, keep suitability ahead of numbers and have a constructive conversation with your manager.
Finance professionals8 minPlan

Who is the Stress, Burnout & Work–Life Balance course for?

Freshers preparing for or starting their first job, and finance professionals such as mutual fund distributors, investment advisers and relationship managers. Core lessons suit everyone; one lesson is written for each of the two groups.
