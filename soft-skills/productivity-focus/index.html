Beat phone distraction, protect deep-work time and get the important things done.

Lessons in this course

LessonForReadAccess
Beating Digital Distraction
Cut unnecessary notifications, place your phone where it cannot pull you away, and break the automatic scroll habit. You will leave with a notification plan and a simple routine for checking your phone on your terms.
Everyone7 minFree
Deep-Work Blocks for Focused Results
Plan and protect blocks of uninterrupted time for your most demanding work. You will learn to define a clear finish line, guard the block from interruptions and end it with a next step ready.
Everyone7 minPlan
Planning Your Week in 20 Minutes
Run a simple 20-minute weekly planning routine: review last week, capture everything, choose three outcomes and place them in your calendar. You will also learn a five-minute daily check to keep the plan realistic.
Everyone7 minPlan
Busy vs Productive: Doing What Matters
Tell the difference between looking busy and moving important work forward. You will learn to spot busy-work, judge your day by results, renegotiate low-value requests and stop over-polishing small tasks.
Everyone7 minPlan
Balancing a Job, Exams and Upskilling
Build a realistic weekly routine that fits exam preparation or a course around a full-time job. You will learn to map your real free time, choose one main goal per season, use fixed study slots and handle busy work periods without guilt.
Freshers8 minPlan
Running a Productive Day of Client Meetings and Paperwork
Plan a day of client meetings so that preparation, travel, notes and paperwork fit together. You will learn to cluster meetings, prepare in ten minutes, record notes straight after each meeting and close the day with nothing left hanging.

FAQs

Who is the Productivity & Focus course for?

Freshers preparing for or starting their first job, and finance professionals such as mutual fund distributors, investment advisers and relationship managers. Core lessons suit everyone; one lesson is written for each of the two groups.

What do I get with a plan?

Full access to all 6 lessons, 60 quiz questions with explanations and 18 practice scenarios for the length of your plan: 10 Days ₹199, 15 Days ₹279, 20 Days ₹349, 30 Days ₹449. Prices include GST.

Can I try it first?

Yes. The first lesson, “Beating Digital Distraction”, is free with its quiz and scenarios.
