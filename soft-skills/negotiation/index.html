Negotiation & Persuasion

Prepare your ask, find win–win outcomes and persuade people without pressure.

Lessons in this course

LessonForReadAccess
Preparing to Negotiate
Prepare for any negotiation by fixing a clear goal, a walk-away point and a Plan B. You will also learn to map what the other side really needs before you say a word.
Everyone7 minFree
Creating Win–Win Offers
Build offers that give both sides more of what they value, by bringing several issues to the table and trading instead of conceding. You will learn to present options and close with a clear written agreement.
Everyone7 minPlan
Handling "No" and Tough Counter-Offers
Respond calmly when someone says no or makes a tough counter-offer. You will learn to find the reason behind a refusal, reply with criteria instead of emotion, and walk away politely when needed.
Everyone7 minPlan
Persuading Without Pressure
Persuade colleagues, friends and clients by linking your idea to their goals, backing it with evidence and respecting their right to say no. You will learn to spot and avoid manipulative tactics.
Everyone7 minPlan
Negotiating Deadlines and Workload in Your First Job
Handle new tasks, tight deadlines and overload in your first job without saying a blunt no or quietly burning out. You will learn to clarify requests, ask your manager to prioritise and propose realistic options early.
Freshers7 minPlan
Negotiating Fees and Terms with Clients Ethically
Discuss fees, commissions and service terms with clients openly, negotiate only what you are allowed to change, and never let a sale override suitability. You will learn phrases for fee conversations and how to record what you agree.
Finance professionals8 minPlan
