Money Skills for Young Earners

Plan your salary, budget without stress, avoid debt traps and understand investing basics.

Last updated 5 November 2026 · Our editorial process

Lessons in this course

LessonForReadAccess
Your First Salary: A Simple Plan
Understand what actually reaches your bank account each month and set up a simple plan for your first salary. You will learn to build an emergency fund first and automate saving so it happens without willpower.
Everyone7 minFree
Budgeting That Actually Works
Set up a simple budget you can actually stick to, using a split like 50/30/20 as a starting point. You will learn to spot where small UPI payments leak money and plan for yearly expenses before they surprise you.
Everyone7 minPlan
Credit Cards, Loans and Buy-Now-Pay-Later Traps
Use credit cards, EMIs and buy-now-pay-later without falling into expensive debt. You will learn why paying only the minimum due is costly, what affects your credit score and how to check the true cost of an EMI.
Everyone8 minPlan
Investing Basics: Compounding, Risk and Time
Understand the core ideas behind investing: compounding, inflation, risk versus return, diversification and time horizon. You will learn to judge claims critically and see why starting early and investing regularly matter.
Everyone8 minPlan
Money Scams Targeting Students and Freshers
Recognise the money scams most often aimed at students and freshers, from fake job fees to UPI collect-request fraud. You will learn the warning signs, what never to share, and exactly what to do if you are targeted.
Freshers8 minPlan
Helping Clients Build Better Money Habits
Hold useful goal and habit conversations with clients while staying within your role and regulatory responsibilities. You will learn to educate without pushing products, keep conversations suitable and honest, and know when to refer a client to a registered adviser.
Finance professionals8 minPlan

FAQs

Who is the Money Skills for Young Earners course for?

Freshers preparing for or starting their first job, and finance professionals such as mutual fund distributors, investment advisers and relationship managers. Core lessons suit everyone; one lesson is written for each of the two groups.

What do I get with a plan?

Full access to all 6 lessons, 60 quiz questions with explanations and 18 practice scenarios for the length of your plan: 10 Days ₹199, 15 Days ₹279, 20 Days ₹349, 30 Days ₹449. Prices include GST.

Can I try it first?

Yes. The first lesson, “Your First Salary: A Simple Plan”, is free with its quiz and scenarios.
