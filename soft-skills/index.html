How the courses work

Each course has six lessons: four core lessons for everyone, one for freshers and one for finance professionals such as mutual fund distributors, advisers and relationship managers. Every lesson takes 5–8 minutes to read and ends with a 10-question quiz and three scenarios where you choose what you would say and see why it works. Plans are per course: 10, 15, 20 or 30 days of full access, from ₹199.
