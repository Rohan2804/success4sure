Soft skills for your career

Our soft skills courses online cover the skills employers notice from day one: clear communication, confident interviews, teamwork, working with AI, critical thinking, productivity, networking, negotiation and handling money and stress. There are 16 short courses, each with six bite-size lessons, quick quizzes and realistic workplace scenarios. They suit freshers, students and early-career professionals, and several lessons are written for mutual fund distributors, advisers and relationship managers. Pick a course, try the first lesson free and build one practical skill at a time.

How the courses work

Each course has six lessons: four core lessons for everyone, one for freshers and one for finance professionals such as mutual fund distributors, advisers and relationship managers. Every lesson takes 5–8 minutes to read and ends with a 10-question quiz and three scenarios where you choose what you would say and see why it works. Every course is free — just create a free account.
