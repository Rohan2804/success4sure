About this Personal Branding & Networking course

Your next opportunity often comes from someone who found you, remembered you or was happy to refer you. This personal branding course online helps you get there honestly. You will rewrite your LinkedIn headline and About section with practical LinkedIn profile tips, write posts that sound like you and build networking skills for freshers and experienced professionals alike, from event openers to follow-up messages. You will also learn to ask for referrals the right way. Students get a 30-day starter plan, and finance professionals learn to build trust online while staying compliant.

  • Ask for job referrals and introductions with a specific, forwardable message

FAQs

Who is the Personal Branding & Networking course for?

Freshers preparing for or starting their first job, and finance professionals such as mutual fund distributors, investment advisers and relationship managers. Core lessons suit everyone; one lesson is written for each of the two groups.

Is this course free?

Yes. All 6 lessons, 60 quiz questions with explanations and 18 practice scenarios are free. You can start without an account; log in with Google to save your progress.

How do I build a personal brand on LinkedIn as a fresher?

Pick two or three topics you want to be known for, then create small pieces of proof such as projects, write-ups or certificates and share what you are learning in public. Keep your profile specific and honest, and post or comment regularly. A simple 30-day routine is easier to sustain than bursts of activity.

What should I write in my LinkedIn headline?

Say what you do or want to do, for whom, and with which skills, using words people actually search for. For example, 'Final-year B.Com student | Excel and financial analysis | Interested in banking operations' works better than 'Seeking opportunities'. Keep it truthful and update it as your role changes.

How do I ask someone for a job referral?

Ask people who know you or your work, even slightly, and make the request specific: the role, the job link and why you fit. Write a short message they can forward easily, make it simple to decline, and follow up once. Always report back on the outcome and say thank you.

How can I network if I am an introvert?

Treat networking as building a few genuine relationships rather than collecting contacts. Prepare two or three openers before an event, ask questions and listen, and leave conversations politely when you are ready. Online, send short personalised connection requests and stay in touch by sharing something useful now and then.
