A LinkedIn Profile That Gets Found
Write a LinkedIn headline, About section and skills list that use the words recruiters and peers actually search for. You will turn a generic profile into a specific, honest one that people can find and trust.
Everyone8 minFree
Posting on LinkedIn Without the Cringe
Write short, useful LinkedIn posts and comments that sound like you, not like a template. You will learn simple post formats, a four-line structure and the common habits that make posts feel fake.
Everyone8 minPlan
Networking at Events and Online
Start conversations at events, send connection requests people accept, and follow up so that a single meeting becomes a lasting professional relationship. You will practise openers, graceful exits and follow-up messages.
Everyone8 minPlan
Asking for Referrals and Introductions
Ask for job referrals and introductions in a way that is specific, easy to say yes to and easy to decline. You will write a forwardable request and learn how to follow up and say thank you.
Everyone7 minPlan
Building a Personal Brand with No Experience
Build a credible professional reputation as a student or fresher by choosing a focus, creating small pieces of proof and learning in public. You will plan a simple 30-day routine you can actually keep.
Freshers8 minPlan

Build a credible online presence as a distributor, adviser or bank RM by describing your role accurately, sharing education instead of promises and protecting client data. You will rewrite risky posts into compliant, trustworthy ones.
