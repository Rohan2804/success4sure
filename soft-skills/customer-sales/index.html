Customer & Sales Conversations

Build rapport, understand needs before pitching and follow up without being pushy.

Last updated 5 November 2026 · Our editorial process

See plans & startRead lesson 1 free

Lessons in this course

LessonForReadAccess
Building Rapport Quickly
Open customer conversations in a way that makes people comfortable talking to you. You will learn to prepare, greet well, listen visibly and find genuine common ground without sounding scripted.
Everyone7 minFree
Needs-Based Conversations: Ask Before You Pitch
Use discovery questions to understand what a customer actually needs before recommending anything. You will learn to ask open questions, dig one level deeper, confirm needs in a summary and link your recommendation to what you heard.
Everyone8 minPlan
Follow-Ups That Aren't Pushy
Follow up with customers in a way that is helpful rather than annoying. You will learn to agree the next step before a conversation ends, add value in each message, choose the right channel and timing, and close the loop gracefully.
Everyone7 minPlan
Getting Referrals from Happy Customers
Ask satisfied customers for referrals at the right moment and in a way that feels natural. You will learn to earn the referral first, make a specific ask, respect privacy and consent, and thank both the customer and the person referred.
Everyone7 minPlan
Handling Customer Queries and Complaints in Your First Job
Handle customer questions and complaints calmly in a bank branch, call centre or retail job. You will learn a simple step-by-step approach, phrases that calm people down, how to give honest timelines and when and how to escalate.
Freshers8 minPlan
Ethical Selling: Suitability, Disclosure and Saying No
Run a suitability-first sales process as a distributor, adviser or relationship manager. You will learn to assess a client's situation and risk, disclose risks, costs and your earnings clearly, document the basis of your recommendation and decline sales that do not fit.
Finance professionals9 minPlan

FAQs

Who is the Customer & Sales Conversations course for?

Freshers preparing for or starting their first job, and finance professionals such as mutual fund distributors, investment advisers and relationship managers. Core lessons suit everyone; one lesson is written for each of the two groups.

What do I get with a plan?

Full access to all 6 lessons, 60 quiz questions with explanations and 18 practice scenarios for the length of your plan: 10 Days ₹199, 15 Days ₹279, 20 Days ₹349, 30 Days ₹449. Prices include GST.

Can I try it first?

Yes. The first lesson, “Building Rapport Quickly”, is free with its quiz and scenarios.
