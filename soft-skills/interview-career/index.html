About this Interview & Career Skills course

This interview skills course online prepares you for the moments that decide a selection: the opening question, the HR round, the group discussion and the offer conversation. You will build a strong answer to "tell me about yourself", create STAR stories from your own experience and turn your resume into evidence of results. It works as focused interview preparation for freshers, and it also covers lateral moves and client-facing finance roles. Each lesson includes a realistic scenario, so you practise answering HR interview questions out loud rather than memorising scripts.

What you will learn

  • Build a 60–90 second answer to "Tell me about yourself" using Present, Past, Future
  • Answer behavioural HR questions with clear STAR stories from a ready story bank
  • Rewrite duty-style resume bullets into results and align your LinkedIn profile
  • Understand CTC versus in-hand pay and discuss salary expectations calmly
  • Open, build on others and summarise well in bank PO and MBA group discussions
  • Show client focus and ethics in relationship manager and adviser interviews

  • Bank PO and MBA aspirants preparing for group discussions

  • Candidates for relationship manager, adviser and distributor roles

FAQs

Who is the Interview & Career Skills course for?

Freshers preparing for or starting their first job, and finance professionals such as mutual fund distributors, investment advisers and relationship managers. Core lessons suit everyone; one lesson is written for each of the two groups.

How should a fresher answer "Tell me about yourself"?

Use a simple Present, Past, Future structure: what you are doing now, the relevant study or projects that brought you here, and why this role fits what you want next. Keep it to 60–90 seconds, link each part to the job, and practise the key points rather than memorising a script word for word.

What is the STAR method in interviews?

STAR stands for Situation, Task, Action and Result. It is a way to answer behavioural questions such as "Tell me about a time you handled pressure" with a short, real story. Spend most of your answer on the actions you personally took and end with a specific result or what you learned.

How do I perform well in a group discussion?

Evaluators look for relevant content, listening and teamwork, not volume. Enter with a clear point, build on what others say, invite quieter members in and offer a balanced summary at the end. Avoid shouting over people, hijacking the topic or staying silent for the whole discussion.

What is the difference between CTC and in-hand salary?

CTC is the total annual cost to the company, including fixed pay, variable pay, employer PF contributions and other benefits. In-hand salary is what reaches your bank account each month after deductions such as tax and your PF share. Always ask for the break-up before comparing offers.
