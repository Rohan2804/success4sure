What is SCORES (SEBI Complaints Redress System)?

Last updated 20 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · Free

SCORES (SEBI Complaints Redress System): SCORES is SEBI's online complaints redress system where investors lodge and track complaints against listed companies and SEBI-registered intermediaries.

SEBI launched SCORES in 2011 to bring complaints online and make their handling transparent. An upgraded version, SCORES 2.0, was introduced in 2024 with auto-routing of complaints, shorter timelines and two levels of review. Investors can file complaints through the website or mobile app after registering with their PAN and contact details.

Under the framework, the entity concerned must resolve the complaint within 21 calendar days of receipt (as of 2026 — check the current SEBI circular). If the investor is not satisfied, a first-level review is done by a designated body, such as the relevant stock exchange or depository. If still unresolved, the investor can request a second review by SEBI.

Typical complaints include non-receipt of dividend or shares, problems with transfer or transmission, unauthorised trades by a broker, delays in refund or allotment, and service deficiencies by mutual funds or DPs. SCORES does not handle complaints that are sub-judice, already under arbitration, or that concern entities not regulated by SEBI, such as unregistered tipsters or chit funds.

A misconception is that SEBI will award compensation through SCORES. SCORES pushes the entity to resolve the grievance; it is not a court. If a dispute is not resolved, the investor may move to online dispute resolution (ODR), which can result in a binding arbitral award.

Example

Neha's dividend of ₹3,600 from a listed company was not credited because of an old bank account. She first writes to the RTA; after 10 days with no reply, she files on SCORES. The company must respond within 21 days; it updates her bank details and pays the dividend on day 14. Had the response been unsatisfactory, she could seek a first review by the exchange and then a SEBI review.

FAQs

Do I have to contact the company before filing on SCORES?

Investors are expected to first approach the listed company or intermediary directly. If the grievance is not resolved or there is no response, they can file on SCORES. Complaints should generally be filed within the time limit SEBI specifies from the date of the cause of action.

Can SCORES award me compensation?

No. SCORES is a complaint tracking and escalation system. It ensures the entity responds and resolves the complaint, and lets SEBI monitor and act against non-compliance. For a binding monetary decision on a dispute, investors can use the online dispute resolution mechanism or approach courts.
