What is Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY)?

Last updated 20 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · Free

Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY): Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY) is a government-backed small savings scheme for a girl child, offering a fixed interest rate set quarterly and tax-free returns.

Launched in 2015 under the Beti Bachao Beti Padhao campaign, SSY lets a parent or legal guardian open an account for a girl child below 10 years of age at a post office or authorised bank. Generally up to two accounts per family are allowed, with exceptions for twins or triplets.

The minimum deposit is ₹250 a year and the maximum ₹1.5 lakh per financial year. Deposits are required for 15 years from opening; the account matures 21 years from opening, and the balance continues to earn interest in the remaining years. The government sets the interest rate quarterly; it has been among the highest of small savings schemes, around 8.2% recently, but check the current rate.

Up to 50% of the balance can be withdrawn after the girl turns 18 or passes class 10, for higher education. The account can be closed on her marriage after 18. Like PPF, SSY has EEE tax status: deposits qualify for 80C under the old regime, and interest and maturity are tax-free.

SSY is safe and tax-efficient, but the money is locked for a long time and returns are fixed-income returns. For a goal 18 years away, many families combine it with equity investments.

Example

Ramesh opens an SSY account for his 1-year-old daughter and deposits ₹1,50,000 each year for 15 years, a total of ₹22.5 lakh. At an assumed constant 8.2% compounded annually, the balance after 15 years would be about ₹44.8 lakh, and with six more years of interest without deposits, about ₹71.8 lakh at maturity when she is 22. Actual rates change quarterly, so this is an illustration.

FAQs

Who can open a Sukanya Samriddhi account?

A parent or legal guardian can open it for a girl child below 10 years of age who is resident in India. Generally a family can open accounts for up to two daughters, with an exception for twins or triplets. Check current rules at the post office or bank.

When can money be withdrawn from SSY?

Up to 50% of the balance at the end of the previous financial year can be withdrawn after the girl turns 18 or passes class 10, for education. Full closure is allowed at maturity, 21 years from opening, or on her marriage after age 18, as per scheme rules.
