What is Clearing Corporation?

Last updated 23 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · Free

Clearing Corporation: A clearing corporation clears and settles trades executed on a stock exchange, acting as the legal counterparty to every buyer and seller so that settlement is guaranteed.

Each Indian exchange has a linked clearing corporation: NSE Clearing Ltd for NSE, Indian Clearing Corporation Ltd (ICCL) for BSE, and separate clearing corporations for commodity exchanges such as MCX. They are recognised and regulated by SEBI and are distinct companies from the exchanges themselves.

The key concept is novation. Once a trade is matched, the clearing corporation steps in between the parties: it becomes the buyer to every seller and the seller to every buyer. Your trade is therefore not exposed to the default of the unknown person on the other side, only to the clearing corporation, which manages risk through margins and a Settlement Guarantee Fund (Core SGF).

Clearing involves netting all trades of each clearing member to work out how much money and how many shares they must deliver or receive. Settlement is the actual exchange of funds and securities through clearing banks and depositories. The clearing corporation also collects margins (SPAN, exposure, VaR, mark-to-market), monitors positions, and conducts auctions when sellers fail to deliver.

Because of interoperability between clearing corporations, a broker can clear trades executed on NSE and BSE through a single clearing corporation of its choice. Students sometimes confuse clearing members with trading members: a trading member executes trades; a clearing member settles them, either its own or those of other trading members.

Example

A broker's clients buy 10,000 shares of a stock and sell 7,000 of the same stock on the same day. Instead of 17,000 separate deliveries, the clearing corporation nets them: the broker must receive 3,000 shares and pay the net amount. If a broker on the other side defaults on funds, the clearing corporation still completes the payout to the honest broker using the defaulter's margins and, if needed, the Core SGF.

FAQs

What is novation in a clearing corporation?

Novation is the legal replacement of the original contract between buyer and seller with two new contracts: one between the buyer and the clearing corporation, and one between the seller and the clearing corporation. It gives each party a settlement guarantee and removes direct counterparty risk.

What is the Core Settlement Guarantee Fund?

It is a fund maintained by each clearing corporation to guarantee settlement of trades if a clearing member defaults and its own margins are not enough. It is built from contributions by the clearing corporation, the exchange and clearing members, with sizing based on stress tests prescribed by SEBI.
