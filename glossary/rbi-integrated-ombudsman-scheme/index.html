What is RBI Integrated Ombudsman Scheme?

Last updated 19 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · Free

RBI Integrated Ombudsman Scheme: The RBI Integrated Ombudsman Scheme is a free grievance redressal system under which customers can complain against banks, NBFCs and payment system participants for deficiency in service.

Launched on 12 November 2021, the scheme merged three earlier schemes, the Banking Ombudsman Scheme 2006, the NBFC Ombudsman Scheme 2018 and the Digital Transactions Ombudsman Scheme 2019, under a 'One Nation, One Ombudsman' approach. Complaints can be filed online on RBI's Complaint Management System portal, by email or by post to the Centralised Receipt and Processing Centre at RBI Chandigarh, and help is available on toll-free number 14448.

It covers commercial banks, regional rural banks, scheduled primary urban co-operative banks and larger non-scheduled ones, eligible NBFCs, prepaid payment instrument issuers, payment system participants and credit information companies. Any 'deficiency in service', such as failure to credit a transfer, unauthorised debits, unfair charges or delays, is a valid ground, rather than a fixed list as in older schemes.

Conditions: the customer must first complain to the regulated entity. If it rejects the complaint, gives an unsatisfactory reply or does not reply within 30 days, the customer may approach the Ombudsman within one year of the reply (or one year and 30 days from the complaint if no reply). Complaints already pending in a court or other forum are not entertained. There is no fee.

The Ombudsman first tries conciliation. If that fails, it can pass an award of up to ₹20 lakh for consequential loss plus up to ₹1 lakh for loss of time, expenses, harassment and mental anguish. Either party may appeal to the Appellate Authority, an Executive Director of RBI, within 30 days. Figures are as of 2026; check RBI.

Example

On 3 March, ₹18,000 is debited from Meera's account for a failed ATM withdrawal. She complains to her bank the same day. RBI's turnaround rules require auto-reversal within five working days, with ₹100 per day compensation for delay. The bank does not respond by 2 April (30 days), so she files on the RBI CMS portal. The Ombudsman directs the bank to refund ₹18,000 and pay the delay compensation, without Meera paying any fee.

FAQs

Can I go to the RBI Ombudsman directly without complaining to my bank?

No. You must first complain in writing to the bank or other regulated entity. You can approach the Ombudsman only if the entity rejects the complaint, you are not satisfied with the reply, or it does not reply within 30 days. Keep the complaint reference number as proof.

Is there any fee to file a complaint with the RBI Ombudsman?

No. The Integrated Ombudsman Scheme is completely free for customers. You can file online through the CMS portal, by email or by post without a lawyer. The regulated entity also cannot charge you for complaints. The Ombudsman's award is binding on the entity if you accept it.
