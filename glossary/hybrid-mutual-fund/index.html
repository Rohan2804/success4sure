What is Hybrid mutual fund?

Last updated 26 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · Free

Hybrid mutual fund: A hybrid mutual fund invests in a mix of asset classes, typically equity and debt and sometimes gold, in proportions defined by its SEBI category.

Hybrid funds give investors a ready-made asset allocation inside one scheme. SEBI recognises several types. Aggressive hybrid funds keep 65–80% in equity and the rest in debt. Conservative hybrid funds invest 10–25% in equity and mostly in debt. Balanced hybrid funds keep 40–60% each in equity and debt with no arbitrage allowed. Equity savings funds combine equity, arbitrage positions and debt. Multi asset allocation funds invest in at least three asset classes with at least 10% in each. Dynamic asset allocation or balanced advantage funds can move between equity and debt freely. Arbitrage funds are also classified as hybrid.

The fund manager rebalances internally, which is convenient for investors who might otherwise forget to rebalance. Rebalancing inside the fund also does not trigger capital gains tax for the investor.

Taxation depends on the equity share. Schemes that keep at least 65% in domestic equity, including hedged arbitrage positions, are treated as equity-oriented. Others fall under different rules that have changed in recent years, so check current provisions.

A common mistake is assuming 'hybrid' means low risk. An aggressive hybrid fund with 75% equity can fall almost as much as an equity fund in a crash. Look at the category and actual allocation.

Example

Suresh invests ₹10 lakh in an aggressive hybrid fund holding 70% equity and 30% debt. If equities fall 20% and debt gains 2% in a bad year, the fund would fall roughly (0.70 × −20%) + (0.30 × 2%) = −13.4%, or about ₹1.34 lakh. A conservative hybrid fund with 20% equity would fall roughly (0.20 × −20%) + (0.80 × 2%) = −2.4% in the same year.

FAQs

Which hybrid fund is suitable for a first-time investor?

It depends on the investor's time horizon and comfort with losses, which is why suitability assessment matters. Conservative hybrids suit those wanting mild equity exposure; aggressive hybrids suit longer horizons. A SEBI-registered investment adviser or distributor should consider your risk profile before recommending a category.

Are hybrid funds taxed like equity funds?

Only if the scheme keeps at least 65% of assets in domestic equity, which includes hedged equity in some categories. Aggressive hybrid, equity savings and many balanced advantage funds typically qualify; conservative hybrid funds do not. Check the scheme's stated tax treatment and current law.
