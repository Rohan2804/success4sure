What is European vs American Option?

Last updated 23 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · Free

European vs American Option: A European option can be exercised only on its expiry date, while an American option can be exercised on any day up to and including expiry.

The names have nothing to do with geography; both types trade worldwide. The difference is purely about when the holder can exercise. Because an American option gives the holder an extra right — early exercise — its premium is at least equal to, and sometimes more than, an otherwise identical European option.

In India, all exchange-traded equity options, both index and stock, are European. Stock options were American style when introduced in 2001 but were converted to European style around 2011. Currency options on Indian exchanges are also European. Commodity options on MCX have their own exercise rules set by the exchange.

European exercise does not mean you are locked in. You can always sell the option in the market before expiry. In fact, for American calls on non-dividend-paying stocks, early exercise is rarely optimal because selling captures the remaining time value, while exercising throws it away.

Pricing models differ too. The Black-Scholes model was built for European options. American options need methods such as binomial trees that allow for early exercise. Exam questions often ask which style Indian options follow, and whether a European option can be traded before expiry (yes).

Example

A trader holds a European call with strike ₹500 on a share now at ₹560, with 10 days to expiry; the option trades at ₹64. Exercise is not allowed before expiry, but selling it in the market gives ₹64 per share. Even if the call were American, exercising now would give only ₹60 of intrinsic value, losing ₹4 of time value — so selling is better in either case.

FAQs

Are Indian stock options American or European?

European. Indian stock options and index options on NSE and BSE can be exercised only on the expiry day. In-the-money stock options are physically settled at expiry, and index options are cash settled. Positions can be closed at any time before expiry by trading on the exchange.

When does early exercise of an American option make sense?

Early exercise of a call may make sense just before a large dividend, when the dividend exceeds the remaining time value. Deep in-the-money American puts may also be exercised early when interest rates are high. Otherwise, selling the option usually captures more value than exercising it.
