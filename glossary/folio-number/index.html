What is Folio number?

Last updated 20 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · Free

Folio number: A folio number is the unique account number an AMC assigns to an investor, under which all their holdings and transactions with that fund house are recorded.

Think of a folio as your account with one mutual fund house, much like an account number at a bank. When you invest with an AMC for the first time, its registrar creates a folio linked to your name, PAN, bank account, address, holding mode and nominee. Further purchases in the same or other schemes of that AMC can be added to the same folio.

A folio can contain several schemes, plans and options. Each has its own unit balance, but personal details such as bank mandate and nomination apply across the folio. Changes like a new bank account or address are updated once per folio.

Investors often end up with many folios for the same AMC because they invested through different apps, distributors or with different holding patterns. This makes tracking harder and multiplies paperwork for updates and, later, transmission to nominees. Registrars allow consolidation of folios that have identical holders, PAN and mode of holding.

A folio is not the same as a demat account. Units held in a folio (statement of account form) sit with the registrar; units can also be held in demat form, where the depository records them. The consolidated account statement shows both.

Example

Sunita has three folios with the same AMC: one from 2015 via her bank, one from a 2019 app and one opened by a distributor in 2022, all in her sole name with the same PAN. Each has a different bank account and only one has a nominee. She submits a consolidation request through the registrar's portal, ending with one folio holding her four schemes, one updated bank account and nominees for everything.

FAQs

Can I have more than one folio with the same AMC?

Yes. A new folio may be created when you invest through a different channel or with a different holding pattern, such as joint holding. But multiple folios with identical details can usually be consolidated through the registrar to simplify tracking and updates.

How do I find my mutual fund folio number?

It appears on the account statement or allotment email sent by the AMC or registrar, and on your consolidated account statement. You can also find it by logging into the AMC's or registrar's website with your PAN and registered email or mobile number.
