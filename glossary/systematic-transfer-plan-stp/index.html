What is Systematic Transfer Plan (STP)?

Last updated 23 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · Free

Systematic Transfer Plan (STP): A Systematic Transfer Plan (STP) automatically moves a fixed amount at regular intervals from one scheme to another scheme of the same mutual fund house.

An STP is used when an investor has a lump sum but does not want to put it into equity at one go. The money is first parked in a low-volatility scheme, usually a liquid, overnight or ultra short duration fund. On each STP date, units of the source scheme are redeemed and the proceeds are invested in the target scheme, so the investor gets SIP-like averaging without needing monthly bank debits.

Both schemes must belong to the same AMC. Each transfer is legally a redemption from the source and a purchase in the target, so capital gains on the source units become taxable and any exit load on the source applies. Liquid funds have only a tiny graded exit load for the first week, which is why they are popular sources.

A reverse use is also common: as a goal approaches, investors run an STP from an equity fund into a debt fund to lock in gains gradually. Variants include fixed STP, capital appreciation STP (only the gains are moved) and flexi STP, where the amount depends on market conditions as per the AMC's formula.

STP is not a guarantee against loss. If the equity market rises steadily during the transfer period, an immediate lump sum would have done better.

Example

Neha receives a ₹6 lakh bonus. She invests it in a liquid fund and sets up an STP of ₹50,000 a month into a large cap fund for 12 months. Each month about ₹50,000 of liquid fund units are redeemed and large cap units are bought at that day's NAV. The money waiting in the liquid fund keeps earning short-term debt returns, and her equity entry is spread across a year of market ups and downs.

FAQs

Can I do an STP between two different AMCs?

No. An STP works only between schemes of the same mutual fund house because the AMC processes the redemption and purchase internally. To move money between fund houses you must redeem from one and invest in the other yourself, or set up a SWP into your bank and a separate SIP.

Is STP taxable?

Yes. Every transfer is a redemption from the source scheme, so any gain on the source units is a capital gain for that financial year. Gains on liquid funds over a few months are usually small, but they are still taxable at the applicable rate. Keep records of each transfer for filing returns.
