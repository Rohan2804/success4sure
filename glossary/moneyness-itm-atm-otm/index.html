What is Moneyness (ITM, ATM, OTM)?

Last updated 26 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · Free

Moneyness (ITM, ATM, OTM): Moneyness describes an option's strike price relative to the current underlying price: in the money (ITM), at the money (ATM) or out of the money (OTM).

For a call, the option is ITM when the spot price is above the strike, ATM when spot is at (or nearest to) the strike, and OTM when spot is below the strike. For a put, it is the reverse: ITM when spot is below the strike, OTM when spot is above. Only ITM options have intrinsic value.

Moneyness changes the option's behaviour. Deep ITM options behave almost like the underlying — their delta is close to 1 (or −1 for puts) and their premium is mostly intrinsic value. ATM options have the highest time value and the most sensitivity to time decay and volatility. Deep OTM options are cheap, with low delta and a low chance of paying off.

On expiry day, moneyness decides settlement. ITM index options are cash settled for their intrinsic value. ITM stock options lead to physical delivery of shares. Exchanges also have rules about close-to-money stock options, so traders should check exchange rules on expiry-day risk.

A frequent exam trap is applying call logic to puts. A put with strike ₹500 when the spot is ₹520 is OTM, not ITM. Another is assuming the ATM strike must match spot exactly — in practice, the ATM strike is the one closest to the spot price.

Example

Spot ₹1,020, strikes in steps of ₹20. Calls: ₹980 and ₹1,000 are ITM (intrinsic ₹40 and ₹20), ₹1,020 is ATM, ₹1,040 and ₹1,060 are OTM. Puts: ₹1,040 and ₹1,060 are ITM (intrinsic ₹20 and ₹40), ₹1,020 is ATM, ₹980 and ₹1,000 are OTM. If spot moves to ₹1,050, the ₹1,040 call becomes ITM and the ₹1,040 put becomes OTM.

FAQs

Which is better to buy: ITM, ATM or OTM options?

There is no universally better choice; it is a trade-off. ITM options cost more but move more like the underlying and lose less to time decay proportionally. OTM options are cheaper but need a bigger move to profit. ATM options are most sensitive to volatility and time. The choice depends on the view and risk appetite.

Is an ATM option in the money at expiry?

Only if the spot moves past the strike in the favourable direction. Exactly at the strike, intrinsic value is zero for both the call and the put. In practice, the settlement price usually differs slightly from the strike, making one side marginally ITM.
