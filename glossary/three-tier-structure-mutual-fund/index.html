What is Three-tier structure of a mutual fund?

Last updated 17 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · Free

Three-tier structure of a mutual fund: The three-tier structure means an Indian mutual fund has a sponsor who sets it up, trustees who hold assets for investors, and an AMC that manages the money.

SEBI requires every mutual fund in India to be constituted as a trust under the Indian Trusts Act. This produces three layers. The sponsor is the promoter, much like a company's promoter, and must meet SEBI's eligibility norms on track record and net worth. The sponsor creates the trust and contributes to the AMC's capital.

The trustees, either a board of trustees or a trustee company, are the guardians of unit holders' interests. They appoint the AMC, approve schemes, monitor whether the AMC follows regulations and the scheme documents, and report to SEBI. A majority of trustees must be independent of the sponsor.

The AMC is the investment manager that actually runs the portfolios. Around these three sit service providers appointed with trustee approval: a custodian that holds securities, a registrar and transfer agent that maintains investor records, and auditors.

Why the separation? It prevents the people managing money from also controlling the safekeeping and supervision of that money. Exam questions often ask who appoints the AMC (the trustees, with SEBI approval), who holds securities (the custodian) and who is the settler of the trust (the sponsor). Remember that the investor's relationship is with the trust; units represent beneficial ownership in the scheme's assets.

Example

Take a fictional group, Bharat Finserv Ltd. It acts as sponsor and sets up 'Bharat Mutual Fund' as a trust. It forms 'Bharat Trustee Company Pvt Ltd', where four of six directors are independent. The trustee company appoints 'Bharat Asset Management Ltd' as AMC. Securities bought by Bharat Bluechip Fund are held by a separate custodian bank, and investor folios are maintained by an RTA. If the AMC breaches an investment limit, the trustees must act and report it.

FAQs

Who appoints the AMC of a mutual fund?

The trustees appoint the AMC through an investment management agreement, and SEBI must approve the AMC. The sponsor sets up the trust and usually promotes the AMC, but the formal appointment and ongoing supervision are trustee responsibilities. This is a frequently tested point in NISM mutual fund exams.

Is the custodian part of the three tiers?

No. The three tiers are sponsor, trustee and AMC. The custodian is a service provider that holds the scheme's securities and settles trades. It is appointed with trustee approval and should be independent of the sponsor to avoid conflicts, subject to conditions laid down by SEBI.
