What is Monetary Policy Committee (MPC)?

Last updated 23 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · Free

Monetary Policy Committee (MPC): The Monetary Policy Committee is a six-member statutory body under the RBI Act that fixes the policy repo rate needed to achieve India's inflation target.

India moved to a flexible inflation targeting framework through the 2016 amendment to the RBI Act. Section 45ZB set up the MPC, which first met in October 2016. Earlier, the Governor alone decided the policy rate after consulting a technical advisory committee.

The MPC has six members: the RBI Governor (chairperson), a Deputy Governor in charge of monetary policy, one RBI officer nominated by the central board, and three external members appointed by the Central Government for four years, who cannot be reappointed. The quorum is four, including the Governor or, in his absence, the Deputy Governor. Each member has one vote, decisions are by majority, and the Governor has a casting vote in a tie. It must meet at least four times a year; in practice it meets six times.

The Central Government, in consultation with RBI, sets the target once every five years: 4% CPI inflation with a tolerance band of 2% to 6%. If average inflation stays outside the band for three consecutive quarters, it counts as a failure, and RBI must send a report to the government explaining the reasons, remedial actions and expected time to return to target.

After each meeting the MPC publishes a resolution, and minutes appear on the 14th day. The MPC decides only the policy rate and stance (such as neutral or accommodative); CRR, SLR and other operational tools are set by RBI.

Example

Suppose at an MPC meeting three members vote to cut repo by 25 basis points and three vote to hold. The tie is broken by the Governor's casting vote; if the Governor was among those voting to cut, the repo rate falls, say from 5.50% to 5.25%. SDF moves to 5.00% and MSF and bank rate to 5.50% automatically. If CPI inflation averaged 6.4%, 6.7% and 6.2% in three straight quarters, RBI would have to report its failure to the government.

FAQs

Does the Finance Ministry have a vote in the MPC?

No. The government appoints the three external members and sets the inflation target, but no government official sits on or votes in the MPC. Earlier proposals for a government representative were dropped to protect the committee's independence. The government can, however, convey its views in writing.

Which inflation measure does the MPC target?

Headline CPI (combined) inflation published by the National Statistics Office, covering rural and urban India. It does not target WPI or core inflation, although members study core inflation, food prices and expectations surveys when deciding the rate.
