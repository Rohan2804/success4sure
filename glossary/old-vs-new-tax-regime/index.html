What is Old vs new tax regime?

Last updated 23 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · Free

Old vs new tax regime: India's individual taxpayers can choose between the old tax regime, with higher rates but many deductions, and the new regime, with lower rates but few deductions; the new regime is the default.

The old regime allows deductions such as 80C (₹1.5 lakh), 80D for health insurance, 80CCD(1B) for NPS, house rent allowance, leave travel allowance and home loan interest on a self-occupied house. Its slab rates rise to 30% above ₹10 lakh of taxable income.

The new regime, made the default from FY 2023-24, has more slabs with lower rates, a higher standard deduction (₹75,000 as of 2026) and a larger rebate, but disallows most deductions. Employer contribution to NPS under 80CCD(2) is still allowed. For FY 2025-26, the slabs run from nil up to ₹4 lakh through 5%, 10%, 15%, 20% and 25% to 30% above ₹24 lakh.

Salaried taxpayers can choose each year. Those with business income can switch back to the old regime only in limited circumstances. The right choice depends on how large your deductions are. As a rough pattern, people with modest deductions now usually pay less under the new regime. The Income-tax Act, 2025 replaces the 1961 Act from 1 April 2026 and renumbers sections, so the familiar section name may now map to a new reference; check the income tax department's current guidance.

Do not choose a regime and then buy investments to justify it. Compute tax both ways using deductions you would claim anyway.

Example

Vikram earns ₹15 lakh salary in FY 2025-26. Old regime: deductions of ₹50,000 standard, ₹1.5 lakh 80C, ₹25,000 80D and ₹50,000 NPS leave taxable income of ₹12.25 lakh; tax is ₹12,500 + ₹1,00,000 + 30% of ₹2.25 lakh = ₹1,80,000, or ₹1,87,200 with cess. New regime: taxable income ₹14.25 lakh after ₹75,000 standard deduction; tax is ₹20,000 + ₹40,000 + 15% of ₹2.25 lakh = ₹93,750, or ₹97,500 with cess. The new regime saves about ₹89,700 here. FAQs

Which tax regime is better for salaried employees?

It depends on your deductions. With large HRA, home loan interest and full use of 80C, 80D and NPS, the old regime can still win at some income levels. With few deductions, the new regime is usually better. Calculate tax both ways each year.

Can I switch between old and new tax regimes every year?

Salaried individuals without business income can choose each year while filing the return. Those with business or professional income have limited switching options. Inform your employer of your choice for TDS, though the final choice is made in the return. Check current rules.
