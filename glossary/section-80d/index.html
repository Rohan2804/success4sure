What is Section 80D deduction?

Last updated 26 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · Free

Section 80D deduction: Section 80D allows a deduction from taxable income for health insurance premiums and certain medical expenses paid for oneself, family and parents, under the old tax regime.

As of 2026, the deduction for premiums covering self, spouse and dependent children is up to ₹25,000 a year, or ₹50,000 if the policyholder or spouse is a senior citizen. A separate deduction of up to ₹25,000 (₹50,000 for senior citizen parents) is available for premiums paid for parents, whether or not they are dependent. Preventive health check-up expenses up to ₹5,000 are allowed within these limits.

For senior citizens without health insurance, actual medical expenditure up to the senior-citizen limit can be claimed. Premiums must be paid by any mode other than cash, except for preventive check-ups.

The deduction is available only under the old tax regime. Under the new regime there is no 80D deduction, but health insurance remains essential protection, independent of tax. The Income-tax Act, 2025 replaces the 1961 Act from 1 April 2026 and renumbers sections, so the familiar section name may now map to a new reference; check the income tax department's current guidance.

Common confusion: the limits apply per group (self-family and parents), not per policy, and a multi-year premium paid upfront is spread across the years of cover for the deduction.

Example

Rohit, 38, pays ₹20,000 for a family floater covering himself, his wife and child, and ₹35,000 for his parents, both over 60. Under the old regime he can claim ₹20,000 for his family (limit ₹25,000) and ₹35,000 for his parents (limit ₹50,000), a total of ₹55,000. In the 20% slab, this saves about ₹55,000 × 20.8% = ₹11,440 in tax.

FAQs

Can I claim 80D for my parents' health insurance?

Yes, under the old regime. Premiums paid for parents qualify for a separate deduction of up to ₹25,000, or ₹50,000 if either parent is a senior citizen, as of 2026. The parents need not be financially dependent on you. Pay by a non-cash mode.

Is 80D available in the new tax regime?

No. As of 2026 the new regime does not allow 80D deductions. Health insurance remains important for protection regardless of tax savings. Compare both regimes using your actual deductions to see which gives lower tax.
