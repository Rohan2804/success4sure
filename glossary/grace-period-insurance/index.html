What is Grace Period (Insurance)?

Last updated 17 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · Free

Grace Period (Insurance): The grace period is a fixed number of days after the premium due date within which the policyholder can pay the premium without the policy lapsing and without losing benefits.

In life insurance, the standard grace period is 30 days for yearly, half-yearly and quarterly modes, and 15 days for monthly mode, as set out in IRDAI product regulations. During the grace period the policy remains fully in force. If the life assured dies within the grace period before paying, the insurer pays the claim after deducting the unpaid premium.

If the premium is not paid by the end of the grace period, a traditional policy lapses (or becomes paid-up if it has acquired a paid-up value), and a ULIP moves to the discontinued policy fund if it is within the lock-in. Most insurers do not charge interest for payment within the grace period, though they may add late fees after it.

In health insurance, the grace period for renewal is typically 30 days (some insurers allow more for monthly or instalment payment), and continuity benefits such as waiting periods already served are preserved if premium is paid within it. However, health insurers usually do not cover illnesses or hospitalisation that occur during the grace period before renewal premium is paid, so the policy is continuous but cover for that gap may be excluded. Read the policy wording. Check IRDAI rules for current durations (as of 2026).

Exam traps: grace period does not apply to single premium policies; and it is not the same as the free-look period or the revival period.

Example

Ravi's quarterly premium of ₹6,000 on a life policy with sum assured ₹10 lakh is due on 1 March. The grace period runs 30 days, till 31 March. He dies in an accident on 20 March without having paid. The insurer pays the death claim of ₹10 lakh minus the unpaid ₹6,000, so the nominee receives ₹9,94,000. Had he died on 5 April with premium still unpaid, the policy would have lapsed and the claim could be rejected.

FAQs

Is my life insured during the grace period?

Yes. During the grace period the life policy is in force with full cover. If a claim arises, the insurer settles it after deducting the premium that was due. Once the grace period expires without payment, the policy lapses or becomes paid-up, depending on how many premiums have been paid.

What is the grace period for a monthly premium policy?

For life insurance policies with monthly premium payment, the grace period is 15 days. For yearly, half-yearly and quarterly modes it is 30 days. These durations come from IRDAI product regulations; individual policies may offer more, so check the policy document.
