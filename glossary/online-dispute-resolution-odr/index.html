What is Online Dispute Resolution (ODR)?

Last updated 23 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · Free

Online Dispute Resolution (ODR): Online dispute resolution is SEBI's mechanism for resolving disputes between investors and listed companies or market intermediaries through online conciliation and arbitration.

SEBI introduced a common ODR framework in 2023, creating the Smart ODR portal linked to SCORES. It covers disputes with stock brokers, depository participants, mutual funds, investment advisers, research analysts, listed companies and many other regulated entities. An investor can initiate ODR after first approaching the entity, if the grievance is not resolved satisfactorily — with or without having gone through SCORES.

The process has two stages. First, an independent conciliator appointed by an empanelled ODR institution tries to help the parties reach a settlement through online meetings. If conciliation fails, the investor can move to online arbitration, where an arbitrator hears both sides and passes a binding award under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.

SEBI prescribes timelines: conciliation is to be completed within about 21 days (extendable on request) and arbitration within about 30 days of appointment, as of 2026 — check current circulars. Fees for investors are kept low and depend on the claim size, while the intermediary bears a share of costs. Intermediaries must also be able to initiate ODR against clients.

ODR does not apply to disputes already in court or claims time-barred under law, and it is not available against unregistered entities. Investors sometimes confuse ODR with SCORES; SCORES is complaint handling, ODR is formal dispute resolution leading to a binding outcome.

Example

Rohit claims his broker squared off his positions without a margin call, causing a ₹1.8 lakh loss. The broker rejects his complaint. Rohit files on the Smart ODR portal. In conciliation, the broker offers ₹60,000, which he declines. He opts for arbitration; after reviewing the margin records and call logs, the arbitrator awards him ₹1.2 lakh, which the broker must pay within the timeline set by the award.

FAQs

Is the ODR outcome binding?

A settlement reached in conciliation is binding once both parties sign it. An arbitral award passed by the arbitrator is binding and enforceable like a court decree under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, though it can be challenged on limited grounds before a court.

Can I use ODR without going through SCORES?

Yes. The investor should first take up the grievance directly with the entity. If unresolved, the investor may go to SCORES or directly to the ODR portal. Using SCORES is not mandatory before ODR, though many investors try it first because it is free.
