What is Small cap fund?

Last updated 23 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · Free

Small cap fund: A small cap fund is an equity mutual fund that invests at least 65% of its assets in small cap companies, those ranked 251st and below by market capitalisation.

Small cap companies are younger or niche businesses with room to grow several times over, which is why small cap funds have delivered some of the highest long-term returns in good periods. The same features make them risky: earnings are less stable, management depth is thinner and research coverage is limited.

Liquidity is the hidden risk. Many small cap stocks trade in small volumes. If many investors redeem at once, the fund may have to sell holdings at depressed prices. Since 2024, AMFI and SEBI have required AMCs to disclose stress test results showing how many days it would take to liquidate a set portion of the portfolio. Some AMCs have also restricted lump sum inflows into small cap funds when valuations ran high.

Drawdowns can be severe. Small cap indices in India have fallen more than 50% from peak in past bear markets and taken years to recover. A long holding period, often suggested as seven years or more, and a SIP approach help investors ride these swings.

Investors often chase small cap funds after a year of very high returns. Recent performance is a poor guide; small caps move in long cycles.

Example

Two investors each put ₹2 lakh into a small cap fund at a market peak. A year later the fund is down 35%, worth ₹1.3 lakh. Investor A panics and redeems, locking in a ₹70,000 loss. Investor B continues a ₹10,000 monthly SIP through the fall, buying units cheaply. If the fund recovers to its old peak NAV in three years, B's earlier lump sum is back to ₹2 lakh and every SIP unit bought below that level shows a gain.

FAQs

What is the minimum holding period for a small cap fund?

There is no legal minimum apart from any exit load period. But because small caps are volatile and move in long cycles, many advisers suggest small cap funds only for goals seven or more years away, and only for a part of the equity allocation.

What is a stress test in small cap funds?

It is a disclosure that estimates how many days a fund would need to sell a stated share of its portfolio, such as 25% or 50%, under normal market volumes. More days indicates lower liquidity. AMCs publish these results periodically on their websites and AMFI's.
