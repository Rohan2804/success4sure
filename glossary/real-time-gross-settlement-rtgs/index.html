What is Real Time Gross Settlement (RTGS)?

Last updated 23 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · Free

Real Time Gross Settlement (RTGS): RTGS is RBI's payment system for transferring funds between banks on a real-time, transaction-by-transaction basis, used mainly for high-value payments of ₹2 lakh and above.

'Real time' means the payment is processed as soon as it is received, not held for a batch. 'Gross settlement' means each transaction is settled individually, not netted against others. Once settled in RBI's books, the payment is final and irrevocable. India started RTGS in March 2004, and since 14 December 2020 it runs 24x7 throughout the year.

The minimum amount for RTGS is ₹2 lakh; there is no upper limit set by RBI. It is therefore the preferred route for property payments, large business transactions and inter-bank settlements. Banks must credit the beneficiary within 30 minutes of receiving the message; if they cannot, the funds must be returned within one hour of receipt. RBI has waived charges on online RTGS transactions by savings account holders, and branch charges are capped (check RBI for current rules as of 2026).

Because RTGS settles in central bank money in real time, it removes settlement risk, which is crucial for large payments. Banks maintain current accounts with RBI and need enough balance to settle each transaction; RBI provides intra-day liquidity against collateral to keep payments flowing.

Exam traps: RTGS has a minimum (₹2 lakh) but no maximum; NEFT has neither. RTGS settles individually; NEFT settles in half-hourly batches. Both are run by RBI, while IMPS and UPI are run by NPCI.

Example

Meena pays ₹18 lakh to a builder for a flat booking. NEFT would also work, but RTGS gives immediate settlement and confirmation. She initiates it at 8:40 pm; the builder's bank receives and credits it within minutes. A payment of ₹1.5 lakh could not go through RTGS because it is below the ₹2 lakh minimum; it would use NEFT or IMPS instead.

FAQs

What is the minimum and maximum amount for RTGS?

The minimum amount for an RTGS transaction is ₹2 lakh. RBI has not set any upper limit, though banks may set their own caps for online channels based on risk management. Amounts below ₹2 lakh should be sent through NEFT, IMPS or UPI.

Can an RTGS payment be reversed?

Once RTGS settles in RBI's books, it is final and irrevocable. The sender's bank cannot pull it back. If money went to a wrong account, the sender must request the beneficiary's bank to contact the account holder, and return depends on the recipient's consent or legal action.
