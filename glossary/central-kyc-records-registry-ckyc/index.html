What is CKYC (Central KYC Records Registry)?

Last updated 23 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · Free

CKYC (Central KYC Records Registry): CKYC is a central repository of customer KYC records, run by CERSAI, that lets customers complete KYC once and use it across banks, insurers, securities intermediaries and pension funds.

The Central KYC Records Registry was set up under the Prevention of Money-laundering (Maintenance of Records) Rules, and the government authorised the Central Registry of Securitisation Asset Reconstruction and Security Interest of India (CERSAI) to operate it. Entities regulated by the RBI, SEBI, IRDAI and PFRDA must upload KYC records of new customers to the CKYC registry.

Each customer gets a 14-digit KYC Identification Number (KIN), also called the CKYC number. When the customer approaches another regulated entity, that entity can download the record using the KIN or other identifiers instead of collecting all documents again, though it may still verify details and collect additional information required for its own risk assessment.

CKYC and KRA KYC co-exist in the securities market. KRAs handle KYC specific to SEBI intermediaries, while CKYC spans the entire financial sector. SEBI has required intermediaries to upload records to CKYC as well, and has worked on interoperability between the two systems.

People sometimes think CKYC replaces all verification. It reduces paperwork but does not stop an institution from carrying out enhanced due diligence for higher-risk customers. Another misconception is that the KIN is secret like a password; it is an identifier, though it should still be shared only with genuine financial institutions.

Example

Farhan opened a savings account in 2022, and the bank uploaded his KYC to CKYC, generating a 14-digit KIN. In 2026 he buys a term insurance policy online. The insurer searches CKYC using his PAN, downloads his record and only asks him to confirm his current address and upload a selfie. He completes the purchase without resubmitting identity documents.

FAQs

How can I find my CKYC number?

Your CKYC number (KIN) may be mentioned in an SMS or email from CERSAI or from the institution that uploaded your KYC. Banks and some financial platforms can also look it up using your PAN. Some third-party sites offer searches, but use only trusted official or institutional channels.

Is CKYC the same as KRA KYC?

No. KRA KYC is specific to SEBI-regulated intermediaries in the securities market, managed by KYC registration agencies. CKYC is a central registry across banking, insurance, securities and pensions, run by CERSAI. Many investors have both, and regulators are working on making them interoperable.
