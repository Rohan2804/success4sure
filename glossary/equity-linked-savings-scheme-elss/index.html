What is Equity Linked Savings Scheme (ELSS)?

Last updated 20 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · Free

Equity Linked Savings Scheme (ELSS): An Equity Linked Savings Scheme (ELSS) is a diversified equity mutual fund with a three-year lock-in that qualifies for the Section 80C deduction under the old tax regime.

ELSS is the only mutual fund category eligible for the familiar 80C deduction, alongside PPF, EPF, life insurance premiums and others within the overall annual limit (₹1.5 lakh as of 2026). SEBI requires an ELSS to invest at least 80% in equities. Each AMC may run only one ELSS scheme.

Every investment is locked in for three years from the date of allotment. With a SIP, each instalment has its own three-year clock, so the last instalment unlocks three years after it was made. After lock-in the units can be held as long as you wish.

The deduction is available only under the old tax regime. Under the new default regime there is no 80C deduction, so ELSS becomes simply an equity fund with a lock-in. On redemption, gains are taxed as equity long-term capital gains because the holding always exceeds one year. Note also that the Income-tax Act, 2025 replaces the 1961 Act from April 2026 and renumbers sections, so check the current reference for this deduction.

Compared with other 80C options, ELSS has the shortest lock-in but the highest volatility. It is unsuitable for anyone whose money is needed in exactly three years, since equity can be down at that moment.

Example

Pooja, under the old regime in the 30% slab, invests ₹1,50,000 in ELSS through a monthly SIP of ₹12,500. Her taxable income falls by ₹1.5 lakh, saving about ₹46,800 in tax including 4% cess (₹1,50,000 × 31.2%), assuming no other 80C investments. Her April instalment unlocks next on that date three years later, her March instalment a year after that.

FAQs

Can I withdraw ELSS before three years?

No. The three-year lock-in is mandatory and applies to each purchase separately. Only in the case of the investor's death can the nominee or legal heir redeem earlier, after one year from allotment, as per the scheme terms. Plan ELSS investments with money you will not need for at least three years.

Is ELSS useful under the new tax regime?

The new regime does not allow the 80C deduction, so there is no tax saving on investment. ELSS still works as a diversified equity fund, but the lock-in becomes a restriction without a matching benefit. Compare it with other flexi cap or index funds before choosing.
