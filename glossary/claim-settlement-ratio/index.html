What is Claim Settlement Ratio (CSR)?

Last updated 20 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · Free

Claim Settlement Ratio (CSR): Claim settlement ratio is the percentage of claims an insurer settles out of the total claims it receives in a year, used as an indicator of its claim-paying record.

Formula: CSR = Claims settled ÷ (Claims received during the year + Claims pending at the start of the year) × 100. Many reports simplify this to claims settled ÷ total claims for decision. IRDAI publishes insurer-wise data on death claims in its annual report, and insurers disclose it in public disclosures.

There are two versions. CSR by number counts policies, so a small claim and a large one each count as one. CSR by amount uses the value of claims settled. An insurer may have a high CSR by number but a lower one by amount if it rejects or delays a few very large claims, so looking at both gives a better picture. Repudiation ratio (claims rejected as a share of claims decided) and pending claims are also published.

Timelines matter too. IRDAI regulations set limits for settling claims after all documents are received, with tighter limits introduced by the 2024 master circular, such as settlement within 15 days for death claims not needing investigation and completion of investigation within 45 days. Delays beyond limits attract interest payable to the claimant. Check IRDAI rules (as of 2026).

Common misconception: a 99% CSR does not guarantee that your claim will be paid. Most rejections happen due to non-disclosure of material facts, lapsed policies or excluded causes. Accurate disclosure and keeping the policy in force matter more than small differences in CSR.

Example

In a year, an insurer had 1,200 death claims pending at the start and received 18,800 new claims, total 20,000. It paid 19,500, rejected 300 and 200 remain pending. CSR by number = 19,500/20,000 = 97.5%. By amount, if claims totalled ₹1,600 crore and ₹1,480 crore was paid, CSR by amount = 92.5%. The gap suggests some large claims were rejected or are pending.

FAQs

Where can I find an insurer's claim settlement ratio?

IRDAI publishes insurer-wise claim data, including settlement ratios by number and amount, in its annual report. Insurers also publish claim data in their public disclosures on their websites. Use the latest available year and compare insurers of similar size and product mix.

What is the difference between CSR by number and by amount?

CSR by number shows the share of claims settled counted as individual policies. CSR by amount shows the share of total claim value paid. A large gap between the two may indicate that bigger claims face more rejections or delays, which matters most to buyers of high cover.
