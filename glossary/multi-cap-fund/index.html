What is Multi cap fund?

Last updated 20 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · Free

Multi cap fund: A multi cap fund is an equity mutual fund that must invest at least 75% in equities, with a minimum of 25% each in large cap, mid cap and small cap stocks.

Before 2021, a multi cap fund only needed 65% in equity with no cap-wise limits, and most such funds were dominated by large caps. SEBI changed the rules from January 2021 to ensure the category truly spans the market. Funds that did not want mandatory small cap exposure moved to the new flexi cap category.

The fixed allocation makes multi cap funds more predictable. At least a quarter of the portfolio is always in small caps and a quarter in mid caps, so the fund typically carries higher volatility than a large cap or most flexi cap funds, along with higher potential return over long periods.

The fund manager still has discretion over the remaining 25% of equity and the up to 25% outside equity. Benchmarks such as the Nifty 500 Multicap 50:25:25 index reflect the structure.

Investors sometimes think a multi cap fund is automatically the most diversified choice. It is diversified across market caps, but its mandatory small cap allocation means it can fall sharply in small cap sell-offs, and it may face liquidity constraints when AUM is large.

Example

A multi cap fund with ₹4,000 crore AUM must hold at least ₹1,000 crore each in large, mid and small caps, and at least ₹3,000 crore in equity overall. Suppose it holds 40% large, 27% mid, 28% small and 5% cash. If small caps fall sharply and that slice drops to 23% of the portfolio, the manager must rebalance back to at least 25% within SEBI's permitted window.

FAQs

Why did SEBI change multi cap fund rules?

Many multi cap funds held mostly large cap stocks, so investors did not get the spread across market caps the name suggested. SEBI made the 25% minimums mandatory from 2021 so that the category matches its label, and created flexi cap for funds wanting full freedom.

Is a multi cap fund riskier than a flexi cap fund?

Often, though not always. A multi cap fund must keep at least 25% in small caps and 25% in mid caps, while a flexi cap fund may hold mostly large caps. So a typical multi cap fund carries more small and mid cap risk. Compare actual portfolios to be sure.
