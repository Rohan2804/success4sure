What is Delta (Option Greek)?

Last updated 23 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · Free

Delta (Option Greek): Delta measures how much an option's premium is expected to change for a ₹1 change in the price of the underlying asset.

Call deltas range from 0 to 1, and put deltas from −1 to 0. A call with delta 0.6 should gain about ₹0.60 when the underlying rises by ₹1; a put with delta −0.4 should gain about ₹0.40 when the underlying falls by ₹1. At-the-money options have deltas near ±0.5, deep in-the-money options near ±1, and far out-of-the-money options near 0.

Delta also gives the equivalent position in the underlying. One lot of calls with delta 0.6 behaves like 60% of a lot of the underlying for small moves. Option writers use delta to hedge: a short call with delta 0.6 on 500 shares can be offset by buying 300 shares, making the position delta-neutral for small moves.

Delta is not fixed. It changes as the underlying price moves (measured by gamma), as time passes and as volatility changes. Near expiry, ATM option deltas swing quickly between 0 and 1, which makes hedging difficult. Many traders also use delta as a rough probability that the option will expire in the money, though this is only an approximation.

A common slip is to forget the sign. Long calls and short puts have positive delta (they gain when price rises); long puts and short calls have negative delta. Adding deltas across positions gives the portfolio's net directional exposure.

Example

A stock trades at ₹800 and a ₹780 call has delta 0.62; the lot size is 500. If the stock rises to ₹810, the call premium should rise by about 0.62 × ₹10 = ₹6.20, or ₹3,100 per lot. A trader who sold 2 lots of this call has a delta of −0.62 × 1,000 = −620 shares; buying 620 shares would make the position approximately delta-neutral.

FAQs

What is the delta of an at-the-money option?

Roughly 0.5 for a call and −0.5 for a put. In practice, because of interest rates and the way the Black-Scholes formula works, ATM call deltas are often a little above 0.5. As expiry approaches, the ATM delta becomes more sensitive and can jump quickly towards 0 or 1.

What does delta-neutral mean?

A delta-neutral position has a total delta of about zero, so small moves in the underlying do not change its value much. Option market makers and volatility traders use it to remove directional risk and focus on volatility or time decay. The position must be rebalanced as delta changes.
