What is Consolidated Account Statement (CAS)?

Last updated 19 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · Free

Consolidated Account Statement (CAS): A consolidated account statement (CAS) is a single statement showing an investor's holdings and transactions across all mutual funds, and usually demat accounts, linked to their PAN.

Before CAS, an investor with five AMCs received five separate statements. SEBI now requires a consolidated statement across all mutual fund folios under the same PAN. It is generated by the registrars (CAMS and KFintech) for mutual fund-only investors, or by the depositories (NSDL or CDSL) for those who also have a demat account, so that mutual fund units and shares appear together.

A CAS is sent monthly for any month in which there was a transaction, and half-yearly otherwise, by email if registered. It shows each scheme and plan, units, NAV, current value, transaction details and nominee status. It also discloses the commission paid to distributors and the scheme's expense ratio, so investors in regular plans can see what they are paying for distribution.

Investors can also request a CAS on demand for any period from the registrars' or depositories' websites, which is handy for tax filing and reviewing old forgotten folios.

Common issue: folios with a different PAN, a minor's PAN or a missing PAN do not appear in the CAS. Check that every investment carries the correct PAN and KYC status, and that your email is updated so statements reach you.

Example

Divya invests through two apps and a bank. Her August CAS shows six schemes across four AMCs, current value ₹8,45,300, and a line stating that ₹2,140 was paid as commission in the half-year on her regular plan holdings. It also flags that one old folio has no nominee. She switches future SIPs to direct plans after reviewing costs and updates the nomination through the registrar's portal.

FAQs

Who sends the consolidated account statement?

If you have a demat account, the depository (NSDL or CDSL) where your demat is held sends the CAS covering mutual funds and shares. If you hold only mutual funds, the registrars CAMS or KFintech send it. Statements go to your registered email or postal address.

Why is a mutual fund missing from my CAS?

The most common reasons are a different or missing PAN on that folio, holding in a minor's name, or recent transactions not yet updated. Check the folio details with the AMC or registrar and update PAN and KYC so it gets linked.
