What is Physical Settlement of Stock Derivatives?

Last updated 19 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · Free

Physical Settlement of Stock Derivatives: Physical settlement means that at expiry, open stock futures and in-the-money stock options are settled by actual delivery of shares rather than by a cash difference.

SEBI moved stock derivatives in India from cash settlement to compulsory physical settlement in phases between 2018 and 2019. Index derivatives remain cash settled because an index cannot be delivered. Physical settlement aligns the derivatives market more closely with the cash market and reduces manipulation through settlement prices.

At expiry, a long stock futures position must take delivery and pay the full value of the shares; a short position must deliver the shares. For options, an in-the-money long call leads to buying shares at the strike, a short call leads to delivering shares, a long put leads to delivering shares and a short put leads to receiving shares and paying the strike.

Because delivery needs full money or shares, brokers and clearing corporations levy increasing delivery margins in the days before expiry for positions likely to be settled physically. Many brokers square off open positions themselves before expiry if clients lack funds or shares. The settlement follows the normal equity cash market cycle.

Traders are sometimes caught off guard by a cheap option that ends slightly in the money at expiry, creating a large delivery obligation. Others forget that a short put that expires ITM means they must pay for the full lot of shares. Checking expiry-day exposure is essential.

Example

Arun holds 1 lot (700 shares) of a ₹900 call on a stock that settles at ₹912 on expiry. The call is ITM, so he must buy 700 shares at ₹900 — paying ₹6,30,000 — and receives shares worth ₹6,38,400. If he has only ₹50,000 in his account, his broker will likely square off the call before expiry. The writer of that call must deliver 700 shares or face short-delivery consequences.

FAQs

Are index options physically settled?

No. Index futures and options are cash settled because an index is not a deliverable asset. Only stock futures and in-the-money stock options are physically settled at expiry. Currency and some commodity derivatives have their own settlement rules set by the exchanges.

How can I avoid physical delivery in stock F&O?

Close or roll over positions before expiry. Most brokers also have their own cut-off times after which they square off open positions that could lead to delivery. If you intend to take or give delivery, ensure you have the full funds or shares ready in advance.
