What is Cut-off time (mutual funds)?

Last updated 25 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · Free

Cut-off time (mutual funds): Cut-off time is the time of day by which a valid mutual fund transaction must be received for it to get that business day's NAV.

SEBI sets uniform cut-off times for all AMCs. For purchases in equity and most debt schemes, the cut-off is 3:00 pm. For liquid and overnight funds it is 1:30 pm. Redemptions follow similar timings: a valid redemption request received before 3:00 pm in an equity scheme gets that day's NAV; after the cut-off it gets the next business day's NAV.

For purchases, the time stamp of your application is not enough. Since February 2021, the applicable NAV for all purchases, regardless of amount, depends on when the money is actually realised in the scheme's bank account before the cut-off. A UPI or net banking payment made at 2:55 pm may still get the next day's NAV if the funds reach the AMC late. SIP debits follow the same rule.

Liquid and overnight funds have a special rule: if the application and funds arrive before 1:30 pm, the investor gets the NAV of the calendar day before the next business day, which means interest for the intervening days.

The common confusion is believing you can 'lock' a NAV by placing an order during a market fall. Because NAV is calculated after closing prices, nobody knows the NAV at 3 pm; you get forward pricing either way.

Example

On a Monday Arvind places a ₹1 lakh purchase in a flexi cap fund at 2:30 pm through net banking. His bank transfers the money, but it reaches the AMC's collection account at 3:20 pm. He gets Tuesday's NAV. Had the money arrived by 2:59 pm, he would have received Monday's NAV. His colleague who redeems at 2:45 pm on Monday gets Monday's NAV, because redemptions depend on the request time, not money movement.

FAQs

What is the cut-off time for liquid funds?

For liquid and overnight funds, the cut-off is 1:30 pm for both the application and receipt of funds. Meeting it gets you the NAV of the day immediately before the next business day. Missing it pushes allotment to the following day's applicable NAV.

Does the cut-off time apply to SIPs?

Yes. SIP units are allotted at the NAV of the day the instalment amount is realised in the scheme's account, subject to the cut-off. Since banks process NACH debits in batches, your SIP NAV date may be a day after the debit date shown in your bank statement.
