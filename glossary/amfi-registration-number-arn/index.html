What is AMFI Registration Number (ARN)?

Last updated 23 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · Free

AMFI Registration Number (ARN): An AMFI Registration Number (ARN) is the unique registration code AMFI issues to a mutual fund distributor, required before the distributor can earn commission on mutual fund sales.

Anyone who sells mutual funds for commission in India must be registered with AMFI. Individuals first pass the NISM Series V-A Mutual Fund Distributors certification, complete KYD (know your distributor) verification, and then apply to AMFI for the ARN. Corporate distributors such as banks and broking firms get an ARN for the entity, while each employee who interacts with investors gets an Employee Unique Identification Number (EUIN).

The ARN is mentioned in the application form or online transaction. It tells the AMC whom to pay commission to and creates accountability: if advice was mis-sold, the distributor can be identified. Transactions where the ARN is left blank are processed under the direct plan.

Registration is not permanent. The ARN must be renewed periodically, typically every three years, by passing the certification again or completing continuing professional education, as AMFI and NISM rules prescribe. Distributors must also follow AMFI's code of conduct.

An ARN holder is a distributor, not a SEBI-registered investment adviser. Distributors earn commission from AMCs on regular plans and can give incidental advice on products they sell. Investors who want fee-only advice should look for an RIA. Investors can verify a distributor's ARN status on AMFI's website.

Example

Karan, a commerce graduate, passes NISM Series V-A, completes KYD with his PAN and biometrics, and receives ARN-123456 (illustrative). His client invests ₹10,000 a month through a SIP in the regular plan of a large cap fund, quoting his ARN. The AMC pays Karan a trail commission, a small percentage of the client's assets each year, which is already included in the regular plan's expense ratio. If the client had left the ARN blank, the SIP would go to the direct plan.

FAQs

Which exam is needed to get an ARN?

Individuals must pass the NISM Series V-A Mutual Fund Distributors certification examination. AMFI also has provisions for a simpler category for certain distributors, linked to other NISM certifications. Requirements can change, so check the current AMFI and NISM guidelines before applying.

How can I check if a distributor's ARN is valid?

AMFI's website has a search facility where you can enter the ARN or the distributor's name to see registration status and validity date. Checking this before transacting is a simple way to avoid unregistered intermediaries.
