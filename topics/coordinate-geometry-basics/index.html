Coordinate Geometry Basics: Distance, Slope and Area

Coordinate geometry is a small but regular topic in SSC CGL and CHSL, usually one or two questions, and it appears in CAT and CMAT within geometry and algebra. Bank exams do not test it. The questions are formula-based: distance between points, the point dividing a segment, the slope of a line, intercepts and areas. Each formula is short, so accuracy with signs is the main challenge.

Last updated 25 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · Free

Distance and mid-point

The distance between (x₁, y₁) and (x₂, y₂) is √[(x₂ − x₁)² + (y₂ − y₁)²]. The mid-point is ((x₁ + x₂)/2, (y₁ + y₂)/2). Many questions use Pythagorean triples, so distances like 10 or 13 appear often.

d = √[(x₂ − x₁)² + (y₂ − y₁)²]

Section formula

The point dividing the segment from A(x₁, y₁) to B(x₂, y₂) internally in the ratio m : n is ((m x₂ + n x₁)/(m + n), (m y₂ + n y₁)/(m + n)). The centroid of a triangle is the average of its three vertices.

P = ((mx₂ + nx₁)/(m + n), (my₂ + ny₁)/(m + n))

Slope and equations of lines

Slope m = (y₂ − y₁)/(x₂ − x₁). A line with slope m and y-intercept c is y = mx + c. For ax + by + c = 0, the slope is −a/b. Parallel lines have equal slopes; perpendicular lines have slopes whose product is −1.

m = (y₂ − y₁)/(x₂ − x₁)

Intercepts and area

The line x/a + y/b = 1 cuts the x-axis at a and the y-axis at b, forming a triangle of area |ab|/2 with the axes. The area of a triangle with vertices (x₁, y₁), (x₂, y₂), (x₃, y₃) is ½|x₁(y₂ − y₃) + x₂(y₃ − y₁) + x₃(y₁ − y₂)|. If this area is zero, the points are collinear.

Area = ½|x₁(y₂ − y₃) + x₂(y₃ − y₁) + x₃(y₁ − y₂)|

Shortcuts and tricks

  • To find intercepts of ax + by = c, divide by c: 3x + 4y = 12 becomes x/4 + y/3 = 1.
  • A point on the x-axis has y = 0; on the y-axis, x = 0 — use this to find where lines cut the axes.
  • Perpendicular slope = −1/m: a line perpendicular to slope 4 has slope −1/4.
  • Check collinearity quickly by comparing slopes of AB and BC.
  • Sketch the points roughly; a picture often reveals a right angle or symmetry.

Worked examples

Example 1

Find the distance between (2, 3) and (8, 11).

Solution

Differences are 6 and 8, so d = √(36 + 64) = √100 = 10 units — a 6-8-10 triangle.

Example 2

Find the mid-point of (−4, 6) and (10, −2).

Solution

((−4 + 10)/2, (6 − 2)/2) = (3, 2).

Example 3

Find the point dividing the segment from (1, 2) to (7, 8) internally in the ratio 1 : 2.

Solution

x = (1 × 7 + 2 × 1)/3 = 3; y = (1 × 8 + 2 × 2)/3 = 4. The point is (3, 4), one-third of the way from the first point.

Example 4

Find the slope and equation of the line through (2, 5) and (6, 13).

Solution

Slope = (13 − 5)/(6 − 2) = 2. Using y = 2x + c with (2, 5): 5 = 4 + c, so c = 1. Equation: y = 2x + 1.

Example 5

Find the area of the triangle with vertices (1, 1), (4, 5) and (7, 1).

Solution

½|1(5 − 1) + 4(1 − 1) + 7(1 − 5)| = ½|4 + 0 − 28| = 12 square units. Check: base from (1, 1) to (7, 1) is 6 and height is 4, giving 12.

Example 6

Find the area of the triangle formed by 3x + 4y = 12 with the coordinate axes.

Solution

Intercepts: x = 4 and y = 3. Area = ½ × 4 × 3 = 6 square units.

Practice questions

Question 1

The distance between (−1, 2) and (5, 10) is:

  1. 8
  2. 14
  3. 12
  4. 10
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 10

Differences are 6 and 8, so the distance is √(36 + 64) = √100 = 10 units.

Question 2

The mid-point of (3, −5) and (7, 9) is:

  1. (4, 2)
  2. (5, 7)
  3. (5, 2)
  4. (2, 7)
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. (5, 2)

Average the coordinates: ((3 + 7)/2, (−5 + 9)/2) = (5, 2), the mid-point of the segment.

Question 3

The slope of the line 2x − 3y + 6 = 0 is:

  1. −2/3
  2. 2/3
  3. 3/2
  4. −3/2
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 2/3

For ax + by + c = 0 the slope is −a/b = −2/(−3) = 2/3; rewriting gives y = (2/3)x + 2.

Question 4

The area of the triangle formed by 2x + 5y = 10 and the coordinate axes is:

  1. 10 square units
  2. 7 square units
  3. 2.5 square units
  4. 5 square units
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 5 square units

The intercepts are x = 5 and y = 2, so the area is ½ × 5 × 2 = 5 square units.

Question 5

The slope of a line perpendicular to y = 4x − 7 is:

  1. −1/4
  2. 4
  3. 1/4
  4. −4
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. −1/4

Perpendicular slopes multiply to −1, so the required slope is −1/4 for a line with slope 4.

FAQs

Is coordinate geometry important for bank exams?

No, IBPS, SBI and RBI papers do not normally include it. It matters for SSC CGL and CHSL and for MBA entrance exams like CAT and CMAT. Bank aspirants who are not taking SSC can give it low priority.

How do I check whether three points are collinear?

Find the area of the triangle they form; if it is zero, they lie on a line. Alternatively, compute the slopes of AB and BC — equal slopes through a shared point mean the points are collinear.
