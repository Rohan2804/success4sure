HCF and LCM Shortcuts with Real-Life Word Problems

HCF (highest common factor) and LCM (least common multiple) questions are standard in SSC CGL, CHSL and MTS, usually two questions per paper, and they support many bank-exam topics such as time and work (LCM of days) and circular-track meetings. The concepts are simple, but word problems hide them: bells ringing together point to LCM, while cutting the largest equal pieces points to HCF.

Last updated 19 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · Free

Finding HCF and LCM

Write each number in prime factors. HCF takes the lowest power of each common prime; LCM takes the highest power of every prime present. 72 = 2³ × 3² and 120 = 2³ × 3 × 5, so HCF = 2³ × 3 = 24 and LCM = 2³ × 3² × 5 = 360.

Product rule

For two numbers only, HCF × LCM = product of the numbers. If HCF = 12, LCM = 336 and one number is 48, the other is 12 × 336 ÷ 48 = 84. This rule does not hold for three or more numbers.

a × b = HCF × LCM

HCF word problems

Use HCF when the question asks for the largest size, the greatest number dividing several numbers, or cutting things into the biggest equal pieces. If remainders are given, subtract them first: the largest number dividing 245 and 1029 leaving 5 is HCF(240, 1024) = 16.

LCM word problems

Use LCM when events repeat and you want them to coincide (bells, traffic lights, runners) or for the smallest number divisible by several numbers. For "leaves remainder r in each case", answer = LCM + r.

Fractions

HCF of fractions = HCF of numerators ÷ LCM of denominators; LCM of fractions = LCM of numerators ÷ HCF of denominators.

LCM(a/b, c/d) = LCM(a, c) / HCF(b, d)

Shortcuts and tricks

  • HCF always divides LCM, and HCF ≤ smaller number ≤ LCM — use this to reject options.
  • Co-prime numbers have HCF 1, so their LCM is their product.
  • For different remainders with a constant difference (divisor − remainder same), answer = LCM − that difference.
  • Tiles question: number of square tiles = area ÷ (HCF side)².

Worked examples

Example 1

Find the HCF and LCM of 72 and 120.

Solution

72 = 2³ × 3², 120 = 2³ × 3 × 5. HCF = 24, LCM = 360. Check: 24 × 360 = 8,640 = 72 × 120.

Example 2

Three bells toll at intervals of 6, 8 and 12 minutes. If they toll together at 9:00 a.m., when will they next toll together?

Solution

LCM(6, 8, 12) = 24 minutes, so at 9:24 a.m.

Example 3

Find the largest number that divides 245 and 1029 leaving a remainder of 5 in each case.

Solution

Subtract the remainder: 240 and 1,024. HCF(240, 1024) = 16. Answer: 16.

Example 4

Find the smallest number that leaves a remainder of 4 when divided by 12, 15 and 20.

Solution

LCM(12, 15, 20) = 60. Answer = 60 + 4 = 64.

Example 5

A hall floor is 6.24 m by 4.32 m. Find the largest square tile that fits exactly and the number of tiles needed.

Solution

In cm: 624 and 432. HCF = 48 cm. Tiles = (624/48) × (432/48) = 13 × 9 = 117. Converting to centimetres first matters, because the HCF of decimals is awkward to find directly.

Example 6

Find the HCF and LCM of the fractions 2/3, 4/9 and 8/15.

Solution

HCF = HCF(2, 4, 8) ÷ LCM(3, 9, 15) = 2 ÷ 45 = 2/45. LCM = LCM(2, 4, 8) ÷ HCF(3, 9, 15) = 8 ÷ 3 = 8/3. Check that 8/3 ÷ 4/9 = 6, a whole number, so 8/3 is indeed a multiple of 4/9.

Practice questions

Question 1

The LCM of 18, 24 and 36 is:

  1. 144
  2. 36
  3. 216
  4. 72
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 72

18 = 2 × 3², 24 = 2³ × 3 and 36 = 2² × 3², so the LCM is 2³ × 3² = 72.

Question 2

The HCF of 84 and 126 is:

  1. 21
  2. 14
  3. 42
  4. 28
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 42

84 = 2² × 3 × 7 and 126 = 2 × 3² × 7, so the HCF is 2 × 3 × 7 = 42.

Question 3

The HCF of two numbers is 16 and their LCM is 480. If one number is 96, the other is:

  1. 60
  2. 120
  3. 160
  4. 80
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 80

Other number = 16 × 480 ÷ 96 = 7,680 ÷ 96 = 80, using HCF × LCM = product.

Question 4

Three lights blink every 20, 30 and 45 seconds. If they blink together now, they next blink together after:

  1. 3 minutes
  2. 90 seconds
  3. 2 minutes
  4. 6 minutes
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 3 minutes

LCM(20, 30, 45) = 180 seconds, which equals 3 minutes, when all three coincide again.

Question 5

The largest number that divides 410, 751 and 1,030 leaving remainder 7 in each case is:

  1. 29
  2. 31
  3. 37
  4. 13
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 31

Subtract 7 to get 403, 744 and 1,023, whose HCF is 31 (13 × 31, 24 × 31 and 33 × 31).

FAQs

How do I decide between HCF and LCM in a word problem?

Ask whether the answer should be smaller or larger than the given numbers. Cutting, dividing or grouping into the largest equal parts gives a smaller answer, so use HCF. Repeating events meeting again or a number divisible by all gives a larger answer, so use LCM.

Does HCF × LCM = product work for three numbers?

No. The rule holds only for two numbers. For three numbers, compute HCF and LCM from prime factorisation directly. Applying the two-number rule to three numbers is a common trap in SSC questions.
