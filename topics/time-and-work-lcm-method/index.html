Time and Work: LCM Method, Formulas and Solved Questions

Time and work is one of the most predictable arithmetic topics: IBPS PO, SBI PO and RBI Grade B prelims ask one or two questions, and SSC CGL and CHSL ask two or three. CAT uses the same ideas inside harder multi-stage problems. The fastest approach is to assume the total work as a convenient number of units (the LCM of the given days), which turns fractions into simple whole-number division.

Last updated 19 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · Free

Work rate

If A finishes a job in 12 days, A does 1/12 of it per day. Rates add when people work together: A (12 days) and B (18 days) do 1/12 + 1/18 = 5/36 per day, so they finish in 36/5 = 7.2 days. Time taken = 1 ÷ combined rate.

Together = ab / (a + b) days

LCM or total-units method

Take the total work as the LCM of the individual days. For A = 20 days and B = 30 days, total = 60 units; A does 3 units a day and B does 2. Together they do 5 units, so the job takes 12 days. This avoids fractions entirely and makes leaving-midway questions easy.

Man-days formula

Work is proportional to men × days × hours. If 12 men finish a job in 18 days, the job is 216 man-days, so 8 men need 27 days. When the amount of work also changes, divide each side by its work.

M1 × D1 × H1 / W1 = M2 × D2 × H2 / W2

Someone leaves or joins

Compute the work done in the joint period with the combined rate, subtract from total units, and let the remaining workers finish the rest at their rate. Always add the two time periods if total time is asked.

Shortcuts and tricks

  • Two people: together time = ab/(a + b). For 10 and 15 days, 150/25 = 6 days.
  • If A + B take T days and A alone takes a days, B alone takes aT/(a − T) days.
  • Efficiency is the inverse of time: A twice as efficient as B means A takes half B’s time.
  • Three people with times a, b, c: use LCM units rather than the product formula; it is quicker and safer.
  • If the answer must be in days and hours, convert the fractional part: 0.2 of an 8-hour day is 1.6 hours.

Worked examples

Example 1

A can finish a task in 12 days and B in 18 days. How long will they take together?

Solution

Combined rate = 1/12 + 1/18 = 3/36 + 2/36 = 5/36. Time = 36/5 = 7.2 days. LCM method: total 36 units, A does 3 and B does 2 per day, so 36 ÷ 5 = 7.2 days.

Example 2

A and B together can do a job in 8 days. A alone can do it in 12 days. How long would B take alone?

Solution

B’s rate = 1/8 − 1/12 = 1/24, so B alone takes 24 days. Using total 24 units: together 3 units a day, A 2 units, so B does 1 unit a day.

Example 3

A can do a job in 10 days and B in 15 days. They start together, but A leaves after 4 days. In how many days is the job finished?

Solution

Total = 30 units; A does 3, B does 2 per day. In 4 days together they do 20 units. B finishes the remaining 10 units in 5 days. Total time = 9 days.

Example 4

12 men can build a wall in 18 days. How many days will 8 men take to build the same wall?

Solution

Work = 12 × 18 = 216 man-days. Days for 8 men = 216 ÷ 8 = 27 days.

Practice questions

Question 1

A can complete a work in 10 days and B in 15 days. Working together, they finish in:

  1. 5 days
  2. 7.5 days
  3. 6 days
  4. 12.5 days
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 6 days

Using 30 units, A does 3 and B does 2 per day, so together 5 units a day finish 30 units in 6 days.

Question 2

A and B together finish a job in 6 days. A alone takes 10 days. B alone takes:

  1. 12 days
  2. 16 days
  3. 4 days
  4. 15 days
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 15 days

B’s rate is 1/6 − 1/10 = 2/30 = 1/15, so B alone needs 15 days for the job.

Question 3

15 men can finish a work in 16 days. How many days will 20 men take?

  1. 10 days
  2. 12 days
  3. 14 days
  4. 21 days
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 12 days

The work is 15 × 16 = 240 man-days, and 240 ÷ 20 = 12 days for twenty men.

Question 4

A, B and C can do a job in 24, 36 and 72 days respectively. Working together, they finish in:

  1. 10 days
  2. 15 days
  3. 12 days
  4. 18 days
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 12 days

Total 72 units; daily output is 3 + 2 + 1 = 6 units, so the job takes 72 ÷ 6 = 12 days.

Question 5

A is twice as efficient as B. Together they finish a job in 14 days. B alone would take:

  1. 42 days
  2. 28 days
  3. 21 days
  4. 36 days
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 42 days

Efficiencies are 2 : 1, so total work = 3 × 14 = 42 units and B at 1 unit a day needs 42 days.

FAQs

Is the LCM method allowed in exams?

Yes — the exam only checks the final answer. The LCM method is simply a way of choosing a convenient size for the total work so that daily outputs are whole numbers. It gives the same answer as the fraction method, usually faster and with fewer errors.

How are pipes and cisterns related to time and work?

They use exactly the same logic. A filling pipe is like a worker adding work, and an outlet or leak is like a worker undoing work, so its rate is subtracted. Practising one topic automatically builds speed in the other.
