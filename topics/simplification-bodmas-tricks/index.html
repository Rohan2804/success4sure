Simplification and BODMAS Tricks for Bank Exams

Simplification questions — evaluate an expression or find the value of "?" — form a block of five in IBPS Clerk, SBI Clerk and RBI Assistant prelims and appear in IBPS PO as well. SSC papers include similar arithmetic. These are pure speed questions: the concepts are school-level, so marks depend on applying BODMAS correctly and on mental shortcuts for percentages, fractions, squares and cube roots.

Last updated 23 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · Free

BODMAS order

Brackets first, then Orders (powers and roots), then Division and Multiplication from left to right, then Addition and Subtraction from left to right. Division and multiplication have equal priority, so 48 ÷ 6 × 4 = 8 × 4 = 32, not 48 ÷ 24 = 2.

B → O → D/M (left to right) → A/S (left to right)

Percentages and fractions

Convert percentages to fractions where possible: 25% of 480 = 480/4 = 120; 37.5% = 3/8; 12.5% = 1/8; 16.67% = 1/6. A mixed expression like "25% of 480 + 3/8 of 640" becomes 120 + 240 = 360 without long multiplication.

Squares, cubes and roots

Memorise squares to 50 and cubes to 25. For square roots of perfect squares, use the last digit and the range: √1764 ends in 2 or 8 and lies between 40 and 50; 42² = 1,764. For cube roots, the last digit of the cube fixes the last digit of the root (2197 ends in 7, so the root ends in 3).

Mixed fractions

Split mixed numbers into whole and fractional parts and add them separately, using the LCM of the denominators for the fractions. This keeps numbers small and is usually faster than converting everything into improper fractions, especially when three or more mixed numbers appear in one expression.

Decimals

Convert decimals to fractions or scale them up: 0.25 × 0.4 ÷ 0.05 = (25 × 4 ÷ 5) × (10⁻² × 10⁻¹ ÷ 10⁻²) = 20 × 0.1 = 2. Counting decimal places separately avoids misplaced points.

Shortcuts and tricks

  • "Find ?" questions: move known terms to one side first — 126 + ? = 195 gives ? = 69 instantly.
  • a² − b² = (a + b)(a − b): 53² − 47² = 100 × 6 = 600.
  • Squares ending in 5: 65² = 6 × 7 followed by 25 = 4,225.
  • Multiply by 25 by dividing by 4 and multiplying by 100: 25 × 36 = 900.
  • Cube roots: endings map 1→1, 8→2, 7→3, 4→4, 5→5, 6→6, 3→7, 2→8, 9→9, 0→0.

Worked examples

Example 1

48 ÷ 6 × 4 + 18 − 5 × 3 = ?

Solution

Division and multiplication left to right: 48 ÷ 6 = 8, 8 × 4 = 32, 5 × 3 = 15. Then 32 + 18 − 15 = 35.

Example 2

25% of 480 + 3/8 of 640 = ?

Solution

25% of 480 = 120; 3/8 of 640 = 240. Sum = 360.

Example 3

√1764 + ∛2197 = ?

Solution

√1764 = 42 (since 42² = 1,764) and ∛2197 = 13 (since 13³ = 2,197). Sum = 55.

Example 4

(0.25 × 0.4) ÷ 0.05 = ?

Solution

0.25 × 0.4 = 0.1, and 0.1 ÷ 0.05 = 2.

Example 5

15% of 840 + ? = 30% of 650

Solution

15% of 840 = 126 and 30% of 650 = 195. So ? = 195 − 126 = 69. Check by substituting: 126 + 69 = 195, which matches the right-hand side.

Example 6

2¾ + 3⅙ − 1 5/12 = ?

Solution

Convert to improper fractions with the common denominator 12: 11/4 = 33/12, 19/6 = 38/12 and 17/12. So 33/12 + 38/12 − 17/12 = 54/12 = 4.5. A faster route is to handle whole numbers and fractions separately: 2 + 3 − 1 = 4, and 3/4 + 1/6 − 5/12 = (9 + 2 − 5)/12 = 1/2, giving 4½.

Practice questions

Question 1

72 ÷ 8 × 3 − 4 × 5 + 6 = ?

  1. 7
  2. 13
  3. 33
  4. 1
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 13

Left to right: 72 ÷ 8 = 9, 9 × 3 = 27, 4 × 5 = 20, so 27 − 20 + 6 = 13.

Question 2

40% of 350 + 1/6 of 540 = ?

  1. 230
  2. 220
  3. 240
  4. 210
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 230

40% of 350 is 140 and one-sixth of 540 is 90, so the total is 140 + 90 = 230.

Question 3

√2025 − ∛1331 = ?

  1. 36
  2. 32
  3. 34
  4. 24
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 34

√2025 = 45 because 45² = 2,025, and ∛1331 = 11, so the difference is 45 − 11 = 34.

Question 4

1.2 × 0.5 ÷ 0.03 = ?

  1. 2
  2. 20
  3. 18
  4. 200
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 20

1.2 × 0.5 = 0.6, and 0.6 ÷ 0.03 = 60 ÷ 3 = 20 after scaling both by 100.

Question 5

3/4 of 2/5 of 1,200 = ?

  1. 480
  2. 320
  3. 400
  4. 360
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 360

2/5 of 1,200 is 480, and 3/4 of 480 is 360, so the expression equals 360.

FAQs

Do simplification questions use the "of" operator before division?

In many Indian textbooks, "of" is treated as multiplication done before division, and it often appears as "x% of y". In modern bank papers, expressions are written to avoid ambiguity, so just apply BODMAS and evaluate "of" as multiplication immediately.

How much time should a simplification question take?

Aim for 20–30 seconds each. With five questions in the block, finishing them in about two minutes leaves time for DI and arithmetic. Daily practice of squares, cubes and fraction conversions is the main way to reach that speed.
