Calendar Questions: Odd Days Method and Repeating Years

Calendar questions ask for the day of the week on a given date, or which year will have the same calendar as another. SSC CGL, CHSL and MTS ask one almost every year, and bank clerk and PO prelims include calendar logic inside scheduling puzzles. The entire topic rests on one idea — odd days, the days left over after complete weeks. With a handful of memorised values you can find the day for any date in under a minute.

Last updated 17 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · Free

Odd days

Odd days are the remainder when a number of days is divided by 7. An ordinary year has 365 days = 52 weeks + 1 odd day. A leap year has 366 days = 2 odd days. Count odd days, then move forward that many weekdays.

Leap-year rule

A year is a leap year if it is divisible by 4, except century years, which must be divisible by 400. So 2024 and 2000 are leap years; 1900 and 2100 are not.

Odd days in centuries

100 years have 5 odd days, 200 years have 3, 300 years have 1, and 400 years have 0. Because 400 years have 0 odd days, the calendar cycle repeats every 400 years.

Day code: 0 = Sunday, 1 = Monday, …, 6 = Saturday

Month odd days

January 3, February 0 (1 in a leap year), March 3, April 2, May 3, June 2, July 3, August 3, September 2, October 3, November 2, December 3.

Repeating calendars

Two years have identical calendars when they start on the same weekday and are both leap or both ordinary. Add odd days year by year until the total is a multiple of 7, checking the leap status matches.

Shortcuts and tricks

  • Going forward n days from a known day: move n mod 7 weekdays ahead. 100 days after Wednesday is 100 mod 7 = 2 days ahead: Friday.
  • 1 January of the next year is one weekday later after an ordinary year and two later after a leap year.
  • An ordinary year's calendar repeats after 6 or 11 years; a leap year's repeats after 28 years.
  • Dates in the same month are 7, 14, 21 or 28 days apart if they share a weekday.
  • For famous dates, check your method: 15 August 1947 was a Friday and 26 January 1950 was a Thursday.

Worked examples

Example 1

What day of the week was 15 August 1947?

Solution

1600 years: 0 odd days. Next 300 years: 1. Years 1901–1946 contain 11 leap years and 35 ordinary years: 22 + 35 = 57, which leaves 1. In 1947 up to 15 August: 3 + 0 + 3 + 2 + 3 + 2 + 3 + 15 = 31, which leaves 3. Total 0 + 1 + 1 + 3 = 5 → Friday.

Example 2

If 1 January 2026 is a Thursday, what day is 1 January 2027?

Solution

2026 is an ordinary year with 1 odd day, so the day moves one step: Friday.

Example 3

Which year will have the same calendar as 2026?

Solution

Add odd days: 2026 → 1, 2027 → 1, 2028 (leap) → 2, 2029 → 1, 2030 → 1, 2031 → 1, 2032 (leap) → 2, 2033 → 1, 2034 → 1, 2035 → 1, 2036 (leap) → 2. Running total reaches 14 at the end of 2036, a multiple of 7, and 2037 is an ordinary year like 2026. Answer: 2037.

Example 4

If 5 March of a year is a Monday, what day is 30 March of the same year?

Solution

30 − 5 = 25 days later; 25 mod 7 = 4. Four days after Monday is Friday.

Practice questions

Question 1

What day of the week was 26 January 1950?

  1. Friday
  2. Wednesday
  3. Sunday
  4. Thursday
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Thursday

Odd days up to 1900 total 1, years 1901 to 1949 add 61 which leaves 5, and 26 January adds 5. Total 11 leaves 4, which is Thursday.

Question 2

How many odd days are there in a leap year?

  1. Two
  2. One
  3. Three
  4. Zero
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Two

A leap year has 366 days, which is 52 full weeks plus 2 extra days. Those two remaining days are the odd days.

Question 3

If today is Wednesday, what day of the week will it be after 100 days?

  1. Thursday
  2. Friday
  3. Saturday
  4. Tuesday
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Friday

100 divided by 7 leaves a remainder of 2. Moving two days ahead of Wednesday gives Friday.

Question 4

Which year will have the same calendar as 2027?

  1. 2033
  2. 2035
  3. 2038
  4. 2044
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 2038

2027 is an ordinary year starting on Friday. Adding odd days year by year, the total first becomes a multiple of 7 before 2038, which is also an ordinary year.

Question 5

If 1 February 2028 is a Tuesday, what day is 1 March 2028?

  1. Tuesday
  2. Thursday
  3. Monday
  4. Wednesday
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. Wednesday

2028 is a leap year, so February has 29 days, which is one odd day. One day after Tuesday is Wednesday.

FAQs

Why is 1900 not a leap year though it is divisible by 4?

Century years are leap years only if divisible by 400. This correction keeps the calendar aligned with the solar year. So 1600 and 2000 were leap years, but 1700, 1800 and 1900 were not, and 2100 will not be either.

Do I need to memorise month odd days?

It helps a lot. Most months with 31 days give 3 odd days and those with 30 give 2; February gives 0, or 1 in a leap year. Once memorised, you can find the day for any date quickly instead of counting days.
