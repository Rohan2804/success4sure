Cubes and Dice Reasoning: Painted Cubes and Opposite Faces

Cube and dice questions are a regular part of SSC CGL, CHSL and MTS reasoning and occasionally appear in bank clerk exams and MAT. Painted-cube questions ask how many small cubes have a certain number of painted faces after a big painted cube is cut; dice questions ask which face is opposite another. Both rely on a few formulas and rules that are quick to learn and almost never change from paper to paper.

Last updated 25 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · Free

Painted cube cut into n × n × n

A cube painted on all faces and cut into n³ equal cubes gives: 8 corner cubes with three painted faces; 12(n − 2) edge cubes with two; 6(n − 2)² face-centre cubes with one; and (n − 2)³ inner cubes with none.

3 faces: 8 | 2 faces: 12(n−2) | 1 face: 6(n−2)² | 0 faces: (n−2)³

Standard dice

On a standard die, opposite faces add up to 7: 1–6, 2–5 and 3–4. Some questions use non-standard dice with letters or colours, where you must work out the opposite pairs from the views given.

Two views with one common face

If two views of the same die show one common face in the same position, read the other faces clockwise from the common face; faces in matching positions are opposite each other. This is the most-tested dice rule.

Adjacent faces cannot be opposite

Any two faces seen together in one view are adjacent, so they cannot be opposite. List all faces that appear with a given face; the one never seen with it is its opposite.

Shortcuts and tricks

  • Total cubes check: 8 + 12(n−2) + 6(n−2)² + (n−2)³ = n³. For n = 5: 8 + 36 + 54 + 27 = 125.
  • Only-one-face cubes are at the centre of each face; there are (n−2)² per face.
  • For a 3 × 3 × 3 cube, exactly one cube (the very centre) has no paint.
  • If 1 is seen with 2, 3, 4 and 5 across views, 1 is opposite 6.
  • Unfolded dice nets: in a straight row of three faces, the first and third are opposite.

Worked examples

Example 1

A cube painted red on all faces is cut into 64 equal smaller cubes. How many smaller cubes have exactly two faces painted?

Solution

64 = 4³, so n = 4. Two-faced cubes = 12(n − 2) = 12 × 2 = 24.

Example 2

A painted cube is cut into 125 equal cubes. How many have no face painted and how many have exactly one face painted?

Solution

n = 5. No face: (5 − 2)³ = 27. One face: 6 × (5 − 2)² = 54.

Example 3

Two views of a die: View 1 shows 1 on top with 2 and 3 on the visible sides. View 2 shows 1 on top with 4 and 5 on the visible sides. Which number is opposite 1?

Solution

1 is adjacent to 2, 3, 4 and 5 because it appears with them. The only remaining face is 6, so 6 is opposite 1 — consistent with the standard die rule.

Example 4

A die net has faces A, B, C in a straight row, with D above B and E below B, and F to the right of C. Which face is opposite A?

Solution

In a straight row of three, the first and third are opposite, so A is opposite C. D and E, on either side of the middle face B, are opposite each other, and B is opposite F.

Practice questions

Question 1

A painted cube is cut into 27 equal cubes. How many small cubes have no face painted?

  1. 8
  2. 1
  3. 6
  4. 12
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 1

27 = 3³, so n = 3. Unpainted cubes number (3 − 2)³ = 1, which is the single cube at the very centre.

Question 2

A painted cube is cut into 125 equal cubes. How many small cubes have exactly two faces painted?

  1. 24
  2. 54
  3. 36
  4. 48
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 36

With n = 5, two-faced cubes are on the edges: 12 × (5 − 2) = 36.

Question 3

On a standard die, which number is opposite 2?

  1. 4
  2. 6
  3. 3
  4. 5
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 5

Opposite faces on a standard die add up to 7. The number opposite 2 must therefore be 7 − 2 = 5.

Question 4

A painted cube is cut into 64 equal cubes. How many small cubes have exactly one face painted?

  1. 24
  2. 8
  3. 16
  4. 36
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 24

With n = 4, each face has (4 − 2)² = 4 one-face cubes. Six faces give 6 × 4 = 24.

Question 5

However large the cut, how many cubes always have three faces painted when a painted cube is cut into equal smaller cubes (n ≥ 2)?

  1. 6
  2. 8
  3. 12
  4. 4
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 8

Only the corner cubes have three painted faces, and a cube always has eight corners, so the count stays 8.

FAQs

What if the cube is painted on only some faces?

Then count face by face. A corner cube gets paint only from the painted faces it touches. Draw the cube, mark which faces are painted, and adjust the standard formulas. Such questions are less common but do appear in SSC papers.

Are exam dice always standard?

No. Many questions use dice with letters, symbols or numbers that do not follow the sum-of-7 rule. Use the given views to find adjacent faces and the common-face method to fix opposites. Apply the 7 rule only when the question says the die is standard.
