Ratio and Proportion: Rules, Tricks and Solved Questions

Ratio and proportion is less a chapter than a language used across the quant section: partnership, ages, mixtures, time and work and most DI sets are ratio problems in disguise. IBPS Clerk, SBI Clerk and SSC CHSL ask one or two direct questions, and CAT expects you to manipulate ratios fluently in algebra. Students who think in "parts" instead of writing equations usually finish these questions in under 40 seconds.

Last updated 23 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · Free

Thinking in parts

A ratio 3 : 5 means the quantities are 3k and 5k for some k. If their total is ₹640, then 8k = 640 and k = 80. The single unknown k turns a two-variable problem into one division. When a change happens (adding 10 to each), write the new ratio with k and cross-multiply.

Combining ratios

To merge A : B and B : C, make the common term B equal. For A : B = 2 : 3 and B : C = 4 : 5, scale the first by 4 and the second by 3: A : B : C = 8 : 12 : 15. The value of A : C alone is the product of the two ratios: (2/3) × (4/5) = 8/15.

Proportionals

If a : b = c : d, then d is the fourth proportional = bc/a. The third proportional to a and b is b²/a, and the mean proportional between a and b is √(ab). These three definitions are asked directly in SSC papers.

Mean proportional = √(ab)

Inverse ratios

Dividing ₹5,200 in the ratio 1/2 : 1/3 : 1/4 means first converting fractions to whole numbers using the LCM of the denominators (12): 6 : 4 : 3. Shares go in proportion to these numbers, not to 2 : 3 : 4.

Shortcuts and tricks

  • If a/b = c/d, then (a + b)/(a − b) = (c + d)/(c − d) (componendo–dividendo); useful in CAT algebra.
  • Coin-bag questions: convert the count ratio to a value ratio first. 5 : 6 : 8 coins of ₹1, 50p, 25p have values 5 : 3 : 2.
  • When the same number x is added to both terms of a ratio, solve with cross-multiplication; options can often be checked faster than solving.
  • A share is (its ratio term ÷ sum of terms) × total. For 2 : 3 : 4 of ₹3,600, the largest share is 4/9 × 3,600 = ₹1,600.
  • Duplicate ratio of a : b is a² : b²; sub-duplicate is √a : √b. These terms appear in SSC questions.

Worked examples

Example 1

If A : B = 2 : 3 and B : C = 4 : 5, find A : B : C.

Solution

Make B common: multiply the first ratio by 4 and the second by 3. A : B = 8 : 12 and B : C = 12 : 15, so A : B : C = 8 : 12 : 15.

Example 2

Divide ₹5,200 among A, B and C in the ratio 1/2 : 1/3 : 1/4.

Solution

LCM of 2, 3, 4 is 12. Multiplying gives 6 : 4 : 3, total 13 parts. One part = 5,200 ÷ 13 = ₹400. Shares: A ₹2,400, B ₹1,600, C ₹1,200.

Example 3

Find the fourth proportional to 4, 9 and 12, and the mean proportional between 9 and 16.

Solution

Fourth proportional = 9 × 12 ÷ 4 = 27. Mean proportional = √(9 × 16) = √144 = 12.

Example 4

Two numbers are in the ratio 3 : 5. If 10 is added to each, the ratio becomes 5 : 7. Find the numbers.

Solution

Let them be 3x and 5x. Then (3x + 10)/(5x + 10) = 5/7, so 21x + 70 = 25x + 50 and x = 5. The numbers are 15 and 25. Check: 25/35 = 5/7.

Example 5

A bag has ₹1, 50 paise and 25 paise coins in the ratio 5 : 6 : 8 by number. The total value is ₹210. How many coins of each kind are there?

Solution

Value ratio = 5 × 1 : 6 × 0.5 : 8 × 0.25 = 5 : 3 : 2, total 10 parts = ₹210, so one part = ₹21. Values: ₹105, ₹63, ₹42. Number of coins: 105 one-rupee, 126 fifty-paise and 168 twenty-five-paise coins.

Practice questions

Question 1

If A : B = 3 : 4 and B : C = 6 : 7, then A : C is:

  1. 9 : 14
  2. 3 : 7
  3. 18 : 21
  4. 1 : 2
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 9 : 14

A : C = (3/4) × (6/7) = 18/28 = 9/14, so the ratio of A to C is 9 : 14.

Question 2

The third proportional to 8 and 12 is:

  1. 16
  2. 18
  3. 20
  4. 24
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 18

Third proportional = b²/a = 12² ÷ 8 = 144 ÷ 8 = 18, so 8 : 12 = 12 : 18.

Question 3

₹3,600 is divided in the ratio 2 : 3 : 4. The largest share is:

  1. ₹1,600
  2. ₹1,200
  3. ₹1,800
  4. ₹1,440
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. ₹1,600

There are 9 parts of ₹400 each, so the largest share is 4 × 400 = ₹1,600.

Question 4

Two numbers are in the ratio 4 : 7. If 6 is subtracted from each, the ratio becomes 1 : 2. The larger number is:

  1. 35
  2. 28
  3. 49
  4. 42
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 42

With 4x and 7x, 2(4x − 6) = 7x − 6 gives x = 6, so the numbers are 24 and 42.

Question 5

The mean proportional between 0.08 and 0.18 is:

  1. 0.13
  2. 0.0144
  3. 0.12
  4. 0.26
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 0.12

Mean proportional = √(0.08 × 0.18) = √0.0144 = 0.12, which lies between the two numbers.

FAQs

Is ratio the same as fraction?

Numerically a ratio a : b can be written as a/b, but a ratio compares two quantities of the same kind, while a fraction is a part of a whole. In a ratio 3 : 5, the first quantity is 3/8 of the total, not 3/5. Confusing these is a common error.

How do I handle three-term ratios quickly?

Find the common term in the two given ratios and scale each ratio so that the common term matches, using the LCM of its two values. Write the merged ratio in a single line. Practise ten such questions and the scaling becomes automatic.
