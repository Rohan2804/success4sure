DI Calculation Shortcuts: Percentages, Ratios, Fractions

Every DI set in IBPS PO, SBI PO, RBI Grade B, SSC CGL Tier 2 and CAT ultimately reduces to a handful of calculations: percentages, percentage change, ratios, averages and fraction comparison. Candidates who know the concepts but calculate slowly run out of time in the DI section. This page collects the calculation habits that save the most time, each with a worked example on DI-style numbers.

Last updated 20 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · Free

Fraction benchmarks

Know the percentage values of common fractions: 1/3 = 33.33%, 1/6 = 16.67%, 1/7 ≈ 14.29%, 1/8 = 12.5%, 1/9 ≈ 11.11%, 1/11 ≈ 9.09%, 1/12 ≈ 8.33%. Then 16.67% of 7,230 is just 7,230 ÷ 6 = 1,205, and 1,847/6,112 is quickly seen to be a little over 30%.

Comparing fractions

To decide which of a/b and c/d is larger, compare a × d with c × b. For 347/521 and 412/617: 347 × 617 = 2,14,099 and 412 × 521 = 2,14,652, so 412/617 is slightly larger. When fractions are far apart, compare each with a benchmark like 2/3 instead.

Percentage change by fractions

An increase of 12.5% means × 9/8, 20% means × 6/5 and 25% means × 5/4. So 6,480 grown by 12.5% is 6,480 + 6,480/8 = 6,480 + 810 = 7,290. For the change between two values, look for a simple ratio: 2,450 → 2,940 is a rise of 490, which is one-fifth of 2,450, i.e. 20%.

Assumed-mean averages

For numbers close together, pick a convenient middle value, average the deviations and add. For 528, 532, 541, 519 and 530, deviations from 530 are −2, +2, +11, −11, 0, which average 0, so the mean is 530.

Mean = assumed mean + average deviation

Approximate early, exact late

Round figures to two or three significant digits for a first answer. Only if two options fall within about 2% of each other should you go back and calculate exactly.

Shortcuts and tricks

  • x% of y = y% of x: 24% of 50 = 50% of 24 = 12.
  • Percentage growth comparison: compare increase ÷ base, not raw increases — 240 → 300 (25%) beats 360 → 440 (22.2%).
  • For ratios like 3,600 : 4,800, cancel common factors first (36 : 48 = 3 : 4).
  • Multiplying by 1.5 = adding half; by 0.75 = subtracting a quarter; by 1.25 = adding a quarter.
  • When options differ widely, estimate with one significant figure and pick immediately.

Worked examples

Example 1

Which is larger: 347/521 or 412/617?

Solution

Cross-multiply: 347 × 617 = 2,14,099; 412 × 521 = 2,14,652. The second product is larger, so 412/617 > 347/521. Both are close to 2/3, which is why a benchmark alone was not enough here.

Example 2

Approximately what percent of 6,112 is 1,847?

Solution

1,847/6,112 ≈ 1,850/6,100. Since 6,100 × 0.3 = 1,830, the answer is a little over 30% — about 30.2%.

Example 3

A value of 6,480 grows by 12.5%. Find the new value.

Solution

12.5% = 1/8, and 6,480/8 = 810. New value = 6,480 + 810 = 7,290.

Example 4

Find the percentage change from 2,450 to 2,940.

Solution

Change = 490. Since 2,450 ÷ 5 = 490, the change is one-fifth, i.e. 20%.

Example 5

Which grew faster: A from 240 to 300, or B from 360 to 440?

Solution

A: 60/240 = 25%. B: 80/360 ≈ 22.2%. A grew faster even though B’s absolute increase was larger.

Example 6

Find the average of 528, 532, 541, 519 and 530.

Solution

Assume 530. Deviations: −2, +2, +11, −11, 0; total 0. Average = 530.

Practice questions

Question 1

Which is larger: 5/7 or 7/10?

  1. 5/7
  2. 7/10
  3. Both are equal
  4. Cannot be determined
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 5/7

Cross-multiplying gives 5 × 10 = 50 and 7 × 7 = 49, so 5/7 is slightly larger than 7/10.

Question 2

3,600 is what percent of 4,800?

  1. 70%
  2. 80%
  3. 66.67%
  4. 75%
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 75%

3,600/4,800 simplifies to 3/4 after cancelling 1,200, and three-quarters is 75 percent.

Question 3

16.67% of 7,230 is:

  1. 1,250
  2. 1,446
  3. 1,205
  4. 1,180
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 1,205

16.67% is one-sixth, and 7,230 ÷ 6 = 1,205, so no long multiplication is needed.

Question 4

The percentage change from 1,250 to 1,100 is:

  1. 12% decrease
  2. 15% decrease
  3. 12% increase
  4. 13.6% decrease
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 12% decrease

The fall is 150 on a base of 1,250, and 150/1,250 = 0.12, a 12 percent decrease.

Question 5

The average of 248, 252, 255 and 245 is:

  1. 251
  2. 250
  3. 249
  4. 252.5
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 250

Deviations from 250 are −2, +2, +5 and −5, which sum to zero, so the average is exactly 250.

FAQs

Is it worth learning Vedic maths for DI?

A few techniques help — multiplying numbers near a base, squaring numbers ending in 5 — but the biggest gains come from fraction benchmarks and approximation, which apply to nearly every DI question. Learn those first and add other tricks only if they feel natural.

How accurate do DI calculations need to be?

Usually only accurate enough to separate the options. If options are 10% apart, rough estimates work. If two options are within 1–2%, calculate more carefully. Looking at the options before calculating tells you how precise you need to be.
