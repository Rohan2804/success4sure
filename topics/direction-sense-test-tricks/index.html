Direction Sense Test: Turns, Distances and Shadow Questions

Direction sense questions appear regularly in SSC CGL, CHSL and MTS and in IBPS and SBI prelims, where the newer form is a point-based set ('A is 5 m east of B...') with three questions. Most problems need only two tools: tracking which way a person faces after each turn, and Pythagoras for the straight-line distance. Shadow questions add a small twist based on the position of the sun.

Last updated 23 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · Free

The eight directions

North at the top, east on the right, south at the bottom, west on the left. The four corners are north-east, south-east, south-west and north-west. A person facing north has east on the right; a person facing south has east on the left. Always turn relative to the direction the person is facing.

Turns in degrees

A right turn is 90° clockwise and a left turn is 90° anticlockwise. A 45° clockwise turn from north gives north-east. For multiple turns, add clockwise angles and subtract anticlockwise ones, then reduce modulo 360.

Shortest distance

Net out all east–west movement and all north–south movement separately, then combine them with Pythagoras.

Distance = √(net east–west² + net north–south²)

Shadow rule

In the morning the sun is in the east, so shadows fall to the west. In the evening the sun is in the west and shadows fall to the east. At noon in India the shadow is very short and is generally ignored in these questions.

Shortcuts and tricks

  • Treat the start as (0, 0); east is +x and north is +y. Add each leg as a coordinate change.
  • Common Pythagorean triples save time: 3-4-5, 6-8-10, 5-12-13, 8-15-17.
  • If the final x and y are both positive, the person is north-east of the start; both negative means south-west.
  • Three left turns equal one right turn, and two turns either way reverse the direction.
  • For shadow questions, first fix where the shadow points (west in the morning), then work out which side of the person that is.

Worked examples

Example 1

Sameer walks 6 km north, turns right and walks 8 km. How far and in which direction is he from the start?

Solution

After facing north, a right turn makes him face east. Position: 8 km east, 6 km north. Distance = √(8² + 6²) = √100 = 10 km, towards the north-east.

Example 2

Pooja walks 5 km east, turns left and walks 3 km, turns left and walks 9 km, then turns left and walks 3 km. Where is she now?

Solution

East 5 → (5, 0). Left from east is north: (5, 3). Left from north is west, 9 km: (−4, 3). Left from west is south, 3 km: (−4, 0). She is 4 km west of the start.

Example 3

Early one morning Ravi stands facing a pole. The pole's shadow falls exactly to his right. Which direction is Ravi facing?

Solution

In the morning the sun is in the east, so the shadow points west. The shadow is on his right, so his right side is west. A person whose right is west is facing south.

Example 4

Point P is 4 m west of point Q. Point R is 3 m north of Q. What is the distance and direction of R from P?

Solution

Take Q at (0, 0). P is (−4, 0), R is (0, 3). From P, R is 4 m east and 3 m north. Distance = √(16 + 9) = 5 m, and R lies north-east of P.

Practice questions

Question 1

Anil walks 12 m south, turns left and walks 5 m. How far is he from his starting point?

  1. 13 m
  2. 17 m
  3. 7 m
  4. 15 m
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 13 m

Turning left while facing south makes him face east. He is 12 m south and 5 m east, so the distance is √(144 + 25) = 13 m.

Question 2

A man faces north. He turns 90° clockwise, then 180° anticlockwise, then 45° clockwise. Which direction does he face now?

  1. North-east
  2. North-west
  3. South-west
  4. West
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. North-west

North plus 90° clockwise is east. Turning 180° anticlockwise from east gives west. A further 45° clockwise from west points north-west.

Question 3

Kiran walks 10 km west, turns right and walks 4 km, then turns right again and walks 10 km. Where is Kiran from the start?

  1. 10 km east
  2. 4 km south
  3. 4 km north
  4. 14 km north-west
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 4 km north

West 10, then right (north) 4, then right (east) 10. The east and west legs cancel, leaving Kiran 4 km north of the start.

Question 4

In the evening, Meena stands so that her shadow falls directly behind her. Which direction is she facing?

  1. East
  2. North
  3. South
  4. West
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. West

In the evening the sun is in the west, so shadows fall towards the east. If the shadow is behind her, her back is to the east and she faces west.

Question 5

Point M is 6 km east of point N. Point O is 8 km south of M. How far and in which direction is O from N?

  1. 10 km south-east
  2. 14 km south-east
  3. 10 km south-west
  4. 10 km north-east
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 10 km south-east

O is 6 km east and 8 km south of N. The distance is √(36 + 64) = 10 km, and the direction is south-east.

FAQs

How do I avoid left-right confusion while turning?

Write the current facing direction after every turn. Facing north, right is east; facing south, right is west; facing east, right is south; facing west, right is north. Writing one arrow per leg on your rough sheet is quicker than rotating mentally and avoids the most common mistake.

Do shadow questions consider noon?

Most exam questions use early morning or evening, when the sun is clearly in the east or west. At noon the shadow is directly below or very short in India, so questions usually avoid it. If a question mentions noon, read the options carefully for 'no shadow' or 'cannot be determined'.
