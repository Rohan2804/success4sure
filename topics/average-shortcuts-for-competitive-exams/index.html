Average Shortcuts: Replacement, Cricket and Speed Questions

Averages give at least one question in most IBPS, SBI and SSC papers and form the hidden logic behind DI questions that ask for "average sales per year". The chapter rewards a deviation mindset: instead of adding everything up, track how far each new value sits from the existing average. This approach turns teacher-joins-the-class, batsman-innings and replacement questions into one-line calculations.

Last updated 23 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · Free

Definition and total

Average = sum of observations ÷ number of observations. Most questions are easier if you convert averages into totals: an average of 52 for 40 students means a total of 2,080 marks. Work with totals when people join or leave the group, then divide at the end.

Total = Average × Count

Deviation method

When a new value joins, the change in average equals (new value − old average) ÷ new count. A new person who is 30 kg heavier than the existing average of 9 people raises the average by 30/10 = 3 kg. This avoids computing totals altogether.

Replacement

If one member of a group of n is replaced and the average rises by d, the newcomer is n × d heavier (or older) than the person who left. For 8 people and an increase of 2.5 kg, the newcomer weighs 20 kg more than the person replaced.

New − Old = n × change in average

Average speed

For equal distances at speeds x and y, the average speed is 2xy/(x + y), the harmonic mean, not (x + y)/2. For equal times, it is the simple mean. Going at 40 km/h and returning at 60 km/h gives 48 km/h, not 50.

Average speed = 2xy / (x + y)

Shortcuts and tricks

  • Average of consecutive numbers is the middle number (or the mean of the two middle numbers).
  • Average of the first n natural numbers = (n + 1)/2; of the first n even numbers = n + 1; of the first n odd numbers = n.
  • Wrong entry: corrected average = old average ± (difference ÷ count). Reading 75 instead of 45 among 50 numbers lowers the true average by 30/50 = 0.6.
  • Overlapping groups: if the first six and last six of eleven numbers share the sixth, that number = sum of both groups − total of all eleven.
  • In cricket questions, the run scored in the new innings = new average + (old innings × increase).

Worked examples

Example 1

The average age of 30 students is 14 years. When the teacher’s age is included, the average becomes 15 years. Find the teacher’s age.

Solution

Total with teacher = 31 × 15 = 465; total of students = 30 × 14 = 420. Teacher’s age = 45 years. Deviation view: the teacher adds 1 year to each of 31 people, so she is 31 years above 14, i.e. 45.

Example 2

A batsman scores 87 runs in his 17th innings and thereby raises his average by 3. Find his average after the 17th innings.

Solution

Let the old average be a. Then 16a + 87 = 17(a + 3), so a = 87 − 51 = 36. New average = 39. Shortcut: new average = 87 − 16 × 3 = 39.

Example 3

The average of 50 numbers is 38. Later it is found that 45 was wrongly read as 75. Find the correct average.

Solution

The total was overstated by 30, so the average was overstated by 30/50 = 0.6. Correct average = 38 − 0.6 = 37.4.

Example 4

A cyclist rides from Pune to Lonavala at 40 km/h and returns at 60 km/h. Find the average speed for the round trip.

Solution

Distances are equal, so average speed = 2 × 40 × 60/(40 + 60) = 4,800/100 = 48 km/h.

Practice questions

Question 1

The average of the first 20 even natural numbers is:

  1. 21
  2. 20
  3. 22
  4. 19
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 21

The first n even numbers average n + 1, so for n = 20 the average is 21 (sum 420 ÷ 20).

Question 2

The average weight of 8 people rises by 2.5 kg when a person weighing 65 kg is replaced by a new person. The new person weighs:

  1. 80 kg
  2. 67.5 kg
  3. 90 kg
  4. 85 kg
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 85 kg

The newcomer is 8 × 2.5 = 20 kg heavier than the person replaced, so the weight is 65 + 20 = 85 kg.

Question 3

The average of 11 numbers is 50. The average of the first six is 49 and of the last six is 52. The sixth number is:

  1. 50
  2. 56
  3. 54
  4. 58
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 56

Sixth number = 6 × 49 + 6 × 52 − 11 × 50 = 294 + 312 − 550 = 56, as it is counted twice.

Question 4

A batsman scores 90 runs in his 12th innings and his average increases by 4. His new average is:

  1. 42
  2. 44
  3. 46
  4. 48
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 46

New average = 90 − 11 × 4 = 46, because the extra runs above the new average lift the 11 earlier innings by 4 each.

Question 5

The average of three numbers is 20. When a fourth number is added, the average becomes 22. The fourth number is:

  1. 28
  2. 24
  3. 26
  4. 30
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 28

The new total is 4 × 22 = 88 and the old total is 60, so the fourth number is 28.

FAQs

When should I use the deviation method instead of totals?

Use deviations when one value joins, leaves or is replaced and the change in average is small and given. It saves multiplication of large totals. When several groups merge, such as two sections of a class, totals are usually clearer and less error-prone.

Why is average speed not the simple mean of two speeds?

Because you spend more time at the slower speed when distances are equal. Average speed is total distance divided by total time, which gives the harmonic mean 2xy/(x + y). The simple mean applies only when equal time is spent at each speed.
