Alphanumeric Series: Counting, Position and Symbol Rules

Alphanumeric series questions give a line of letters, digits and symbols and ask you to count or locate elements under a condition. They are a favourite of IBPS Clerk, SBI Clerk and IBPS RRB style papers, and sometimes appear in PO prelims as a three-to-five-question set. Nothing here needs reasoning skill so much as disciplined counting. A clean method — numbering the series once and reusing it — turns this into one of the fastest scoring sets in the paper.

Last updated 23 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · Free

Number the series once

Write small position numbers under every element before reading any question. Every later question ('7th from the left', '12th from the right') then becomes a lookup instead of a fresh count. With 22 elements, 12th from the right is position 22 − 12 + 1 = 11 from the left.

Relative position questions

'Which element is 5th to the left of the 12th from the right?' has two steps: find the reference element, then move. Moving left reduces the left-position; moving right increases it. Do the arithmetic on positions, not by counting with your finger.

Immediately preceded and followed

'A number immediately preceded by a letter' means the element just before the number is a letter. 'Followed by' looks at the element just after. Questions combining both ('symbols preceded by a number and followed by a letter') require checking three consecutive elements.

Removal and rearrangement

Some questions say 'if all symbols are removed' or 'if the series is reversed'. Write the new series quickly in a separate line instead of adjusting positions mentally.

Shortcuts and tricks

  • k-th from the right in a series of n = (n − k + 1)-th from the left.
  • For 'preceded by' questions, scan pairs left to right with two fingers; it halves the error rate.
  • Vowels are A, E, I, O, U — mark them once with a dot if questions refer to vowels.
  • When symbols are removed, only positions after the first symbol change; count from there.
  • Middle element of an odd-length series of n is at position (n + 1) / 2; for even n there are two middle elements.

Worked examples

Example 1

Series: R 4 # T 7 K @ 2 M 9 $ B 5 E % 3 W D 8 & J 6. Which element is 7th from the left, and how many elements are there in all?

Solution

Numbering gives R(1), 4(2), #(3), T(4), 7(5), K(6), @(7). So the 7th element is @. Counting to the end gives 22 elements.

Example 2

In the same series, which element is 5th to the left of the 12th element from the right?

Solution

12th from the right is position 22 − 12 + 1 = 11 from the left, which is $. Five to the left is position 6: K.

Example 3

In the same series, how many numbers are immediately preceded by a letter?

Solution

Check each number: 4 (after R) yes, 7 (after T) yes, 2 (after @) no, 9 (after M) yes, 5 (after B) yes, 3 (after %) no, 8 (after D) yes, 6 (after J) yes. Total: 6.

Example 4

In the same series, how many symbols are immediately preceded by a number and immediately followed by a letter?

Solution

Symbols: # (4 before, T after) yes; @ (K before) no; $ (9 before, B after) yes; % (E before) no; & (8 before, J after) yes. Answer: 3.

Practice questions

Question 1

Series: R 4 # T 7 K @ 2 M 9 $ B 5 E % 3 W D 8 & J 6. If all the symbols are removed, which element is 8th from the right?

  1. E
  2. 3
  3. B
  4. 5
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 5

Without symbols the series reads R 4 T 7 K 2 M 9 B 5 E 3 W D 8 J 6, which has 17 elements. Counting eight from the right lands on 5.

Question 2

In the series R 4 # T 7 K @ 2 M 9 $ B 5 E % 3 W D 8 & J 6, how many symbols are there?

  1. Five
  2. Four
  3. Six
  4. Three
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. Five

The symbols are #, @, $, % and &, which makes five symbols in a series of 22 elements.

Question 3

In the series R 4 # T 7 K @ 2 M 9 $ B 5 E % 3 W D 8 & J 6, which element is 7th from the right end?

  1. W
  2. 3
  3. E
  4. %
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 3

With 22 elements, 7th from the right is position 22 − 7 + 1 = 16 from the left. Counting E as 14 and % as 15, position 16 is the digit 3.

Question 4

In the series R 4 # T 7 K @ 2 M 9 $ B 5 E % 3 W D 8 & J 6, which element is 4th to the right of the 11th element from the left?

  1. E
  2. 5
  3. %
  4. 3
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. %

The 11th element from the left is $. Moving four places to the right reaches position 15, which is the symbol %.

Question 5

In the series R 4 # T 7 K @ 2 M 9 $ B 5 E % 3 W D 8 & J 6, how many vowels are there?

  1. Two
  2. None
  3. Three
  4. One

Answer: D. One

The only vowel among the letters R, T, K, M, B, E, W, D and J is E, so there is exactly one vowel.

FAQs

How can I avoid counting errors in long series?

Write position numbers under the series once, in small digits, before attempting any question. Convert every 'from the right' position into a left position with n − k + 1. This takes about 30 seconds but saves repeated counting across five questions and removes most errors.

Are alphanumeric series still asked in bank exams?

They appear less often in PO prelims than a decade ago but remain common in clerk-level and RRB-style papers. Because they need no concept learning, a few practice sets are enough to make them a reliable source of quick marks whenever they do appear.
