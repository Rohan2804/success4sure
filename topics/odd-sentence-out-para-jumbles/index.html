Odd Sentence Out: Spotting the Sentence That Does Not Fit

Odd sentence out (odd one out in para jumbles) gives four or five sentences, all but one of which form a coherent paragraph. You must find the sentence that does not belong. CAT asks a few every year in its verbal section, and IBPS PO, SBI PO and RBI Grade B mains have used similar 'find the incoherent sentence' formats. The trap is that the odd sentence is usually on the same broad topic; it differs in focus, time frame or tone rather than subject.

Last updated 17 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · Free

Build the paragraph first

Try to arrange the sentences into a logical order, as in a para jumble. The sentence that refuses to attach to any other, or breaks the chain wherever you place it, is the odd one.

Same topic, different focus

If four sentences discuss the causes of urban flooding and one discusses the history of a city's drainage department, all mention drainage, but the fifth shifts focus. Topic overlap alone does not make a sentence fit.

Linking words and pronouns

Sentences in a coherent paragraph are linked by pronouns ('this', 'they'), repeated nouns and connectors. A sentence with no link to the others, or whose pronoun has nothing to refer to, is a strong candidate.

Tone and perspective

A shift from neutral analysis to personal opinion, or from general discussion to a narrow anecdote, often marks the odd sentence.

Time frame

If four sentences describe current trends and one describes a historical event without a bridge to the present, the historical one may be the odd sentence.

Watch for the decoy

Setters sometimes include a sentence that seems odd because it introduces a contrast ('However…'), but it actually fits as the turn in the argument. Check whether a contrast sentence links back to an earlier point before marking it odd.

Shortcuts and tricks

  • Find a pair of sentences that clearly link; build outward from that pair.
  • Identify the paragraph's specific focus in one phrase, then test each sentence against it.
  • A sentence stating a general fact that the others do not develop is often the odd one.
  • Do not mark a sentence odd just because it starts with 'However' or 'But'.
  • In CAT, write the sentence numbers in your arranged order on the rough sheet; the leftover number is your answer.
  • If two sentences seem odd, the one that cannot be placed anywhere is the answer; the other usually fits at the start or end.

Worked examples

Example 1

1. Small finance banks were set up to serve underbanked customers. 2. They focus on small loans to micro-enterprises and farmers. 3. Many of them grew out of microfinance institutions. 4. The Reserve Bank of India was established in 1935. 5. Their deposit base has been growing steadily. Which sentence is odd?

Solution

Sentences 1, 2, 3 and 5 discuss small finance banks, linked by 'They', 'them' and 'Their'. Sentence 4 mentions banking but gives an unrelated historical fact about the RBI. Answer: 4.

Example 2

1. Regular exercise improves heart health. 2. It also helps manage stress. 3. However, many urban professionals find little time for it. 4. Short workouts at home can solve this problem. 5. Gyms charge high membership fees in metro cities. Which is odd?

Solution

1–4 form a chain: benefit, more benefit, problem (time), solution. Sentence 5 shifts to gym costs, which none of the others discuss. Note that 3, starting with 'However', fits as the turn. Answer: 5.

Example 3

1. Monsoon rainfall is vital for Indian agriculture. 2. A weak monsoon can reduce kharif output. 3. This often pushes up food prices. 4. Higher food prices affect household budgets. 5. The word 'monsoon' comes from an Arabic word for season. Which is odd?

Solution

1–4 form a cause–effect chain from rainfall to budgets. Sentence 5 is a word-origin fact with no link to the chain. Answer: 5.

Question 1

  1. Sentence 1
  2. Sentence 3
  3. Sentence 2
  4. Sentence 4

Answer: B. Sentence 3

Sentences 1, 2 and 4 discuss online learning platforms and are linked by 'They' and 'Many'. Sentence 3 is an unrelated historical fact.

Question 2

Which feature most often marks the odd sentence?

  1. It starts with 'However'
  2. It is the longest sentence
  3. It shares the broad topic but shifts focus
  4. It contains a number
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. It shares the broad topic but shifts focus

The odd sentence usually stays on the broad subject but moves to a different focus or time frame. A 'However' sentence often fits as the turn in the argument.

Question 3

1. Electric vehicles produce no tailpipe emissions. 2. Their batteries, however, need critical minerals. 3. Mining these minerals raises environmental concerns. 4. Cricket is the most popular sport in India. Which is odd?

  1. Sentence 1
  2. Sentence 2
  3. Sentence 3
  4. Sentence 4

Answer: D. Sentence 4

Sentences 1 to 3 form a chain about electric vehicles, batteries and mining. Sentence 4 has no link to that chain, so it does not belong.

Question 4

1. Savings rates among young earners have fallen. 2. Rising rents take a large share of income. 3. Easy credit encourages spending. 4. The rupee was decimalised in 1957. Which is odd?

  1. Sentence 4
  2. Sentence 1
  3. Sentence 2
  4. Sentence 3

Answer: A. Sentence 4

Sentences 1 to 3 discuss why young earners save less. Sentence 4 is a historical fact about currency that does not explain falling savings.

Question 5

When two sentences both seem odd, how should you decide?

  1. Choose the shorter one
  2. Choose the one that cannot be placed anywhere in the paragraph
  3. Choose the one with a pronoun
  4. Choose the first one listed
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. Choose the one that cannot be placed anywhere in the paragraph

One of the two usually fits at the start or end as an introduction or conclusion. The sentence that cannot be linked anywhere is the real odd one out.

FAQs

Is odd sentence out asked with options in CAT?

In recent CAT patterns it has usually been a type-in-the-answer question without options, so you must enter the number of the odd sentence. Check the official pattern for the current year. Without options, careful paragraph-building matters even more than in MCQ versions.

How is it different from a normal para jumble?

In a para jumble every sentence belongs and you find the order. In odd sentence out, one sentence does not belong, so you build the paragraph and identify the leftover. The skills overlap heavily, so practising both together is efficient.
