Successive Percentage Change, Population and Depreciation

Successive percentage change is the idea behind back-to-back price hikes, salary cuts followed by raises, population growth and asset depreciation. IBPS and SBI prelims use it in arithmetic word problems and in quantity-comparison pairs, while SSC CGL frames it as area and volume change when dimensions change. CAT and CMAT hide it inside multi-step growth questions. One clean formula handles all of these, provided you keep signs straight for decreases.

Last updated 23 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · Free

The net-change formula

When a quantity changes by a% and then by b%, the overall change is a + b + ab/100 percent. Use a negative sign for a decrease. For +20% and −10%, the net change is 20 − 10 − 2 = +8%. For three changes, apply the formula to the first two, then combine that result with the third.

Net % = a + b + ab/100

Equal rise and fall

A rise of x% followed by a fall of x% (or the reverse) always gives a net loss of x²/100 percent. A salary cut of 25% followed by a raise of 25% leaves the employee 6.25% poorer, not back to the start, because the raise is calculated on a smaller base.

Population growth

If a town’s population grows at r% a year, after n years it is P(1 + r/100)ⁿ. This is the compound-interest formula with people instead of rupees. For different rates in different years, multiply the separate factors, e.g. 1.05 × 1.08.

Pₙ = P(1 + r/100)ⁿ

Depreciation

Machines, vehicles and computers lose value at a fixed percentage of their current value each year. Value after n years = P(1 − r/100)ⁿ. The amount lost in the first year is larger than in later years because each year’s fall is calculated on a lower value.

Vₙ = P(1 − r/100)ⁿ

Shortcuts and tricks

  • Area change when both sides change: use a + b + ab/100. Length +10%, breadth +10% gives area +21%.
  • Radius change r% in a circle: area changes by 2r + r²/100 percent. Radius +10% gives area +21%.
  • For three equal rises of 10%, remember 1.331: the total rise is 33.1%.
  • Price rises 25% then falls 20%: 25 − 20 − 5 = 0, so the price is back to the original. Watch for this pair in options.
  • Depreciation of 10% for two years = factor 0.81; of 20% for two years = 0.64. Learn these factors to skip multiplication.
  • To reverse a change, divide by the factor: if a value after a 25% rise is ₹500, the original is 500 ÷ 1.25 = ₹400.

Worked examples

Example 1

The price of a refrigerator is increased by 10% and then by 20%. What is the overall percentage increase?

Solution

Net = 10 + 20 + (10 × 20)/100 = 30 + 2 = 32%. Check with ₹100: after 10% it is ₹110; 20% more makes it ₹132, an increase of 32%.

Example 2

A town has 50,000 people and the population grows at 4% a year. What will it be after 2 years?

Solution

Population = 50,000 × 1.04 × 1.04 = 50,000 × 1.0816 = 54,080.

Example 3

A printing machine costing ₹2,00,000 depreciates at 10% a year. What is its value after 3 years?

Solution

Value = 2,00,000 × 0.9³ = 2,00,000 × 0.729 = ₹1,45,800. The total fall is ₹54,200, which is 27.1%, not 30%.

Example 4

The length of a rectangular plot is increased by 20% and its breadth is decreased by 10%. Find the percentage change in area.

Solution

Area change = 20 + (−10) + (20 × −10)/100 = 20 − 10 − 2 = +8%. The area increases by 8%.

Example 5

An employee’s salary is cut by 25% and later increased by 25%. What is the net effect?

Solution

Equal rise and fall gives a loss of 25²/100 = 6.25%. On ₹40,000 the salary first becomes ₹30,000 and then ₹37,500, which is ₹2,500 or 6.25% lower.

Practice questions

Question 1

A price is increased by 30% and then decreased by 20%. The net change is:

  1. 4% increase
  2. 10% increase
  3. 4% decrease
  4. 6% increase
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 4% increase

Net change = 30 − 20 − (30 × 20)/100 = 10 − 6 = +4%, so the price rises by 4%.

Question 2

A village has 64,000 people. If its population falls by 5% every year, what will it be after 2 years?

  1. 57,600
  2. 58,240
  3. 57,760
  4. 57,800
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 57,760

Population = 64,000 × 0.95 × 0.95 = 64,000 × 0.9025 = 57,760 after two years.

Question 3

If the radius of a circle is increased by 10%, its area increases by:

  1. 10%
  2. 20%
  3. 11%
  4. 21%
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 21%

Area depends on r², so the change is 10 + 10 + (10 × 10)/100 = 21 percent.

Question 4

A scooter bought for ₹80,000 depreciates at 15% a year. What is its value after 2 years?

  1. ₹57,800
  2. ₹56,000
  3. ₹58,800
  4. ₹57,200
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. ₹57,800

Value = 80,000 × 0.85² = 80,000 × 0.7225 = ₹57,800 after two years of depreciation.

Question 5

A price rises by 10% three years in a row. The total rise is:

  1. 30%
  2. 33%
  3. 33.1%
  4. 31.1%
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 33.1%

The factor is 1.1³ = 1.331, so the total rise is 33.1 percent over the original price.

FAQs

Is successive percentage the same as compound interest?

The arithmetic is the same: both multiply growth factors one after another. Compound interest is the special case where the rate is the same every period and is applied to money. The a + b + ab/100 rule works for any two changes, equal or different, increases or decreases.

How do I handle three successive changes quickly?

Combine the first two with a + b + ab/100, then combine that answer with the third using the same formula. Alternatively multiply the factors, for example 1.1 × 0.9 × 1.2, if the numbers are friendly. Both methods give the same result.
