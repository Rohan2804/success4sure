Linear Equations in Two Variables: Exam Word Problems

Linear equations are the hidden engine behind ages, partnership, mixtures and many data-sufficiency statements. SSC CGL and CHSL ask direct questions on pairs of equations and word problems, IBPS PO and SBI PO use them inside arithmetic and DS, and CAT expects fast algebraic translation. The main skills are writing the right equations from words and solving them with the least arithmetic, often by adding or subtracting the equations whole.

Last updated 20 November 2026 · By the Success4Sure editorial team · Free

Elimination

Make the coefficient of one variable equal in both equations and add or subtract. For 3x + 2y = 16 and 5x − 2y = 16, adding eliminates y: 8x = 32, x = 4, then y = 2. Elimination is usually the fastest method in exams.

Symmetric equations

When the equations are mirror images, such as 5p + 3n = 110 and 3p + 5n = 130, add them to get p + n and subtract them to get p − n. Then solve the two simple equations. This trick saves time in price-of-items questions.

(sum + difference)/2 = larger

Translating word problems

Name each unknown, write one equation for each fact and check units. Coins: count equation and value equation. Digits: a two-digit number with tens digit a and units digit b is 10a + b; reversing gives 10b + a, so the difference is 9(a − b).

Consistency

For a₁x + b₁y = c₁ and a₂x + b₂y = c₂: a unique solution if a₁/a₂ ≠ b₁/b₂; no solution if a₁/a₂ = b₁/b₂ ≠ c₁/c₂ (parallel lines); infinitely many if all three ratios are equal (same line).

No solution: a₁/a₂ = b₁/b₂ ≠ c₁/c₂

Shortcuts and tricks

  • Two numbers from sum S and difference D: (S + D)/2 and (S − D)/2.
  • Reversing a two-digit number changes it by 9 × (difference of digits).
  • Substitute options back into both equations — often faster than solving in MCQs.
  • If the question asks for x + y, look for a combination of equations that gives it directly.
  • Keep fractions as fractions until the last step to avoid rounding errors.

Worked examples

Example 1

Solve 3x + 2y = 16 and 5x − 2y = 16.

Solution

Add: 8x = 32, so x = 4. Then 3(4) + 2y = 16 gives y = 2.

Example 2

The sum of two numbers is 50 and their difference is 14. Find the numbers.

Solution

Larger = (50 + 14)/2 = 32, smaller = (50 − 14)/2 = 18.

Example 3

A purse has 40 coins of ₹2 and ₹5, worth ₹119 in all. How many ₹5 coins are there?

Solution

x + y = 40 and 2x + 5y = 119. Subtract twice the first: 3y = 39, y = 13. So 13 coins of ₹5 and 27 of ₹2.

Example 4

A fraction becomes 1/2 when 1 is added to its numerator and 1/3 when 1 is added to its denominator. Find the fraction.

Solution

(n + 1)/d = 1/2 gives d = 2n + 2; n/(d + 1) = 1/3 gives d = 3n − 1. So 2n + 2 = 3n − 1, n = 3 and d = 8. The fraction is 3/8.

Example 5

For what value of k does kx + 2y = 5, 3x + y = 1 have no solution?

Solution

Need k/3 = 2/1 ≠ 5/1. So k = 6; the third ratio 5 ≠ 2 confirms the lines are parallel. Geometrically, both equations then describe lines with the same slope but different intercepts, so they never meet and no (x, y) satisfies both.

Practice questions

Question 1

If 2x + 3y = 13 and x − y = −1, then x + y = ?

  1. 4
  2. 6
  3. 5
  4. 7
Show answer & explanation

Answer: C. 5

From x = y − 1, 2(y − 1) + 3y = 13 gives y = 3 and x = 2, so x + y = 5.

Question 2

The sum of two numbers is 75 and their difference is 15. The larger number is:

  1. 30
  2. 45
  3. 40
  4. 50
Show answer & explanation

Answer: B. 45

Larger number = (sum + difference)/2 = (75 + 15)/2 = 45, and the smaller is 30.

Question 3

5 pens and 3 notebooks cost ₹110; 3 pens and 5 notebooks cost ₹130. The price of a notebook is:

  1. ₹10
  2. ₹15
  3. ₹25
  4. ₹20
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. ₹20

Adding gives p + n = 30 and subtracting gives n − p = 10, so a notebook costs ₹20.

Question 4

The digits of a two-digit number add up to 9. Reversing the digits increases the number by 27. The number is:

  1. 45
  2. 27
  3. 63
  4. 36
Show answer & explanation

Answer: D. 36

a + b = 9 and 9(b − a) = 27 give b − a = 3, so a = 3, b = 6 and the number is 36.

Question 5

For what value of k do 2x + ky = 7 and 4x + 6y = 9 have no solution?

  1. 3
  2. 2
  3. 6
  4. 12
Show answer & explanation

Answer: A. 3

Need 2/4 = k/6, so k = 3, while 7/9 is different from 1/2, confirming parallel lines.

FAQs

Which method is fastest for two linear equations in exams?

Elimination is usually fastest, especially when coefficients match or are mirror images. Substitution works well when one equation already gives a variable directly, like x = y − 1. In MCQs, substituting the options is often quickest of all.

How do linear equations appear in data sufficiency?

Each statement often gives one equation. Two independent equations in two unknowns are sufficient together; one alone usually is not. Watch for statements that are multiples of each other — they give the same line and are not independent.
